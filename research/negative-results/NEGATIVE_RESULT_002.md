# Negative Result 002 — Fights in Tight Spaces is derivative recurrence

- Date: 2026-08-12
- Original claim: Fights in Tight Spaces passes the Protocol 002 lineage gate
  as an independent-family supporter candidate for the previewed hostile-
  disruption loop.
- Claim status: `Confirmed`
- Evidence quality: `Direct`
- Confidence: `High`
- Outcome: rejected.

## Evidence

The [Mode 7 publisher design account](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/?p=145986)
states that Fights in Tight Spaces was pitched as a combination of Slay the
Spire's deckbuilding strategy and Into the Breach's positional tactics. It also
documents a physical-board prototype, but independent implementation medium
does not negate the named design lineage for the interaction under test.

[`GAME-0047`](../../knowledge/games/a-f/fights-in-tight-spaces.md) independently
verified the mechanical facts: committed attack preview, displacement,
friendly fire and ordered hostile resolution recur. It also rejected three
genes from the larger `COMB-0014`, producing the smaller recurring
[`COMB-0047`](../../knowledge/combinations/COMB-0047.md).

## Rejection reason

Protocol 002 separates mechanical recurrence from independent-family
recurrence. A distinct hand / momentum action economy and separate studio are
not sufficient when the publisher explicitly names the target game's
positional tactics as a design source. The earlier search applied its lineage
gate inconsistently: it excluded Fogpiercer for declared inspiration while
allowing substantially similar disclosure for Fights in Tight Spaces.

## Affected records

- `GAME-0047` remains a valid reviewed game and its complete genome is unchanged.
- `COMB-0047` remains a valid recurring interaction, now explicitly labelled
  derivative recurrence rather than independent-family evidence.
- `COMB-0014` remains supported only by Into the Breach.
- `INDEPENDENT_RECURRENCE_SEARCH_001` is corrected in place and cannot terminate
  the independent-recurrence question positively.
- No gene lifecycle, genome signature or taxonomy change is justified.

## Method consequence

Schedule `INDEPENDENT_RECURRENCE_SEARCH_002` with every declared Into the Breach
derivative, including Fights in Tight Spaces and Fogpiercer, excluded from
positive termination. Apply the same source-disclosure standard regardless of
how different the surrounding action economy appears.
