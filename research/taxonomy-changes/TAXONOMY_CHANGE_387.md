  weapon. ACT-294 instead configures sides and control types
  of a fixed match. ACT-459 covers live stance.
- SYS-1850 allows an opposed weapon contact and a separate
  no-wound balance loss. SYS-625 classifies directional guards.
  SYS-1787 classifies partial and perfect resistance by timing.
- SYS-1851 removes affected fighting capabilities through
  local impairment. SYS-402 instead opens monster wounds
  for a distinct Focus Strike and its resulting rewards.
- SYS-1852 permits decisive contact without needing to
  deplete a displayed pool or win successive rounds.
  SYS-522 owns the separate round cycle and result count.
- SYS-1853 encodes replay followed by a distinct live
  Story successor. SYS-1000 couples Arcade succession with
  a same-stage Continue decision after loss.
- INF-595 distinguishes cues and stance without a numeric
  health bar or match timer. INF-210 discloses paired
  vitality, match state and a clock.
