# Taxonomy Change 082: Isolate spatial lures, staged alarm recovery and conditional perception radar

## Status

- Proposal status: `Accepted`
- Claim status: `Confirmed`
- Evidence quality: `Direct`
- Confidence: `High`
- Date: 2026-09-21
- Trigger: `GAME-0339` Metal Gear Solid lower-ID scan.
- Scope: new `ACT-489`, `SYS-958` and `INF-364`; supporting-carrier
  evidence for fourteen reused genes; no earlier game signature, combination,
  family, wording or lifecycle change.

## Problem

The stable vocabulary already represented direct movement, posture, contextual
fixtures, perception-driven investigation, live combat, checkpoint recovery,
directed visibility, authored gates, bounded survival and local information.
It did not distinguish three causal structures in the original Metal Gear
Solid opening: a deliberate non-damaging sound placed at adjacent geometry;
recovery from detection through two named search phases with reacquisition;
and a conditional radar that joins local actor markers to directed perception
regions, then disappears under the same awareness pressure it helps avoid.

## Accepted change

- `ACT-489` isolates a contextual tap on compatible adjacent world geometry
  whose product is a localized lure sound rather than damage, movement or an
  inventory object.
- `SYS-958` isolates the reversible Alert-to-Evasion-to-Infiltration sequence,
  including separate countdowns and immediate reset to Alert on rediscovery.
- `INF-364` isolates an availability-gated local radar that exposes eligible
  actors or cameras together with directed perception regions and is removed
  in declared alert, search or narrow-space states.

## Complete lower-ID transfer test

- Earlier local-noise carriers remain incidental movement, emitted abilities,
  thrown objects or autonomous world events; none gains `ACT-489` without the
  adjacent contextual tap and positioned non-damaging lure.
- Earlier detection carriers use binary pursuit, one decay timer, retained
  wanted severity or scripted escalation; none gains `SYS-958` without both
  successive recovery phases and the documented reacquisition edge.
- Earlier maps, threat indicators, vision-cone displays and radar layers do not
  jointly satisfy conditional availability, local actor/camera markers and
  directed perception geometry; none gains `INF-364`.
- The remaining fourteen `GAME-0339` genes retain their existing boundaries;
  Metal Gear Solid adds only carrier evidence and no prior signature changes.

## Migration and compatibility

Three new Active IDs are appended. Existing IDs, definitions, Ukrainian copy,
game signatures, comparisons, families and verified combinations remain
unchanged. Metal Gear Solid introduces no combination candidate because its
three new boundaries do not form a repeatedly verified irreducible conjunction
across the lower-ID corpus.

## Validation expectation

Repository validation must report no duplicate Active definition, every new ID
must have reviewed Ukrainian copy and `GAME-0339` must remain the only initial
carrier of `ACT-489`, `SYS-958` and `INF-364` after the complete lower-ID scan.
