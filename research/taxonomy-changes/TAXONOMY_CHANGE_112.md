# Taxonomy Change 112: Cell feeding, revision and stage succession

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Confirmed
- Evidence quality: Direct
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-09-23
- Trigger: `GAME-0373` Spore, original Windows PC Cell stage from fresh
  control to Creature-stage entry.
- Scope: twelve additive Active genes; no earlier signature, lifecycle or
  verified combination changes.

## Problem

Generic direct movement, autonomous locomotion and real-time progression do
not explain a mouth-compatible feeding economy that grows the body, world-part
discovery that unlocks an editor palette, mating access to that editor, saved
functional body revision and a chosen transition into the next evolutionary
stage. Treating all five Spore product stages as one genome would also violate
the bounded decision-loop rule.

## Accepted change

- `ACT-510` gates a live Cell Creator visit by a mate call and approach;
  `ACT-511` revises and saves functional cell-body parts.
- `SYS-1014` credits mouth-compatible feeding to DNA and growth;
  `SYS-1015` changes size and food demands at growth milestones;
  `SYS-1016` unlocks collected world parts for later editing;
  `SYS-1017` applies the saved design's abilities;
  `SYS-1018` resolves offensive or defensive part contact;
  `SYS-1019` retains the feeding path as a successor trait.
- `CON-677` gates food by mouth and relative prey size;
  `CON-678` gates editor placement by discovered part, DNA, geometry and
  complexity.
- `INF-380` shows progress, DNA, health and available parts;
  `OBJ-216` sets the chosen Cell-to-Creature advance as the packet endpoint.

## Lower-ID transfer and rejection test

- `ACT-008` transfers for direct cell navigation, `SYS-045` for independently
  moving water agents and `TIM-003` for continuous live decisions. Their
  definitions are neither renamed nor broadened.
- Ordinary food consumption `ACT-165` is a carried-food use that restores
  hunger, unlike mouth contact that credits both DNA and growth. Fixed gear
  slots and generic stat upgrades do not represent saved lineage geometry or
  mate-gated Cell Creator entry. A future exact/near comparison is derived
  deterministically from the complete lower-ID signatures.
- No verified combination is a proper subset of this signature and no new
  combination is asserted from one carrier alone.

## Evidence and limits

Electronic Arts' original PC manual, pp. 3–8, 12–13, 18–19 and 30–31,
directly documents the five stages, Cell feeding, part collection, mate call,
Cell Creator, DNA/complexity limits, contact combat, Progress Bar and
stage-dependent successor traits. The current EA product listing confirms
product identity but not a particular installed build. No executable,
save, exact numeric threshold or Cell-stage defeat/retry trace was inspected.
