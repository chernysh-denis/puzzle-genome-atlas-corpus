- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-23.
- Scope: [Sega Worldwide Soccer '97](../../knowledge/games/s-z/sega-worldwide-soccer-97.md),
  GAME-0665, original North American Sega Saturn rules. One ordinary
  Exhibition through the actionable result continuation.
- Decision: add ACT-883, ACT-884, ACT-885, ACT-886, CON-880 and SYS-1912.
  Reuse twenty definitions. No earlier signature or definition changes.

| Gene | Boundary | Nearest rejected equivalence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ACT-883 | Quickly repeat the same button to choose an alternative kick | A charge hold or post-contact grade changes timing, not discrete kick identity |
| ACT-884 | Configure tactics, formation, defence and team for direct control | ACT-304 configures an autonomous match and dual tactical plan |
| ACT-885 | Choose the next setup after a settled ordinary result | Replay playback and an automatic season successor do not retain or revise a one-match setup on demand |
| ACT-886 | Issue a live positional defence order while retaining individual steering | ACT-187 communicates to teammates; ACT-317 changes a different unit-formation boundary |
| CON-880 | Ball possession and keeper handling change command identity | CON-877 switches cue identity by a rhythm phase, not by who holds a live ball |
| SYS-1912 | Change the single active marker by declared proximity and goalkeeper policy | ACT-052 is the input for transfer and does not by itself select the closest actor or react to a keeper catch |

COMB-0161 requires all of its genes. The documented passing and keeper
rules support individual actions but do not establish the complete
modern assist model in SYS-458. Neither the nineteen-gene overlap nor
the games' common sport justifies filling that gap.
