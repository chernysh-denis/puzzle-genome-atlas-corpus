# Taxonomy Change 283 — PRAGMATA live hacking

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-17.
- Trigger: [GAME-0536](../../knowledge/games/m-r/pragmata.md), the first
  SectorGuard encounter in the full PS5 version.

| Addition | Reviewed boundary | Counterexample |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ACT-733` | Discrete directed steps extend one world-target-linked draft. | `ACT-016` describes a compound trace gesture; `ACT-233` selects a scanner upload. |
| `SYS-1549` | Intermediate route cells add modifiers to the result at execution. | `CON-108` makes a required waypoint part of route validity. |
| `SYS-1550` | Completed manual routing deals damage and temporarily opens armor. | `SYS-1126` erodes a shield using an illumination action. |
| `SYS-1551` | A draft continues after voluntary disengagement, while specified damage or failure invalidates it. | `TIM-035` pauses a target clock for transfer rather than preserving a local draft in a live scene. |
| `SYS-1552` | Successful hacks charge a separate pulse which opens and immobilizes. | A generic recharge or a damage-only burst does not capture the causal relationship to executed hacks. |
| `SYS-1553` | Impact expands a net and temporarily restrains susceptible targets in its splash. | `SYS-1534` is restraint by a placed field while it persists. |
| `INF-520` | Target grid shows draft and preview; external enemy cues identify resolved vulnerability. | `INF-318` exposes remaining guardian health. |

Eighteen definitions transfer, including the simple path `CON-029`,
continuous combat `TIM-003`, and the separate contextual action and
reach constraints for the close Critical Shot. Exact damage, timings,
phase thresholds, equipment capacities and board layouts remain parameters.
No verified combination is a strict subset of this signature.
