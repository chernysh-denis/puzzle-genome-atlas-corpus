Six existing definitions are reused unchanged: ACT-008, ACT-223, INF-116,
INF-178, OBJ-282 and TIM-003. Slide commitment, hold distance and recovery
are local movement parameters; a second damage/invulnerability action is not
invented. Contact outcomes remain one receiving-state classifier rather than
one new gene for every athlete, push amount or rear-hit animation. The generic
fixed-pair objective already includes simultaneous-threshold adjudication;
no sports-specific objective duplicate is needed.

The original flyer, visible tutorial/court displays, independently authored
original-game accounts and qualified sparse recordings support these
boundaries. All twelve additions remain Medium confidence. The exact native
lead-margin predicate, post-whistle event ordering, score-sensitive frame
windows and mandatory-mash claims are not certified; the game ledger records
their source conflicts/limits. No earlier definition, signature, lifecycle,
history, threshold or published mapping changes, and no external novelty is
claimed.
