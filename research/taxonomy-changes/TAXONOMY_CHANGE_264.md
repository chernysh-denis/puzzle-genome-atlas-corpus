Seventeen existing definitions transfer without revision. Research Ship
construction satisfies a primary flag. A completed topic instead adds
capability. Capture order, attachment and delivery are different transitions.
The target is replaced if it becomes unavailable without qualification.
None of the 274 prior combinations is a subset of this signature.
