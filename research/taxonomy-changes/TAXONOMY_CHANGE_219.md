## Evidence and lower-ID transfer test

The original Sony English PSP booklet, launch announcement and creator interview
establish indirect field control, division, growth and ordinary/full clear.
Contemporary routes support first-stage narrow passages and stationary events;
their placement and numeric threshold remain Limited/Medium, not direct play.
Whole-game enemies and later hazards are not imported into this packet.

`ACT-519` already tilts a stage instead of an avatar and transfers unchanged.
its Ukrainian label/definition are corrected on touch from ball/stick-specific
wording to the existing canonical moving-body/continuous-directional boundary.
The earlier English gene, includes example and game signatures do not change.
Global ground bump does not transfer direct avatar movement/jump. A requested
geometric division/reunion is not army-slot allocation. `SYS-002`'s autonomous
numerical collision merge does not fit an explicitly requested, population-
conserving form change. Berry birth differs from regrouping and from rescuing
a supplied actor. `SYS-037` covers contact pickup but not prior hidden-bud
activation. Stationary eligible local reactions have rule consequences, unlike
idle decoration or unshipped rhythm scoring. `SYS-036` covers gravity/contact/
wind without duplicating `SYS-1028`'s ball-specific response. Passage clearance
is physical form, not `CON-032`'s mutable occupied board cells. Local living-
participant eligibility is not whole-level compulsory rescue quota or mass.
`OBJ-019` requires saving supplied actors to its minimum; here berries create
individuals and maximising the finish count remains optional. `OBJ-002` abstract
score alone does not express this living-population objective. Full-clear parts
and mode eligibility are terminal rewards, not later house/Time Attack play.

## Disposition

Accept eleven bounded genes and seven unchanged reuses: `ACT-519`, `SYS-036`,
`SYS-037`, `INF-192`, `INF-268`, `OBJ-181`, `TIM-003`. Local view, current
teaching and ordinary clear/map replay retain their boundaries. Physical
response coefficients, exact passage geometry, join radius, singing thresholds
and original executable behaviour remain unmeasured. One existing physics
family and the existing PSP platform suffice; real time alone does not prove
escalating pressure. Eighteen uses have a named causal salience partition and
complete reviewed bilingual plain language. No new verified combination, direct
play, public publication or deploy is claimed.
