# Taxonomy Change 425 — Jeanne d'Arc's temporary form and renewed activation

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-24.
- Trigger: [Jeanne d'Arc, Stage 2](../../knowledge/games/g-l/jeanne-darc.md).
- Decision: admit INF-625, SYS-1950 and SYS-1951. Preserve all
  existing IDs and all combination signatures.

| Gene | Accepted boundary | Nearest reuse considered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| INF-625 | Before confirmation, display separate damage and likelihood for the attack and the possible counter. | INF-190 bundles available movement and attack forecast along with hex navigation. It does not require a display with two distinct directions and probabilities. INF-119 still covers the current resource panel. |
| SYS-1950 | Temporarily change a tactical unit's form and form-specific commands, then restore its ordinary form on the battle counter. | SYS-608 transfers control to a separate temporary combat body whose health and moveset are independent. The accepted fixture follows one persistent tactical unit and makes no separate-body claim. |
| SYS-1951 | Refresh the currently transformed actor's movement and action on defeating a target in the same phase. Each later defeat can repeat it without extending the form. | SYS-534 spends and refreshes ordinary personal action points at a squad phase boundary. TIM-044 is a one-use bonus for a perfect placement. Neither describes a repeatable renewal conditioned by an armoured actor's defeats. |

The first trigger is scripted. The later manual Transform command,
Soul Power costs outside this fixture and the precise expiry on
failed or abandoned turns were not tested. Definitions distinguish
the active form, the earned action and the separate phase counter.

No earlier game signature is changed. No verified combination is a
proper subset of the 14-gene fixture.
