  belongs to each aligned pair, allowing a wildcard or a non-removing gap.

  CON-019 gates a board swap on a match. Neither describes this placement.

  resources. SYS-164 requires acting enemies and an actor order. No
  autonomous enemy-choice order or common hit-point balance is assigned.

  use costs side by side. INF-061 exposes next hostile action categories
  and known magnitude, without guaranteeing this composition.

  returning to the itinerary. OBJ-029 requires incapacitating enemies.
  The Tractor result does not physically destroy the other vehicle.

  bonus within that same phase. TIM-005 permits a set of flexible
  commands before committing the player phase. The opening packet
  teaches a specific bonus predicate and cap.

current instruction and acknowledgment. SYS-736 advances when the
