## Boundaries

- ACT-860 moves the dragon relative to its enemies. ACT-690 rotates the
  firing view along a forced route. INF-600 communicates relative risk and
  current position, where INF-489 marks eligible target locks.
- SYS-1867 banks complete action units only while stationed and idle.
  TIM-026 covers independently filling actor gauges. TIM-046 freezes the
  fight and the local bank while the player chooses a command. The
  squad radial in TIM-027 and the ship order pause in TIM-030 have
  different availability and resolution.
- SYS-1868 automatically acquires eligible nearby enemies for one homing
  volley with fixed cost. ACT-691 and SYS-1411 require individual
  acquisition with a held input before release. ACT-161 covers one aimed
  gun target and CON-634 covers charge affordability.
- SYS-1869 credits separate experience and currency values affected by
  evaluation. SYS-822 assigns the grade. The two Close Call settlements
  have different payouts, so this analysis leaves the formula open.
