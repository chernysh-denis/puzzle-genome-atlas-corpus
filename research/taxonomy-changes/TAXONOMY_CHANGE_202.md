## Evidence and lower-ID transfer test

The [game ledger](../../knowledge/games/a-f/boom-blox.md) combines Nintendo's
publisher-supplied Wii description of targeted, strength-dependent throws with
a contemporary original-Wii guide's A-release and B-camera controls and
Warrior strategy. No disc or executable was inspected. The bounded level has
ordinary blocks and gem targets; bomb/chemical effects are outside scope.

`ACT-113` requires a fixed launcher adjusted before click/release; `ACT-499`
samples a physical release but commits a lane-bound roll. Neither covers a
pointer-directed, motion-sampled free throw at a chosen block surface, so
`ACT-615` isolates it. `ACT-095` changes legal route geometry by projection
and `ACT-610` follows a directly steered actor. Warrior's B-camera orbit
instead changes which rigid support is exposed to the aimed ball without
changing the construction, so `ACT-616` isolates that decision.

`SYS-036`, `SYS-822`, `INF-001`, `OBJ-007` and `TIM-003` transfer. Ordinary
displacement does not establish `SYS-147`'s damage-broken support. The guide's
third-throw Bronze threshold does not by itself prove `CON-020`'s immediate
failure on exhaustion; the over-bronze UI is left unmodelled.

## Disposition

Add the two English action boundaries and reviewed Ukrainian translations in
the same game unit. No previous game genome or combination is modified.
