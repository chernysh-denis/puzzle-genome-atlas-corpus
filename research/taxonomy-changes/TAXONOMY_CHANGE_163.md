## Problem and accepted change

The original manual separates three causal changes around one Food Miracle: the god positions a one-shot effect at the Village Store, the leashed creature observes and may need repeated examples, and supplying the raised Food Desire can increase village belief. Treating all three as a generic companion command or a single spell effect would erase the decision to teach while addressing a current need. The bounded objective marks a reviewed observation window, not campaign victory or guaranteed Creature mastery.

## Transfer and rejection tests

- Reuse `ACT-036` for committing a behavioural role to one autonomous agent: the Learning Leash changes how the creature attends without steering every action.
- Reuse `CON-269` for the one-shot's available charge and legal target, and `TIM-003` for concurrent village and creature activity.
- Reject `ACT-333`: no spell card hand or turn priority. Reject `SYS-1111`: the Nintendogs spoken-cue association is not observational learning from god-hand actions.
- Reject `SYS-407` as a substitute for learning: ordinary follower help does not preserve a demonstration-dependent persistent lesson. Reject `INF-065`: the village flag is not an exact group-resolve trajectory.
- Do not add full moral alignment, rival village conversion, miracle economy, exact learning percentages or a second creature to the scoped signature.

## Evidence and limits

The [original Lionhead manual scan](https://d1.xp.myabandonware.com/f/lvlb/Black-White_Manual_Win_EN.pdf) shows the Learning Leash and repeated teaching (printed pp. 11–13), Village Store and desire/belief link (pp. 18–19), and one-shot miracle use (pp. 29–30). A [contemporary Land One walkthrough](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/914356-black-and-white/faqs/23229) places these systems together at Guide's Aztec village. [Molyneux's developer postmortem](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/postmortem-lionhead-studios-i-black-white-i-) describes a persistent independently acting learner. No disc or direct play was inspected; exact patch, stock, percentage and belief changes are not claimed.
