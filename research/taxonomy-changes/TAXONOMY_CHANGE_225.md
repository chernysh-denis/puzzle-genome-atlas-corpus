## Evidence and complete-packet review

The original US longbox booklet distinguishes Single Race from Championship,
weapon-free Time Trial and linked play. Its mode, control, HUD, shield and
weapon pages were visually checked. A dated 1995 writer's firsthand account
supports pitch, fixed speed-pad and start-thrust observations. Neither source
is our direct play or measured native timing. The guide's uncertain colour
association does not prove item randomness; its shorthand unlock description
does not override the booklet's six-course plus overall points-table condition.

The complete packet retains all six one-player function types and the third
lap's classified result. No terminal, shield gate or weapon effect is removed
to reduce novelty. Ordinary mine/rocket/shockwave variants are parameters of
the admitted typed-function resolution, not six new genes.

## Standalone first-place objective parameter generalisation

Before: one downhill Single Event Race won by classified first place. After:
one finite standalone rival race won by classified first place without
required career reward or cup points. Downhill versus circuit topology,
vehicle/rider and lap count are carrier parameters of the same objective,
not separate positive predicates. Lower place still settles but does not win.

The complete existing `GAME-0450` Garibaldi record was re-read, including its
original booklet/firsthand PS2 evidence and explicit Single Event versus World
Circuit boundary. It remains the only prior carrier and retains its exact
eleven-gene signature, scoped descent and positive result. `OBJ-134` retained
career currency, `OBJ-150` accepted progression place, `OBJ-226` unfinished-cup
points and `OBJ-180` complete-cup outcome remain excluded. No historical
genome, neighbour score, primary-loop field or title is materially revised.

## Decision and limitations

Admit four distinct supply, contact, launch and disclosure boundaries; reuse
all fitting input, live effect, course, objective and time boundaries. Neither
native program inspection nor direct play is claimed. Full-slot replacement,
selection, exact frame/physics values, internal checkpoints and simultaneous
precedence remain open. All seventeen family boundaries were tested; physics,
tactical rival response and live pressure apply. Health advisories require a
same-unit disposition; lexical suggestions cannot mutate taxonomy. Public
release mapping, published revisions and deployment remain untouched.
