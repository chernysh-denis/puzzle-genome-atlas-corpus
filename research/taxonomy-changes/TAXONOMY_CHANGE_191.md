# Taxonomy Change 191: Touch-flick rhythmic assembly and terminal passage

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-09-30
- Trigger: GAME-0454, first original-Nintendo-DS Built to Scale.
- Scope: four new Active boundaries; no earlier definition, signature or

## Evidence and boundary tests

The [bounded game record](../../knowledge/games/m-r/rhythm-heaven.md) separates
Nintendo's original manual, creator testimony and first-hand demo/DS accounts
from direct play. Exact chart parity, timing and grade coefficients remain
unmeasured. The whole collection's gestures are not merged into one exercise.

- ACT-603: contact, rapid directed stroke and release commit a rhythmic
  response. ACT-261 explicitly excludes a rhythm sequence as the whole
  objective; ACT-533 requires reproducing instructor symbols; ACT-572
  manipulates a current food/utensil task; ACT-588 maps contextual runner
  swipes to navigation. None owns this intervention.
- SYS-1197: live authored approach events judge response timing and animate
  assembly/miss while continuing the performance. SYS-1058 alternates
  instructor phrases; SYS-1059/SYS-1013 maintain live failure gauges;
  SYS-1085 judges directional foot-panel chart commands. Neither free aim
  nor these chart-specific decisions is this flick assembly system.
- INF-437: musical/moving approach cues, local outcome and terminal rank
  expose response timing and quality. INF-194 excludes graded beat input;
  INF-001's fully visible state does not fit late preview restriction;
  INF-299 requires category-plus-aggregate reporting absent here.
- OBJ-258: finish one authored exercise with a passing terminal grade and
  ordinary stage passage. OBJ-233 requires a live Good instructor rating;
  OBJ-215 and OBJ-239 require survival-gauge song completion; OBJ-249
  concerns an entire food recipe, irrespective of its medal tier.

## Reuse and rejected duplication

Reuse SYS-822 for terminal timing-quality evaluation. Its existing parameters
already include retention and later legality, so autosave, next exercise and
the optional Superb medal do not create a duplicate system boundary.
Reuse TIM-003 for progression with explicit pause; authored tempo variation
is a parameter. No independent constraint duplicates gesture recognition,
timing judgement or terminal-grade legality.

## Decision

Accept four reviewed bilingual boundaries and six total uses. Preserve
original-DS versus Wii geometry, ordinary clear versus Perfect challenge,
source-derived cases versus executed tests and terminal quality versus live
survival. No regional timing, hidden threshold, global campaign or external
novelty claim is admitted.
