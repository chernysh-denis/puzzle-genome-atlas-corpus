# Taxonomy Change 051: Generalise the carried-signal terminal cluster beyond one summit rescue

## Status

- Proposal status: `Accepted`
- Claim status: `Observation`
- Evidence quality: `Corroborated`
- Confidence: `High`
- Date: 2026-09-09
- Trigger: Codex integration review of `GAME-0285` The Long Dark.

## Current classification

- Stable IDs: `ACT-364`, `SYS-669`, `CON-537` and `OBJ-125`.
- Prior carrier: `GAME-0203` PEAK.
- Prior boundary: carry and ignite PEAK's Flare in the summit region so a
  helicopter rescue settles the dated island expedition.

## Detected problem

- The Long Dark's `Hopeless Rescue` Challenge has the same portable decision
  structure but parameterises it with a Distress Pistol, a Lighthouse and an
  authored Challenge result.
- Keeping the old PEAK nouns in four canonical labels would either reject a
  valid recurrence or force a quest-specific duplicate cluster.
- The transferable relation is not the vehicle or named location: it is
  preserving a usable carried signal across a bounded survival route and
  activating it while alive in an eligible terminal region.

## Evidence

- PEAK evidence and transitions remain in
  [`GAME-0203`](../../knowledge/games/m-r/peak.md).
- The Long Dark evidence and transitions are recorded in
  [`GAME-0285`](../../knowledge/games/s-z/the-long-dark.md), especially
  `TLD-003`, `TLD-004`, `TLD-009` and `TLD-011`.
- Carrier audit: both games require prior route progress, a living controlled
  agent, terminal-region presence, a usable carried signal, an explicit player
  activation and a positive authored settlement. Arrival alone is insufficient
  in both.
- Counterevidence: the signal is ignited in PEAK and fired from a loaded pistol
  in The Long Dark; the response is a helicopter sequence in one and a Challenge
  result in the other. Those are parameter differences already exposed by the
  four genes, not different decisions.

## Change

- `ACT-364` becomes **Activate a carried signal item in an eligible region**.
- `SYS-669` becomes **Convert an eligible signal into bounded route
  settlement**.
- `CON-537` becomes **Route settlement requires life, terminal presence and a
  usable signal**.
- `OBJ-125` becomes **Complete a bounded survival route through a
  location-bound signal**.
- Definitions, includes, parameters and evidence lists are widened only enough
  to cover ignition or firing, the two terminal regions and their authored
  result surfaces.

## Genome and combination impact

- No gene is added, removed, merged, split or deprecated by this change.
- `GAME-0203` keeps the same signature and every old sentence remains true.
- `GAME-0285` reuses all four IDs.
- New `COMB-0272` records their strict shared subset; no other gene is imported
  merely because both packets involve survival or vertical travel.
- No earlier reviewed game signature changes.

## Decision

- Decision: `Accepted`.
- Rationale: the item, activation verb, location and response vary together as
  parameters around the same dependency and settlement structure. Four new
  quest-named genes would duplicate that structure and violate the portable
  vocabulary rule.

## Ukrainian review

- All four Ukrainian labels, definitions, inclusions and exclusions are
  corrected in this unit under `UK-GAME-0203-0285`.
- Named items and locations remain only in the inclusion examples and game
  presentation.

## Change history
