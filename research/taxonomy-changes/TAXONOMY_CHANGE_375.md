# Taxonomy Change 375 — Cave stock and live part restoration

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-21.
- Scope: [Dark Cloud](../../knowledge/games/a-f/dark-cloud.md), GAME-0629.
  The original North American PlayStation 2 Wheel-to-Norune packet.
- Decision: add ACT-836, SYS-1830, SYS-1831, INF-584 and OBJ-338 as Active.
  Preserve prior signatures and combinations.

## Boundaries

- ACT-139 places or moves a whole acquired foundation. ACT-836 instead
  moves a loose detail into a matching slot on a particular foundation,
  including one that already occupies the village map. There is no new
  crafted item or separately positioned decoration.
- SYS-1830 grants an identified construction part from a one-time
  enclosure in a separate exploration area. Ordinary loot and currency
  are distinct inventories. Revisit persistence follows the manual's
  Atla rule, while death and reload are outside this packet.
- SYS-1831 shares the altered settlement between editing and walking.
  A mounted foundation immediately occupies playable space and may
  still have open component requirements. This differs from
  independently loading a newly saved course or seeing a blueprint.
- INF-584 exposes the loose stock and existing fitted state. A resident
  can explain a slot with initially unknown meaning. INF-011 discloses
  an exact input-output assembly schema, which would overstate what is
  known at the first site choice.
- OBJ-338 requires the recovered part's actual fit to an accessible
  structure. A collected part and the house being positioned are each
  insufficient by themselves. Whole-village completion is separate.

See DCL-001 through DCL-007 in the bounded analysis.
