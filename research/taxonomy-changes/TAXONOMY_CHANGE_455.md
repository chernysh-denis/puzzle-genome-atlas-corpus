# Taxonomy Change 455 — Independent foot control and local walking recovery

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-24
- Scope: [GAME-0709](../../knowledge/games/a-f/baby-steps.md), the
  first controllable ascent on the English PS5 base game.
- Decision: admit ACT-925, SYS-1990 and SYS-1991. The corpus has
  4,865 definitions and 4,807 Active genes.

| Gene | Accepted boundary | Nearest reuse considered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ACT-925 | Lift either walking foot with its associated held input, guide it and release that input to lower it. | ACT-859 addresses contact of a selected or suggested limb on a climbing wall. ACT-008 covers local navigation without specifying individual foot inputs. |
| SYS-1990 | Transfer support to the viable point where the foot lands and make the previous foot available for the next step. | SYS-1865 qualifies rock contact as secure grip or brace. SYS-036 covers live forces and collisions without identifying when a new foot becomes the support for gait. |
| SYS-1991 | After losing balance and coming to rest on the route, resume upright foot control in the resulting local terrain. | SYS-662 resolves physical fall into an injury. SYS-1632 returns a failed crossing to recent support. The two scoped Baby Steps captures show continued travel from the local fall area without a restoration to a prior support position. |

Reuse ACT-008, SYS-036, INF-258, OBJ-026 and TIM-003. No separate
gene for trigger labels, step order, terrain material or a conjectured
recovery command. No earlier signature or verified combination changes.
