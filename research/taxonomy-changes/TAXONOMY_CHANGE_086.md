# Taxonomy Change 086: Bound the first retained Dagger power

## Status

- Proposal status: `Accepted`
- Claim status: `Observation`
- Evidence quality: `Corroborated`
- Confidence: `High`
- Date: 2026-09-21
- Trigger: `GAME-0344` Prince of Persia: The Sands of Time lower-ID scan.
- Scope: one new Active objective, one wording-only System generalisation and
  supporting-carrier evidence for reused genes; no earlier signature,
  lifecycle, combination or family-definition change.

## Problem

The vocabulary already represented direct traversal, held guard, health and
water recovery, breakable barriers, staged tutorial information, finite
resource-priced abilities and branchable recent-history restoration. It did
not represent a bounded opening whose accepted terminal is the acquisition of
one unique carried artefact, completion of its authored demonstration and
return to control with its first granted capability retained.

`SYS-398` also required the acquired capability to remain available without
carrying its source. That wording covered intrinsic movement and world-action
abilities but excluded the same causal retention when the source is one unique
non-consumed artefact permanently carried in the continuing character state.

## Accepted change

- `OBJ-201` owns the complete route-to-artefact terminal: reach the Dagger,
  accept its falling-rock Power of Revival demonstration and regain ordinary
  control with both artefact and first capability retained.
- `SYS-398` is generalised from source-independent capability retention to
  include one unique non-consumed retained source artefact. A disposable key,
  consumable effect or temporary buff remains excluded.
- `ACT-044`, `CON-351` and `TIM-007` receive supporting-carrier evidence for a
  rewind whose accepted use consumes a Sand Tank and whose horizon is exposed
  by the Time Circle.

## Complete lower-ID transfer test

- `GAME-0150` Hollow Knight: Silksong and `GAME-0337` Super Metroid acquire
  intrinsic retained traversal or world-interaction capabilities. Their
  `SYS-398` transitions have the same authored acquisition, continuing-state
  retention and later legal-use boundary; whether the capability's source
  remains visibly carried does not change that resolution.
- `GAME-0197` Valheim ends after a separately earned progression key reaches a
  matching fixture and makes the first capability available. This opening has
  no separate key/fixture handoff and therefore does not fit `OBJ-120`.
- `GAME-0293` Ori and the Will of the Wisps requires a later restorative save
  after its first retained attack capability, so `OBJ-172` is too broad.
- `GAME-0337` Super Metroid requires an ordered capability chain, guardian and
  reopened parent route, so `OBJ-198` is too broad.
- `OBJ-025` credits one puzzle-room token toward later access and `OBJ-026`
  ends at location arrival. Neither requires a unique artefact's capability
  settlement and returned control.
- `ACT-044`, `CON-351` and `TIM-007` already cover the chosen rewind input,
  bounded reserve legality and branchable restored history; Sand Tank, Time
  Circle, Dagger and ten-second maximum are parameters.

## Migration and compatibility

`OBJ-201` is appended as Active. `SYS-398` changes wording only, retains its
existing ID and keeps all earlier signatures valid. English and Ukrainian
records are introduced together. No verified combination or lower-ID genome
changes.

## Validation expectation

Repository validation must find no exact Active-definition collision, complete
Ukrainian coverage for `OBJ-201`, the full `GAME-0344` signature and unchanged
lower-ID signatures. Deterministic comparison and combination scans replace
the provisional neighbour row before acceptance.
