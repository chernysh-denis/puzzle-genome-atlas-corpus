# Taxonomy Change 398 — Tactical chip offers and independently spent live queues

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-23.
- Scope: [Mega Man Battle Network](../../knowledge/games/m-r/mega-man-battle-network.md),
  GAME-0652, third classroom fight on the original North American GBA release.
- Decision: add ACT-865, SYS-1883 through SYS-1885, CON-873, CON-874 and
  INF-604. Reuse six definitions and preserve earlier signatures.

## Boundaries

- ACT-865 commits to an interval without sending chips. SYS-1884 retains
  the existing offer and widens its next appearance. SYS-026 replaces
  used hand positions on a different trigger.
- SYS-1883 individually executes and consumes the live queue head when
  addressed. SYS-090 resolves the entire prepared queue from one command.
- SYS-1885 fills a gauge during live play and gives permission to reopen
  preparation. TIM-046 owns the pause then, including suspension of the
  opposing actor and readiness. SYS-813 uses a separate evasion-only phase
  and cannot describe the freely commanded attack and movement here.
- CON-873 separates card-group compatibility and transmission capacity
  from the number of cards offered. CON-874 confines movement independently
  of attack reach by current panel ownership.
- INF-604 distinguishes the preparation information from the live
  ready-chip, health, spatial and gauge cues after sending.
