- Scope: [GAME-0612](../../knowledge/games/m-r/pipistrello-and-the-cursed-yoyo.md),

ACT-813 defines the learned alternate release of the same reusable
tool. ACT-161 remains the ordinary nearby strike and ACT-008 covers
direct navigation and jumps. The normal throw and the detached
throw use distinct effective reach. SYS-398 and CON-349 already
cover retaining the trick and its gating of a compatible mechanism.

SYS-1805 distinguishes an impact-triggered span across a gap from
SYS-1743’s actual pull along an attached tether and SYS-934’s
countable breakable crates. The demonstrated throw reaches the
switch before the avatar crosses. Reuse SYS-1735 where the later
setup shifts an existing bridge between two layouts. CON-854
distinguishes landing on solid support from attempting to settle
an ordinary jump on unsupported water.

SYS-1806 records the closed encounter passages and the transition
that releases them after the final threat. OBJ-080 describes the
separate success predicate of actually arriving through the trapdoor.
INF-179 supports visible route state and TIM-003 live decisions.
