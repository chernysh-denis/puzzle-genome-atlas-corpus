# Taxonomy Change 127: physical clues and answer-level interview proof

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-09-24
- Trigger: `GAME-0388` L.A. Noire, North American 2017 PS4 base-game Traffic
  case The Driver's Seat.
- Scope: six additive Active genes; no previous genome, lifecycle, family
  definition or verified combination changes.

## Problem and accepted change

The existing court-case genes address individual revisitable testimony lines
and finite wrong-objection marks; investigation genes cover generic clue-led
progression. Neither boundary represents a physically examined object whose
informative face must be exposed, a growing notebook that joins scene clues
to people and destinations, or a live interview answer assessed through a
credibility stance and possibly a matching clue. This case also closes by
apprehending its subject and receiving a graded report, not by a judge's
verdict or an exact structured fate assignment.

- `ACT-528` isolates held-object detail examination before clue registration.
- `ACT-529` submits one existing case clue against the current interview
  answer after Accuse, not against an addressable court line.
- `SYS-1046` resolves the stance and optional proof into an authored response
  or lead, including a fallback route after an unsuccessful interview.
- `CON-692` makes a matching recorded contradiction necessary for a positive
  accusation; Bad Cop remains a distinct suspicion-only option.
- `INF-393` exposes the growing notebook of clues, persons, questions and
  destinations across multiple sites.
- `OBJ-229` closes the bounded investigation at apprehension and case report,
  without confusing case completion with a five-star grade.

## Transfer and rejection test

`ACT-008`, `ACT-232`, `ACT-341`, `SYS-680`, `INF-299`, `TIM-002` and
`TIM-003` retain their existing boundaries for movement, authored response,
world interaction, clue-to-lead update, report, deliberation and live pursuit.
`ACT-527` and `SYS-1041` require a selected courtroom testimony line; this
case uses an answer produced in a local interview. `INF-391` is a court
record, not an investigation notebook with physical locations. `CON-690`
spends court marks, whereas wrong L.A. Noire questioning can withhold a lead
and leave a tail route. `OBJ-227` ends in acquittal, not apprehension. No
earlier game signature or verified combination is changed.
