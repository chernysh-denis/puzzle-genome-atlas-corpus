## Evidence and lower-ID transfer test

The [bounded game record](../../knowledge/games/s-z/the-house-of-the-dead.md) combines Sega's original arcade operator manual, Sega's original-cabinet page and a contemporary first-hand arcade route. `ACT-490` captures physical gun aim and a playfield shot, but `ACT-183` requires transferring finite reserve ammunition and `SYS-1160` refills on a protective pedal transition. Neither describes the original cabinet's off-screen trigger that refills its gun without a carried reserve. Generic authored encounter scrolling (`SYS-915`) does not determine the bridge's front-door-versus-sewer route from the threatened researcher's survival. Ordinary damage resolution (`SYS-215`) does not encode Chariot's protected chest weak point and later armour loss. Generic contact collection (`SYS-037`) does not capture shooting scenery to reveal a special item and shooting that item to claim a benefit.

## Disposition

Accept the four bounded action/system distinctions while reusing existing live combat, health, magazine compatibility, local disclosure, guardian exit and real-time genes. Exact hidden item contents, branch timing, cabinet software revision, Chariot hit counts and sensor sampling remain unmeasured. These genes are not inferred for *Time Crisis*, *Duck Hunt*, later House of the Dead entries, PC/Saturn ports or the remake. The separate `TAXONOMY_CHANGE_211` file restores an already accepted prior unit's missing decision record; this unit does not reopen that genome.
