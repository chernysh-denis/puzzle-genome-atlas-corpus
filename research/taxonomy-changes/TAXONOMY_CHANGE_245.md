available officer alternative, first Thank You, Come Again local interventions,
population custody/proof, mission evaluation and retained HQ stress/availability
form the complete primary loop. The next mission's availability is terminal
evidence, not permission to enter it. Locked first-mission traits, Ironman,
Practice, DLC and cosmetic unlocks do not become hidden supporting genes.

Official release text and attributed launch frames corroborate the packet.
Work-in-progress creator briefings and earlier equipment/treatment evidence
retain their dates and qualifications. No original executable, own input,
exact coefficient, checksum or bug-free queued command is asserted. Bleeding,
night vision and headwear protection remain Limited/Medium where native
original-1.0 transitions are unverified. The source game owns its full ledger,
source-inspection qualifications and sole derived numeric comparison.

## Unchanged transfers and rejected redundancy

The game uses 30 existing boundaries without modification. Movement/posture,
weapon switching, aimed attack, magazine reload, grenade throw/effect, squad
movement directive, equipped loadout, breach and contextual interaction remain
generic. Local perception/pathing/hostile commitment, combat/cover/material
response, equipment capabilities and conduct settlement remain distinct from
the new police procedure and personnel consequences. Ceramic durability does
not imply steel wear. Sidearm-compatible shield transfer retains Medium
source limitations rather than asserting native equip rejection.

Local scene, personal condition, weapon/supply, checklist and terminal categories
are reused, not minted again for changed labels. Police contact stress and
retained officer stress are different actors/transitions; camera request and
camera information are different types. Stable game titles, numerical slot
allowance, group colors, input bindings, identities, exact percentages and tool
names remain parameters. Neither rarity nor visual salience changes signatures.

## Corpus comparison and combination decision

All 507 lower-ID signatures and all 274 verified combinations were computed
under genome-jaccard-v1. The canonical game owns the sole exact pair fraction
and selected-neighbour interpretation. No exact match or proper-subset
combination is supported. HITMAN's shared direct combat/conduct boundaries do
not transfer disguise permissions, Instinct or its closed designated-target
exit into police custody. No prior signature changes and no combination is
created from a visually plausible handful of these genes.
