# Taxonomy Change 142: Guarded transport restores route choice

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-09-25
- Trigger: `GAME-0404` *Ratchet & Clank*, original PlayStation 2 first
  Novalis crater mission.
- Scope: admit `SYS-1083` and `OBJ-237` as Active; do not alter earlier
  genomes or verified combinations.

## Problem and accepted change

Two contemporary original-game written routes independently distinguish the
critical rescue of the chairman and courier ship from the optional waterworks
Infobot purchase and Gold Bolt detour. Existing movement, weapon selection,
live combat, health, ammunition and information genes capture the ordinary
route actions. The remaining causal boundary is that clearing the finite
guards grants access to a rescued actor's information-and-transport reward
(`SYS-1083`); the bounded objective requires both parts of that handoff
(`OBJ-237`). Reaching the ship, killing an unrelated enemy or disclosing a
planet name alone does not close the packet.

## Transfer and rejection test

- `CON-402` holds combat-room *exits* shut; Novalis instead conditions a
  living actor's reward and use of a parked ship on guard clearance.
- `OBJ-143` repairs disabled personal transport, travels to the next world
  and saves after a further interaction. Here the original ship remains
  crashed, a different courier ship becomes usable, and the analysis stops
  before travel.
- `SYS-1082` reveals a fixed key chest when a ghost room brightens; it does
  not hand off both route information and transport permission.
- Weapon names, guard count, planet name and exact ammunition values are
  parameters, not new gene classes. The optional bolt-crank branch is not a
  compulsory gadget gate.

## Evidence and limits

- [Sony's original PS2 title
  announcement](https://sony.mediaroom.com/2002-05-01-Ratchet-Clank-Revolutionizes-the-Action-Platform-Genre)
  establishes edition identity and broad planet-travel design, not Novalis
  implementation.
- [RStein's original-game written
  guide](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps2/561107-ratchet-and-clank/faqs/20119),
  Novalis mission list, separates the three guarded ship shooters from the
  optional mechanic's 500-bolt Aridia Infobot. It uses an English-language
  European release, so no exact North American binary identity is inferred.
- [ZoopSoul's independent original-game Novalis
  route](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps2/561107-ratchet-and-clank/faqs/20673)
  marks the crank/Gold Bolt path optional and corroborates the subsequent
  crater, Kerwan Infobot and ship.
- No original disc, executable or gameplay capture was inspected. The
  exact death/checkpoint state and weapon-selection pause are unresolved.
