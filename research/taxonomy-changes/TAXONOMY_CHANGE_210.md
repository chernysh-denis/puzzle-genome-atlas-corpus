## Evidence and lower-ID transfer test

The [bounded game record](../../knowledge/games/a-f/eternal-darkness-sanitys-requiem.md) uses Nintendo's original-game overview for identity and the sanity premise, then contemporary written Ellia routes for the chapter's contact loss, finisher recovery, tomb fixtures and exit. `SYS-592` and `CON-501` specifically require low-sanity shadow entities becoming corporeal hostile actors in *Don't Starve Together*. The Ellia evidence instead supports deceptive audiovisual presentation without that physical-state claim. `INF-119` reports the character's meters but does not tell the player whether a perceived local event persists in the room. Existing movement, combat, limb-state, fixture, item, health and authored-gate boundaries transfer unchanged. The first Pious chapter has no sanity meter and is not silently included.

## Disposition

Accept two bounded definitions. Do not encode a fixed hallucination schedule, exact meter thresholds, a universal candle solution or later runic spells without version-pinned direct evidence. Retain all earlier signatures and verified combinations.
