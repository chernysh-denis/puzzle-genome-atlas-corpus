# Taxonomy Change 279 — Hades II Omega moves and Hecate's ward

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-17.
- Trigger: [GAME-0532](../../knowledge/games/g-l/hades-ii.md), the
  standard Hecate guardian encounter in native Nintendo Switch 2 v1.0.

Sixteen definitions transfer. Magick cost, channel duration, number and
position of witches, health breakpoints, curse duration and ring width are
parameters. The seven additions describe separate observed decisions.
No verified combination is a proper subset of the packet.
