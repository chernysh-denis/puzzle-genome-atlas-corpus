## Evidence and transfer tests

Publisher-booklet HTML/OCR and original-PS1 written sections support the
complete battle. Restricted indexed excerpts are not full-guide access.
No native executable, save, audiovisual or direct play was inspected.

- `ACT-014` and `ACT-019` transfer legal selected relocation and ability/target.
  `ACT-346` requires real-time frontage/grouping; `ACT-651` commits discrete
  actors to eligible starting panels. `CON-323` transfers the active-party cap.
- `SYS-388` rerolls initiative, `TIM-026` uses a live battle clock and
  `CON-034` locks movement after acting. Reject all three. `SYS-1283`
  accumulates Speed and subtracts by used opportunities, retaining overshoot.
  `CON-752` allows one Move and one Act in either order; `ACT-652` relinquishes
  unused opportunities. Costs and thresholds are parameters.
- `ACT-064` reverses an actor for an action cost. `ACT-653` selects end-turn
  facing. `ACT-094`/`ACT-095` alter rule-bearing projection and `ACT-610`
  follows a steered avatar; `ACT-654` changes only tactical view. `CON-753`
  preserves Move/Jump height/path, not an unverified sword-weight rule.
  `CON-001`/`CON-011` address vertical panels, not flattened 2D overlap.
- `SYS-389` requires spell/concentration/surface mechanics; `SYS-1284`
  resolves declared starting physical/basic command parameters and evasion.
  `SYS-004` transfers eligible chance, not an invented seed or guaranteed rear
  hit. `CON-269` transfers abilities, not ordinary Attack. `SYS-1213` consumes
  held items automatically; `SYS-1285` settles selected restorative stock or
  own-health payment and compatible result. Heal removes status, not HP.
- `SYS-390` rolls death saves, `SYS-538` settles wounds and `SYS-1266`
  concerns bounty bodies. `SYS-1286` maintains the local fallen count,
  loss/remains and protected guest/Ramza exceptions. `SYS-1287` consumes a
  spatial chest/crystal opportunity, not all victory loot automatically.
- `SYS-299` transfers EXP thresholds; `SYS-362` transfers encounter rewards.
  `SYS-1288` credits current-job JP and party spillover, not Formation spending.
  `SYS-537` uses a squad phase; `SYS-1289` orders a particular enemy/guest's turn.
- `INF-119` transfers own resources/build. `INF-361` includes quantity-bearing
  hex stacks; `INF-343` discloses AP/turn order. `INF-462` exposes height,
  current CT/actor, facing, reach and fallen count without perfect forecasts.
  `OBJ-029` transfers required-hostile defeat; `TIM-001` transfers discrete
  command resolution, not one command per entire turn or live deadline.

## Decision and limitations

Admit the boundaries without converting numerical parameters or job names into
genes. Preserve CT overshoot and protected loss exceptions. Initial counts,
skills, tie/countdown details and native success remain limited. Health
advisories require a same-unit disposition, not automatic taxonomy mutation.
No publication or earlier platform/combination changes follow this decision.
