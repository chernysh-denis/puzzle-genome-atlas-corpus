## Evidence and lower-ID transfer test

The [bounded game record](../../knowledge/games/s-z/vib-ribbon.md) combines PlayStation's original-game description, a contemporary European menu/controls guide and independently documented PAL `SYSTEM3.FSL` structure. The exact original-disc executable, selected music CD and input trace were not obtained; numerical thresholds and an exact audio-analysis algorithm are not admitted.

The player's timed shape-specific button or paired chord is not `ACT-533`'s teacher-phrase reply, `ACT-545`'s footstep chart or `ACT-603`'s touchscreen flick. The system's custom external-audio obstacle supply is not `SYS-496`'s one fixed authored soundtrack/geometry clock; the script documentation also prevents claiming that audio alone uniquely fixes the route. The local judge differs from a fret-strum chart, and the rabbit/frog/worm/Queen ladder differs from Guitar Hero's Rock Meter: mistakes can demote without immediate failure and later clears can restore form. Shape/form cues are not an omniscient chart view, and selected-track survival is not an authored-chart Rock Meter objective.

`INF-192`, `OBJ-002` and `TIM-003` transfer as local lookahead, optional score pursuit and real-time progression. Built-in tracks, `All Tracks`, `Speed`, `Auto` and freestyle scoring are separate modules rather than imported into this one-track packet.
