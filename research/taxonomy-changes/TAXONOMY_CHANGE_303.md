connection, item consumption and equipment transfer, ordinary aimed combat,
wall posture, contextual fixtures and deliberate noise; perception/diversion,
combat response, life, authored route gates, local refill and checkpoint retry;
sight, finite weapon-ammunition legality and event-specific save legality; discovered map/current view; live
progression and the configured real-world clock. Ground drag parameterises
ACT-048's retained object offset; no existing definition is broadened. The
carrier restriction is separately explicit in CON-822.

The named GAME-0557 salience review partitions the complete thirty-seven-gene scoped genome
without numeric weights. No previous gene, signature, family definition,
release mapping or historical receipt changes. This decision does not claim
cross-platform numeric parity, direct play or external novelty. The standing
singleton advisory is retained as comparative-review risk rather than a quota
or a reason to merge distinct causal predicates.

CON-578 is explicitly reused with solar energy as the finite compatible gun
ammunition. An empty gun can fail its shot predicate under positive sunlight;
a full gun can pass that predicate while zero sunlight independently blocks
purification under CON-823. The two constraints are not duplicates.

## Environmental heat review

SYS-1622 retains the original manual's excessive-light interval and temporary
weapon interruption, with the attributed script's repeated-overheat clock
cutoff kept qualified. The script says sunset; an account that says sunrise
must not silently replace it. Native thresholds, exact reset and pre-clear
activation are not claimed. The later tutorial is not proof of early immunity.
SYS-738 requires firing heat and timed active cooling; SYS-1432 shares heat
between weapons and flight; neither fits environmental exposure. CON-571 is
reused unchanged for independent firing readiness; it permits passive recovery
and does not require inventing an active-cooling minigame. Exposure can fill
energy and create heat at the same time, so neither reserve is a substitute
for the other. Long deliberate overheat experiments remain a potential module.
