# Taxonomy Change 318 — Use a weapon card for route access

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: Medium
- Date: 2026-11-20.
- Trigger: [GAME-0572](../../knowledge/games/m-r/neon-white.md), the initial
  run of Rebirth 1-5 Elevate on Nintendo Switch.

| Addition | Accepted boundary | Rejected substitution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ACT-776 | Commit the discard input to consume one active dual-use copy, losing its own firing option. | ACT-161 attacks with the currently equipped tool and is reused. Firing does not request the discard effect. |
| SYS-1679 | Spend Elevate to gain height during a jump, once per held copy. | SYS-1386 restores extra jumps by finding eligible support. Here the second impulse needs a card. |

| CON-835 | Permit two ordinary types and up to three copies of each. | CON-578 governs ammunition reserves, not held-copy capacity. |
| INF-545 | Expose the current card and ammunition alongside required enemies and the live timer. | INF-251 exposes a time-trial result, not the local state for deciding the next spend. |
| OBJ-314 | Settle the full level after both finite clearance and terminal contact, then submit time to grading. | CON-402 is reused for the early lock and SYS-711 for time grading. A lock opening or a particular medal alone cannot settle the level. |

Other reused definitions cover direct movement, conventional attacks, the
authored restart and live progression. Exact ammo accounting across copies,
optional card placement and original Switch medal cutoffs are unmeasured.
There is no external novelty claim. The earlier definitions and signatures
stay unchanged.

Six Ukrainian gene entries and all thirteen bilingual player cards were reviewed.
