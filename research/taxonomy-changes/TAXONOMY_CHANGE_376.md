## Boundaries

- ACT-837 covers contextual support, defence and passing during debate.
  ACT-838 instead commits a single ballot to an eligible participant.
  Neither choice directly settles the group result.
- SYS-1832 resolves a unique maximum tally into removal from later
  participation. It has no tie rule and does not reveal true allegiance.
- INF-585 distinguishes the active roster and the player's own role from
  the terminal report disclosing every original participant's true role.
  INF-268 covers the authored prompt for the current decision.
- OBJ-339 covers the crew's win after its last hidden opposing role leaves
  participation. A vote alone, a crew removal or a tally is insufficient.
- TIM-045 records the visible procedural counter in a bounded discussion
  phase that precedes a separate ballot. This is not a real-time deadline
  or a refillable action budget.

See GN-001 through GN-006 in the bounded analysis.
