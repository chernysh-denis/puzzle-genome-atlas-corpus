# Taxonomy Change 160: Rhythmic formation orders and Fever

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-09-27
- Trigger: `GAME-0422` *Patapon*, original PSP first Patata Plain hunt.
- Scope: admit `ACT-560`, `SYS-1116`, `SYS-1117` and `INF-414` as Active; reuse live time and a level-exit-with-retained-optional-progress objective without changing earlier signatures.

## Problem and accepted change

The player does not echo a teacher for a rating or step on chart arrows. Four drum beats encode a chosen army instruction; the formation then answers and moves or attacks on its own. Correct consecutive calls can move that combat response into Fever. The live beat pulse and attack-ready cue are distinct decision information from the command input and its autonomous resolution. Four typed boundaries keep input, formation response, accumulated empowered state and information separate.

## Transfer and rejection tests

- Reject `ACT-533` and `SYS-1058`: the first hunt teaches Attack, but subsequent March/Attack phrases are selected by the player rather than repeated after each instructor call.
- Reject `ACT-545`: no physical directional foot panels or scrolling chart are involved.
- Reject `SYS-215`: it excludes autonomous squad engagement and cannot stand in for the phrase-to-formation response.
- Reject `CON-071`: it describes a selectable commander-led squad relocated as a unit; the original PSP phrase addresses the available formation's behaviour.
- Reuse `OBJ-181`: crossing the goal and returning to a selectable map with optional credited spoils is a level completion, not a requirement to kill every prey.
- Do not invent exact beat windows, Fever thresholds, prey count or UMD revision from a manual.

## Evidence and limits

The [original Sony PSP manual](https://manuals.plus/m/022be6c58003e45d11649414457926eeb0088f6e624a743a3006b6a45791a966.pdf), English pp. 2–8, gives the drum patterns, beat cue, formation response, mission goal, health and Fever; the [contemporary original-PSP guide](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/psp/942065-patapon/faqs/51882) places Attack teaching and hunting in the first Patata Plain mission. The [PlayStation producer's contemporary tips](https://blog.playstation.com/2008/02/29/patapost-friday-pata-tips-1/) independently name that first hunt and describe chained-command Fever. None is a direct input or audiovisual trace. The first-hunt goal position and exact timing remain unverified.

## Decision

Accept four scoped genes with reviewed bilingual boundaries and no earlier signature or verified combination changes. Require deterministic comparison, localisation, art, repository and browser gates before the authorised one-unit local commit.
