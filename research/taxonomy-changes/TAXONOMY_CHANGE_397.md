- Proposal status: Accepted

- Scope: [The Plucky Squire](../../knowledge/games/s-z/the-plucky-squire.md),

- Decision: add ACT-864, SYS-1881, SYS-1882, CON-872 and INF-603. Reuse six
  definitions and preserve earlier signatures.

## Boundaries

- ACT-864 extracts or seats a literal word in reachable authored text.
  ACT-048 still owns carrying and setting down a loose item. ACT-060 puts
  a phrase token into a structured answer rather than transporting a
  world object between sentence locations.
- SYS-1881 applies a completed authored modifier to its local referent.
  SYS-017 parses spatially arranged rule statements, with SYS-018 binding
  their class properties. A partially blank moat sentence in this scope
  can preserve the existing water state until the replacement is set.
- SYS-1882 gives the elevated route access to a switch. The inverse water
  state then exposes a lower route while retaining the earlier switch
  result. SYS-061 instead depends on continued occupancy of its switch.
- SYS-220 carries an avatar and its held item through an active portal.
  CON-872 binds legal transfer positions to authored points of this
  exposed spread, with ordinary exterior navigation between them.
- INF-603 shows readable local text, its editing affordance and the
  crossing prompt while both portable words and portal state matter.
