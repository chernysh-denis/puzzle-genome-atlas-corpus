# Taxonomy Change 174: Table service, satisfaction and chaining in Diner Dash

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High

- Trigger: `GAME-0437` *Diner Dash*, original Windows PC v1.0 early Career shift.
- Scope: admit `ACT-574`, `SYS-1145`, `SYS-1146`, `SYS-1147` and `CON-712` as Active. No earlier signature or verified combination changes.

## Problem and accepted change

One finite restaurant shift has several coupled but separable decisions. The
player assigns waiting parties to clean available tables, then interleaves
their ordered order, kitchen, meal, payment and cleanup stages. Each party's
satisfaction changes its tip, while repeating one kind of service action across
tables adds a separate chain bonus. A table that has not been cleared cannot be
reseated. These are different direct-input, state-transition and eligibility
boundaries, not five names for a generic time-management genre.

## Transfer and rejection tests

- Reuse `ACT-341` for contextual ready-stage clicks and `ACT-091` for delivering
  a prepared meal to its addressed requester; neither assigns a waiting group
  to one of several eligible tables.
- Reuse `SYS-030` for arrival, `CON-620` for local request expiry, `INF-330`
  for current requests and waiting pressure, `OBJ-013` for a finite-opportunity
  score target, and `TIM-003` for live progression.
- Reject `SYS-823`: no preceding player-controlled stock-gathering activity
  determines this shift's menu. Reject `SYS-975`: a service-action batch is not
  a continuous traversal-trick combo.
- Treat the number of two-seat tables, the two-item hand limit, colour classes,
  tip goal and chain multiplier as parameters; no arithmetic is invented.

## Evidence and limits

[PlayFirst's original v1.0 Windows README](https://oldgamesdownload.com/readme/diner-dash-windows-readme-english/), preserved by an archival host, directly documents the stage sequence, clean seating, customer happiness and tips, limited carrying, repeated-action bonus, and shift/star rule. [The licensed Shockwave listing](https://www.shockwave.com/gamelanding/dinerdash) identifies the Windows mouse game and the same basic tasks. [A first-hand original-PC service guide](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/926256-diner-dash/faqs/63909) corroborates how ready tables and customers behave. [A level-specific community route](https://dinerdash.fandom.com/wiki/Walkthrough:Flo%27s_Diner_(Diner_Dash)) suggests the early 1-2 layout but was available only through indexed text; exact table count and scripted arrivals are not universal gene claims. No original executable or direct play was inspected, so exact timing and scoring arithmetic remain unknown.

## Decision

Accept five distinct typed boundaries with reviewed Ukrainian copy, original
artwork and complete repository/browser gates. One local game-unit commit is
authorised; no push, public publication or deployment.
