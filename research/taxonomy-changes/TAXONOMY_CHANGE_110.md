# Taxonomy Change 110: Route-bound photography and reviewed species images

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Confirmed
- Evidence quality: Direct
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-09-23
- Trigger: `GAME-0371` Pokémon Snap, original Nintendo 64 rules represented
  by Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack.
- Scope: nine additive Active genes and one clarified transferred gene;
  no earlier game signature, lifecycle or combination changes.

## Problem

An ordinary camera control label does not explain Pokémon Snap's causal
packet. A fixed moving tour gives the photographer only transient scenes;
unlocked food changes a subject's behaviour; finite film constrains captures;
one image per species is chosen after the tour; Oak scores that image and
retains the best species entry. Existing Dead Rising `ACT-502` isolates a
shutter commitment, but its wording overfit battery/processing readiness even
though those are carrier parameters. Dead Rising's immediate PP award
`SYS-1001` cannot stand in for Oak's delayed selection and best-shot report.

## Accepted change

- Clarify `ACT-502` to require a ready camera with a valid capture allowance.
  Dead Rising uses charge and processing; Pokémon Snap uses remaining film.
  The action remains the same instantaneous, content-sensitive live capture.
- `ACT-506` places a non-damaging item lure near an autonomous subject.
- `ACT-507` marks one captured frame per species for post-course appraisal.
- `SYS-1008` resolves an eligible subject's food response.
- `SYS-1009` scores the selected photo and retains the better species record.
- `CON-673` separates camera orientation from an authored moving route.
- `CON-674` bounds one excursion by a finite photo roll.
- `CON-675` limits appraisal to one candidate of each subject type.
- `INF-378` exposes recognition and remaining film before capture.
- `OBJ-214` joins course completion to the declared photo-appraisal checkpoint.

## Lower-ID transfer and rejection test

- `SYS-045` transfers for automatic ZERO-ONE locomotion, `INF-299` for the
  post-course category report, and `TIM-003` for real-time missed
  opportunities. `ACT-502` transfers after its readiness clarification;
  Dead Rising's original carrier and signature do not change.
- `ACT-489` is a wall-tap sound lure, not a thrown food item placed beside a
  creature. `SYS-1001` awards immediate PP for a live photograph and does
  not choose a prior-best per-species Report image.
- `CON-143` lets perspective affect an Echochrome walker's route; Pokémon
  Snap's look direction cannot steer ZERO-ONE. `INF-376` exposes rechargeable
  camera charge and processing cues, not finite film plus species recognition.
- No already reviewed combination is a verified proper subset of this new
  packet; one carrier does not establish a recurring composition.

## Evidence and limits

Nintendo's original English instruction booklet directly documents the
Beach course, moving ZERO-ONE, focus sensor, 60-photo roll, goal gate,
Pokémon Food, per-species Camera Check mark, Oak's score categories and
best-shot Report comparison. Nintendo's current Switch Online announcement
supports the licensed distribution identity. No binary, cartridge, direct
play, exact item unlock threshold or hidden scoring coefficients were
inspected. The saved-state entry with Food unlocked is a declared analytical
condition, not a claim about a particular observed play trace.
