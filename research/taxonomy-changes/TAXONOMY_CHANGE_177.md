## Problem and accepted change

The original booklet's desert example has a specific causal sequence: `Refresh Him` hints at a need without naming an answer; the player submits an eligible noun through the Notepad; a corresponding world object appears; the player gives it to the wanderer; and the Starite appears but still requires Maxwell to reach it. The accepted noun gate is distinct from whether a produced item helps this actor. The Object Meter separately limits simultaneous experiments. These are portable action, world-response, constraint and information boundaries rather than genes for lemonade, the game's word count or a specific DS input gesture.

## Transfer and rejection tests

- Reuse `ACT-008` for Maxwell's direct movement, `ACT-341` for the actor interaction and `OBJ-025` for collecting a single puzzle-gated progress token.
- Reject `ACT-091`: its carried-inventory hand-in is not evidenced when a newly spawned world object is dragged onto the wanderer.
- Reject *Baba Is You*'s text-block action and `SYS-037`: its placed word objects rewrite a spatial rule sentence; the DS Notepad instead creates one corresponding object.
- Reject recipe selection or ordinary crafting: the player supplies a recognised noun rather than choosing a finite local recipe, and the resulting object remains manipulable before an independent need test.
- Reject `TIM-003`: the manual calls the actor wandering but establishes no forced timed decision window or deadline in this puzzle.
- The accepted noun list, precise Object Meter maximum, scoring economy and full object interaction rules remain parameters or unknowns, not invented genes.

## Evidence and limits

The [original DS instruction booklet](https://db.hfsplay.fr/files/2020/07/14/Manual_AHhWSCb.pdf), pp. 5–8 in the scanned copy, documents Notepad word entry, object appearance and manipulation, Object Meter deletion, blocked terms and the complete desert-wanderer example. [Mykas0's first-hand original-game guide](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ds/955689-scribblenauts/faqs/57775) identifies The Gardens Puzzle 1-5 and records water and rain as further refreshing solutions; it is not a complete word whitelist. [5th Cell's postmortem](https://media.gdcvault.com/GD_Mag_Archives/GDM_November_2009.pdf) describes authored object properties and categories, without proving an exact internal predicate for this puzzle. No original DS cartridge was run here.
