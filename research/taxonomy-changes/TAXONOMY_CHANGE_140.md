# Taxonomy Change 140: Daily expedition policy and river-risk settlement

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Confirmed
- Evidence quality: Direct
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-09-25
- Trigger: `GAME-0402` *The Oregon Trail*, original 1985 MECC Apple II
  school disk A-157, first Independence-to-Kansas crossing interval.
- Scope: admit `ACT-538`–`ACT-541`, `SYS-1075`–`SYS-1078`, `CON-695`,
  `INF-400`, `OBJ-236` and `TIM-029` as Active. No earlier genome or
  verified-combination change.

## Problem and accepted change

The original A-157 instructional booklet supplies an unusually explicit
model: pace and rations remain set across days; distance depends on oxen,
health, terrain and weather; the group spends food and may sicken; hunting,
barter and rest cost days; the Kansas River offers condition-dependent
methods whose accidents and prices differ. Existing generic randomness
`SYS-004` is retained. Twelve new boundaries separate player policies and
resupply inputs, automatic daily and crossing responses, survival and
information limits, one far-bank terminal, and choice-paced time.

## Transfer and rejection test

- `ACT-286` is a driver's timed sleep at an eligible road stop, not editable
  group pace/rations or a trail rest; `ACT-178` is a polity's foreign purchase
  rule, not a one-day barter with a traveller. Direct-avatar movement
  (`ACT-008`) cannot describe menu-priced wagon days.
- `SYS-626` couples campaign food with battle-wound recovery but does not
  resolve A-157's ration level, changing weather, collective health, illness
  and animal-conditioned pace. `SYS-816` moves a clock through continuous
  boating and actions, while this game charges integral simulated days after
  menu decisions; `TIM-029` owns that decision rhythm.
- `OBJ-026` requires a directly controlled avatar made spatially connected;
  the first Kansas crossing transports a whole party and wagon through a
  measured risk and closes only one leg (`OBJ-236`).
- A-157's farmer $400 and banker $1600 are edition parameters. H-106 gives
  its banker $2500; that difference is an edition audit, not a new gene.
- Named rivers, animal species, historic month, $5 ferry price and 100-pound
  meat cap are parameters of the accepted genes, not extra taxonomy entries.
