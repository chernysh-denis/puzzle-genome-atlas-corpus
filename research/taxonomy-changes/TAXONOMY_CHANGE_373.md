# Taxonomy Change 373 — Held route control and stationary reveal

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-21.
- Scope: [Killer7](../../knowledge/games/g-l/killer7.md),
  GAME-0627, first North American retail GameCube encounter on Deadly.
- Decision: add ACT-832, ACT-833, ACT-834, SYS-1828 and INF-582 as Active.
  Preserve earlier definitions, memberships and verified combinations.

## Boundaries

- ACT-832 covers held movement along an authored route and discrete local
  reversal or branch choice. ACT-530 consumes die-counted board movement
  and SYS-1745 carries a rider automatically after boarding a cart.
- ACT-833 replaces route controls with stationary first-person aim for
  the duration of a held input. ACT-679 changes magnification, while
  ACT-823 toggles persistent firing forms. Those actions do not own this
  temporary exchange of traversal for combat.
- ACT-834 requires a manual pause and refill of a bounded ready stock,
  without spending a limited carried reserve. ACT-183 transfers tracked
  reserve ammunition and SYS-1271 reloads empty clips automatically.
  The release-era technical description and the visible Reload episode
  establish the scoped timed refill.
- SYS-1828 reveals the active target and enables ordinary attack against
  it. INF-538 reads information about a selected enemy. CON-273 concerns
  allied vision and detection under fog. This active local reveal is
  independently commanded from the stationary view.
- INF-582 is the source-reported warning before visibility. INF-298
  shows direction and levels of hostile perception, while INF-516
  replaces ordinary vision with a focused sound representation.

Treat the exact disc revision, timing and critical mark behaviour outside
Deadly separately. See K7-001 through K7-007.
