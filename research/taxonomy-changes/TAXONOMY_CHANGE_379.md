## Boundaries

- ACT-842 checks one offered description against a live observed
  subject. ACT-751 is a submitted object for somebody else to identify.
  The new rule fills the guide without changing the subject's membership.
- ACT-843 combines a chosen position and a separate command to sing.
  INF-587 provides own and reference spacing plus progress. The
  alignment does not follow a fixed beat chart.
- CON-864 gates the interaction on a recovered family whistle.
  Identification or a universal stock of consumables cannot replace it.
- SYS-1834 evaluates the submitted song in its current spatial state.
  A spooked target gives no new member. The system's verdict does not
  determine the objective without observable following.
- OBJ-340 ends with one active follower. Unlike qualifying a resident
  in Viva Piñata or calling existing eligible idle sheep, this case
  settles the approach and song applied to the selected wild target.
