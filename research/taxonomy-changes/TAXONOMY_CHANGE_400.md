## Boundaries

- ACT-866 opens live follow-up replies. ACT-232 selects one after
  opening, and ACT-526 instead selects an addressed testimony line.
- ACT-070 covers the reusable placement operation. CON-875 retains
  identity and image compatibility for a fixed gap and orientation.
  CON-141 allows gapless coverage as the board condition, and ACT-780
  does not rotate the selected fragment.
- SYS-1888 exposes a separate clue after puzzle completion settles the
  encounter. SYS-119 requires the board to open a durable traversal
  gate and is excluded by the observed alternate passage.
- OBJ-349 includes pickup of the marked object and restored movement.
  OBJ-048 instead couples its object roster to a required exact cover
  and immediate gate. Mere passage without the fragment is a different
  outcome.
- INF-606 pairs the world view with the simultaneously usable local map,
  and allows the pair to present the dialogue and puzzle in their own
  roles. INF-148 describes the contextual response information.
