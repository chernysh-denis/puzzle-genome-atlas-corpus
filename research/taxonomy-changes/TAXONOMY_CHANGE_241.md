## Complete packet and evidence boundary

The complete Story Chapter 1 runs from ordinary New London control after the
recorded prologue choice through capital coal/shelter development, research
and mine construction, Council Hall and Vote of Confidence, western discovery,
the grave response, separately populated Old Dreadnought, completed trail and
required incoming oil rate, then ordinary Chapter 2 control. Finding oil alone
is not the terminal. Officer with Survivor off is a reproduction preset, not
a claimed preset in publisher footage. Exact installed executable, costs,
coefficients, native input, randomness and save writes are unverified.

The official launch-day guide, creator design account and publisher launch
confirmation establish qualitative released rules. Complete manual transcript
and seven actual muted paused frames establish source displays, not native
execution. Contemporary chapter/council/economy guides retain separate roles.
Overdrive accident/repair and grave consequences remain explicitly qualified;
indexed-only stored-resource transfer evidence does not become direct play.
The disputed universal-whiteout shutdown rule is rejected, not silently fixed.
This packet exits before the later whiteout objective. The archived October 4
protest-unblock fix establishes intended recovery and a launch bug, not proof
that an original executable reliably unblocks a resolved protest.
