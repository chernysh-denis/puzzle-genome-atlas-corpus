## Problem and accepted change

The original Vita release separates front-screen paper peeling from rear-pad drumming. Peeling changes what surface is available; rear tapping launches the messenger only when the corresponding drum is occupied. Collapsing these into one generic platform contact would erase the distinct input channels and their order. The admitted scope is a source-supported analytical route example, not a verified fixed chapter or exact geometric trace.

## Transfer and rejection tests

- Reuse `ACT-008` for direct movement of the messenger, `OBJ-026` for reaching a bounded upper surface, and `TIM-003` for live motion.
- Reject `SYS-1056` as the whole launch rule: its LittleBigPlanet 2 bounce pad launches on contact, whereas the Vita drum needs a rear touch while occupied.
- Reject a separate contact constraint: contact and mapped rear touch form the condition of `SYS-1125`; no independent resource, cooldown or fixed time limit is established.
- Do not transfer later independent jumping, PS4 remake controls, enemies, cosmetics or full-story victory into this early packet.

## Evidence and limits

[Sony's contemporary hands-on](https://blog.playstation.com/archive/2013/05/29/hands-on-with-tearaway-media-molecules-new-ps-vita-adventure) describes symbol-marked rear-touch surfaces and a tap that launches Iota or Atoi from a drum. A [first-hand 2013 review](https://gameranx.com/features/id/18851/article/tearaway-review-the-vita-s-greatest-adventure/) reports a paper coating peeled to expose a bouncy platform and says early Iota cannot independently jump. [A second early preview](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2013/10/25/tearaway-preview/) corroborates rear-touch platform-to-platform launching; [Pocket Gamer's lead-designer walkthrough](https://www.pocketgamer.com/tearaway/e3-2013-media-molecules-rex-crowle-walks-us-through-the-first-levels-of-tearaway/) describes manipulable tabs. No original cartridge or direct play was inspected. The precise combined sequence and map geometry remain inference, not a documented named level.
