## Confirmed portability defect

The old `OBJ-120` label named Eikthyr, its trophy and the first Forsaken Power.
Its definition repeated those product objects and described almost the same
key-to-fixture transition as `SYS-636`. That made the canonical Objective read
like a Valheim route instruction and obscured the transferable terminal.

The two genes remain legitimate typed siblings. `OBJ-120` owns completion of
the whole staged route and the accepted end state. `SYS-636` owns the automatic
state transition produced when the compatible carried key is applied to its
matching fixture. Shared carrier, timing and result do not merge an Objective
with the System transition that delivers its terminal.

## Objective transfer scan

All 165 Active Objective definitions were checked. The closest existing
boundaries remain distinct:

- `OBJ-014` ends when one designated payload reaches its receiver; it does not
  require an earlier guarded encounter or make payload possession an
  explicitly incomplete middle state on a route to a retained capability.
- `OBJ-025` ends at acquisition of a puzzle-gated progress token; `OBJ-120`
  instead requires its separately earned key to continue beyond possession.
- `OBJ-047` restores a persistent world service by completing collection
  groups, without a guarded encounter and a separately matched key fixture.
- `OBJ-048` makes a traversal passage persistent through an exact-cover
  arrangement, rather than accepting a guarded-route result at another fixture.
- `OBJ-054` terminates a production dependency chain by launching its built
  project; no progression key or retained character capability is required.
- `OBJ-080` ends after a mandatory route guardian and the opened spatial
  threshold are crossed; it does not carry a guaranteed result to a separate
  fixture for a later capability terminal.
- `OBJ-083` combines a first hostile encounter with later eligible-actor
  admission, but has no carried progression key or matching-fixture condition.
- `OBJ-127` settles an investigation by handing physical proof to an authored
  recipient and retaining quest completion, not by reaching first capability
  availability through a matching progression fixture.

The remaining Objective definitions fail at least one of the guarded-route,
separately earned key, matching fixture and retained-capability predicates.
No existing Objective can replace `OBJ-120` without losing an evidenced
decision boundary or importing an unrelated terminal.

## Carrier and evidence review

`OBJ-120` has one carrier, `GAME-0197` Valheim, and occurs in one registered
combination, `COMB-0195`. The accepted `VLH-003`, `VLH-009`–`VLH-012` ledger
already establishes the whole route: authored wayfinding selects the generated
encounter site; an exact offering opens the guarded encounter; settlement
guarantees a progression key; possession alone is non-terminal; the matching
central fixture is required before the first retained capability is available.

This correction changes only the abstraction of those accepted observations.
It makes no new claim about product version, runtime behaviour or direct play,
so no new external source, video or audio evidence is required.

## Decision

- Rename `OBJ-120` to **Complete a staged progression-key route to a retained
  capability**.
- Define the Objective by its accepted terminal: every required stage of the
  bounded route is satisfied and its first capability is persistently
  available after the separately earned key has reached the matching fixture.
- Keep the product objects in the Valheim `Includes` example and game-scoped
  interpretation, not in the transferable label or definition.
- Keep `SYS-636` unchanged as the matching-key transition owner.
- Do not create, merge, split, deprecate or migrate any ID.

## Rejection condition

Reopen this decision only if a later carrier shows that one of the defining
predicates is optional: the staged route, the separately earned progression
key, the distinct matching fixture or persistent first-capability terminal.
Shared encounter completion, item delivery, unlock wording or a common carrier
alone is insufficient to merge the Objective into another type or boundary.
