## Boundaries

- ACT-814 describes committing a carried cover against the actor currently
  wielding the required object. ACT-087 addresses fixed door hardware in
  the following operation. ACT-091 requires a requested voluntary hand-in.
- SYS-1807 resolves the interruption into a pickable instance of the same
  tool. The tool remains outside inventory until ACT-089. It is not a
  generated reward from a depleted or defeated target.
- SYS-1483 covers a temporary arena weapon's finite uses and impact-based
  disarm. The Drifter's scripted cover interruption has no such weapon
  resource. SYS-113 grants a capability component in exchange for the
  recipient's requested item; rebar appears on the floor.

application, released tool and separately selected rebar at the door.
The contemporary walkthrough corroborates the intermediate floor pickup.
See TD-003, TD-004 and TD-005. No claim is made about a later combat system
or real-world procedure.
