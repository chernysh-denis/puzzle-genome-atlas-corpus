## Evidence and lower-ID transfer test

The [bounded game record](../../knowledge/games/g-l/gravity-rush.md) uses PlayStation's contemporary traversal and combat descriptions alongside original-Vita chapter guides. No Vita executable, direct play or measured gauge trace was available. The original personal-gravity fall toward a visible wall is not Manifold Garden's `ACT-097` orthogonal surface selection and global `SYS-125` gravity-frame update: Kat changes her own direction of fall without turning the whole city. Ordinary movement, ground attack, continuous physics and real-time combat keep their earlier genes.

The directed airborne kick is a separate committed action because the player first enters gravity aim, then launches an attack along that fall, unlike `ACT-295`'s ordinary ground kick. The finite gauge constrains both travel and attacks, while `INF-445` describes the local aim cue rather than an omniscient future collision forecast. Contact acquisition still reuses `SYS-037`. The fountain's ten-gem threshold plus explicit interaction and the ordered combat-to-service terminal are not captured by a pickup or finite combat objective alone. Exact payment, gauge duration and later upgrades remain excluded.

## Disposition

Add seven bounded definitions and their reviewed Ukrainian versions in this game unit. Preserve all earlier signatures and verified combinations. This is an evidence-bounded taxonomy decision, not a claim that the exact Vita build or constants were locally measured.
