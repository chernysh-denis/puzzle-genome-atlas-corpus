## Boundaries

- SYS-1891 debits the current air supply on a sonar emission. This
  mechanic is separate from the background depletion and refill under
  SYS-543 and from the action boundary under ACT-571.
- SYS-1892 settles one rescue after the helpers arrive at the blocked
  site with the player. SYS-752 permits general commanded assistance.
  SYS-1002 credits delivery of the escorted person. SYS-067 requires an
  assignable weight and carrier-strength rule that the scene does not
  establish.
- SYS-1893 separately makes the onward route usable after reporting the
  rescue to the mother. It has a living actor obstruct a flow, with no
  inventory key or learned-song barrier.
- Reuse CON-460 for breathable reachability and OBJ-026 for reaching the
  newly passable destination. Do not import the Genesis game's returned
  directional map or compatible glyph song from the title alone.
