# Taxonomy Change 173: Cloth bridge, scarf charge and anonymous co-presence in Journey

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-09-27
- Trigger: `GAME-0436` *Journey*, original PlayStation 3 Bridge chapter.
- Scope: admit `ACT-573`, `SYS-1142`, `SYS-1143`, `SYS-1144`, `CON-711` and `INF-423` as Active. No earlier signature or verified combination changes.

## Problem and accepted change

The bounded passage does not reduce to generic movement: a local call or contact releases cloth into separate bridge spans, charged scarf power is spent on movement and recovered from cloth or optional proximity to a stranger, and online matching may introduce a nameless traveller without making two-player play mandatory. The visible scarf and bridge surface guide a crossing even when the bridge remains incomplete. These are separate player-input, world-transition, resource, social, legality and information boundaries.

## Transfer and rejection tests

- Reuse `ACT-008`, `OBJ-026` and `TIM-003` for direct traversal, reachable destination and live motion.
- Reject `SYS-1060` because an engineer does not enter a bridge-repair hut; cloth responds locally. Reject `SYS-097` because a rigid log does not settle in water.
- Reject `CON-633` because Noita's continuously held upward levitation is not the same scarf-powered jump and glide loop.
- Reject mandatory cooperative objectives because the PS3 Bridge can be crossed solo; partner contact is one optional recharge channel.
- Reject complete reconstruction as a terminal requirement: PlayStation's original `Threshold` trophy explicitly recognises the incomplete crossing.

## Evidence and limits

[PlayStation's original trophy list](https://blog.playstation.com/archive/2012/02/27/journey-the-trophies/) directly establishes the incomplete crossing. [Jenova Chen's pre-release creator interview](https://blog.playstation.com/2011/01/11/jenova-chen-explains-journey-social-relevance-and-artistic-inspirations/comment-page-2/) explains responsive cloth and borrowed power; its provisional controller details are not imported. [PlayStation's first-person post-release retrospective](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=224007) reports the anonymous encounter, call and optional mutual scarf recharge. [A contemporaneous first-hand PS3 route](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps3/997885-journey/faqs/63951) bounds the Bridge passage and ordinary controls. No original PS3 executable or direct play was inspected, so exact energy units, shortest crossing geometry and binary revision remain unknown.

## Decision

Accept six narrowly typed boundaries with complete Ukrainian review, original artwork and repository/browser gates. One local game-unit commit is authorised; no push, public publication or deployment.
