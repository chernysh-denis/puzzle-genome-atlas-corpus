## Problem

The vocabulary represented direct movement, continuous body dynamics, world
pickup, health, staged instruction, timed failure, score and graded results.
It did not represent Jet Set Radio's coupled rule chain: temporary rail/wall
attachment, a collision-breakable traversal trick chain, an in-range
size/stock-gated directional graffiti trace, surface replacement, progress-
triggered police waves and complete required-surface settlement into successor
stages.

  cited route explicitly labels the threshold approximate.

## Complete lower-ID transfer test

- `ACT-008` owns direct skate movement and jump but not the distinct graffiti
  trace. `ACT-222` modifies a chosen turn-based combat action and therefore
  cannot own live surface painting.
- `SYS-036` owns gravity and collision but not contact-bound carriage along a
  compatible authored rail or wall. Vehicle/path-following systems control a
  different actor relation.
- `SYS-717` and `SYS-873` chain rapid kills or matched dish orders, not
  connected traversal tricks terminated by collision.
- `SYS-222` collects spray stock but does not spend it or change a world
  surface. Generic inventory consumption lacks the typed graffiti transition.
- `SYS-057` begins from perception of the player; it does not schedule authored
  hostile roles from objective-surface progress.
- `CON-068` closes the attempt at expiry but does not express per-surface
  size/range/stock legality.
- Existing HUD boundaries do not join tag state and directional trace with
  spray, stamina, time and pursuit cues.
- Existing exhaustive-clear objectives target collectibles, hostiles or route
  gates; none settles a required set of mutable surfaces into three successor
  stage choices.
