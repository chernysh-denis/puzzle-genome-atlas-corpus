# Taxonomy Change 107: Photographic opportunity and civilian delivery

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: Medium
- Date: 2026-09-23
- Trigger: GAME-0368 Dead Rising, licensed Xbox One 2016 re-release, fresh
  72 Hour Mode through closed Case 1-2 and first manual save.
- Scope: four new Active boundaries; no earlier signature or lifecycle change.

## Problem

Direct combat, item use, timed incidents and world-fixture saving already have
adequate genes. A screenshot-style action does not capture a game-rule camera
that scores the current live scene. Generic experience progression does not
score composition. A temporary civilian escort to a safe-room boundary is not
the same as a combat ally's follow-and-assist relation. Existing HUD genes do
not reveal the camera's battery, processing readiness and PP subject cues.

## Accepted change

- `ACT-502` commits a legal photograph of the framed live scene.
- `SYS-1001` evaluates photographed content and credits eligible PP; `SYS-299`
  remains responsible for level thresholds and later capacity gains.
- `SYS-1002` routes and settles a recruited, vulnerable civilian at the
  designated safe boundary. `ACT-189` already covers an addressed goal point.
- `INF-376` discloses battery, processing and pre-shot scoring cues.
- Reuse `CON-597` for expiring Case and Scoop opportunities rather than
  `CON-068`, whose expiry terminates the entire attempt. Reuse `CON-621`
  for manual saving at an eligible restroom. No global 72-hour terminal is
  inferred from a missed Case inside this first segment.

## Lower-ID transfer test

- Viewfinder's picture placement changes traversable geometry; Dead Rising's
  camera captures a live scene for PP and does not instantiate a world plane.
- Pokémon capture uses a consumable device to recruit a target, not a
  photograph for scored observation.
- Half-Life's `SYS-752` follows with combat or fixture assistance; the Dead
  Rising rooftop civilians require safe arrival to credit their rescue.
- Detroit: Become Human already provides the bounded expiring incident
  opportunity `CON-597`; no new deadline gene is necessary.
- One new game does not establish a recurring verified combination.

## Evidence and limits

The official Capcom manual directly documents camera battery, processing,
PP markers, photo reward, escort controls, Case/Scoop time indicators and
designated saves. The Capcom Xbox One support page confirms fixture saving
in the 2016 release; two independent text routes corroborate the rooftop
reunion and Case 1-2 sequence. No Xbox One executable, save or audiovisual
trace was obtained; exact scoring weights and build-specific timing are open.
