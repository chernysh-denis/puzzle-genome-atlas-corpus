# Taxonomy Change 192: Original-Dreamcast charge, recovery and ring decisions

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Direct
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-09-30
- Trigger: GAME-0455, one original-Dreamcast Soulcalibur VS match.
- Scope: seven new Active boundaries; no earlier definition, signature,
  combination or published corpus revision changes.

## Evidence and boundary tests

The [game's ledger](../../knowledge/games/s-z/soulcalibur.md) cites visually
inspected Namco manual pages 4–23. Source-derived transitions are not a claimed
GD-ROM execution. Exact timings and final simultaneous VS presentation remain
unmeasured. Restricted player guides contribute only explicitly bounded
indexed default/stance testimony.

- ACT-604 commits a charge/alternate cancellation branch, not an attack
  contact (ACT-295), weapon-form selection (ACT-459) or a spent meter.
- ACT-605 requests compatible downed, Quick Roll or stagger recovery.
  Ordinary translation remains ACT-008; crouch remains ACT-202 and a
  recovery strike remains ACT-295. No duplicated input for those actions.
- SYS-1198 resolves a timed repel/parry into a brief freeze, defender
  initiative and an opposing counter-impact exception. SYS-777 requires
  knife wear; SYS-215's ordinary contact does not isolate this restricted
  reactive-priority contest.
- SYS-1199 resolves two charge branches into counter-like or selected
  unblockable effects and clears them on forced guard. SYS-868 changes
  weapon command form; SYS-360 owns character-specific resource/stance
  changes rather than this universal guard-cancellable charge rule.
- SYS-1200 awards the independent ring-departure round, including simultaneous
  departure's both-side draw. SYS-522 still owns its KO/timeout reset branch;
  SYS-638 spends platform-fighter stocks and does not fit round markers.
- CON-733 constrains opponent-relative lateral placement by an exposed
  finite ring. CON-667 explicitly has no ring-out terminal, CON-625 requires
  walls, and CON-443 is a side-view line. CON-446 remains the separate
  vitality/clock/required-win bound, not the only termination predicate.
- OBJ-259 admits required fixed-pair round wins through KO, timeout or ring
  departure. OBJ-099's unchanged wording includes only KO/time-over, while
  platform-fighter stock exhaustion is another objective. Do not silently
  broaden an earlier boundary or infer fighter/theme novelty.

## Reuse and decision

Reuse ACT-223 for eligible timed defence, including directional impact; its
general boundary fits without introducing a duplicate action. Reuse ordinary
contact, guard, throws, pose/range eligibility, weapon forms, information and
live time. Accept seven new definitions and twenty-four total uses. Preserve
the distinction between held guard, timed impact, charge, weapon form and
spatial victory; forbid later-edition meter/reversal mechanics and automatic
publication. All seven new definitions have reviewed Ukrainian equivalents.
