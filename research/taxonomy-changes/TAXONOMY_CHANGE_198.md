## Evidence and boundary tests

The [canonical game ledger](../../knowledge/games/s-z/the-sims.md) uses the original Maxis/EA PC manual. No game binary, saved household or direct day trace was inspected. The day and chair are research choices rather than authored victory conditions.

- `ACT-612` is the player's Buy Mode placement/orientation action after a priced chair is selected. `ACT-130` covers the costed acquisition, while `ACT-257` directs a resident's later use. Combining all three would obscure the manual's failed placement and blocked approach branches.
- `CON-739` tests the furniture's lot footprint and overlap at placement time. It differs from player-reach tile placement (`CON-208`) and from later contextual resident access (`CON-380`); legal furniture may still block a path.
- `INF-442` exposes private household cash plus itemised daily income and expense. `INF-058` explicitly excludes private household accounts and remains unchanged.
