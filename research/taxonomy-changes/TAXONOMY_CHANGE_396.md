# Taxonomy Change 396 — Recoverable aircraft armament and stage settlement

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-22.
- Scope: [Einhänder](../../knowledge/games/a-f/einhander.md), GAME-0650.
  Astraea FGA Mk. I, original North American release, Stage 1.
- Decision: add ACT-862, ACT-863, SYS-1877 through SYS-1880, CON-871 and
  INF-602. Reuse seven definitions and preserve earlier signatures.

## Boundaries

- ACT-862 equips a recoverable enemy weapon during live flight. ACT-771
  removes a held weapon from an active hostile's hand. SYS-1877 determines
  whether the component survives the carrier's disabling hit. SYS-1680
  issues a fixed weapon card after defeating a designated enemy.
- ACT-863 swaps two concurrently usable mount positions. ACT-550 cycles
  an arsenal into a single active channel. ACT-763 selects a single or
  composite mode from two retained components. CON-825 permits duplicates
  and composition. CON-871 admits two active distinct types and treats
  matching recovery as upkeep of the fitted type.
- SYS-1878 uses part- and weapon-weighted destruction credit. The eligible
  defeat and token chain in SYS-1844 has different credit criteria.
- SYS-1879 spends a finite reserve attached to the fitted unit. The same
  type can repair and refill it, and depletion expels it. A reload of an
  otherwise persistent weapon does not share that terminal behaviour.
- SYS-1880 separately settles enemy defeats, acquisitions, elapsed boss
  time and retained craft into points added to live score. OBJ-288 concerns
  successful boss clearance and successor control regardless of receipt
  formula. INF-602 exposes individual mount and salvage conditions during
  play rather than only reporting their stage result.
