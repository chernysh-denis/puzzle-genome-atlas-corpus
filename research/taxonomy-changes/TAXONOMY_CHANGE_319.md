# Taxonomy Change 319 — Excavation, occupation and indirect dungeon defence

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: Medium
- Date: 2026-11-20.
- Trigger: [GAME-0573](../../knowledge/games/a-f/dungeon-keeper.md), complete
  Eversmile in the original DOS campaign.

| Addition | Accepted boundary | Rejected substitution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ACT-777 | Mark or clear a terrain job before an eligible worker takes it. | ACT-318 commands a selected worker at a resource. ACT-475 directly strikes terrain. |
| ACT-778 | Carry owned creatures in a bounded hand and release them on claimed floor as autonomous actors. | ACT-036 gives a selected agent a role without relocating it. Transit control or possession is a different mode. |
| ACT-779 | Pay a worker-count-dependent price to create an Imp at a claimed tile. | ACT-347 hires a settlement recruit into a force. The Imp is created at a targeted slab and does not use Portal recruitment. |
| SYS-1681 | Carry out marked excavation and establish subsequent connected floor ownership. | SYS-550 completes a foundation or repairs a building. Excavation and ownership precede functional room placement here. |
| SYS-1682 | Admit enabled types periodically through a claimed Portal and enforce local eligibility. | CON-514 governs manual recruitment from stock for payment. Ordinary Portal arrivals require no purchase action. |
| SYS-1683 | Creatures service their own sleeping, food and wage needs from physical dungeon facilities. | SYS-694 is the narrower mercenary pay-to-rebellion rule. It does not capture multi-service room use or species exemptions. |
| SYS-1684 | Release two authored assaults after preparation, population, time and prior defeat conditions. | SYS-1650 uses a repeating mining-then-assault cycle. Eversmile progresses through two guarded stages with no mining timer expiring into a restart. |
| CON-836 | Gate a paid room tile on ownership, usable floor and unlocked type. | CON-466 requires a foundation and a builder assignment. This placement is immediate, after Imp work has already made the floor legal. |
| INF-546 | Expose current scenario requirements, room status and combat in a partial map. | INF-126 owns assignment, resource and worker disclosure without tutorial and live-battle feedback. |
| OBJ-315 | Defeat the dispatched final hero assault while the home Heart survives. | OBJ-159 uses a fixed wave schedule and a surviving defence stock. Here both preparation and earlier clearance gate the assault, and the protected target is the Heart. |

Reused ACT-152 defines the functional room footprint. SYS-051 covers
autonomous engagement. SYS-549 applies to carrying mined finite gold to compatible
storage. TIM-003 covers continuous job and battle progression.

Room sizes, price, species, creature cap, exact countdowns and notifications
are parameters. The documented transition model keeps exact DOS script
identity open. No earlier definition or signature was changed. Ten
Ukrainian gene records and fourteen bilingual player cards were reviewed.
