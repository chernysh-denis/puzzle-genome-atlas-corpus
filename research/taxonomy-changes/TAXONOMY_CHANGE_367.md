# Taxonomy Change 367 — Leader-selected shared detour

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-21.
- Scope: [Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds](../../knowledge/games/s-z/sonic-racing-crossworlds.md),
  `GAME-0621`, commercial Xbox Series X|S opening Donpa Grand Prix race.
- Decision: add ACT-827, ACT-828, CON-861, INF-579, OBJ-333 and
  SYS-1820 through SYS-1823 as Active. Earlier definitions remain intact.

## Boundaries

- ACT-827 records steering into an offered gate as the selection input.
  CON-861 reserves the decision to the leader at the boundary. SYS-1820
  applies the decision to the entire field and preserves race progress.
  An individual shortcut or a route selection in a menu lacks this bundle.
- INF-579 covers the route information visible at the offered gate,
  separate from access or choice. SYS-1821 governs the altered final lap
  on the opening track, separate from the shared visiting lap.
- ACT-828 ties successful directional aerial input to a landing boost.
  SYS-1823 covers entering water and using the resulting vehicle form.
  SYS-1822 covers the ring stock and hit response. These rules reuse
  ordinary racing input and immediate pad acceleration independently.
- OBJ-333 requires the classified finish and the associated points retained
  for the current cup. OBJ-226 and OBJ-257 instead focus on first place.
  A cup win remains outside the observed endpoint.

The commercial manual and launch material support the authority rule.
The Xbox recording shows the selected shared route and result. It
does not identify the chooser from the sampled frames. See SRC-003
through SRC-008.
