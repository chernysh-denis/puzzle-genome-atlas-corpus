# Taxonomy Change 389 — Charged propulsion through the first castle

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-22.
- Scope: [Rocket Knight Adventures](../../knowledge/games/m-r/rocket-knight-adventures.md),
  GAME-0643. North American Genesis cartridge T-95046.
  Easy primary trace from Stage 1 entry to live Stage 2.
- Decision: add ACT-856, CON-869, INF-597 and SYS-1856
  through SYS-1859. Reuse six definitions. Preserve all
  earlier definitions, signatures and verified combinations.

## Boundaries

- ACT-856 uses the ordinary attack input to prepare a held
  action and selects a lunge or spin on release. ACT-749
  releases from an arena boundary and can choose a separately
  funded super-dash. ACT-797 changes a supported jump by holding
  its jump control.
- CON-869 gates the charged action at a full held gauge. CON-809
  instead gates and bounds expenditure from a persistent charge
  stock. In the admitted case, ordinary attacks need no charge.
- INF-597 exposes local held charge alongside separate vitality,
  score and stock. INF-347 shows a platform-stage timer and
  attempt resources. Time from a speedrun overlay is external.
- SYS-1856 redirects the active lunge on ordinary solid
  surfaces. SYS-1557 depends on specialised classed bumpers.
- SYS-1857 changes the control set after a temporary pickup in
  the route. SYS-910 covers the common contact pickup and applies
  to both vitality and mobility effects. A short directed burst
  remains distinct from sustained flight.
- SYS-1858 separates a boss head's changing point of entry from
  its travelling body. SYS-1535 locks boss damage at a health
  break pending a summoned wave. The precise head damage values
  and invulnerability window remain unmeasured.
- SYS-1859 closes on the final boss defeat and credits the clear
  and remaining-vitality bonuses separately. SYS-905 begins at
  a separate finish marker. SYS-1415 specifies an awarded
  retry-stock carry, which this tally does not isolate.
- OBJ-329 is reused. The final adversary's defeat triggers the
  result directly, without an exit token. Earlier route fights
  do not replace that final requirement.

See RK-001 through RK-008. The commercial cartridge documentation
and the two visible clear cases define the claim boundaries.
