# Taxonomy Change 286 — Blast Corps protected-route clearance

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-17.
- Trigger: [GAME-0539](../../knowledge/games/a-f/blast-corps.md),
  original Nintendo 64 Simian Acres.

| Addition | Reviewed boundary | Counterexample |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SYS-1560` | The separate transport advances on an authored path and ends the attempt on contact with a remaining blocker. | `SYS-384` advances or reverses a convoy through contest or nearby allies. `SYS-1388` concerns a pursuing hazard. |
| `INF-523` | An obstruction is marked because it overlaps the protected actor's route. | `INF-017` predicts a path formed by mutable routing geometry. The semi's bearing points to an exit. |
| `OBJ-299` | Clear the corridor before its moving protected occupant reaches each obstacle, then accept safe closeout. | `OBJ-030` protects an actor during hostile clearance. `OBJ-181` preserves acquired optional credits at a normal closeout without specifying a carrier route. |

Eight definitions transfer, including seated vehicle control,
impact-driven removal, local collectible activation and a mission-gated
exit. Obstacle width, destructible materials and the exact path timing
remain carrier parameters. No verified combination is a proper subset.
