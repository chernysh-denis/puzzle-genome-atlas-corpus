# Taxonomy Change 350 — Commission the first repair specialist

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: Medium
- Date: 2026-11-21.
- Scope: [GAME-0604](../../knowledge/games/s-z/the-alters.md),
  first Technician and the commercial Xbox prologue departure.

ACT-805 is the commitment to a marked alternate life, INF-567
the story-and-role preview that makes it actionable, and SYS-1777
the resulting separate crew member with memories and an authored skill.
An assignment of an existing worker or a character respec cannot
produce that result. CON-136 covers the prior failed attempts, calls,
sequential access to the branch and the dependency of the repair on
the awakened specialist. SYS-1108 requires ongoing assigned room
repair under hazards and is outside the sampled scripted repair.

SYS-1778 describes the surveyed deposit footprint, distinct from the
bearing/distance disclosure of INF-279. CON-849 separates the registered
extraction endpoint from an operational relay path back to the base.
CON-232 instead requires a shaft and mechanically covered power demand.

CON-850 separates the current mass-dependent fuel predicate from
the taught collection target. INF-568 records that the stock and
requirement are disclosed before the move. The fixed beacon-jump fuel
and route graph in CON-704 are outside the scope. OBJ-327 requires
the assisted repair followed by the observed first departure. OBJ-143
would additionally require a reached destination and verified retention.

No earlier definitions or signatures change. No combination is added.
