# Taxonomy Change 344 — Separate casting, hooking and landed weight

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-21.
- Scope: [GAME-0598](../../knowledge/games/s-z/sega-bass-fishing.md),
  first Lodge area in the 1999 Dreamcast Arcade Mode.

ACT-794 addresses an aimed cast that leaves a controllable tether.
ACT-795 is the separate bite-time strike. ACT-796 preserves the
independent controls for winding and steering while the line is out.
ACT-199 already covers the compatible lure choice. ACT-476 assumes
the reel command follows the bite, so its fishing action is too narrow
for this sequence.

SYS-1759 covers approach to an actively worked lure and the ensuing
hook opportunity. SYS-1444 covers the separate disturbance and
pre-bite reeling escape rules of New Leaf. SYS-1760 tracks retrieval,
recoverable strain and possible breakage while the fish is attached.
The first game does not require the sequel's landing confirmation
meter. INF-561 describes the distinct exposure of line out, tension
and contextual control cues.

SYS-1761 separates the hit-time award from the catch-time award.
The area is still live at both events and the first awards no weight.
The carryover boundary of SYS-1204, pickup event in SYS-1556 and
single paid-fare bonus in SYS-1104 do not capture this split.
OBJ-324 requires the minimum sum of landed mass under CON-068
expiry, not a count of distinct creatures under OBJ-242 or the
size of a single body under OBJ-218.

No new combination is proposed. Earlier definitions and game
signatures remain intact.
