# Taxonomy Change 189: Capsule separation and anchored colour targets

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-09-30
- Trigger: `GAME-0452`, original NES *Dr. Mario*, one single-player bottle.
- Scope: admit `SYS-1187` and `CON-729`; preserve every earlier gene definition,
  game signature and verified combination.

## Evidence and accepted boundaries

The [bounded source record](../../knowledge/games/a-f/dr-mario.md) combines
Nintendo's contemporary NES-VU-USA booklet, later first-party rule
corroboration and the expressly described capsule/virus patent embodiment.
The printed booklet's cascade diagram shows a removed partner leaving its
survivor to fall into a second clear. Fixed target cells are distinguished
from movable capsule components; no original executable was run.

## Transfer and rejection tests

- Reuse active falling-piece pose/rate input, variable selection, timed
  descent, blocked-descent locking and successor introduction.
- Reuse `SYS-010`'s qualifying geometry/minimum-size parameters for a
  four-or-more colour line; do not fork a gene merely for the number four.
  Reuse `SYS-011` for eligible vacancy falls and `SYS-012` for repeated
  resolution. `CON-729` independently limits the class eligible to fall.
- `SYS-008` shifts whole occupancy rows, not individual eligible capsule
  components. `SYS-1159` allows unbonded Lumines columns to land independently,
  not an intact capsule whose bond survives ordinary support loss.
- `SYS-1187` changes pair membership only when a matched half disappears.
  Losing support alone retains the pair. Clearing every component regardless
  of colour or merely drawing its survivor round would not meet this rule.
- `CON-729` does not make viruses indestructible; their same-colour match can
  remove them. Generic static barriers and immutable clue assignments cannot
  express both their removal eligibility and exemption from capsule gravity.
- `SYS-006` already owns an acceleration-schedule parameter, so the booklet's
  ten-capsule speed growth does not warrant another gene. Colour, dimensions,
  score, speed and setup level also remain parameters.
- Reuse visible-state/one-successor information, score/target-clear objectives
  and live placement time. Do not import swap, refill, hold, opponent garbage,
  finite move budget, special skills or a campaign union.

## Decision

Accept the two Active boundaries and their reviewed Ukrainian pairs. This is
a causal separation, not an external novelty claim or proof of byte-identical
patent/retail code. A future original-cartridge trace should refine ordinary
support and collision edges without silently changing earlier signatures.
