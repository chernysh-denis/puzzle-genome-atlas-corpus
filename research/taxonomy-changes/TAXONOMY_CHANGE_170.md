## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted

- Confidence: High

- Trigger: `GAME-0433` *Ecco the Dolphin*, original English Genesis Bay of Medusa passage.
- Scope: admit `ACT-571`, `SYS-1137` and `INF-422` as Active. No earlier signature, definition or verified combination changes.

## Problem and accepted change

One directional player song can request an echo map or address a crystal. A key glyph grants a matching song that releases a later barrier; the lock is not a consumed carried key. The requested returned echo gives local spatial information about rock, air and glyphs, not a fully visible global map. These action, world-resolution and information boundaries are causally separate from direct swimming and a continuously draining breath reserve.

## Transfer and rejection tests

- Reuse `ACT-008` for direct movement, `SYS-543` and `CON-460` for breath, `INF-075` for the visible meter, `OBJ-026` for the opened destination and `TIM-003` for live progression.
- Reject `ACT-471`: its Odradek pulse surveys terrain and cargo, but does not issue a song that can also address a keyed glyph.
- Reject `SYS-063`: Ecco learns an acoustic capability; no physical key is consumed.
- Reject `INF-001`: the player requests a local echo; the remaining cavern is not already fully disclosed.
- Later 3DS save-anywhere and no-breath Super Dolphin mode do not apply to the original Genesis scope.

## Evidence and limits

[Sega's hosted Genesis booklet](https://manuals.sega.com/genesismini/pdf/ECCO_THE_DOLPHIN.pdf) directly covers the original buttons, breath, sonar map and glyph classes. [Sega's later explanation](https://archives.sega.jp/3d/ecco/) explicitly separates the key crystal's taught song and the matching gate crystal, while documenting portable-only conveniences excluded here. An [independent first-hand written route](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/genesis/563323-ecco-the-dolphin/faqs/31465) supports the Bay of Medusa ordering. No original cartridge revision, direct play, measured air duration or audiovisual run was inspected.

Accept three typed boundaries with reviewed Ukrainian copy, complete lower-ID comparison, coherent original art and repository/browser gates. This unit authorises one local commit only; no push, public corpus publication or deployment.
