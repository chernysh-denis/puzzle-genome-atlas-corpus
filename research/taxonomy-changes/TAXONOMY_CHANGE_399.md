# Taxonomy Change 399 — Acoustic masking, breathing and readable sound levels

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-23.
- Scope: [A Quiet Place: The Road Ahead](../../knowledge/games/a-f/a-quiet-place-the-road-ahead.md),
  GAME-0653, native Xbox Series X Hospital garage and immediate successor.
- Decision: add SYS-1886, SYS-1887 and INF-605. Reuse eight definitions
  and preserve earlier signatures.

## Boundaries

- SYS-1452 already turns surface and movement rate into footstep stimuli.
  SYS-1886 lets independently present local background cover a stimulus
  without replacing its source. SYS-057 governs the listener's resulting
  investigation or diversion. No global alert or exact masking threshold
  is assumed.
- SYS-1887 tracks a noise-relevant respiratory condition attenuated by
  an inhaler. It does not consume a supply of protected breathing time
  as in SYS-796, or restore ordinary health. The route has no proven
  hard requirement to use the item.
- INF-605 pairs current ambient and actor-emitted noise on one tool.
  It is distinct from our general meter and status gene INF-119 and
  from the optional creature-awareness light.
