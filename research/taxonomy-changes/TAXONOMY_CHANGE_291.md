- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-19.
- Trigger: [GAME-0544](../../knowledge/games/s-z/space-channel-5.md),

| Addition | Reviewed boundary | Counterexample |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ACT-742`, `SYS-1573` | Respond to remembered direction and timing with A for an alien or B for a human. | Dance-only symbol reproduction lacks the target-type beam choice. Free projectile aim lacks the prior ordered demonstration. |

| `INF-525` | Read turn ownership, timed calls, typed targets, numeric rating and applicable hearts. | `INF-396` requires the ordinal rap display rather than this numeric and typed-target presentation. |
| `OBJ-301` | Finish the whole first report through its boss, survive live failure channels and meet its audience minimum. | A perfect-run target, song survival alone and a terminal grade without a live gauge differ. |

Four definitions transfer unchanged: `ACT-533`, `SYS-1058`, `SYS-369`
and `TIM-003`. The original manual resolves a reversed button description
in one guide. Its boss-heart failure is retained without inventing exact
ordinary-scene zero handling, input tolerances or rating coefficients.
No existing signature, definition or verified combination changes.
