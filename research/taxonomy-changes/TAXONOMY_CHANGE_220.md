## Evidence and lower-ID transfer test

The original EA Xbox booklet was rendered and visually read. Two independent
original-Xbox routes corroborate first-contract entry, panels, wild ammo, capture
and store return. Shop inventory conflicts; no-buy is an explicit configuration,
not an invented absence. Quiksave details have one written source and remain
Limited/Medium. No executable, gameplay audiovisual or direct play was inspected.

- `ACT-164` selects one occupied active item and does not cover independently
  assigned barrel loads (`ACT-639`). `ACT-202` explicitly excludes a camera
  change; `ACT-640` changes perspective together with shooting/fast-traversal
  affordances. A mere camera orbit is insufficient.
- `ACT-194` and `CON-276` commit a creature-capture device and recruitment
  attempt, not held collection of an incapacitated bounty body. `ACT-491`
  carries/repositions a world body rather than removing it into deferred
  capture inventory. `ACT-641`/`CON-748` preserve those differences.
- `ACT-211` treats a selected wound and `ACT-407` consumes a carried
  restorative. Shake healing (`ACT-642`) commands stamina conversion instead.
  `SYS-798` explicitly prices attack, evade, sprint and guard; original
  Stranger's melee/impact/heal and quiet recovery use `SYS-1270` without
  broadening that Dark Souls definition. Personal health still reuses `SYS-578`.
- `ACT-161` transfers for melee/breakable attacks but not every critter
  placement. `ACT-406` excludes firing a weapon; `ACT-184` requires a thrown
  grenade. `ACT-643` aims/fires live ammo at actors, eligible fixtures or trap
  surfaces without silently broadening these earlier inputs.
- Ordinary contact loot reuses `SYS-037`; hunted ammo first requires a
  subdued wild critter and compatible capacity (`SYS-1264`). `CON-578` covers
  finite reserve; clip readiness/automatic downtime (`SYS-1271`) differs from
  reserve, a player reload (`ACT-183`) and graded active reload (`SYS-924`).
- Loaded electrical charge (`SYS-1265`) is not a release-charged vehicle drift
  or a finite special-energy pool. Compatible powered-hit fixture response
  (`SYS-1272`) changes a linked gate/crane, not `SYS-065`'s continuous platform
  traversal or `SYS-755`'s object destruction itself.
- `SYS-778` creates a temporary close follow-up attack, not mutually distinct
  recovery/expiring-body outcomes (`SYS-1266`) or web/gas incapacity
  (`SYS-1267`). These are status transitions; species are parameters.
- `SYS-051` already covers deployed autonomous combat and transfers to placed
  Fuzzles. `SYS-057` already covers perception-triggered diversion and transfers
  to Chippunks. `SYS-045`, `SYS-215`, `SYS-755` and `CON-077` transfer unchanged;
  no global shared alarm, guaranteed path, or one new gene per critter is added.
- Completed held body collection (`SYS-1268`) and state-valued store payout
  (`SYS-1269`) are separate. `SYS-649` is repeated incapacitation into custody
  and hostage return; `SYS-906` grants persistent rescue credit, neither has
  alive/dead-valued deferred captures. A defeat alone does not bank this payout.
- Existing equipment/personal/local information transfers as `INF-073`,
  `INF-119`, `INF-115`. `INF-364` includes conditional radar suppression that
  is absent here; `INF-458` instead exposes nearby classified actors, facing,
  alert and lure range. `INF-122` exposes a capture probability, not the
  progress/incapacitation/pending-bounty disclosure in `INF-459`.
- `CON-282`, `SYS-369`, `TIM-003` and `TIM-007` transfer authored order,
  saved failure return, live time and reversible snapshots unchanged.
  `OBJ-155`'s authored neutralise/collect/escape loop is not contracted,
  state-valued return settlement (`OBJ-272`). Whole-campaign surgery is absent.

## Decision and limitations

On-touch Ukrainian `SYS-369` includes/excludes were corrected to preserve
the complete unchanged English checkpoint boundary and examples. The missing
Highway Heist example and Ori opening-route qualifier are restored; unsupported
manual-slot/restorative-object exclusions are removed. This is a reviewed
presentation correction, not a taxonomy or earlier-signature mutation.

Admit these eighteen boundaries with complete Ukrainian localisation, named
causal-role review and game-specific bilingual plain language. All carrier
timings, species, amounts and named targets remain parameters. The original
binary, route geometry, shop conflict and exact save retention require later
lawful execution. No earlier gene definition or signature changes, verified
combination additions, automatic suggestion decisions or new platform identity
are authorised. Taxonomy-health advisory metrics receive an explicit unit
disposition after regeneration, not an automatic freeze or mutation.
