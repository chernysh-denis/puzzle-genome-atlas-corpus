# Taxonomy Change 114: First garden-residency transition

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Confirmed
- Evidence quality: Direct
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-09-23
- Trigger: `GAME-0375` Viva Piñata, original Xbox 360 first-garden Whirlm
  residency packet.
- Scope: five additive Active genes; no prior signature, lifecycle or verified
  combination changes.

## Problem

The contemporary manual distinguishes a piñata's appearance near the
garden, its visit and its eventual residence. A visible visitor is not yet
resident. The player clears junk, can later sow persistent grass, inspects
Condition Status and changes the garden until one Whirlm visibly gains
resident colour. Existing crop, zoo-placement and calendar wildlife genes
do not jointly explain this staged, habitat-qualified transition.

## Accepted change

- `ACT-512` clears individual tutorial garden junk with a shovel.
- `SYS-1021` evaluates and applies one wild-visitor-to-resident change.
- `CON-679` keeps species appearance, visit and residence gates distinct.
- `INF-382` exposes the highlighted species' Condition Status.
- `OBJ-217` ends one bounded path at a coloured resident Whirlm.

## Lower-ID transfer and rejection test

- `TIM-003` transfers live progression while the player edits the garden.
  Its definition is unchanged.
- `ACT-213` plants or tends one crop, not tutorial junk clearance;
  `SYS-921` selects real-calendar wildlife availability, not a garden
  individual becoming resident; `ACT-458` transfers a stored animal directly
  into a prepared enclosure; `CON-635` validates that enclosure. The booklet
  documents grass sowing but does not prove it is required for the first
  Whirlm; no grass action gene is admitted. None of these existing genes is
  widened to erase the distinct first-garden conditions.
- No existing verified combination is asserted by theme. A proper-subset
  genome scan is required before acceptance.

## Evidence and limits

The original Microsoft Game Studios Xbox 360 Viva Piñata instruction booklet,
pp. 8–9, 14–17, documents shovel clearance, Whirlm approach, the grass
packet, species-specific stages, Condition Status and the coloured-resident
transition. It does not state an exact numeric Whirlm threshold or arrival
timer. No disc or direct-play trace was inspected.
