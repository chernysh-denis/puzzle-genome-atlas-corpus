# Taxonomy Change 348 — Score the ring route and a controlled glider landing

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-21.
- Scope: [GAME-0602](../../knowledge/games/m-r/pilotwings-64.md),
  Albatross Nest on North American Nintendo 64.

ACT-008 still describes direct local steering. The separate ACT-803
is a held flare, which brakes the unguided approach and changes the
pilot’s contact posture. ACT-321 describes conventional powered
aircraft and runway handling, while ACT-680 starts a previously
undeployed gliding mode. Neither supplies this landing command.

SYS-1772 persists the unpowered exchange between speed and height.
Local rising air replenishes height. SYS-556 requires the powered
fixed-wing runway-to-stop envelope. SYS-1773 registers the geometric
ring passage and retains one weighted credit per ring per attempt.
CON-438 remains ordered checkpoint and lap progression.

CON-847 checks contact instead of ring count, distinguishing the
target pad and controlled touchdown from short or unsafe contact.
SYS-1774 presents the result through separately measured ring credit,
pad accuracy and impact. The observed score combines all three.
No universal formula for invalid contact is asserted. OBJ-326
retains the badge tied to the scored flight. OBJ-133 is time-based
track settlement and OBJ-300 assumes a bounded stunt race.

INF-565 describes the flight instrument and radar feedback, including
relative target heights and thermal areas. The existing slalom
balance and gate display in INF-424 does not describe it. TIM-003
retains live decision progression in flight. No previous definition
or signature changes, and no combination is added.
