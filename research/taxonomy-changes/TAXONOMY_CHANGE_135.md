# Taxonomy Change 135: engineer-restored bridge connectivity

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-09-25
- Trigger: `GAME-0397` *Command & Conquer: Red Alert 2*, original English PC
  Allied opening mission through the Soviet supply-base terminal.
- Scope: admit `SYS-1060` as Active; no older genome, lifecycle or verified
  combination changes.

## Problem and accepted change

The original Westwood instruction booklet specifies that an Engineer can
enter a bridge-repair hut to restore a destroyed crossing. Independent written
routes for the first Allied mission use that exact interaction after Fort
Bradley becomes available. The repaired crossing changes where the ground
force can travel before the enemy-base assault. `SYS-1060` owns this
specialist-to-hut map transition; the destination order remains `ACT-189`.

## Transfer and rejection test

`SYS-097` turns a water-settled object into a traversable bridge without a
specialist or hut. Ordinary building repair restores health, not a destroyed
crossing's path topology. The bridge's location, repair timing and the
Engineer's exact travel route remain parameters. Tanya's swimming alternative
does not perform this transition and is outside the declared ground-crossing
route. No earlier game signature is modified.

## Evidence and limits

- [Original Westwood instruction booklet](https://oldgamesdownload.com/manual/command-conquer-red-alert-2-windows-manual-english/),
  hosted by a mirror, explains Engineer entry into a bridge hut; the mirror
  is not an independent gameplay witness.
- [Original-game Allied campaign guide, 2001](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/476890-command-and-conquer-red-alert-2-yuris-revenge/faqs/12650)
  and [separate written first-mission route](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/914165-command-and-conquer-red-alert-2/faqs/43153)
  independently place an Engineer at the Fort Bradley bridge during *Lone
  Guardian*. The first guide's host category says *Yuri's Revenge*, but its
  text explicitly declares original Red Alert 2 v1.006; no expansion rule is
  imported.
- No executable, controller trace, save, screenshot, video or audio was
  inspected. Exact animation and pathing timing remain unverified.
