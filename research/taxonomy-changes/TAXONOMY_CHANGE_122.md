# Taxonomy Change 122: persistent household selection and live desires

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-09-24
- Trigger: GAME-0383 The Sims 2: Legacy Collection, a bounded two-adult Live
  Mode household day in EA's 2025 Windows reissue.
- Scope: four additive Active genes and an edition-neutral clarification of
  `INF-168`; no earlier game signature or verified combination is revised.

## Problem and accepted change

EA's current Legacy control guide and original PC manual establish persistent
household-member selection, contextual directions, motive/mood pressure,
autonomous activity, relationships and displayed Wants/Fears. Maxis' design
diary corroborates aspiration-linked desire generation. The original booklet
describes core Live Mode, not necessarily every numeric parameter of the 2025
reissue with its bundled expansions.

- `ACT-524` selects a different persistent household member as command owner
  without replacing or freezing the other resident.
- `SYS-1039` applies a matched displayed Want or Fear event to aspiration and
  reward feedback.
- `INF-389` exposes the current desire/fear and aspiration state independently
  of bodily needs.
- `OBJ-225` captures one fulfilled displayed Want as a bounded local positive
  goal; the study's next-08:00 boundary is not a game victory.
- `INF-168` now says "mood or emotional state" so its existing The Sims 4
  supporter and new The Sims 2 supporter share one accurate interface boundary.

## Lower-ID transfer and rejection test

`ACT-257`, `SYS-205`, `SYS-431`, `SYS-432`, `CON-380`, `INF-072`, `INF-168`
and `TIM-003` transfer at their existing causal boundaries. `ACT-228` changes
direct movement/combat protagonist authority; here the other resident keeps
living autonomously. `SYS-433`, `INF-067` and `OBJ-052` are tied to authored
staged scenarios, not an open-ended household's current Wants/Fears. No
combination containing those scenario genes is falsely supported. The
deterministic selected-neighbour comparison is in the canonical game record.

  corroborates aspiration-linked player-facing desire selection.
- No Legacy executable, save, patch, interaction trace or exact tuning was
  inspected. Pack-exclusive effects and exact icon counts are not inferred
  from the older manual.
