# Taxonomy Change 346 — Street basketball style and stored special shots

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-21.
- Scope: [GAME-0600](../../knowledge/games/m-r/nba-street-vol-2.md),
  one standard North American PS2 Pick Up Game.

ACT-798 distinguishes the contextual basket command from ACT-413,
which requires separate control of release timing. ACT-799 isolates
a dribble trick that can unbalance a live defender and feed style
credit. ACT-800 separates the active choice of shooting, banking or
cancelling a live special-shot opportunity from later score settlement.

SYS-1764 covers the joined style values, bonus multiplier, defensive
exception and diminishing repeat credit. SYS-717 is a kill and
experience chain. SYS-1765 is the team's style-fed gauge with passive
decay, defensive loss and a transient full opportunity. SYS-1766
requires an explicitly stored first charge, refill and the rival's
ability to cancel storage. SYS-1767 is conditional on an actual made
special basket and changes both match scores. SYS-762 still represents
ordinary weighted baskets. SYS-1768 is the different, player-owned
Turbo reserve. It pays move tier, unlike a shared vehicle reserve
under SYS-988 or the team Gamebreaker gauge.

CON-845 covers an observed possession-clock violation. CON-585
additionally requires rim contact or scoring after release; that
reset predicate was not established here. INF-562 distinguishes
style credit, match points, personal reserve and live and stored
team signals, without requiring a match clock as under INF-290.
OBJ-325 uses a minimum score and lead rather than a match clock
or a finite scoring-action allotment as under OBJ-013.

Shared basketball, switching, team coordination and terminal genes
are reused where the established definitions fit. No new
combination is proposed. Earlier definitions and signatures remain
intact.
