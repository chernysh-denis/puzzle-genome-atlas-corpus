# Taxonomy Change 121: world-addressed brush restoration

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-09-24
- Trigger: GAME-0382 Ōkami HD, 2017 PS4 opening route through Kamiki's fruit.
- Scope: five additive Active genes; no earlier signature or verified
  combination is revised.

## Problem and accepted change

Capcom's original Ōkami manual describes a dedicated Celestial Brush view,
drawn world effects, Rejuvenation and Power Slash. Two independently written
PS4 HD routes agree on the first constellation grant, river repair, statue
sword repair, second grant, route cutting and fruit terminal. Existing direct
movement, reflector combat, health, acquired-power route gate and reserve
genes transfer, but the world-addressed drawing and authored restoration do
not fit a static puzzle-answer mark or an ordinary weapon strike.

- `ACT-523`: submits a mark over the current embodied world's visible target.
- `SYS-1036`: validates its learned pattern and context before an effect.
- `SYS-1037`: reconstructs missing authored geometry, enabling a route or
  linked constellation.
- `SYS-1038`: completes a constellation to retain another brush technique.
- `OBJ-224`: closes the bounded two-power route by cutting the fruit that
  restores Kamiki's cursed landscape.

## Lower-ID transfer and rejection test

`ACT-008`, `ACT-161`, `ACT-341`, `SYS-215`, `SYS-578`, `SYS-755`,
`CON-349`, `CON-351`, `INF-119`, `INF-372` and `TIM-003` remain within their
existing boundaries. `ACT-521` marks and submits one static Layton answer;
it does not address a traversable scene. `ACT-161` is an equipped-tool strike,
not a brush-screen mark. `SYS-755` removes a damaged obstruction but cannot
describe restoring a missing river or sword. `OBJ-026` reaches a place; this
terminal requires the acquired powers and the fruit-triggered change to a
world site. The selected lower-ID near comparison lives in the canonical
game record and is not a basis for changing an earlier signature.

## Evidence and limits

- [Capcom's original Ōkami
  manual](https://static.capcom.com/okami/manuals/PS2_Okami_Manual.pdf),
  pp. 12–17, establishes brush operation, finite refilling ink, Rejuvenation
  and Power Slash. It does not establish the exact PS4 binary or buttons.
- [Capcom's PS4 HD product
  listing](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/product/UP0102-CUSA08418_00-OKAMIHD000000001)
  establishes the named 2017 remaster and single-player scope.
- [chris-williams's PS4 opening
  route](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps4/221040-okami-hd/faqs/79107/kamiki-village)
  and [SubSane's independent PS4
  guide](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps4/221040-okami-hd/faqs/76318)
  corroborate the ordered world-state changes and fruit terminal.
- No disc, install, controller trace, video, audio, screenshot or save was
  inspected. Exact brush-cost values and death-restoration state are not
  promoted to facts.
