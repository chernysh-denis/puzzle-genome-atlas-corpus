# Taxonomy Change 166: Editable course, trial and historical clear proof

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Confirmed
- Evidence quality: Direct
- Confidence: High

- Trigger: `GAME-0428` *Super Mario Maker*, original 2015 Wii U course-making packet.
- Scope: admit `ACT-567`, `ACT-568`, `SYS-1129`, `SYS-1130`, `CON-707` and `OBJ-247` as Active. Reuse direct movement, live body dynamics, visible state, local viewport, self-paced editing and real-time trial. No earlier signature or verified combination changes.

## Problem and accepted change

An ordinary platform game's route is developer-authored. Here the player changes the course grid outside live play, immediately tests that current layout with direct platform movement, retains it as a named local course and—under the original online service's rules—had to prove a full clear before upload. Treating all of that as generic platform traversal would erase the editor, while conflating trial, save and upload would misstate which conditions apply to which transition.

## Transfer and rejection tests

- Reuse `ACT-008`, `SYS-036`, `INF-001`, `INF-192`, `TIM-002` and `TIM-003` in their appropriate editing or trial phases.
- Reject `ACT-162`: GamePad course tiles are not survival-world held items constrained by carried material, avatar reach and support.
- Reject `TIM-006`: the trial is directly controlled platform play, not a locked autonomous deterministic machine run.
- `CON-707` describes an **historical** service gate, not a requirement to save or trial and not an assertion that uploading is currently available. Nintendo ended Wii U course upload in 2021 and Wii U online communication in 2024.
- The illustrative gap-and-block layout is not represented as a witnessed course or a measured maximum jump; checkpoint rules and post-launch editor elements are outside this packet.

## Evidence and limits

[Nintendo's original electronic manual, Create](https://microsite.nintendo-europe.com/super-mario-maker-manual/enGB/page_01.html) establishes GamePad placement/erasure, trial play and editor return. [Nintendo's saving instructions](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/15180/c/950) distinguish a named Coursebot save. [Nintendo's Upload manual](https://microsite.nintendo-europe.com/super-mario-maker-manual/enGB/page_05.html) requires a creator clear before sharing. [Nintendo's discontinuation FAQ](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/53854/p/603) supplies the later service boundary. No Wii U executable or actual saved course was inspected.

## Decision

Accept six distinct boundaries only with deterministic comparison, reviewed Ukrainian presentation, original rule-valid artwork and all repository/browser gates before this unit's one authorised local commit. No push or public publication is authorised by this record.
