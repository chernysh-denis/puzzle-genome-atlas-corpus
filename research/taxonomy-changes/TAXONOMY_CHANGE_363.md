# Taxonomy Change 363 — Separate sensory inspection and first-request confirmation

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: Medium
- Date: 2026-11-21.
- Scope: [Strange Antiquities](../../knowledge/games/s-z/strange-antiquities.md),
  `GAME-0617`, original Nintendo Switch opening Hunter's Boon request.
- Decision: add ACT-819, INF-576 and OBJ-331 as Active. Keep the existing
  definitions, carriers and combinations within their reviewed scope.

## Boundaries

- ACT-819 selects an authored diagnostic channel for a single held view.
  INF-576 records the channel-dependent traces and separate reference clue.
  INF-530's botanical evidence comparison does not require choosing among
  multiple sense controls for one inspected object. ACT-370 follows a
  world-space trail rather than changing the sensory axis of an isolated item.
- Reuse CON-821 and SYS-1603 for the authored identity gate, outcome feedback,
  retained specimen and authored entry. The botanical examples remain intact.
  The material of the specimen does not change those predicates.
- Reuse ACT-341 for calling and addressing the current visitor and ACT-752
  for the separate, optional label. Do not infer an item debit or copy the
  botanical all-day objective.
- OBJ-331 stops at the acknowledgement and new reference entry from one
  request. OBJ-305 covers the whole authored botanical day and following
  clue. The label and physical carry state do not define either objective.

The Nintendo Switch captures show the inspection, acknowledgement and
successor page. The two language versions also document the difference
between the user-written label and the result issued by the game.
