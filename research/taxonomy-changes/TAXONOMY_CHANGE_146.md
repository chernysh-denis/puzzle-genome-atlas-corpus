# Taxonomy Change 146: Own-body inhalation and recoverable copied ability

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Confirmed
- Evidence quality: Direct
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-09-26
- Trigger: `GAME-0408` *Kirby's Adventure*, original NES Vegetable Valley
  stage `1-1` through the goal jump and expanded Level 1 map.
- Scope: admit `ACT-548`, `ACT-549`, `SYS-1090`, `SYS-1091`, `CON-697`,
  `INF-404` and `OBJ-240` as Active. Reuse `ACT-008`, `ACT-190`,
  `SYS-036`, `SYS-045`, `SYS-578`, `SYS-911`, `INF-192` and `TIM-003`.
  No older genome or verified combination changes.

## Problem and accepted change

Normal-form inflation is an always-available mode, not an acquired hover
pickup. The directly controlled body inhales one eligible nearby enemy and
holds it until the player swallows or expels it; swallowing an ability-bearing
enemy changes the active command. Damage or SELECT returns that copy as a
recoverable star, while a live vitality/life display discloses the risk.
The first-stage exit and its goal-jump closeout reveal new map doors without
a boss, all-secret condition or immediate travel into the successor stage.
These seven boundaries isolate those causally different transitions.

## Transfer and rejection test

- `ACT-008` transfers for direct movement, `ACT-190` for using an acquired
  Beam command, `SYS-036` for live gravity/collision, `SYS-045` for moving
  enemies, `SYS-578`/`SYS-911` for vitality and finite-life recovery,
  `INF-192` for local side-view visibility and `TIM-003` for live pressure.
- Super Mario World's `ACT-493`, `SYS-978` and `CON-660` require a ridden
  companion's tongue and mouth. Kirby's own directly controlled body is the
  ingesting actor, and the swallowed enemy grants a retained active ability
  rather than a mount shell effect.
- `ACT-473` requires a temporary inflation capability acquired for an ASTRO
  BOT stage. Kirby's Normal form itself inflates and repeatedly flaps.
  `SYS-902` begins with platform pickups, not a swallowed hostile and
  recoverable copy star.
- `OBJ-164` transitions immediately into a next playable region;
  `OBJ-194` requires every hidden detour; `OBJ-230` requires a boss and fixed
  retained weapon. None describes an ordinary stage exit that expands the
  level-selection map. Beam, Waddle Doo, exact vitality count and bonus-jump
  height remain parameters rather than separate gene IDs.
- No prior signature is retrofitted to make the new genes appear more reused.

## Evidence and limits

- [Nintendo's preserved original NES
  booklet](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/clv/manuals/en/pdf/CLV-P-NAAPE.pdf)
  directly states the input, Normal flight, mouth resolution, copy, ability
  loss/recovery, HUD, health/lives, goal jump and map reveal.
- [Nintendo's abbreviated official
  guide](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/clv/manuals/en/pdf/CLV-P-NAAPE_en.pdf)
  corroborates the inhale gate and recovery sequence in text-readable form.
- [Brian Sulpher's original-NES written
  walkthrough](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/nes/563432-kirbys-adventure/faqs/28822)
  locates Waddle Doo/Beam and the high wall in `1-1`; this stage-local route
  has not been independently played on a verified cartridge revision.
