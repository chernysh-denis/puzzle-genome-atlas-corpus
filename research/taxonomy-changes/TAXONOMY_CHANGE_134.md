# Taxonomy Change 134: instructor-led rhythm response and live rap rating

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: Medium
- Date: 2026-09-25
- Trigger: `GAME-0396` *PaRappa the Rapper Remastered*, PS4 first Normal dojo
  stage through a Good-rated finish.
- Scope: admit `ACT-533`, `SYS-1058`, `SYS-1059`, `INF-396` and `OBJ-233`
  as Active; no existing signature, lifecycle or verified combination changes.

## Problem and accepted change

Sony identifies the 2017 PS4 edition, preserved original gameplay, the first
dojo teacher and button-to-rhythm composition. The original-game written
guide describes an instructor phrase followed by a timed player answer and
live performance ratings. The PS4 trophy list identifies first-stage Good
and optional Cool outcomes. The selected Good finish avoids overclaiming
whether Bad/Awful song-end results unlock another stage in this edition.

`ACT-533` records the delayed response commitment, `SYS-1058` the authored
call/answer alternation, `SYS-1059` the live rating/failure rule, `INF-396`
the actionable turn/beat/rating display, and `OBJ-233` this one-stage Good
terminal. The teacher identity, song, beat symbols, exact timings and PS4
visual/vibration aids are parameters, not independent genes.

## Transfer and rejection test

Guitar Hero III's `ACT-508`, `SYS-1010`, `INF-379` and `OBJ-215` require a
continuous fret-plus-strum chart with its own Rock Meter; they do not imply
an instructor's preceding call. Simon reproduces an order but not a live
musical response with an ordinal rap rating. `TIM-003` is reused for the
continuing song; no redundant new time or constraint gene is added. No prior
game's genome is altered.
