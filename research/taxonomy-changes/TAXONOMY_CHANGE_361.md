## Boundaries

- ACT-797 already covers holding then releasing a supported jump.
  CON-856 records the spatial condition that an active mark accepts
  the designated trick there. Performing an ollie away from the
  mark still illustrates the action but does not clear the mark.
- SYS-1810 records the completed mark set, instructing fixture
  destruction and the opening of its forward route. SYS-1485
  attaches a contact-latched switch set to a gate without a
  designated trick and destroying the teaching fixture.
- OBJ-026 separately records reaching the next lane by riding
  through the exposed exit. A reward glimpse or an open portal
  does not by itself establish arrival.
- No minimum combo total, exact input duration, simultaneous
  positioning, known alternative order or cross-port parity is
  admitted.

Two independent commercial PS5 recordings, official input context
and the written Chapter 1 route corroborate SKST-002 through SKST-006.
