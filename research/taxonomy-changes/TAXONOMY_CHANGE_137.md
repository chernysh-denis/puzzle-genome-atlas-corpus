# Taxonomy Change 137: dependent companion and shared gate

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-09-25
- Trigger: `GAME-0399` *Ico*, original North American PS2 tower opening
  from first control to the first shared couch.
- Scope: admit `ACT-535`, `SYS-1064`, `SYS-1065` and `OBJ-235` as Active;
  no older signature or verified combination changes.

## Problem and accepted change

The original Sony PS2 instruction manual documents calling Yorda, taking
her hand, pulling her up ledges, her exclusive authority over Idol Doors,
shadow-portal capture and saving when both sit on a couch. Two contemporary
written opening routes place these rules together between her cage release
and the first couch. Existing direct movement, switch, strike, room view,
combat, actor-permission and save-fixture genes are reused. Four new genes
separate the addressed companion input, her resulting position-dependent
movement, the shadow's recoverable portal abduction and the paired
gate/checkpoint terminal.

## Transfer and rejection test

- `ACT-408` and `SYS-752` describe a toggled guard or scientist whose local
  role includes autonomous combat or healing. The opening Yorda cannot be
  treated as that substitutable helper. `SYS-407` similarly grants an
  autonomous persistent party companion independent attack or support.
- `CON-076` already expresses the unequal traversal and interaction
  permissions: Ico can climb the route and Yorda opens the Idol Door.
  A duplicate permission gene would be unjustified.
- `OBJ-026` accepts a directly controlled avatar's destination arrival;
  reaching this door without Yorda cannot finish the packet. `OBJ-235`
  requires her gate action and both actors at the first couch. `CON-621`
  separately retains the fixture's save eligibility; it does not by
  itself define the route's positive terminal.
- The cage, lever, R1 button, stone door, stick, couch and visible shadow
  silhouette remain carrier parameters. The manual's later swords, bombs
  and shadow variants do not enter this opening genome.

## Evidence and limits

- [Transcription of Sony's original North American PS2 manual](https://icoshrine.neocities.org/gallery/manual_transcripts),
  pp. 4, 10, 12–14, 16–18 and 20–22, documents control, companion,
  gate, save and spirit boundaries. The transcription is not a directly
  inspected disc or verified scan.
- [Grand_Admiral's 2002 PS2 route](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps2/367472-ico/faqs/19202)
  and [black_hole_sun's independent 2002 PS2 route](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps2/367472-ico/faqs/18667)
  corroborate the tower lever, cage release, first shadow and Idol Door;
  the former specifies the first shared couch.
- No original disc was played. Exact AI paths, damage, ledge-height
  thresholds and rescue seconds are not asserted.
