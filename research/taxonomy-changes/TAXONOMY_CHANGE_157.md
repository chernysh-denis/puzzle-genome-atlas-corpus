## Problem and accepted change

A puppy is not a directly controlled avatar or a household resident ordered to carry out a contextual task. The player's stylus contact may please or annoy depending on duration; a microphone utterance must be learned and is attention-sensitive; a selected care supply performs a feeding or grooming interaction. Name registration and the first sit lesson jointly unlock menus. Dog Status exposes condition and learned commands, while the analytical first-session endpoint includes an explicit save without a game-wide win. These nine typed boundaries prevent false transfer from Sims household motives, zoo habitat welfare or unconditional command buttons.

## Transfer and rejection tests

- Reuse `TIM-003` only for live puppy attention and contact/voice response while input remains possible. No forced session deadline or exact frame window is asserted.
- `ACT-557` requires direct touch contact with the animal; `ACT-558` requires a spoken cue; `ACT-559` requires selecting and applying an available care item. These are independent player choices.
- `SYS-1111` separates learned speech association and attention-gated response; `SYS-1112` separates beneficial from overlong petting; `SYS-1113` describes the care operation without inventing numeric need changes.
- `CON-705` needs both naming and the first sit lesson. `INF-412` shows current per-puppy condition and learned vocabulary. `OBJ-244` marks a reproducible opening observation window, not an authored victory.
- Reject `ACT-257` resident contextual orders, `SYS-431` personal-motive pressure, `SYS-867` habitat-species welfare, and `TIM-017` offline decay. None is established by the publisher's bounded first-home instructions.

## Evidence and limits

Nintendo of America's [original Nintendogs instruction booklet](https://csassets.nintendo.com/noaext/image/private/t_KA_PDF/DS_Nintendogs?_a=DATC1RAAZAA0), printed pp. 8–23, describes the name and first-trick tutorial, touch and microphone contact, Dog Status, care supplies and save. [Nintendo's official edition page](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Games/Nintendo-DS/Nintendogs-Labrador-Friends-272057.html) corroborates product identity. No cartridge, DS hardware, voice trace, direct play or condition measurement was inspected. Exact recognition tolerances, care deltas and future-day effects remain out of scope.

## Decision

Accept nine first-home typed boundaries, reuse one timing gene and `FAM-017`, preserve all earlier genomes and require deterministic comparison, Ukrainian review, original artwork and full repository/browser gates before the authorised local commit.
