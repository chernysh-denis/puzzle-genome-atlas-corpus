## Reuse and rejected redundancy

SYS-453 already represents equipment-granted commands, so a grimoire-shaped
ability system is rejected. CON-737 already requires active tool assignment
to the two action slots: offhand-only book compatibility is its instance
parameter, not a proposed but rejected book-only assignment rule. Neither rejected candidate
is admitted or creates a new stable record.

ACT-161 and SYS-215 retain direct attack and live resolution, including
context-qualified stealth damage; ACT-235 requires neutralisation, which a
damage-only unaware strike cannot guarantee. SYS-409, ACT-419 and CON-589 retain
the stun opportunity, close command and temporary reach/live-target predicate
without adding a guaranteed kill. SYS-578 retains the continuous health pool
with scoped revival; food supplies its recovery rather than creating a new
health bar. ACT-341 retains keys, doors, secret switch, boat and contextual
disarm rather than inventing separate verbs for every fixture.

Ilora's release combines ACT-232 dialogue, actual ACT-341 physical unlocking,
CON-336 retained branch eligibility, SYS-407 allied behavior and SYS-215 hostile
combat. A new named-actor allegiance system is unnecessary for this evidenced
composition. The later Escape Plan is explicitly excluded.

CON-304 retains load-dependent movement limitation; weighted equipment above
capacity slows movement and prevents sprint. INF-128 retains the visible
current weight, limit and compatibility. This is not a hard rejection of
another pickup, a new weighted-inventory gene or an automatic claim that the
island permits later camp services. P1 and the inspected post-release S12
account qualify this reuse without asserting native threshold coefficients.
