- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: Medium
- Date: 2026-11-20.
- Trigger: [GAME-0567](../../knowledge/games/s-z/superhot-vr.md), original
  standalone Quest SUPERHOT VR, ordinary complete Airport checkpoint group.

Eleven definitions are reused without edits: ACT-048, ACT-161, ACT-714,
SYS-036, SYS-215, SYS-369, SYS-706, SYS-755, CON-773, INF-142 and OBJ-029.
SYS-215 resolves continuous direct combat; TIM-037 owns its variable rate.
CON-773's current vitality is binary here, without inventing a health bar or
finite lives. SYS-036 and SYS-706 apply to physical released props, without
claiming a universal damage formula or gravitational bullet path.

The complete twenty-gene scope includes all successive Airport fights to the
next-location transition. It neither stops after the first shot nor silently
adds later MindWave, postgame modes or campaign-ending metanarrative actions.
All material, reach, hand-count, scene-count and coefficient choices remain
parameters. Indexed conflicting recovery numbers are explicitly not accepted.

Nine complete four-field Ukrainian definition pairs and all twenty manually
written bilingual player cards are reviewed within this same unit. Existing
signatures, definitions, lifecycles, historical records and r000025 remain
unchanged. This internal taxonomy addition claims no external novelty.
