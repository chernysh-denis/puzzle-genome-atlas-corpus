## Evidence and lower-ID transfer test

The [bounded game record](../../knowledge/games/s-z/starcraft.md) distinguishes Blizzard's original Terran worker and production rules from secondary written first-mission routes. Existing RTS movement, fog, attack, production, mining and supply genes transfer. The first-mission terminal does not reduce to a finite hostile-clear objective: Raynor must be found and survive, a Barracks must be finished and ten ordinary living Marines must be controlled at once. `OBJ-266` captures that ordered condition, not cumulative training count or a general victory condition.

The lower-ID selected-worker genes `ACT-318`, `SYS-550` and `CON-469` encoded a human *villager* in their labels though their causal boundary was selected economic labour with world-targeted orders, carried resource return, construction and repair. SCVs occupy the same boundary as mechanically controlled workers; original StarCraft's one-SCV construction maximum is a parameter, not a new gene. The Age of Empires II original uses and signatures remain valid. This does not generalise to autonomous construction robots or globally scheduled work.

## Disposition

Add the bounded objective and reviewed Ukrainian rendering; use role-neutral selected-worker labels and examples without migrating any earlier game genome. Retain uncertainty on original build constants, initial SCV count and exact mission failure state.
