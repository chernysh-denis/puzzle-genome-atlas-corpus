# Taxonomy Change 349 — Fit a component and race a draft-charged bicycle

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-21.
- Scope: [GAME-0603](../../knowledge/games/s-z/wheel-world.md),
  first Lunardo Hill Boys race in the early commercial PC release.

ACT-804 replaces a physical part in one position on an existing
bike. CON-848 addresses independently fitted positions. The rear
and front wheel positions accept their own fitted choice. ACT-283
and CON-425 describe a different Mod and capacity contract.
ACT-341 covers opening the part box before the fitting step.

SYS-1775 reflects the first-party independent linked-wheel model,
front-wheel steering and per-wheel surface friction. The scope has
no broad vehicle or occupant damage model as in SYS-320.
SYS-1776 derives the active configuration's ratings from its fitted
parts. Owning the wheel is insufficient to alter the build, and
the rule does not recompute momentary drafting charge as a part
change.

The first PC traces distinguish earning acceleration charge by
following a rider from holding the command to spend it. ACT-309,
SYS-765 and SYS-691 already own the action, gain and resolution.
SYS-540 requires boost pads and SYS-892 settles on drift release,
which are different resource paths. INF-566 records available
versus active cycling boost without a damage or Heat indicator.
The first ranked finish and its separate retained award reuse
OBJ-134 and INF-208. No previous signature or definition changes,
and no combination is added.
