# Taxonomy Change 340 — Contact designation and reciprocal physical placement

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: Medium
- Date: 2026-11-21.
- Scope: [GAME-0594](../../knowledge/games/a-f/bionic-bay.md),
  chapter 2 Swap, PlayStation 5 commercial base game.

| Addition | Boundary | Nearest distinct operation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ACT-790 | A directed contact command marks a free physical body for a separate operation. | ACT-405 observes at range; ACT-048 temporarily carries the object; ACT-067 executes an adjacent unit exchange immediately. |
| SYS-1751 | A live link follows a moving body and can disconnect with separation. | SYS-747 retains tactical information about a living actor. SYS-1513 records a temporary detonation mark applied by a dash. |
| SYS-1752 | A separate command exchanges positions and leaves both bodies subject to world physics. | ACT-067 addresses adjacency via movement, SYS-1516 addresses authored panel pairs, and control transfer leaves the two positions in place. |
| SYS-1753 | A free prop obstructs an active beam or jet while its geometry intersects the actual path. | SYS-1661 uses a held object to catch a discrete incoming shot. Absorbing a hit and disabling the emitter have separate consequences. |
| SYS-1754 | Contact with a moved body triggers an authored blast that removes a compatible route obstruction. | SYS-470 is a timer on a placed explosive; SYS-147 models damage and support cascades without a contact-triggered charge requirement. |

ACT-190 covers tool invocation, SYS-036 the bodies' ongoing physics and
SYS-061 the alternate weight holding a platform. A marked target is
not an automatic safety guarantee. Exact link range, cooldown and
transfer of velocities are unverified. Earlier definitions and
combination records remain unchanged.
