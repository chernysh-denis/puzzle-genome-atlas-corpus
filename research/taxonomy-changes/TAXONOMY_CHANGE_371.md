# Taxonomy Change 371 — A touch slash and an authored spar handoff

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: Medium
- Date: 2026-11-21.
- Scope: [Ninja Gaiden Dragon Sword](../../knowledge/games/m-r/ninja-gaiden-dragon-sword.md),
  GAME-0625, opening North American retail Nintendo DS practice bout.
- Decision: add ACT-831, SYS-1826 and OBJ-336 as Active. Preserve
  every earlier definition, signature and verified combination.

## Boundaries

- ACT-831 is a crossing stroke on the live playfield that requests an
  attack against the crossed opponent. ACT-019 selects a unit ability and
  a separate target. ACT-438 binds later facing to a chosen opponent.
  Neither owns the immediate strike gesture. The stylus is a parameter
  of ACT-008 when contact instead directs local movement.
- SYS-1826 owns the automatic end of the encounter while ordinary
  victory over the instructor remains unavailable. SYS-1455 retries a
  bounded tutorial action after a mistake. SYS-1174 settles a boxing
  decision from competitive results. Neither transfers ordinary
  control after a non-competitive live spar.
- OBJ-336 concerns the player's progression to the forest after the
  practice. It requires neither the competitive result of OBJ-256 nor
  the guardian defeat and passage of OBJ-080. TIM-003 covers time
  continuing during the exchange. No exact duration or health predicate
  is required to identify the documented handoff.

See NGD-002 through NGD-006. The printed controls and two independent
routes corroborate the evidence. Physical stylus movement and exact
outcome thresholds were not measured.
