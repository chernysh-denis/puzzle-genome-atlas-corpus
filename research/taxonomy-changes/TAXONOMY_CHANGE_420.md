# Taxonomy Change 420 — Wave Race: Blue Storm Stunt Mode

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-24.
- Scope: [GAME-0674](../../knowledge/games/s-z/wave-race-blue-storm.md).
  Original English North American GameCube rules, one Normal
  Lost Temple Lagoon attempt through its scored result.
- Decision: admit ACT-899, CON-887, INF-621 and SYS-1935–SYS-1939.
  Reuse ACT-290, ACT-720, INF-208, OBJ-002, SYS-1501 and TIM-003.
  No verified proper combination subset.
- Evidence: North American booklet pp. 6–7 and 21–25, two separately
  attributed same-course completions and an independently identified disc.

| Gene | Admission boundary | Nearest rejected reuse |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ACT-899 | Enter a context-specific stick sequence, with optional modifier, for one watercraft stunt. | ACT-828 links airborne direction tricks to later boost strength. It omits the separate running command and can require boost rather than a scored stunt. |
| CON-887 | Cross the physical arch in each live block before its deadline or lose the attempt and its unretained score. | CON-438 tracks ordered checkpoint and lap completion. The scored block also has an expiring gate allowance and a distinct loss result. |
| INF-621 | Show live speed, score and remaining time with per-event credits from three sources. | INF-524 exposes stunt credit, a passing-star target and engine heat. The Blue Storm interface distinguishes rings and checkpoint-time awards and has no mandatory passing-star target. |
| SYS-1935 | Add a growing flat bonus based on previous contiguous ring passages and reset the bonus on a miss. | SYS-975 connects movement tricks in a breakable score chain. This is per-ring additive credit that has already been retained within the attempt when the bonus resets. |
| SYS-1936 | Convert block time into a separate awarded score and renew the countdown on a valid gate crossing. | SYS-1859 pays stage-clear and retained-vitality bonuses at stage end. This reward is elapsed-resource dependent and resolves inside the same moving lap. |
| SYS-1937 | Pay a recognised watercraft stunt according to its type and execution precision. | SYS-1566 pays stunt stars even for some collisions. Watercraft command recognition and precision define this component; a separate gate and ring system pay the other components. |
| SYS-1938 | Reduce the current block's award for repeating the same running stunt. | SYS-998 modifies a trick chain by rotation, stance and repetition. The rule here is scoped to a timed block and a running stunt kind, independent of a combo-chain commit. |
| SYS-1939 | Pass through a specific turbo ring to activate a temporary burst immediately. | SYS-1860 accelerates by surface pad contact and carries the motion into a spring jump. Turbo Ring activation depends on spatial passage and has no required follow-up jump. |

Exact physical inputs, the precision curve, the measured same-block repeat
penalty and the observed timeout outcome remain open.
