# Taxonomy Change 369 — Committed boost and coded landing

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-21.
- Scope: [Blue Lightning](../../knowledge/games/a-f/blue-lightning.md),
  GAME-0623, Atari Lynx retail card PA2020, Dog Fight.
- Decision: add SYS-1824, CON-863, INF-580 and OBJ-334 as Active.
  Preserve definitions and historical memberships.

## Boundaries

- SYS-1824 distinguishes a committed, fixed afterburner interval
  and a separate time-locked recharge from SYS-988's common
  manoeuvre energy reserve. ACT-496 already describes requesting
  an authored boost or roll.
- CON-863 bounds ordinary descent relative to the ground while
  raised objects remain collision hazards. It is neither full
  terrain immunity nor a generic screen boundary.
- INF-580 separates acquisition and missile-lock cues from the
  boost lamp, missile stock, replacement aircraft and score.
  It does not require a cockpit power display (INF-277).
  SYS-987 already accounts for a finite projectile with bounded
  guidance. SYS-1434's hold and loss intervals are unverified here.
- OBJ-334 couples success to automatic landing, the next briefing
  and a reusable entry code. OBJ-288 requires a terminal boss;
  OBJ-155 uses ordered interactions and a save boundary. The
  two reference endings do not establish a particular point threshold.
- SYS-911 covers the documented finite prototype failure branch
  without attributing a crash to the complete reference sorties.

See BL-001 through BL-007 for the manual and the two complete traces.
