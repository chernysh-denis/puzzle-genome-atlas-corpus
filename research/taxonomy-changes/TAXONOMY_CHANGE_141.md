# Taxonomy Change 141: Expose and capture a first-room ghost

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-09-25
- Trigger: `GAME-0403` *Luigi's Mansion*, original North American GameCube
  first post-training Parlor room.
- Scope: admit `ACT-542`–`ACT-544`, `SYS-1079`–`SYS-1082` and `INF-401`
  as Active. No earlier genome or verified-combination change.

## Problem and accepted change

Nintendo's original booklet separates light exposure, reachable vacuum
contact and an active counter-steered struggle. Two independent written
GameCube routes establish that extinguishing the first Parlor's candles
triggers a finite ghost group and that clearing it lights the room and
reveals an authored key chest. Existing direct movement, contextual chest
interaction, one health pool, authored room tasks and live time remain
reusable. Eight new boundaries are confined to the first light/vacuum room
packet, not the entire marketed game.

## Transfer and rejection test

- `ACT-467` moves loose Slime Rancher entities into and out of a typed
  tank. It does not cover fixture-state change or a resistant ghost whose
  heart must first be exposed. `ACT-542` and `ACT-544` keep those distinct.
- `SYS-465` settles a combat room by opening ordinary exits and possibly
  sampling an award. The Parlor instead brightens and reveals a fixed key
  chest after a candle-triggered encounter, so `SYS-1082` is narrower.
- The flashlight's off/on timing is player action (`ACT-543`); the ghost's
  surprise and heart exposure is system response (`SYS-1080`); the
  counter-steered input (`ACT-544`) is distinct from power depletion and
  capture (`SYS-1081`). `INF-401` records what is currently visible.
- Ghost count, candle count, heart power, controller buttons, mansion name
  and key destination are parameters. Later portrait-ghost vulnerabilities,
  Boos, element medals and sequel devices are excluded, not silently
  generalised into these first-room genes.

## Evidence and limits

- [Nintendo's original GameCube instruction
  booklet](https://manualzz.com/doc/22619081/nintendo-luigi-s-mansion-video-game-instruction-booklet),
  pp. 10–13 and 22–25, documents light, vacuum, resistance, health and
  generic room-clear behaviour. The hosted text is a transcription with
  OCR artefacts, not a played original disc.
- [Nintendo's GameCube product
  page](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Games/Nintendo-GameCube/Luigi-s-Mansion-268236.html)
  independently describes the original flashlight/vacuum interaction.
- [Alxs](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gamecube/516494-luigis-mansion/faqs/19088)
  and [Kirby021591](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gamecube/516494-luigis-mansion/faqs/34284)
  independently describe the first Parlor candle, ghost and key sequence.
- No original disc, footage, screenshot, audio or input trace was inspected.
  Exact stun duration, ghost spawn coordinates, frame windows and the
  lighting-versus-key-pickup micro-order remain unverified. The original
  editorial pixel illustration is not mechanical evidence.
