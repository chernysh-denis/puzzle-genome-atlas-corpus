# Taxonomy Change 392 — Selected climbing contacts and graded piton placement

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-22.
- Scope: [Cairn](../../knowledge/games/a-f/cairn.md), GAME-0646.
  One early Escarpment face in the commercial PS5 base game.
- Decision: add ACT-859, SYS-1865 and SYS-1866. Reuse ten definitions
  for movement, protection, exertion, impact, feedback and the
  grounded objective.

## Boundaries

- ACT-859 chooses and places one limb while the others keep their
  roles. ACT-114 uses independently operated hand channels.
  ACT-362 retains attachment through a grip input and aimed body
  motion. Neither specifies the four-limb selection and placement
  decision in Cairn.
- SYS-1865 separates the resolved contact from the ongoing
  SYS-661 stamina-bounded grip. A placed palm can brace the wall
  without closing on a hold, affecting the next stance.
- ACT-363 and CON-536 already cover requesting a carried aid
  on compatible terrain. SYS-665 covers the resulting tether.
  SYS-1866 specifies how the timing bar grades a piton. SYS-924
  instead grades reloading a weapon. No exact timing windows or
  grade-specific fall thresholds are assigned.
- SYS-662 describes the observed ground impact and health debit.
  SYS-1598 requires arrest on the line without falling all the way.
  The two runs do not isolate that case, so it remains outside scope.
- OBJ-026 covers reaching and walking on the upper terrace.
  No new objective for a score, a save or the summit is warranted.

Claims CAI-002 through CAI-006 ground the accepted boundaries. Earlier
signatures and verified combinations are preserved.
