## Evidence and lower-ID transfer test

The original 1989 Electronic Arts / Bullfrog manual explicitly addresses Amiga and exposes Custom rules. Terrain-point edits are not material excavation (`ACT-443`) or combat cover destruction. Global population policies do not fit selected-agent assignment (`ACT-036`) or formation/stance (`ACT-317`), while their automatic role execution fits unchanged `SYS-046`. A leader-dependent magnet has a separate command and loss/replacement transition, not morale routing (`SYS-617`). Flat contiguous fields determine settlement capacity and advancement; capacity-filled population emits a walker, rather than a queued recruit or unconditional schedule (`SYS-047`). Population/achievement manna does not require Black & White's assigned worship workforce (`SYS-1188`). Terrain-weighted autonomous walker exhaustion differs from embodied-avatar needs (`SYS-327`). Knight conversion spends the leader and possibly its settlement, produces an autonomous attacker and burns target fields; ordinary autonomous target selection still reuses `SYS-051`. Terrain catastrophes share a declared geometry/occupancy resolution boundary, with local earthquake, flat persistent swamp, obstructing volcano and global sea rise as explicit effect parameters, not four names pretending to be four genes. Armageddon instead changes the control regime irreversibly and therefore requires its own transition. Command eligibility separates resource, permission and target predicates from aftermath. World visibility and queried subject transfers do not imply fully visible internal state (`INF-001`).

## Disposition

Accept fourteen bounded genes and reuse seven unchanged boundaries: `ACT-599`, `SYS-002`, `SYS-045`, `SYS-046`, `SYS-051`, `OBJ-103`, `TIM-003`. Same-faith walker merge is automatic compatible replacement; the opponent-wide elimination target admits continuing settlement production rather than a finite encounter (`OBJ-029`). The declared Custom preset permits the analysed powers but is not claimed as Genesis/Conquest defaults. All fields and effect differences are retained in the canonical record and complete bilingual layer. The named salience review reads and partitions all twenty-one uses rather than inferring weights from rarity. Exact executable formulas, direct play and other ports remain uninspected. No new combination or platform identity is proposed.
