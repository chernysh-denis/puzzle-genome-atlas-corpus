# Taxonomy Change 345 — Prepare a board jump and restore a rideable route

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-21.
- Scope: [GAME-0599](../../knowledge/games/s-z/sword-of-the-sea.md),
  from initial PS5 Hoversword control to the first temple approach.

ACT-797 singles out holding and releasing the supported jump
input to make a high launch. ACT-308 describes a vehicle jump,
dodge or orientation change without encoding the held release.
SYS-1386 is a different extra impulse in the air. The local
Resonate interaction remains ACT-341.

SYS-1762 covers momentum while transitioning between dry riding
and the faster restored water. SYS-1181 covers a dedicated
downhill course; SYS-1501 describes wave-geometry response
and rider balance aboard a supported watercraft. Neither
describes changing this board's rideable medium in place.

SYS-1763 represents the authored change of one dry locality to
a rideable water route. It is neither a released cloth span
under SYS-1142 nor a timed lethal flood under SYS-1133. The
restored local surface can be used after the activation flash.
Saving and loading the condition have not been reproduced.

No new combination is proposed. Earlier definitions and game
signatures remain intact.
