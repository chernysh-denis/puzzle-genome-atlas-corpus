# Taxonomy Change 033: Merge the Batch 016 progression trio into portable lower-ID boundaries

## Status

- Proposal status: `Accepted`
- Claim status: `Observation`
- Evidence quality: `Corroborated`
- Confidence: `High`
- Date: 2026-09-07
- Trigger: independent closure review of `GAME-0271` and unresolved taxonomy
  question 2 in
  [`SEARCH_DEMAND_BATCH_016_CLAUDE_CANDIDATE_HANDOFF`](../checkpoints/SEARCH_DEMAND_BATCH_016_CLAUDE_CANDIDATE_HANDOFF.md).

## Current classification

- Exact wording or stable IDs: `SYS-517` — Convert driving-event results into
  Festival progress; `CON-440` — Horizon Invitational requires the Qualifier
  progress threshold; `INF-207` — Festival meter exposes progress and the next
  Wristband gate; plus the Batch 016 candidates `SYS-819`, `CON-614` and
  `INF-328`, which restate the same transition, legality and disclosure for a
  regional resistance measure.
- Files and entries affected: the System Behaviour, Constraint and Information
  registries; Ukrainian gene localisation; the draft `GAME-0271` signature and
  decomposition; its salience and plain-language records; generated indexes and
  deterministic research artifacts; the recorded disposition of the Batch 016
  handoff question; web contract tests and the `GAME-0271` acceptance record.
- Original rationale: the candidate pass treated the kinds of activities that
  pay the measure, the measure's product name and the identity of the unlocked
  successor as boundary-defining differences. It therefore created three new
  IDs rather than edit definitions belonging to an already reviewed game.

## Detected problem

- What is incorrect: each old/new pair has the same causal role after product
  nouns and values are substituted. `SYS-517` and `SYS-819` both accept
  completed eligible activities, add configured amounts to one retained
  progression measure and let that total feed a later gate. `CON-440` and
  `CON-614` both keep an authored successor unavailable until the measure
  reaches a threshold. `INF-207` and `INF-328` both expose the retained amount,
  its threshold and the corresponding successor's lock state before the next
  commitment.
- Why the candidate distinction fails: `SYS-517` already admits both event
  results and rated driving activities and its novelty note calls them
  heterogeneous. A second product adds more activity classes, but the number
  and theme of eligible payers are parameters of the same aggregation rule.
  Likewise, one Invitational versus three regions and Festival Points versus
  Resistance Points are successor and presentation parameters, not new
  Constraint or Information mechanics.
- How the problem was found: a fresh lower-ID scan and a two-way transfer test
  during the independent `GAME-0271` closure unit.

## Evidence

- Primary corpus evidence: the reviewed `GAME-0171` Forza Horizon 6 claim
  ledger and transition table, and the draft `GAME-0271` Far Cry 5 ledger and
  transition table. Each record's source register remains the authority for the
  external product claims.
- Transfer from Forza Horizon 6 to Far Cry 5: substitute the eligible activity
  set, contribution values, measure name and successor identity. The system
  still credits one retained measure, the constraint still tests its threshold
  before admitting a successor, and the interface still discloses the amount,
  threshold and lock state. Nothing else in the three definitions changes.
- Transfer from Far Cry 5 to Forza Horizon 6: restrict the eligible activity set
  to Qualifier results and rated driving activities, name the measure Festival
  Points and the successor the first Invitational. The same three boundaries
  still describe every asserted transition.
- Lower-ID alternatives: `SYS-171` is a single-shape delivery quota,
  `SYS-262` accumulates staffed science, `SYS-503` settles extraction into
  mission progress and `SYS-405` settles one completed hunt. None is a closer
  system home. A mission list, personal experience bar, hidden threshold, timed
  gate and spendable purchase price remain excluded from the survivor trio.
- Counterevidence considered: Far Cry 5 permits unlike activity classes and
  opens several regional choices, whereas the scoped Forza route aggregates a
  narrower activity set before one mandatory event. Those differences affect
  the choice set and the later objective. They do not change how the measure is
  credited, when the gate becomes legal or what the interface discloses.

## Proposed change

- `SYS-517` becomes “Credit one retained progression measure from qualifying
  activity results”. Eligible activity classes, first-completion rules,
  contribution values, region or campaign scope and the measure name are
  parameters. `SYS-819` becomes a `Merged` historical alias.
- `CON-440` becomes “A successor gate requires its accumulated progression
  threshold”. Qualifying activities, threshold and successor identity or count
  are parameters. `CON-614` becomes a `Merged` historical alias.
- `INF-207` becomes “Expose retained progression and its next threshold-gated
  successor”. Exact or proportional presentation, threshold, successor and lock
  state are parameters. `INF-328` becomes a `Merged` historical alias.
- `OBJ-165` remains Active and new. It is not the same boundary as `OBJ-098`:
  Far Cry 5's scoped terminal is the retained resolved-region state and opened
  successor choice, while Forza Horizon 6 still requires the newly available
  Invitational to be completed before its objective settles.
- What does not change: the `GAME-0171` signature, `OBJ-098`, `CON-441`, any
  earlier reviewed game signature, any earlier combination gene set or any
  family. `GAME-0271` is still a draft when its three candidate IDs are replaced
  and is promoted only after its closure review.

## Genome and combination impact

- Definitions remain `2,404`. Active genes fall from `2,359` to `2,356`;
  `Merged` records rise from `43` to `46`; the two `Deprecated` records are
  unchanged.
- `GAME-0271` remains a twenty-gene signature: three candidate IDs are replaced
  one-for-one by their lower-ID survivors. It changes from sixteen reused and
  four new genes to nineteen reused and one new gene.
- Gene usages remain `5,911`. Each survivor moves from one reviewed carrier to
  two carriers, and no prior signature changes.
- Combinations rise from `268` to `269`. No earlier combination contains a
  merged alias. New `COMB-0269` records the three surviving IDs as a strict
  proper subset of both `GAME-0171` and `GAME-0271`, because normalisation gives
  the interaction its required second mechanically independent carrier.
- The selected neighbour for `GAME-0271` remains `GAME-0236` Far Cry 3 because
  the one-for-one substitution changes none of their thirteen shared genes and
  keeps the Far Cry 5 genome size at twenty. Derived comparisons are regenerated
  globally and checked rather than inferred from this statement.

## Decision

- Decision: `Accepted`.
- Decided by: independent closure review, the two-way transfer test and the
  lower-ID scan recorded above.
- Rationale: product nouns and the number or theme of eligible payers are
  parameters. Keeping the candidate trio would encode those parameters as
  three false mechanical distinctions and defeat the Atlas's normalisation
  model.
- Implementation links: `SYS-517`, `SYS-819`, `CON-440`, `CON-614`, `INF-207`,
  `INF-328`, `OBJ-165`, `COMB-0269`, `GAME-0171`, `GAME-0271`.
