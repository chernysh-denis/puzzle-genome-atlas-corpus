## Reuse and counterexamples

Movement, posture, local switch commands and body attacks reuse ACT-008,
ACT-049, ACT-202 and ACT-295. Hold duration, throw direction/weight, dash
run-up and ground-pound height are parameters. ACT-611 admits the booklet's
stunned live opponents, retaining its combat-actor boundary. It is not
broadened to the friendly Professor merely because he can be hit. ACT-048
covers that body's acquisition, carried offset and directional physical
release toward a switch; its definition does not require inherent inanimacy.
This is a held-body command, not a claim about the Professor's idle AI.

SYS-037, SYS-045, SYS-064, SYS-215, SYS-578 and SYS-755 preserve contact
collection, movement, directional rebound, attack resolution, health and
eligible scenery destruction. CON-175 ends one full multi-room attempt at
zero health; unlimited future retries are not recoverable finite lives.
CON-282 preserves route prerequisites. INF-115/119, OBJ-002 and TIM-003
preserve current visibility/resources, separate score and live time.

## Additions and discriminating boundaries

- SYS-1345: switch-started entry return, followed by money-draining overtime
  before exhaustion failure. A hard zero-time terminal and NiGHTS' changed
  flight body/pursuer do not fit.
- SYS-1346: independent clear banking and successor-key credit with discarded
  failed-attempt treasure. DK Returns' checkpoint/life-dependent asymmetric
  letters/pieces rollback is not inherited.
- SYS-1347: apple-induced heavy body recovered by walking. SYS-902 specifically
  uses pickup tiers and damage/expiry demotion; selected combat copying is
  also not equivalent.
- SYS-1348: matching block-group solidity flips in place. SYS-065's travelling
  platform and CON-032's user-assigned cell property are rejected.
- SYS-1349: continuing consumes the mid-attempt suspend record, separate from
  permanent clear credits. This is not reusable checkpoint or emulator rewind.
- SYS-1350: small pickups accumulate toward one main-health unit; they do not
  each give an immediate unit or award finite lives.
- INF-470: current independent treasure/money/health/return feedback without
  finite lives. INF-347 would incorrectly import a life-stock field.
- OBJ-285: activated reverse traversal with ordinary return separate from
  key-conditional successor access. OBJ-283 automatically opens a forward
  next route on ordinary clear and does not preserve this distinction.
