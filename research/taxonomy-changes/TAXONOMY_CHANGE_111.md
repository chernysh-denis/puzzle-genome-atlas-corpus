# Taxonomy Change 111: Charted guitar performance and song survival

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Confirmed
- Evidence quality: Direct
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-09-23
- Trigger: `GAME-0372` Guitar Hero III: Legends of Rock, original PlayStation 3
  solo Easy Quick Play song.
- Scope: nine additive Active genes; no earlier signature, lifecycle or
  verified combination changes.

## Problem

A generic real-time input gene cannot explain Guitar Hero III's two-part
fret-and-strum commitment to an authored coloured chart, long holds,
streak-sensitive note scores, phrase-earned stored Star Power or a separate
live Rock Meter that can fail before the song ends. The nearest earlier
signature shares only the real-time clock and a terminal result report.

## Accepted change

- `ACT-508` commits one matched fret set and timely strum; `ACT-509` chooses
  when to activate earned Star Power.
- `SYS-1010` advances and judges authored notes; `SYS-1011` scales an unbroken
  streak; `SYS-1012` resolves complete star phrases, charge and drain;
  `SYS-1013` resolves Rock Meter survival.
- `CON-676` requires continued hold of every fret after a long-note or
  sustained-chord strum.
- `INF-379` exposes approaching notes and live performance gauges.
- `OBJ-215` sets one survived song and its results screen as the bounded
  evaluation endpoint.

## Lower-ID transfer and rejection test

- `INF-299` transfers for the completed-song breakdown and `TIM-003` for
  forced live progression. Neither is renamed or broadened.
- `ACT-261` is a prompted pointer-in-interval Skill Check during unrelated
  work and explicitly excludes a rhythm sequence whose chart is the objective.
  `INF-194` cues a fixed platforming obstacle route with music; it explicitly
  excludes song-chart note judgement. Batman's combat-chain scoring is not a
  chart-note streak. No lower-ID signature changes.
- No verified combination is a proper subset of the new packet; one carrier
  does not justify a new combination.

## Evidence and limits

Activision's original PS3 instruction booklet, pp. 4–10, directly documents
Quick Play song availability, the Easy fret set, note/chord/sustain controls,
the score multiplier, Rock Meter, star-shaped phrases, Star Power activation
and song results. No disc, PS3 console, exact chart trace, hidden judgement
window or Rock Meter coefficient was inspected. The selected available song is
a declared packet parameter rather than an invented universal starting track.
