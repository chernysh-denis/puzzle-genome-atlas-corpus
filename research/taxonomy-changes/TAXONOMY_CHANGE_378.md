# Taxonomy Change 378 — Recurring thrust preparation and rolling repair

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-22.
- Scope: [Star Wars Episode I: Racer](../../knowledge/games/s-z/star-wars-episode-i-racer.md),
  GAME-0632. Original European PAL Nintendo 64 first Amateur race.
- Decision: add ACT-840, ACT-841 and INF-586 as Active. Reuse
  ACT-290, SYS-320, SYS-516, SYS-1421, INF-205, INF-208, OBJ-150 and
  TIM-003. Preserve earlier signatures and combinations.

## Boundaries

- ACT-840 requires a repeatable forward thrust preparation and a
  new press of the same ordinary accelerator. ACT-695 instead
  directly holds a dedicated boost request after eligibility.
  CON-784 covers only the initial warm-up of the MotorStorm race.
- ACT-841 repairs the moving craft at a current speed cost with a
  held input. It differs from purchasing maintained parts between
  attempts, waiting for a crash respawn or consuming a pickup.
- INF-586 exposes recurring readiness, engaged boost and the
  separate engine warnings and condition diagrams. It does not expose
  hidden temperature units or the initial-only charging count in INF-491.
- SYS-1421 already supplies the boost-to-heat relationship. The
  booklet documents severe consequences. The worked race shows
  heating, warning and continued driving, without a destructive event.

See RAC-001 through RAC-005 in the bounded analysis.
