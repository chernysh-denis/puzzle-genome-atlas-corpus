# Taxonomy Change 390 — Spring jumps and the accepted timed crown

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-22.
- Scope: [WHAT THE CAR?](../../knowledge/games/s-z/what-the-car.md),
  GAME-0644. iPhone Apple Arcade release-era Car With Jumps.
- Decision: add ACT-857, ACT-858, SYS-1860, SYS-1861, SYS-1862
  and INF-598. Reuse five definitions. Preserve earlier
  signatures, gene boundaries and verified combinations.

## Boundaries

- ACT-857 separates continuous steering on the running route
  from its spring action. ACT-008 is reused for map navigation.
  ACT-588 is an authored directional swipe for a local turn
  or obstacle response. It does not cover the free wheel.
- ACT-858 schedules a contextual spring action from a moving
  approach. ACT-797 requires an eligible supporting surface
  before the held-and-released jump. The footage does not assign
  that input sequence to Car With Jumps.
- SYS-1860 couples a road pad's added pace to an adjacent jump.
  SYS-540 instead stores and spends a spatially replenished
  boost reserve. CON-854 is reused for the need to reach support.
- SYS-1861 returns to the launcher of the same separated course
  after a fall. SYS-497 creates the fresh attempt directly at
  the level origin. A failed attempt here is observed before
  the re-launch decision.
- SYS-1862 retains a confirmed result in the same session and
  returns to freely navigable map control. SYS-1779 instead
  restarts the same challenge from the result. The colour
  evaluation itself reuses SYS-711 rather than a new rating gene.
- INF-598 distinguishes a changing local spring state from an
  elapsed medal gauge visible on the result. Neither remaining
  run time nor precision recharge thresholds come from the
  spring indicator.
- OBJ-133 is reused for the valid finish and timed rating of an
  official course. Car With Jumps has no separately shown time
  checkpoint, while the Trackmania comparison has required
  ordered intermediate checkpoints. Gold is unnecessary
  for the witnessed onward route.

See WC-001 through WC-007 for the exact capture and corroboration
boundaries. The crown and collectible card are separate rewards.
