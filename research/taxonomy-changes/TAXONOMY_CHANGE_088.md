# Taxonomy Change 088: Bound one fair-to-era opening packet

## Status

- Proposal status: `Accepted`
- Claim status: `Observation`
- Evidence quality: `Corroborated`
- Confidence: `High`
- Date: `2026-09-21`
- Trigger: `GAME-0346` Chrono Trigger complete lower-ID transfer test.
- Scope: three new Active boundaries and supporting-carrier evidence for the

  family change.

## Problem

The vocabulary already represented direct field navigation, visible authored
encounters, party command selection, encounter rewards, scoped purchases,
ordered mechanism gates and actor-local ATB readiness. It did not represent an
optional challenge whose complete party defeat returns to the same world at one
HP without a terminal or ordinary checkpoint, nor a key-item mechanism reaction
that creates a fixed connection to the corresponding place in another era.

Stopping at Gato would also miss the selected opening's defining settlement:
the same party, reward and interaction state is carried through a fair-currency
exchange into the first time-gate transition. An existing target, mission,
location or artefact objective cannot state that complete terminal without
importing later Truce Canyon play.

## Accepted change

- `SYS-970` owns optional-challenge party defeat, reward suppression and return
  at minimum positive health without route-terminal Game Over.
- `SYS-971` owns the prerequisite-created authored gate that relocates the
  traveller from one place/era to its fixed other-era endpoint.
- `OBJ-203` owns the complete initial-party, fair-challenge, event-transaction,
  mechanism-incident and other-era successor-control terminal.
- `ACT-130`, `SYS-355`, `CON-323`, `INF-119` and `INF-179` receive explicit
  Chrono Trigger support without an operational wording change.

## Complete lower-ID transfer test

- `SYS-369`, `SYS-610` and other checkpoint restore boundaries return a failed
  attempt to an authored recovery state. Gato closes only the optional
  challenge, keeps fair progress and returns the party at one HP.
- `SYS-911` consumes or evaluates a finite life stock; `SYS-957` returns a
  fainted creature party with money loss. Neither describes this free local
  challenge retry or reward suppression.
- `SYS-129` remaps a doorway destination after observation, while `SYS-772`
  resolves one aimed relocation. Neither creates a cross-era endpoint from a
  retained key-item/mechanism reaction.
- `OBJ-026` reaches a designated place, `OBJ-158` settles a dialogue-only
  episode and `OBJ-201` retains a unique artefact and capability. The selected
  route requires a party battle, currency transaction and other-era successor,
  and the Pendant remains a prerequisite rather than the objective reward.

## Migration and compatibility

All three boundaries append as Active. Existing definitions, lifecycle states,
lower-ID signatures, combinations and families remain unchanged. English and
Ukrainian presentation records are introduced together.
