## Evidence and complete packet

series, health-paid solid hits, three common slots, items and current-panel
arrows/advice. Two independently authored original-game written routes agree
on both fork alternatives, rat, crate/lever and final gap. The recorded
speedrunner's text supports airplane health cost and red-health refusal;
uninspected boss-counter speculation is not admitted. No native game,
controller trace, audiovisual play or exact executable revision was inspected.
The 2019 PDF conversion does not change the original 1995 ruleset.

One complete first page includes every available body/tool/helper option,
both mutually exclusive branches, their shared dependency and terminal.
No first-panel-only slice removes inconvenient tools or supporting genes.
Later pages, bosses, continues, grenades and Mystery items not evidenced on
this page require separate admission. Tea fractions conflict, so only the
publisher's qualitative partial restoration is retained.

## System transfer and six additions

`SYS-1308` admits own-health debit for accepted solid-object hits and declared
special actions. `SYS-1217` specifically couples mode-dependent emergency
strikes to enemy contact/use; broadening it would erase its earlier carrier's
boundary. Ordinary enemy damage and partial healing remain `SYS-578`.
No generic attack is assumed to charge health.

`SYS-1309` admits the accepted humanoid attack sequence and finishing member,
with move variation against its guard response. Three accepted hits are a
threshold parameter, not a raw three-button combo. Ordinary live hit/defence
still fits `SYS-215`; humanoid class is not assigned to flying creatures.
No game-specific counter-reset or damage formula is invented.

`SYS-1310` owns the same helper's deployed contacts, local authored discovery,
finite condition/loss and physical-pickup restoration. Autonomous movement
alone is not sufficient to explain why a carried rat activates a switch or
reveals an item; `SYS-045` retains ordinary autonomous hostile travel.
`SYS-066` needs dispatched target-derived work, not unassigned ground help;
party-follow/task systems do not transfer common item storage. Reported
three-hit tolerance is an unmeasured parameter. Pickup heals only the helper.

`SYS-1311` admits automatic outbound/boundary reversal with owner risk.
`SYS-800` requires a reusable embedded tool and explicit recall to a
player-selected return line and excludes uninterruptible boomerang paths.
`SYS-994` reflects an incoming hostile projectile through active guard.
Neither is broadened to the held-input paper airplane. Its action remains
`ACT-295`; health cost is separately `SYS-1308`.

`SYS-020` transfers attack displacement into dangerous terrain/borders.
`SYS-036` transfers continuous fall/support, not post-input grid gravity
`SYS-084`. `SYS-112` transfers persistent accepted switch/tool activation.
`SYS-470` transfers placed-fuse blast including owner. `SYS-755` already
supports durability removal, linked targets and contained output: a marked
crate's destructive output or lost barrel contents is not another item-named
gene. The crate must remain intact until its useful falling destination.
Super Fist consumes one item into an ordinary declared strong combat effect;
it does not establish a newly steerable temporary body under `SYS-608`.
