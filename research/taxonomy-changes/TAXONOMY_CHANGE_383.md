# Taxonomy Change 383 — Openings derived from ground edits

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-22.
- Scope: [Tiny Glade](../../knowledge/games/s-z/tiny-glade.md),
  GAME-0637. Commercial Windows Steam release-era wall and path
  editing through the revision of a path-driven opening.
- Decision: add ACT-848, ACT-849, INF-591 and SYS-1840 as Active.
  Reuse TIM-002. Preserve earlier signatures and combinations.

## Boundaries

- ACT-848 defines the freehand layout of the barrier independently
  of the ground. The closed habitat and gate required by ACT-456
  are absent.
- ACT-849 changes a path footprint by brush coverage. ACT-567
  instead places or removes elements of an editable course.
- INF-591 shows the area affected by the active brush. INF-044
  concerns the predicted fit of a held object in perspective.
- SYS-1840 derives the current aperture from the present path at
  a compatible barrier. SYS-1431 acts on an existing door or
  access blocker. There is no separate objective in this packet.

See GLADE-001 through GLADE-006 in the bounded analysis.
