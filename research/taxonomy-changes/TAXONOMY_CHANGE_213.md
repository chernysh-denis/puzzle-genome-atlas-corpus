# Taxonomy Change 213: sensed exercise route and combat

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Confirmed

- Confidence: High

- Trigger: `GAME-0476`, original Nintendo Switch *Ring Fit Adventure*, World 1 second course through its first ordinary Fit Battle.
- Scope: new Active `ACT-626`–`ACT-629`, `SYS-1238`–`SYS-1240`, `INF-450` and `OBJ-268`; no earlier signature or verified combination change.

## Evidence and lower-ID transfer test

Nintendo's original hardware setup, official Adventure description and Nintendo-hosted early-play guide establish that a leg-mounted Joy-Con reads jogging, while a Ring-Con is pressed or pulled for course effects, guided Fit Skills and held Ab Guard. `ACT-575`/`SYS-1152` refer to Wii Balance Board weight transfer, not a strapped leg and deformable handheld ring. Generic commanded combat does not explain how the *quality of a physical repetition* changes attack damage. `SYS-1180` grades one defensive button press; it cannot express holding the ring against the abdomen through the full enemy strike. Ordinary local course goals do not capture the World 1 second-course plus first ordinary battle progression terminal. Live form guidance and immediate quality grade are an actionable information boundary rather than decorative exercise animation.

## Disposition

Accept nine typed boundaries, preserving earlier signatures. Reuse the continuous heart pool (`SYS-578`), visible personal state (`INF-119`) and player-command/enemy-reply turn structure (`TIM-001`). The precise World 1 node order is secondary and medium-confidence; direct play, exercise-intensity setting, sensor calibration, enemy colour and exact damage arithmetic remain uninspected. The post-World-2 colour bonus and World-3 shop are explicitly excluded. These boundaries are not automatically assigned to *Wii Fit*, *Paper Mario* or later Ring Fit modes.
