selection, manual reload, prompted replacement, grenade throw, temporary

existing Action boundaries. No game-themed duplicates are needed.

Compatible hit/armour layers, live combat, individual threat response, ordered
gates, switch-linked platform movement, finite ability charge, saved state,
persistent health, destructive collision, active light drain and off-only
recharge remain existing System boundaries. SYS-222 is used only for eligible
medkit contact transfer: indexed explicit secondary description plus consistent
paused stock-credit frames, Limited/Medium rather than a captured input trace.

assert downed revival in this carrier, nor armour-first absorption or exact
fractions. The booklet excludes explosive/environment damage from armour.

survival reuse existing Constraints. Three weapon entries, paired pistols and

and contextual control teaching reuse Information genes. The required terminal
destination and live play/permitted retry reuse existing Objective/Time genes.
