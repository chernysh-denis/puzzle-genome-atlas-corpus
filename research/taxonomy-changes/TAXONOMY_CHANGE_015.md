# Taxonomy Change 015: Merge duplicated progression objectives

## Status

- Proposal status: `Accepted`
- Claim status: `Observation`
- Evidence quality: `Corroborated`
- Confidence: `High`
- Date: 2026-08-21
- Trigger: `REGISTRY_NORMALISATION_006` Objective transfer tests

## Detected problem

Two factory finales and two route-guardian thresholds used game object names as
Objective boundaries. Prior Objective normalisation established that subject
identity and control pathway must not duplicate the pursued terminal predicate.

## Change

- Generalise `OBJ-054` to complete and launch a constructed terminal production
  project; merge `OBJ-063` into it.
- Generalise `OBJ-080` to defeat a mandatory route guardian and cross the opened
  authored progression threshold; merge `OBJ-082` into it.

Required products, launch channel, route capabilities, gate count, guardian and
post-threshold region remain parameters. Intermediate factory milestones and
optional bosses remain excluded.

## Decision

- Decision: `Accepted`.
- Rationale: each pair has the same terminal predicate after game-specific
  objects are parameterised.
- Implementation links: `OBJ-054`, `OBJ-063`, `OBJ-080` and `OBJ-082`.
