- Scope: five new Active boundaries; no lifecycle or earlier-signature change.

## Problem

The vocabulary already represents direct 3D navigation, attacks, contextual
interaction, continuous physics, contact collectibles, one health pool, staged
tutorial progress, ordered authored gates, local danger, personal resources and
shared real time. Four scoped mechanisms still fail transfer:

- unequal collectible values crossing a hundred-point threshold into a
  persistent PSI Cadet Rank rather than a life or ordinary experience level;
- zero mental health choosing checkpoint recovery or real-world ejection from
  a finite astral-projection layer;
- enemy/friendly target classes changing a live training result whose pass
  quota opens the next course gate;
- a separately collected typed luggage tag resolving only with its
  corresponding emotional-baggage body.

No existing objective ends a mental curriculum only after real-world control
returns with a different instructor's successor invitation retained.

## Accepted change

- Add `SYS-984` for figment-point accumulation and PSI-rank conversion.

- Add `SYS-986` for class-sensitive live training-target scoring and pass gate.

  Nein's retained invitation.
- Reuse existing navigation, strike, interaction, physics, collection, health,
  tutorial, authored order, information and real-time genes unchanged.

## Complete lower-ID transfer test

- `SYS-299` converts experience thresholds to character levels; figments are
  contact-collected imagination values and the documented output is PSI Cadet
  Rank. `SYS-935` and `SYS-965` instead output an extra life.
- `SYS-369` and `SYS-610` restore checkpoints but do not consume a finite
  projection layer or redirect the no-layer branch to a different world.
  `SYS-911`/`SYS-933` consume a platform-game life and reinstate a body inside
  the same stage, so they also fail transfer.
- `SYS-968`, `SYS-969`, `CON-658`, `INF-366` and `OBJ-202` describe Duck Hunt's
  fixed target count, per-target shot allowance and pass-line round. `Basic
  Braining` instead classifies enemy and protected baby cut-outs inside one
  continuing authored course.
- `CON-028` validates matched endpoints of a completed route; `CON-661` binds a
  separately collected typed tag to one corresponding world body.
- Existing tutorial, qualification, training and stage objectives settle at a
  grade, token, exit, capability or successor region. None requires completion
  of an entered mental course, return to real-world control and retention of a
  different instructor's invitation.

## Migration and validation expectation

Append five Active IDs, preserve every lower-ID signature, add complete
reviewed Ukrainian coverage and require a seventeen-gene `GAME-0358`
signature. The dedicated state reconstruction must cover course order, one
figment-rank threshold, accepted/rejected baggage identities, target-class
gallery progress, astral-layer checkpoint return, zero-layer ejection and the
settled Kid's Cabins / Sasha-button terminal.
