# Taxonomy Change 188: Held ledges and weapon posture in original Tomb Raider

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: Medium
- Date: 2026-09-30
- Trigger: `GAME-0451`, original PlayStation *Tomb Raider* Caves.
- Scope: admit `ACT-595`–`ACT-597`, `SYS-1185`–`SYS-1186`, `CON-727`–`CON-728`
  and `INF-436`. No previous genome or combination definition changes.

## Evidence and accepted boundaries

The [game source ledger](../../knowledge/games/s-z/tomb-raider-1996.md)
identifies the original Eidos manual, its missing pages, a later European
reprint and a first-hand classic Caves route. Manual predicates establish
held ledges, free-hand tasks, look and the distinction between assigned gun
target and current aiming lock. The timed passage is a limited, testable route
observation, not measured executable behaviour. No exact door seconds,
regional binding equivalence or completed run is asserted.

## Transfer and rejection tests

- Reuse ordinary direct locomotion, switches, inventory pickup, consumable use,
  fire, save interaction, body dynamics, perception-triggered pursuit, live
  combat, health, restart, authored support failure, fixture save, arrival and
  live/restoreable time.
- `ACT-362` requires usable stamina and arbitrary-surface grip. A manually
  sustained ledge without that prerequisite is `ACT-596`, not a relaxed
  definition or silent signature migration.
- `ACT-095`/`ACT-098` change projection- or visibility-authoritative spatial
  rules. `ACT-597` only recentres or directs local inspection. `INF-436`
  describes what is disclosed, not the selected camera command.
- `ACT-030` inspects an immutable evidence scene, not a live cave around a
  controlled body. `ACT-390` requires dual reticle/hand preparation and a
  reaction-timed duel draw, not an ordinary reversible weapon posture.
- Drawing/holstering `ACT-595` preserves weapon identity; replacement through
  `ACT-199` or a quick-slot selection is not that posture change. `CON-728`
  independently prevents hand tasks while the weapon remains drawn.
- `SYS-1185` preserves an assigned target while fire is held even after lost
  lock; it cannot guarantee hits or make occlusion irrelevant.
- `SYS-1186` closes a temporary route on expiry without ending the whole
  attempt. Neither a permanent switch nor a terminal global deadline fits.
- `CON-727` limits guarded walking, not every motion or injury. All numerical
  jump ranges, timing, enemy counts and medicine sizes remain parameters.

## Decision

Accept these eight bounded Active records and their reviewed Ukrainian
presentation. Preserve prior evidence and signatures. Cross-family recurrence
and an original-disc falsification run remain future work, not prerequisites
replaced by a novelty assertion.
