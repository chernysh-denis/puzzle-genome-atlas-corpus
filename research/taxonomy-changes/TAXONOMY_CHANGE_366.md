# Taxonomy Change 366 — Weapon rotation and a health-gated dash

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-21.
- Scope: [Alien Soldier](../../knowledge/games/a-f/alien-soldier.md),
  `GAME-0620`, Japanese Sega Mega Drive SUPERHARD Jetsripper encounter.
- Decision: add ACT-822 through ACT-826, SYS-1817 through SYS-1819,
  CON-860 and INF-578 as Active. Earlier definitions and usage are retained.

## Boundaries

- CON-860 permits repeated types and four independent slots. Gunstar
  Heroes' CON-825 and ACT-763 govern two component entries and their
  combination. ACT-822 instead switches the live active slot while
  movement and fire are unavailable. SYS-1817 spends and separately
  restores slot energy, unlike a shared reserve or automatic replacement.
- ACT-823 changes motion and energy cost during sustained fire. ACT-810
  offers concurrent planar aim without this stance choice. ACT-824
  holds an airborne shooting position, unlike SYS-1386's extra jump or
  ACT-680's continued glide. An ordinary move and shot still reuse
  ACT-008 and ACT-161.
- ACT-825 commits a protected dash without homing or holding a separate
  charge. SYS-1818 checks full health for contact damage and SYS-1308
  supplies the self-debit. This does not import ACT-623, ACT-749 or
  SYS-1592's different target, charge and protected-bubble conditions.
- ACT-826 starts the small forward shield. SYS-1819 governs projectile
  destruction and a separate healing pickup. SYS-994 governs reflection;
  the field does not itself restore health by activation.
- INF-578 records a weapon-conditioned marker with a lull or homing
  firing exception, distinct from the general boss gauge INF-318 or
  a selected scan INF-538. The count expires with terminal failure
  under CON-068. OBJ-029 covers the one required boss.

The Japanese retail rule source, Japanese published encounter accounts
and the recorded first clear support the distinct roles. See ALS-002
through ALS-008.
