## Problem and accepted change

A stock trolley can finish this mission, so assembling a scoop vehicle is optional. Nevertheless the original garage and licensed guide establish a distinct player decision: choose and arrange acquired functional parts before a player-choice challenge, then use the resulting directly operated vehicle's contact geometry to push a hot rock toward water. `ACT-561` records that authored vehicle assembly, `SYS-1118` its applied operating capability and `INF-415` the five ratings available while judging the build. The scoop is neither a cosmetic paint selection nor a new mandatory mission prerequisite.

## Transfer and rejection tests

- Reuse `ACT-201` and `SYS-320` for entering and directly driving the vehicle. `ACT-290`'s continuously controlled dedicated racer does not capture on-foot exit and world-vehicle entry.
- Reuse `ACT-048`, `SYS-036`, `OBJ-014` and `TIM-003` for the wrench alternative, free-rock slope motion, repeated water-receiver condition and live deadline. Rock count and timing bands are parameters.
- Reject `ACT-511`/`SYS-1017`: Spore saves biological body parts into a lineage, not an assembled vehicle selected for a timed physical target.
- Reject `TIM-009`: the challenge is interactively driven after garage preparation, not a locked one-shot automatic traversal.
- Reject `OBJ-154`: no enabled extraction exit and debrief are required after the three rocks are cooled.
- Do not infer exact part inventory, numerical mass/force simulation, a required custom build, or direct-play certification from written sources.

## Evidence and limits

The [original Microsoft Xbox 360 manual transcription](https://manuals.plus/microsoft/xbox-360-banjo-kazooie-video-game-manual) describes Mumbo's Motors, acquired parts, five ratings, test/save, vehicle controls and player-choice challenge selection. The [licensed 2008 Prima guide text](https://pdfcoffee.com/banjo-kazooie-nuts-and-bolts-official-prima-guide-pdf-free.html), printed pp. 46–47, identifies the three hot rocks, water condition, timing bands, regular-trolley viability and optional wide-scoop bulldozer and wrench tactics. GameSpot's [contemporary walkthrough](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/banjo-kazooie-nuts-and-bolts-walkthrough/1100-6202576/) corroborates the bounded mission. Neither executable nor direct audiovisual trace was inspected.

## Decision

Accept three bounded genes without editing prior genomes. Require deterministic comparison, reviewed Ukrainian text, a mechanically valid illustration and full repository/browser validation before the one authorised local commit.
