## Boundaries

- ACT-839 records the direct kick and optional directional wind-up
  against a nearby loose ball. ACT-267 belongs to a teammate-targeted
  delivery by a controlled ball carrier. ACT-747 is a physical tracked
  club swing. Neither describes this loose-ball command.
- SYS-457 owns the continuous motion of the shared football between
  contacts. SYS-1833 records an independent peer's entry into that
  exchange and return while player control stays with the same avatar.
  It does not identify latent AI conditions and is separate from
  SYS-531's manager-configured tactical decision-making.
- The scene ends with a recall and transition. TIM-003 captures the
  ongoing local actions within the recess window without inventing a
  scoring target, win condition or precise countdown.
