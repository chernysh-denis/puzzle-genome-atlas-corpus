- Proposal status: Accepted

- Scope: [Pepper Grinder](../../knowledge/games/m-r/pepper-grinder.md),

- Decision: add ACT-829, ACT-830 and CON-862 as Active. Keep prior
  definitions and memberships.

## Boundaries

- ACT-829 records directed travel with an active carried drill inside
  soft material. CON-862 limits that mode to eligible material in the
  same spatial plane. Mole Mania's ACT-815 and CON-855 select a
  different layer at a valid coordinate. Donkey Kong Bananza's ACT-820
  and CON-859 remove a chosen solid volume. Neither models this
  continuous path inside a medium bounded by ordinary solid rock.
- ACT-830 spends timing on an active in-material boost before emergence.
  The player can set the exit heading. A preset cannon (ACT-668),
  held jump from support (ACT-797) and an extra jump already in the
  air (SYS-1386) have different enabling states.
- Reuse ACT-341 for the flag fixture and OBJ-283 for the ordinary stage
  result, next route and optional retained collection. The incomplete
  set in the second Switch trace still opens Wellspring Canyon.
  SYS-905 requires score settlement from attempt measures at its marker.
- Reuse SYS-369 for the lit-lantern retry. Exact restoration for
  individual resources and objects is outside the evidenced boundary.

The booklet describes the inputs and material-dependent movement.
The Switch stills show the path, recovery and outcome. See PG-002
through PG-007.
