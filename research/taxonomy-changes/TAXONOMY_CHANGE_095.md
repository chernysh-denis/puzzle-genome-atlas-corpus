# Taxonomy Change 095: Isolate optical bearing and living-guardian topology

## Status

- Proposal status: `Accepted`
- Claim status: `Observation`
- Evidence quality: `Corroborated`
- Confidence: `High`
- Date: `2026-09-22`
- Trigger: `GAME-0356` Shadow of the Colossus complete lower-ID transfer test.
- Scope: five new Active boundaries and four evidence-backed generalisations.

## Problem

The vocabulary represented thrown locators, static compass and map surfaces,
ordinary free-surface climbing, large-hostile localized wounds, guardian health
and route-guardian terminals. It did not represent a player-focused optical
bearing that depends on direct sunlight, a hostile whose moving body is itself
the changing climb graph, sword-conditioned wound/sigil disclosure or a hunt
that settles only after automatic return to the original hub.

## Accepted change

- Add `ACT-495` for focusing reflected sunlight until visible rays converge
  toward the current world target.
- Add `SYS-983` for one living guardian's posture, motion and localized wound
  response changing the reachability and stability of its climb surfaces.
- Add `INF-369` for the converging-ray bearing that withholds route and distance.
- Add `INF-370` for sword-conditioned disclosure of the eligible local wound
  trigger and health-bearing sigil.
- Add `OBJ-206` for finding, traversing and defeating one guardian followed by
  automatic hub return and restored control.
- Generalise `ACT-362`, `SYS-661` and `CON-534` from compatible static world
  surfaces to compatible static or moving surfaces. Their grip, stamina and
  attachment boundaries do not otherwise change.
- Generalise `INF-318` so one active guardian may be identified by encounter
  presence rather than an explicitly printed name while its dedicated health
  display remains the admitted information.

## Complete lower-ID transfer test

- `ACT-163` and `INF-076` require spending and observing a thrown locator;
  `ACT-495` instead requires environmental light, held focus and aimed optical
  convergence from a reusable sword.
- `INF-279` and `SYS-792` expose a pulse or device-derived resource bearing;
  neither makes disclosure strength depend on the player's continuous aim.
- `ACT-362`, `SYS-661` and `CON-534` already own grip, climb, stamina and
  attachment legality. Extending their surface parameter to a moving body
  preserves those causal boundaries and avoids a colossus-specific duplicate.
- `SYS-402` owns retained wounds whose later destruction yields damage,
  control and material. The first colossus's leg wound instead edits the
  traversable connectivity of the same living body, so `SYS-983` remains
  separate.
- `SYS-928` combines damage and restraint into a contextual boss phase strike;
  it does not make the hostile body the traversed route or a leg wound its
  topology edit.
- `INF-318` is retained and generalised for encounter health; it does not
  disclose which anatomical point can change topology or receive major damage.
- `OBJ-080` crosses a newly opened progression threshold after a guardian;
  `OBJ-156` settles a full multi-region escape attempt at a hub. Neither models
  one optically located guardian whose defeat forcibly returns the player to
  the starting hub before the next hunt.

## Migration and validation expectation

Append five Active IDs, revise only `ACT-362`, `SYS-661`, `CON-534` and
`INF-318`, and add complete reviewed Ukrainian coverage. Earlier game
signatures, combinations and family definitions remain unchanged. Validation
must find a twenty-one-gene `GAME-0356` signature and an executable
reconstruction covering direct-light rejection, converged bearing, call and
mount, stamina-bounded grip, leg-wound kneel, moving-body route, crown damage,
zero-health failure and automatic Shrine return.
