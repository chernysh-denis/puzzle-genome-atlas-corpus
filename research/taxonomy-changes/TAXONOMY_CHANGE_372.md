# Taxonomy Change 372 — Exposed weapon matching and enemy turn delay

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-21.
- Scope: [Fuga: Melodies of Steel](../../knowledge/games/a-f/fuga-melodies-of-steel.md),
  GAME-0626, the first complete battle in the Xbox base game.
- Decision: add SYS-1827 and INF-581 as Active. Preserve earlier
  definitions, memberships and verified combinations.

## Boundaries

- SYS-1827 consumes a presented matching mark on a landed hit and
  postpones an existing enemy activation. SYS-356 already owns the
  visible queue and its progression. SYS-855 awards a bonus activation
  on weakness knockdown, while SYS-1686 shifts the acting unit by its
  command costs. Neither explains the surviving target's postponed turn.
- INF-581 exposes the current weapon class and an independently typed
  target weakness before commitment. INF-538 concerns a learned scan that
  discloses enemy information. INF-343 combines order, Action Points and
  target health. The present decision needs neither a scan nor that
  particular AP model. Seeing the mark is separate from consuming it.
- ACT-019, OBJ-029 and TIM-001 already cover target commitment, finite
  hostile clearance and discrete resolution. SYS-822 covers the graded
  report. Neither formation changes nor the Soul Cannon were admitted.

See FUG-002 through FUG-006. No countdown values, grade thresholds,
multi-mark renewal rule or exact executable revision is inferred.
