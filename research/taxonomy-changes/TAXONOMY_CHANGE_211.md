## Evidence and lower-ID transfer test

The [bounded game record](../../knowledge/games/m-r/metroid-fusion.md) uses the original Nintendo GBA manual and two independent written opening routes. Ordinary defeated infected hosts can release free X that restores compatible resources on contact; Arachnus instead leaves a protected Core-X that must be broken and absorbed to recover Morph Ball. Earlier generic resource pickup, restoration and persistent-ability genes do not express that typed source-to-outcome distinction. The rule does not authorise importing later X types or later Samus abilities into the first mission.

## Disposition

Accept `SYS-1234` with the two documented outcomes. This record restores the numbered taxonomy decision cited by the accepted `GAME-0474` unit; it changes neither that game's signature nor any earlier record.
