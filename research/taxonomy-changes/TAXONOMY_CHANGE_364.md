# Taxonomy Change 364 — Individual activations in the first Saraband battle

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: Medium
- Date: 2026-11-21.
- Scope: [Shining Force III](../../knowledge/games/s-z/shining-force-iii.md),
  `GAME-0618`, commercial North American Sega Saturn opening city fight.
- Decision: add SYS-1814, SYS-1815, CON-858 and INF-577 as Active.
  Earlier definitions, signatures and localisation remain intact.

## Boundaries

- SYS-1814 schedules individual allied and hostile decisions by an
  Agility-influenced order. It does not require SYS-356's exposed
  sequence, SYS-388's established initiative roll and action-resource
  refresh, or SYS-1305's complete party command batch.
- CON-858 orders the movement preview and command within one current
  activation. CON-034 belongs to a whole player phase, while CON-752
  permits the reverse order. Fixed cells and exclusive destinations
  reuse CON-001 and CON-011. ACT-014, ACT-019 and ACT-652 still name the
  separate movement, targeted ability and pass choices.
- SYS-1815 records the directional weapon-class advantage, which changes
  damage and special-attack chance without granting a separate action.
  INF-577 records the local movement and combat cues. Exact damage
  coefficients, tie rules and a complete displayed queue are unproven.
- OBJ-029 concerns the finite required set. The 15-HP monk falls while
  another 14-HP monk is still actionable, so the generic manual description
  of leader defeat cannot define this particular terminal.

The Sega booklet and the observed battlefield distinguish these rules.
See SF3-002, SF3-003, SF3-005 and SF3-006.
