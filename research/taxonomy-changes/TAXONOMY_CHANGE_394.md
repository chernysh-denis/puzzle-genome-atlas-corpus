## Boundaries

- ACT-341 already covers addressing the reachable mower with the
  same general interaction that addresses other fixtures.
- SYS-1870 advances the active cutting path by repeated traversals.
  CON-870 restricts ordinary working motion to each current pass.
  The exact edge threshold and the response to unused inputs remain
  open. SYS-1089 clears a breached enemy lane with a one-use mower.
- INF-601 presents distance through visible depth and changing
  scale without a meter or exact exposed pass count.
- SYS-1871 resolves the authored contact and restores local control.
  OBJ-348 records the completed job. SYS-780 additionally requires a
  recoverable retained successor. No such test was performed here.
- Neither recording supports a separate timer, all-flowers
  requirement, bonus target, item payout or achievement in this edition.
