# Taxonomy Change 347 — Record a playable form during an active battle

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-21.
- Scope: [GAME-0601](../../knowledge/games/a-f/cassette-beasts.md),
  first authored Dominoth recording on Android.

ACT-801 is the command to spend a compatible blank medium and
schedule copying. The recording actor loses its active combat
form. ACT-802 is a different command that plays an owned record
to replace the active body of that same actor. SYS-608 remains
the temporary combat-body state and its move access.

SYS-1769 resolves a pending check whose chance changes in opposite
directions with damage to the recorder and its target. CON-846
is the separately documented protection from target defeat within
that check window. SYS-1771 retains a copy even while the opponent
continues. ACT-194, SYS-307 and OBJ-252 remain capture semantics
where the live target is removed or transferred, a different terminal.
INF-563 exposes the visible recording link and updating percentage.
INF-122 retains its capture-device path.

SYS-1770 covers the personal round grant, cap, carryover and
ordinary move price. SYS-357 instead earns resources from
actions within the turn. SYS-534 receives a phase allowance for
distinct soldier commands. INF-564 exposes the current form,
health and pooled action-point prices. INF-363 prescribes
move-specific use limits and INF-343 includes an initiative
forecast not shown here.

The complete party-planning batch, ordinary mixed move order and
battle result reuse existing definitions. The separate field
movement award uses SYS-398. No previous definition or signature
changes, and no combination is added.
