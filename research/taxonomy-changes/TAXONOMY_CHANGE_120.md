# Taxonomy Change 120: Dam alarms before a non-blocking mission exit

## Status

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: Medium
- Date: 2026-09-24
- Trigger: GAME-0381 GoldenEye 007, original Nintendo 64 Secret Agent Dam.
- Scope: three additive Active genes; no earlier signature or combination
  changes.

## Problem and accepted change

Nintendo's original instruction booklet establishes a mission-objective
briefing and inspectable Q Watch while its damage, weapon and route rules can
mostly reuse existing genes. Two independent original-N64 route guides
identify four alarm targets followed by a platform jump on Secret Agent.

- `CON-688` distinguishes an available exit whose settlement fails if a
  mandatory checklist is unfinished from `CON-593`'s physically gated exit.
- `INF-388` exposes mission obligations and current completion independently
  of local guard sight or personal life/ammunition displays.
- `OBJ-223` defines the finite alarm set plus separately reachable departure.

## Lower-ID transfer and rejection test

Direct navigation, aimed fire, equipping, contextual doors, guard response,
real-time combat, pickups, health and ammunition transfer without revision.
`OBJ-026` only reaches a designated location; it would erase the four-alarm
requirement. `CON-593` rejects exit until a target roster is complete, while
the Dam jump can terminate a failed mission early. `INF-221` joins XCOM 2
concealment/detection tiles with a tactical mission fixture, neither of which
exists in this original GoldenEye packet. The deterministic lower-ID scan and
selected-neighbour interpretation live in the canonical game record.

## Evidence and limits

- [Nintendo's official Classics
  announcement](https://www.nintendo.com/en-ca/whatsnew/goldeneye-007-returns-for-nintendo-switch-online-expansion-pack-members/)
  confirms licensed access to the original 1997 N64 title.
- The [original Nintendo/Rare booklet
  transcription](https://www.world-of-nintendo.com/manuals/nintendo_64/goldeneye_007.shtml)
  documents difficulty, objectives, Q Watch, combat and failure generally.
- [Reptile's Secret Agent Dam
  route](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197462-goldeneye-007/faqs/23689)
  and [liukang's 1998
  guide](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197462-goldeneye-007/faqs/3215)
  independently support the two-task, four-alarm route. The early-jump
  failure is search-index corroboration from [StrategyWiki's Dam
  reference](https://strategywiki.org/wiki/GoldenEye_007/Dam); direct page
  access was unavailable, so that detail remains Medium confidence.
- No direct cartridge or Classics play was conducted. Exact wrapper build,
  initial ammo and result-screen wording are outside the accepted boundary.
