# Taxonomy Change 293 — Burning Rangers crystal-funded rescue

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-19.
- Trigger: [GAME-0546](../../knowledge/games/a-f/burning-rangers.md),
  original English North American Saturn Mission 1, Fallen Memory.

| Addition | Reviewed boundary | Counterexample |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ACT-744`, `SYS-1582` | Choose a homing pulse with collectible flame yield or a full laser that clears stronger threats and destroys that yield. | `ACT-604` expressly excludes charging a projectile. `SYS-1451` extinguishes a light source for visibility and does not create the rescue/protection reserve. |
| `ACT-745` | Direction plus thrust selects a state-compatible aerial burst or ground manoeuvre. | `ACT-308` controls a vehicle; `SYS-1386` spends exactly one extra jump, unlike the admitted chainable thrust. |
| `ACT-746` | Request a current-position voiced instruction without a full map or automatic movement. | A priced hint, world waypoint or passive voice line does not represent the explicit remote navigation query. |
| `SYS-1583` | Direct traversal meeting an eligible obstacle supplies an automatic jump. | Manually requested jump or flight does not model this obstacle-triggered response. |
| `SYS-1584` | Proximity transport spends the shared protection reserve; full payment additionally gives a continue. | A free rescue marker, physical escort and a separate healing currency have different resource transitions. |
| `SYS-1585` | Time and events change environmental danger, with twenty-percent explosion bursts and severe top-band pressure. | `CON-068` requires a terminal attempt deadline. A 100-percent danger value is not that timeout. |
| `INF-527` | Read crystals, charge, current room, applicable card, overall danger and next-burst bar alongside local voice instruction. | An omniscient map, independent health bar or terminal rank alone cannot supply this mission information. |
| `OBJ-303` | Clear the authored facility through its boss, graded result and next mission. | A single rescue endpoint, perfect collection and a conditional route-selected boss/successor require other conditions. |

Sixteen existing definitions transfer unchanged. In particular the crystal
protection reserve reuses `SYS-948` rather than creating a name-based duplicate
of ring loss. Native grace and scatter-cap parameters remain unmeasured;
Sonic's numerical values are not copied. Route progression reuses `SYS-605`,
fixture input `ACT-341`, stagger recovery `ACT-605` and settlement `SYS-773`.
No existing definition, signature or verified combination is changed.
