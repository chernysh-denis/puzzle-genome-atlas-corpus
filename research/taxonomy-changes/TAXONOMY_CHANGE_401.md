# Taxonomy Change 401 — Automatic propulsion and the braking charge

- Proposal status: Accepted
- Claim status: Observation
- Evidence quality: Corroborated
- Confidence: High
- Date: 2026-11-23.
- Scope: [Kirby Air Riders](../../knowledge/games/g-l/kirby-air-riders.md),
  GAME-0655, a single Air Ride Laps race using Warp Star on Nintendo
  Switch 2.
- Decision: add ACT-867, SYS-1889 and SYS-1890. Reuse six definitions
  and preserve the earlier signatures.

## Boundaries

- ACT-867 is the intentional brake hold and release. ACT-468 requires
  one held drift through a corner and a visible charge tier.
- SYS-1889 provides ongoing forward propulsion under direct steering.
  SYS-764 adds assisted steering with discrete path choices. ACT-562
  places the directly steered craft inside a forced flight corridor.
- SYS-1890 resolves a charge within one brake hold and on its release.
  SYS-892 requires ordered tiers with effective drift cornering.
  SYS-765 resolves current manoeuvres into a reserve for later use.
  SYS-1860 starts from spatial speed-pad contact.
- Reuse INF-205 for the running race view and OBJ-257 for the independent
  first-place outcome. Course lap and classification evidence does
  not establish rejection at individual checkpoints.
