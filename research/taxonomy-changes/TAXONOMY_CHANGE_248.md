cast outcomes and specimen dispositions are retained through the player's
chosen settlement save, without imposing a fabricated quota. Museum donation
is not personal caught-species credit. Original manual and release-period
attributed frames anchor the rules; later specifically New Leaf documentation
is qualified corroboration, not proof of original regional/build parity.

documentary reconstructions. No native execution, measured bite interval,
spawn coefficient, controlled reload or save-byte coverage is asserted.
