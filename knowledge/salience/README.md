# Gene Salience

Gene salience is a reviewed presentation layer over a complete canonical game
genome. It answers which admitted genes define the scoped game's mechanical
identity without changing the genome or the comparison formula.

## Roles

- **Defining**: removing this gene would erase or materially replace the
  scoped game's central mechanical proposition.
- **Structural**: the gene creates recurring decisions or progression in the
  analysed loop but does not distinguish the game by itself.
- **Supporting**: the gene supplies general timing, visibility, capacity or an
  affordance required by the loop without being its principal focus.

For every reviewed game, the three arrays must form an exact, disjoint
partition of its complete active genome. Roles are ordinal categories, not
numeric weights. They do not alter Jaccard similarity, gene admission,
combination support or novelty claims.

The public interface may translate the controlled `structural` role as
"Core loop" / "Основний цикл" and `supporting` as "Supporting" / "Підтримка"
to reduce research jargon. These labels do not change the stored role or its
boundary.

The reviewed registry lives in [`pilot.json`](pilot.json). The initial pilot and
its sequential expansions now provide a complete reviewed role partition for
all 124 games through `GAME-0124` Against the Storm. Every later batch still
requires a review of the canonical game scope and claim ledger, not automatic
inference from gene rarity.
