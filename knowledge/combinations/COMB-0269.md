# COMB-0269 — Qualifying activities fill one disclosed successor gate

- Claim status: `Pattern`
- Evidence quality: `Corroborated`
- Confidence: `High`
- Corpus role: verified interaction pattern
- Novelty assessment: no exact duplicate among 268 prior combinations; this is
  the first portable interaction in which mechanically different completed
  activities credit one retained measure, the interface exposes that measure
  together with its next threshold-gated successor, and the successor remains
  unavailable until the accumulated threshold is reached.

## Combination gene set

`SYS-517 + CON-440 + INF-207`

This is a strict three-gene subset of the twenty-six-gene Forza Horizon 6
genome, the twenty-gene Far Cry 5 genome and the sixteen-gene Cities:
Skylines II genome. The activities, their controls and
their local settlement rules remain outside the combination because they vary;
the reusable interaction begins only when their results enter one progression
measure.

## Decision structure

The player may complete any currently eligible activity from a mechanically
different set. Each valid result credits a configured amount to one retained
progression measure. Before choosing the next activity, the interface exposes
the accumulated amount, the next threshold and the successor that threshold
controls. The successor remains unavailable below the threshold and opens when
the measure reaches it, so route choice is a trade among unlike activities that
all answer the same disclosed gate.

## Combination match boundary

A score, progress bar or unlock alone is insufficient. A supporting ruleset
must let at least two mechanically different qualifying results — completed
activities, or declared changes in a quantity the ruleset already tracks —
credit one retained measure, disclose the current measure and its next threshold-gated
successor before the player commits the next activity, and keep that successor
unavailable until the shared threshold is reached. Activity names, point
values, region identities, reward types and the successor's presentation are
parameters.

## Novelty and variants

- Forza Horizon 6 pays several race disciplines into Festival Points and opens
  the Horizon Invitational at the required total; winning that later event is a
  separate objective outside this combination.
- Far Cry 5 pays a story mission, a freed civilian, a destroyed structure and a
  liberated outpost into Resistance Points and opens the region's next authored
  stage at the required total.
- Far Cry 3 converts individual hostile sites but does not pool unlike activity
  results into one disclosed successor gate inside its scoped packet.
- Currency earned from unlike activities is not enough: the retained value must
  directly control a declared successor threshold rather than merely fund
  purchases.
