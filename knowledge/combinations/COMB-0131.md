## Interaction

Player-built barriers retain clean water through forecast droughts and divert
badwater before contamination destroys irrigated food and forestry. The
resulting food, housing and well-being sustain a finite Folktail workforce whose
prioritised errands construct, haul and run material chains. Connected power
and sensor logic stabilise water infrastructure while staffed science unlocks
the final monument. Visible weather, water, colony, goods, power and signal
states guide delivery of the Earth Recultivator's construction and activation
sets until its first launch completes the map.
