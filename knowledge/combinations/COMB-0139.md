## Interaction

Harvested and looted stacks enter bounded inventory, then flow through
hand-crafting, processing fixtures and personal blueprint knowledge into
connected building grades. A Tool Cupboard couples authorisation to ongoing
typed upkeep, while locks separate permission from physical reach. Sleeping
Bags and death drops make personal loss recoverable, but server time leaves the
same sleepers, storage and structure exposed while the owner is absent.
Ordered Workbench progression makes the explosive breach downstream of
research and production, and the scheduled wipe bounds every accumulated state.
