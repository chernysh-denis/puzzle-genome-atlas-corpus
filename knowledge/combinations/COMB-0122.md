## Claim

[Observation | Corroborated | High] The player staffs a live settlement, commits
bounded development choices and manages species welfare while a forecast
three-season cycle converts expansion into escalating Storm pressure; high
Resolve advances the success track, while time and failures advance its coupled
loss track.

## Causal structure

1. The player commits scarce workers and offered buildings to a production web.
2. Drizzle and Clearance permit preparation before the forecast Storm.
3. Expansion and exploitation raise Hostility tiers and activate more severe
   Storm effects.
4. Species needs and jobs produce live Resolve values; low Resolve causes
   departures and sustained high Resolve produces Reputation.
5. Timed events compete for workers and goods before disclosed threats.
6. Reputation reduces Queen's Impatience while advancing toward victory;
   maximum Impatience ends the settlement first.

The set is a proper subset of the supporting genome: world-map embarkation,
Citadel upgrades, traders, concealed glades, extraction and settlement rewards
remain outside this interaction.

## Boundary notes

- This is not generic city happiness: welfare both drives individual departures
  and can directly advance the settlement victory track.
- This is not a fixed survival timer: the repeating forecast phases derive
  their severity from player-created Hostility.
- This is not an unstaffed factory: buildings and events compete for a finite
  reassignable population.
- Exact species, phase durations, offer pools and difficulty values are
  parameters unless later comparisons break the boundary.
