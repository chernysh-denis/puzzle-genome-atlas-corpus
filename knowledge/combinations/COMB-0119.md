## Decision structure

The player repeatedly draws from a persistently edited deck, spends a renewable
shared budget on individually targeted card effects against previewed hostile
intents and accepts health loss that survives the encounter. Victory over the
finite hostile set yields another opportunity to change the deck; those
changes alter later hands on the same terminal-health climb toward a final
boss.

## Combination match boundary

A card battler is insufficient. The played cards must come from a run-
persistent deck, ordinary combat turns must refresh a shared card budget and
draw cycle, hostile intent must be previewed before commitment, health loss
must remain consequential after encounter clearance, and later card rewards
must mutate the same deck used toward a bounded run-ending boss objective.

Balatro's scoped Blind lacks cross-encounter persistent health and deck reward
mutation. Fights in Tight Spaces has hand turnover and hostile phases but its
scoped encounter excludes route-level deck construction and persistent health
attrition, so neither currently supports the complete combination.
