## Decision structure

The player commits Luna inside a role-compatible squad loadout and chooses a
legal defender deployment source. Live movement, fire, operator equipment,
downing and revival contest only the current sector objectives. If attackers
complete the whole objective set, the front closes, defenders fall back and
attacker troop force is replenished. If committed attacker deaths empty that
pool first, the defender result settles immediately; otherwise the same loop
continues through the final sector.

## Combination match boundary

Generic hero shooting, capture points or team tickets alone are insufficient.
A supporting ruleset must bind a selected role loadout and team deployment
network to down/revive combat, close each front only after all active objectives
are secured, replenish an attacker-only life pool on sector completion and
settle defender victory at that pool's exhaustion.

## Novelty and variants

- Battlefield 6 Conquest shares squad loadouts, revival, deployment sources,
  point contest and ticket pressure but leaves all points available and gives
  both teams finite reinforcement pools.
- Overwatch Control shares hero-bound kits, contested ownership and respawn but
  scores symmetric rounds rather than advancing a sector front.
- War Thunder Domination shares vehicles, capture points and team tickets but
  uses match-locked vehicle lineups and no down/revive or sector replenishment.
- Delta Force Operations, King of the Hill, Victory Unite and Black Hawk Down
  remain separate variants because their authority, resources and terminals
  differ.
