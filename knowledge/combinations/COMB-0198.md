## Decision structure

The player commits Luna inside a role-compatible squad loadout and chooses a
legal defender deployment source. Live movement, fire, operator equipment,
downing and revival contest the active sector. Owning all required objectives
completes that sector; ownership of auxiliary deployment objectives does not
determine sector completion. Completing a non-final sector changes the legal
front, requires defender fallback and exposes the next required objective set,
while the separate resource rule replenishes the attacker-only pool. Committed
attacker deaths debit that pool; downing or a completed revive alone does not
establish this debit, and defenders have no equivalent pool. The historical
admitted exhaustion branch ends in an explicit defender Victory, while
accepted final required-sector completion ends in defender Defeat. A zero
readout alone does not certify immediate settlement for every capture/contest
condition or resolve simultaneous zero and final capture; these build-specific
terminal predicates remain unverified.

## Combination match boundary

Generic hero shooting, capture points or team tickets alone are insufficient.
A supporting ruleset must bind a selected role loadout and team deployment
network to down/revive combat, close a non-final front only after all required
sector objectives are secured, replenish an attacker-only pool separately and
reach the explicit defender result of the admitted exhaustion branch. Auxiliary
ownership is not a sector-completion gate. Universal zero/capture settlement and
simultaneous final-capture priority require separate version-qualified evidence.

## Novelty and variants

- Battlefield 6 Conquest shares squad loadouts, revival, deployment sources,
  point contest and ticket pressure but leaves all points available and gives
  both teams finite reinforcement pools.
- Overwatch Control shares hero-bound kits, contested ownership and respawn but
  scores symmetric rounds rather than advancing a sector front.
- War Thunder Domination shares vehicles, capture points and team tickets but
  uses match-locked vehicle lineups and no down/revive or sector replenishment.
- Delta Force Operations, King of the Hill, Victory Unite and Black Hawk Down
  remain separate variants because their authority, resources and terminals
  differ.
