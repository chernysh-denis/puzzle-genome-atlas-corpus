## Decision structure

The player survives one authored real-time gunfight route and may spend a
charged slowdown to control exposure. The stable replaces ordinary combat with
two maintained inputs: opponent focus and draw-hand readiness. Draw order plus
those values settle the apparent shot, but the narrator then rejects that
outcome, restores the approach and exposes the corrected objective. Only the
second stable entry completes the episode.

## Combination match boundary

Generic FPS combat, player-controlled rewind or reactive narration is
insufficient. A supporting ruleset must interrupt live combat with a
simultaneous focus/readiness duel, settle an apparent result, then
authoritatively replace that played state through narration before a distinct
positive terminal.

## Novelty and variants

- The Stanley Parable selects narrator response from the player's traversed
  branch; it does not invalidate a duel and restore a corrected scene.
- Half-Life 2 restores checkpoints after failure and ends at a chapter-location
  transition; its narrator does not replace successful play.
- Red Dead Redemption 2 slows live action and resolves marked shots, but the
  reviewed packet has no two-input draw preparation or forced narrative rewind.
