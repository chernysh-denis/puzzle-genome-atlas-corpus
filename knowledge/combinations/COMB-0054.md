## Decision structure

The player pushes one elongated log from a locally accessible side. Contact
axis determines whether it tips through one footprint transition or rolls
automatically to a blocker or water. A correctly oriented water settlement
turns that same object into persistent traversable topology, after which the
avatar must cross the created connection to the target land. Each push and its
complete roll or settlement resolve before another command is accepted.

## Combination match boundary

Ordinary one-cell block pushing, face-tracked sausage rolling, spending a
bridge token, drawing a road over water or rearranging a map does not
instantiate the interaction. Contact axis must select a distinct log motion,
water settlement must persistently add a traversable connection and success
must require the directly controlled avatar to use that connection.
