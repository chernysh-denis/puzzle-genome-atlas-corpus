## Decision structure

Declared weighted traffic pairs define which directed connections a road graph
must provide. The player edits a branching interchange, then submits the
retained network to a bounded automatic traffic run. Observed route choice and
queue formation guide another edit-and-evaluate cycle, while basic functional
completion still requires every declared pair to remain reachable.

## Combination match boundary

Road drawing alone is insufficient. Required origin-destination relations and
their relative weights must be visible before evaluation; vehicles must traverse
the authored graph automatically; complete directed reachability must gate the
evaluation state; and the retained graph must support repeated bounded traffic
runs after revision. An endless live city, a one-shot unbranched route or a
machine test without weighted road demand falls outside this boundary.
