## Decision structure

The player repeatedly edits finite reusable line topology and reallocates
vehicle capacity while unpreviewed stations and destination demand arrive in
real time. Autonomous vehicles service shape-coded trips, and any queue left
above station capacity for its full grace period terminates the scored session.

## Combination match boundary

A connection puzzle does not instantiate this combination merely because it
contains routes or moving agents. Routes must remain player-editable reusable
infrastructure, vehicles must automatically transport capacity-bounded
destination demand, the next growth events must be random and unpreviewed, and
sustained node overload must provide the terminal pressure.
