## Decision structure

The player crosses an ordered authored route while local sight and sound leave
the autonomous hunter's future state uncertain. Raising a portable sensor
reveals only the direction and distance of current nearby movement, and its own
signal can contribute to detection. Hiding, restrained movement and finite
light trade information against exposure. A known recipe consumes scarce
components into one deliberately placed non-damaging stimulus that can redirect
the hunter. Designated saving then consumes exposed live time and observes a
fixture cooldown. Code and key authority open the remaining gates into a
retained successor segment whose mission save can be restored.

## Combination match boundary

Generic stealth, a minimap dot, automatic checkpoint or thrown noise alone is
insufficient. A supporting ruleset must expose moving-only local actor
direction/distance through a player-raised portable sensor, route an autonomous
hostile from actor or decoy perception, admit attached authored hiding, couple
portable light to finite refill stock, keep personal crafting and designated
save interaction under live pressure and carry the resulting evasion route
through access gates into restorable successor control.

## Novelty and variants

- Fallout 4 shares authored first-person traversal, contextual fixtures, local
  hostile information, inventory/route displays, checkpoint recovery and
  branchable saving, but its reviewed Vault route adds fixed character
  allocation and direct combat rather than moving-only sensing and decoy-led
  evasion.
- Resident Evil 4 (2023) shares survival-action segment settlement, inventory,
  health and checkpoint pressure, but its first chapter centres finite weapon
  combat, stagger, parry and a survival-threshold event instead of a persistent
  reactive hunter.
- Project Zomboid shares local sound, perceived-cue routing, inventory and
  vulnerable crafting, but expands them into a generated persistent survival
  history without this portable sensor or authored successor mission.
- Mission, hunter, device, component, battery, code, keycard, room, save fixture
  and successor identities remain parameters.
