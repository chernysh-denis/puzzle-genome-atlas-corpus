## Decision structure

The player relocates a whole commander-led squad while its individual members
route and fight autonomously. Visible hostile carriers provide enough lead time
to redistribute squads before landing; the carriers then make shore contact
and deploy their groups on the live clock. Success requires repelling the finite
assault rather than issuing each movement step or attack.

## Combination match boundary

A real-time strategy game does not instantiate this combination merely because
units pathfind or enemies arrive in waves. Player authority must stop at the
squad level; members must navigate and engage without per-member commands; a
visible in-world carrier must telegraph and then perform group landing; and the
bounded battle must be completed by repelling that assault. Exact turn-preview
systems, unmarked spawning and individually steered units fall outside it.
