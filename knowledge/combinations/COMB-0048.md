## Combination gene set

- Action: `ACT-062`
- System Behaviour: `SYS-088`
- Information: `INF-025`
- Time: `TIM-010`

The set is a proper subset of the supporting genome. Unit movement, targeted
abilities, collision, reinforcement release, board capacity, occupancy,
allowances and objectives remain outside because they are not necessary to
identify the forecast/revision relation.

## Decision structure

The player builds a bounded tactical draft, asks the system to simulate exact
hostile consequences from that tentative state and either commits the result or
rewinds to an earlier draft step. Revisions cause the consequence forecast to
be recomputed; completed earlier turns remain outside the editable horizon.

## Analysed games

- [`GAME-0048` — Tactical Breach Wizards](../games/s-z/tactical-breach-wizards.md)

## Combination match boundary

Displaying enemy intent is insufficient. The preview must be computed from the
current tentative player draft, exact enough to evaluate the resulting tactical
state, and coupled to cost-free revision inside the uncommitted turn. A
committed hostile attack shown before planning belongs to `INF-009` / `SYS-019`
instead. Discrete puzzle undo without an explicit consequence-simulation phase
also fails.

## Relation to exact intent and simulation history

[`COMB-0047`](COMB-0047.md) commits hostile intent before planning and lets
displacement redirect its later automatic execution. `COMB-0048` instead lets
the player's draft change the hostile response and makes the displayed outcome
reversible until commit. Timelie's `COMB-0031` exposes a random-access
multi-time world trajectory and timestamped commands; this combination stops
at one tactical turn boundary.
