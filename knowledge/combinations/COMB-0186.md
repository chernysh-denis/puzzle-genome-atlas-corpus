## Decision structure

Duty entry temporarily syncs one directly controlled Gladiator and fixes three
NPC allies into the missing healer and DPS roles. The player manipulates enmity
and interleaves globally recast weaponskills with independently ready role
abilities while autonomous allies follow, heal and attack. Clues, switches,
carried keys and bosses open the fixed route. A boss wipe resets the encounter
and strengthens the next attempt with capped Willful protection; final victory
clears that assistance and defeating Denn settles the duty.

## Combination match boundary

Generic party combat or generic dungeon progression is insufficient. A
supporting ruleset must connect a temporary level/action cap, one directly
controlled party role, a complete preset autonomous role roster, threat-ranked
hostile targeting, shared-versus-independent recasts, clue/key/boss route gates,
failure-conditioned boss protection and one bounded instance result.

## Novelty and variants

- Baldur's Gate 3 shares typed party combat and ordered authored gates, but the
  player commands every active party member in turns and no duty sync or
  enmity-ranked autonomous role fill defines that scope.
- Monster Hunter Wilds shares real-time PvE, a timer, an autonomous companion
  and a boss terminal, but its one-hunt route has neither a fixed 1/1/2 NPC
  light party nor switches, keys and three boss gates.
- Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 shares party HUD, rewards and retryable boss
  combat, but its Break/turn system and directly selected party actions replace
  enmity, continuous NPC autonomy and GCD weaving.
- Other FFXIV duties, jobs, human parties and unrestricted runs may support
  variants but remain separate scope modules.
