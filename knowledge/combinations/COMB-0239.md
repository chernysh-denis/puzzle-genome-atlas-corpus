## Decision structure

One human transfers a unique control locus among a five-player side while one
shared ball alternates between held dribble and free pass, shot, deflection and
rebound states. Each possession compresses route selection, teammate spacing,
screen use, passing, release timing and defensive intervention into a separate
clock. Court position changes attempt value; contact and handling law can stop
play and prescribe a turnover, inbound or free throws. Four periods must end in
an unequal weighted score, extending into overtime whenever regulation is tied.
