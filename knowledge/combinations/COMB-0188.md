## Decision structure

The player confirms one ancestry and persistent identity before direct control
enters a scripted disaster. One exclusive escort choice fixes the interior
route. The resulting character acquires and equips gear, spends live health,
magicka and stamina across weapons, spells and a potion, changes crouch posture
to manage local detection and probes one lock with finite fragile picks.
Authored key, lever, bridge and encounter gates retain quest state until the
same character completes `Unbound` and gains exterior-world control.
