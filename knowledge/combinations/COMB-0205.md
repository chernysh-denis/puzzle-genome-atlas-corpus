## Decision structure

Traces incrementally reveal one migrating target. The hunter commits short
real-time attacks while health, stamina, sharpness, supplies, clock and faint
allowance remain under pressure, and an autonomous Palico acts beside direct
control. Great Jagras can become exhausted and feed; the hunter can exploit or
interrupt that recovery. Attacks dull the single weapon, so a live whetstone
interval trades exposure for restored effectiveness. Slay exposes finite
carves, but the positive terminal closes only when the separate results and
assignment completion persist after return to Astera.

## Combination match boundary

Generic real-time combat, tracking or boss defeat is insufficient. A supporting
ruleset must make evidence-fed reacquisition, one close-range maintenance cycle,
observable hostile exhaustion/feeding, an autonomous companion, finite
time/faint failure, embodied post-slay extraction and a distinct retained quest
result jointly causal inside one bounded hunt.

## Novelty and variants

- Monster Hunter Wilds shares the hunt, pursuit, Palico, finite-faint and result
  kernel, but its scoped signature uses Seikret routing, two weapons, wounds,
  Focus Strikes and changing ecology rather than this feeding/sharpening packet.
- The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt follows authored investigative clues, prepares a
  royal-griffin fight and hands a trophy to a quest giver; it has no repeating
  scoutfly reacquisition, sharpness cycle or finite-faint allowance.
- Elden Ring has real-time stamina combat and persistent boss progress but no
  posted-hunt clock, Palico, carving or quest-result return.
- A capture route, another weapon class, multiplayer scaling or Iceborne hunt
  changes the packet and requires separate analysis.
