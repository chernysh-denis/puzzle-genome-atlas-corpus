## Interaction

The player grows finite-deposit production through visible recipe and research
dependencies, then configures supply/demand stations whose eligible carriers
join planetary factories into one material graph. A researched, fuelled warp
regime makes interstellar expansion practical. Configured ejectors and silos
turn repeated factory products into orbiting sails and planned structural
points; receivers convert that stellar output into Critical Photons, antimatter
and finally Universe Matrices consumed by the main-mission research.
