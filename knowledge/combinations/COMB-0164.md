## Decision structure

Rome turns one Settler into a spaced hex city, assigns finite citizens to map
yields, places adjacency-sensitive districts, sequences parallel Technology and
Civic graphs, configures typed government policies, sends traders that create
income and roads, negotiates or fights rival civilizations and commits whole
empire turns until one Spaceport completes the Satellite, Moon Landing and all
three Mars modules before any rival terminal.

## Combination match boundary

A city builder, turn-based tactics game or technology tree alone is
insufficient. The ruleset must bind founded hex cities, individually worked
territory, separate adjacency-sensitive districts, concurrent Technology and
Civic progression, regime-defined policy slots, trader-built routes,
civilization diplomacy/combat and the base-game ordered Mars launch terminal in
one reproducible turn sequence.

Anno 1800 shares population growth, production chains and trade but lacks
sequential hex-unit turns, separate district adjacency, typed policy cards and
the Mars project terminal. Factorio shares research dependencies and constructed
logistics but lacks sovereign city territory, diplomacy and alternating empire
turns. Humankind shares hex empires and diplomacy but not this exact district,
policy and five-project base-Civilization-VI launch chain.
