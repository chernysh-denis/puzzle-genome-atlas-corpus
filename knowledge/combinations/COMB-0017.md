## Decision structure

The player commits visible card subsets either to score or to spend a separate
redraw allowance against a concealed but fixed deck order. Scoring automatically
classifies the best applicable pattern and applies a player-ordered persistent
modifier sequence before a terminal Hand budget is exhausted.

## Combination match boundary

A card game does not instantiate this combination merely because it draws a
hand or recognises poker patterns. It must separate scoring Hands from
non-terminal Discards, preserve concealed draw order as current state, evaluate
patterns automatically and let an ordered persistent modifier tableau change
the final arithmetic result.
