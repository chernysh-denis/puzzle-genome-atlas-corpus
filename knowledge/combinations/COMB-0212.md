## Decision structure

Tommy accepts each authored passenger and must choose an actual city route to
the named destination. Traffic, collision, speed and police observation remain
live. The limiter can lower speeding risk; a minor citation can be settled, but
flight or a serious offence escalates toward arrest. Five valid fare settlements
trigger a forced vehicle-to-foot transition, after which live incoming fire and
route choice matter until Tommy reaches Salieri's Bar.

## Combination match boundary

Generic driving, a taxi minigame or police pursuit alone is insufficient. A
supporting ruleset must directly bind successive authored passengers to
destinations, expose a reversible compliance control and a citation/arrest
branch, then make completion of that finite service chain unlock a mandatory
on-foot safe-location escape.

## Novelty and variants

- Grand Theft Auto V shares direct vehicles, traffic, police search and authored
  on-foot missions, but lacks the admitted five-fare service chain and payable
  roadside citation branch.
- Euro Truck Simulator 2 shares direct road control, road law and fines, but
  carries cargo under one commercial contract and immediately debits violations
  rather than converting noncompliance into arrest GAME OVER.
- Need for Speed Payback shares ordered vehicle stages and an authored terminal,
  but its Highway Heist uses hostile wreck quotas and vehicle handoffs rather
  than passengers, traffic-law compliance and an on-foot escape.
