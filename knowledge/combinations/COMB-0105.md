This is a proper subset of the ten-gene Outer Wilds genome. Navigation, the
ordinary lift trigger, destination arrival and generic live progression support
the packet but do not define the cross-loop knowledge-to-access relationship.

## Decision structure

The player acquires one exact operational fact through dialogue and becomes
paired to a loop-memory system. A fixed live cycle or earlier death restores the
body and mutable world at the authored origin while retaining that learned fact.
The reset lift is still physically closed, yet it now accepts activation because
the retained credential is known; authored dialogue explicitly acknowledges
that the fact came from a prior iteration.

## Analysed games

- [`GAME-0105` — Outer Wilds](../games/m-r/outer-wilds.md)

## Combination match boundary

A checkpoint restart with player memory outside the simulation is insufficient.
The game must register a fact through play, reset embodied and world state, make
the retained fact explicitly available afterward, and let that fact authorise a
freshly reset mechanism without reacquisition or a carried token.
