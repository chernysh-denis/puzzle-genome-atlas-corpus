## Decision structure

The player accepts one typed field offer and its borrowing deduction, collects
and couples the supplied tractor and spreader, then converts direct steering and
tool activation into accepted fertilizer coverage while reading field, fill and
progress state. Only enough target-field treatment makes the contract
collectable; explicit collection releases the temporary fleet and credits the
net payment.

## Combination match boundary

A farming game, vehicle, attachment or paid task alone is insufficient. A
supporting ruleset must supply a temporary compatible vehicle-tool set through
one field contract, require the player to couple and operate it, convert a
moving material-consuming footprint into persistent assigned-surface progress
and close that progress through explicit economic collection.

## Novelty and variants

- Euro Truck Simulator 2 also supplies employer equipment for one paid job,
  but its truck arrives as a loaded articulated cargo state and closes at a
  destination bay under route, damage, fatigue and deadline rules.
- Stardew Valley changes crop tiles with hand tools across calendar time but
  has neither a powered coupled footprint nor a borrowed field contract.
- A Farming Simulator harvesting contract would add crop collection, trailer
  transfer and selling-point delivery; Precision Farming would add soil samples
  and variable-rate treatment. Neither is inherited by this base packet.
