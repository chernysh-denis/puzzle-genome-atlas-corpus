## Decision structure

The player keeps environmental protection viable while locating and mining
finite inputs. Personal recipes and one placed, fuelled processor produce
different components. These restore technology slots, enabling a rechargeable
survey pulse and repaired transport. Tutorial predicates reveal the next
dependency. Launch changes movement scale, but success still requires the first
guided building interaction and a save/reload check after returning to and
exiting the vehicle.

## Combination match boundary

Generic survival crafting, a repair task or free vehicle travel is insufficient.
A supporting ruleset must couple live environmental viability, finite local
resource extraction, two distinct production commitments, addressed technology
restoration, a repair-gated rechargeable local survey, staged tutorial
predicates, directly restored transport and a retained first guided destination
after explicit interaction.

## Novelty and variants

- Subnautica shares survival pressure, resource collection, crafting,
  fabrication and direct vehicle control, but its scoped chain uses
  breath-limited dives, fragment blueprints, habitat integrity and fabrication
  of a new Seamoth rather than repaired starting transport.
- Valheim shares generated survival, extraction, crafting and placed processing,
  but boss discovery and defeat replace damaged slots, scanner cooldown and a
  verified first-destination save.
- Don't Starve Together shares survival meters, recipes and fixtures, but
  requires two humans and ghost revival without a repaired starter vehicle.
