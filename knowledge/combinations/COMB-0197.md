## Decision structure

The fixed eligible car commits a disclosed street-race packet. Route rivals and
ordered checkpoints produce a classified finish while skilled driving creates
a spendable Burst reserve. The finish exposes cash and raises Heat rather than
ending the trace. Police perception must then be broken through a complete
search interval before garage entry converts the exposed result into retained
campaign state; a bust removes the at-risk cash.

## Combination match boundary

An ordinary race, boost meter or wanted chase alone is insufficient. A
supporting ruleset must make the selected driving event generate a classified
ordered result, create its acceleration reserve from live driving technique,
carry race-created Heat into a required perception/search pursuit and delay
durable earnings until escape-gated garage entry.

## Novelty and variants

- Forza Horizon 6 shares car/event commitment, difficulty-scaled rivals and
  ordered result validation but settles rewards without a forced police phase.
- Grand Theft Auto V shares traffic, wanted escalation and unseen clearance but
  its ordinary race result does not create garage-gated exposed cash.
- Rocket League spends a vehicle boost reserve collected from spatial pads and
  has no pursuit or persistent race payout.
- Later Unbound buy-ins, side bets, Qualifiers and Online play remain separate
  variants because they change entry risk, authority or terminal progression.
