## Decision structure

The player begins with four concealed soldiers and interleaves commands within
their personal two-point budgets while reading movement, cover, detection and
attack odds. After the squad has been revealed and the hostile pod activated,
the analysed reaction branch arms Overwatch with remaining authority and
carries that preparation across squad-phase closure. Eligible hostile-phase
movement may trigger the shot; player-phase activation reactions are outside
this deliberately narrowed branch, not declared impossible in XCOM 2. Success
still requires both an adjacent X4 interaction and finite hostile clearance,
with tactical health settling into wounds or permanent loss.
