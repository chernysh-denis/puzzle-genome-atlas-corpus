## Decision structure

The player interleaves two-point commands among four concealed soldiers while
reading movement bands, cover, detection and attack odds. Reveal activates a
hostile pod. A soldier may spend final authority on Overwatch, whose prepared
shot resolves only if eligible hostile movement occurs after the player closes
the squad phase. Success then requires both an adjacent X4 interaction and
finite hostile clearance, with tactical health settling into wounds or
permanent loss.

ruleset must couple freely interleaved personal action budgets, concealment
