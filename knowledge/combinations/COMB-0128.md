## Decision structure

The player spends finite materials to build a street-connected radial city,
assigns a finite population to production and adjusts shifts or heat modes while
coal and weather continuously recompute the safety of every occupied building.
Irreversible laws change production and welfare rules; illness, food, Hope and
Discontent turn those choices into cascading pressure. The combined city must
remain viable through the authored terminal Great Storm.
