## Decision structure

The player reserves one Damage slot before the lobby exists, influences the
map through a collective ballot and selects one unique role-legal hero. Direct
movement and weapon/ability use then interact with cooldown, ultimate, perk and
respawn cycles. Combat gains value only when the team captures the active
neutral point, holds it for percentage and preserves legal pressure through
overtime. Local point state resets between submaps while round wins persist;
the second round closes the match.

## Combination match boundary

A supporting ruleset must fix a role before matchmaking, expose a collective
map ballot, allow a unique hero and match-local build choices inside that role,
return knocked-out heroes through team spawn, convert exclusive neutral-point
ownership into retained percentage and reset local point state while retaining
a first-to-two round score. Generic hero combat or control-point capture alone
is insufficient.

## Novelty and variants

  aggregates several points and deaths into reinforcement-ticket depletion.

  commitment, composition or settlement boundary and are outside this pattern.
