## Decision structure

The player selectively enters corpse-linked reconstructions from a stable
indexed evidence set, cross-references them against a disclosed roster and
records provisional compound identity-and-fate hypotheses. The system withholds
per-entry feedback until enough correct records coexist, then confirms and
locks the batch. Self-paced revisits and revisions progressively reduce the
remaining roster until the exact ledger is complete.

## Combination match boundary

A mystery game does not instantiate this combination merely because it has
clues or a notebook. Evidence must be fixed, selectively revisitable and
linked to an explicit finite identity roster; the player must enter structured
compound subject records; correctness feedback must be delayed until a batch
threshold and then lock accepted entries. Direct reveal, free-form narrative
notes or immediate answer checking falls outside the boundary.
