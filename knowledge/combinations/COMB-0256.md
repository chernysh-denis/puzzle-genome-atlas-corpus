## Decision structure

The player follows a bounded authored route while apparently ordinary nearby
props cannot be trusted as non-hostile. Local observation or testing can force
one autonomous threat from its copied-prop form into revealed combat. A finite,
equipment-gated GLOO shot applies temporary live control, creating a bounded
opportunity for direct Wrench action. Authored evidence supplies a matching
code for the dependent office fixture; the required office interaction advances
the objective, and save/load verifies the retained successor state.

## Combination match boundary

Generic disguise, ordinary object duplication, a freezing weapon or a locked
door alone is insufficient. A supporting ruleset must allow an autonomous
hostile to occupy both a deceptive copied-object appearance and a revealed
combat form, make ordinary appearance insufficient evidence of safety, permit
player testing, apply a finite selected live-control effect before direct
follow-up, pass authored matching authority into a dependent route fixture and
retain the resulting objective handoff for reload verification.
