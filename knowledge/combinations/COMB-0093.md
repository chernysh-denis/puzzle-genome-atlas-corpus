## Decision structure

The player rotates one visibly marked monument component through its authored
local arc. While it moves, no traversal decision is valid. At a supported snap
pose, the system rebuilds screen-projected navigation-node connections and
marks the destinations Ida can currently reach. The player then selects a
reachable remote goal once, and the system pathfinds Ida across the intervening
nodes until she arrives. The camera remains fixed throughout.

## Combination match boundary

Visual alignment alone is insufficient. One addressed architectural component
must physically move to an authored settled state; projected node connectivity
and visible reachability must update only after that settle; and a selected
reachable world destination must trigger multi-node avatar traversal without
step-level steering. Global camera rotation, continuously live perspective,
ordinary physical bridges, direct locomotion and cosmetic optical illusion
remain outside the boundary.
