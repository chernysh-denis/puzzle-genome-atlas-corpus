## Decision structure

The player converts independently contested control points into complete
sectors and preserves enough complete sectors to impose majority pressure on a
finite team resource. During that same live fight, one compatible current-
weapon attachment can be replaced without redeployment, and one shared-
capacity support vehicle can be requested at a legal local position. Both
adaptations are subordinate to the zero-reinforcement team terminal.

## Combination match boundary

Generic territory control, equipment selection or a vehicle spawn is
insufficient. A supporting ruleset must group several independently owned
points into all-owned sectors, convert sector majority into ongoing shared
attrition, permit a compatible attachment change during live control and let
an eligible combatant place a shared-capacity vehicle delivery in the world.

## Novelty and variants

- Battlefield 6 shares point capture, revival, vehicles and the same
  zero-ticket objective, but drains for individual points and lacks this Plus
  plus player-positioned call-in relation.
- Battlefield V adds authored Fortifications and earned squad-leader
  Reinforcements, but does not use the same all-point-sector aggregation or
  any-player shared-capacity vehicle delivery.
- World of Tanks and War Thunder share direct vehicle combat; they do not make
  one live infantry request create a locally placed support vehicle inside the
  same nested sector terminal.
