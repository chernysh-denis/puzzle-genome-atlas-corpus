## Decision structure

The player directly controls one car through ordered race gates and earns a
classified result, but that finish does not settle the night's reputation.
Notoriety still modifies both exposure and the later return reward. Police
can be avoided or evaded; damage can justify a service detour, yet every
roadside restoration consumes the same session allowance. Reading condition
beside exposure makes the route to an eligible refuge a continuing decision.
Voluntary banking closes the packet; a captured or wrecked car does not.

## Combination match boundary

A single first-place reward, a required cash-carrying pursuit or an ordinary
vehicle repair is insufficient. A matching packet must preserve race
classification, session notoriety and reputation exposure, conditional police
pressure, the globally shared roadside recovery allowance and a later
voluntary refuge terminal.

## Novelty and variants

- Unbound shares direct driving and delayed garage settlement but forces its
  analysed chase and exposes cash with Burst Nitrous rather than this pairing
  of persistent HEAT, REP and quota-limited roadside restoration.
- A different race name, car, repair-count parameter or reward amount alone
  would not make a new combination if every causal boundary remains.
- Crew, High Heat parts and online racing are not implied by this pattern.
