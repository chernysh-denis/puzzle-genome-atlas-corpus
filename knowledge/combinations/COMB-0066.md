## Decision structure

The player fires boundary experiments into one fixed concealed occupancy field.
Each hidden orthogonal ray resolves to a retained hit, reflection or paired
exit without exposing its internal route. Those indirect results constrain an
exact-cardinality spatial proposal. Check quantifies over every possible probe:
it accepts a proposal with the same complete outcome mapping or returns one
distinguishing experiment. The loop ends at observational equivalence rather
than necessarily identical hidden coordinates.

## Combination match boundary

A fixed secret and generic feedback are insufficient. The player must choose
external spatial probes; the system must resolve hidden propagation and retain
probe-specific outcomes; a complete fixed-cardinality layout must be submitted;
and correctness must compare the full probe mapping, including acceptance of
indistinguishable layouts. Direct cell reveal, exact-coordinate-only checking,
ordered-code scoring and visible path construction are outside the boundary.
