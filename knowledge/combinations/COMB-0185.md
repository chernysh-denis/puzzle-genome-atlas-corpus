## Decision structure

real-time combat and return in shared waves. BLU proximity moves a supplied cart
while RED proximity blocks it; secured checkpoints award time, lock rollback and
alter spawn access. The final route state is resolved through touch-renewed
overtime into either cart delivery or defence.
