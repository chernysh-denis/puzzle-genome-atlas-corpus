## Decision structure

The adviser advances from resource and building work to a composition-checked
regiment, live repair and ferry transport. Passengers must disembark before a
second settlement can exist; soldiers then preserve ownership of exposed
economic targets. Mercenary bowmen solve the prescribed ranged breach but
create continuing gold liability, and burning the barracks remains
intermediate until the Academy/Town Hall instruction settles the mission.

## Combination match boundary

Generic real-time strategy economy or tutorial text is insufficient. A
supporting ruleset must make selected workers, spatial building, local unit
queues, formation composition, ferry embarkation and disembarkation, guardable
economic ownership, wage-dependent mercenaries, delayed building fire and a
later authored teaching terminal jointly causal in one bounded route.

## Novelty and variants

- Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition shares workers, construction, housing,
  building queues and formations, but its reviewed Skirmish has no admitted
  ferry, capture lesson, mercenary rebellion, burning-building step or tutorial
  terminal.
- Hearts of Iron IV shares concurrent production, research and objective
  dependencies, but operates through national fronts rather than selected
  peasants, transport containment and building ownership.
- Cossacks 3 `War` removes the settlement economy; Random Map removes the fixed
  teaching order and final Town Hall settlement. Each requires separate scope.
