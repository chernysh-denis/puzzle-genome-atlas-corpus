## Decision structure

One icon advances horizontally through a fixed music-linked obstacle sequence.
The player times one press/hold/release stream; cube support converts it to a
jump, ship flight converts it to vertical force, and authored portals switch
between those laws. A local scrolling view and the soundtrack cue the next
timing. Any unsafe contact clears the attempt to 0% while retaining best
progress; only one continuous finish settles ordinary completion.

## Combination match boundary

A real-time platformer, rhythm soundtrack or instant retry alone is
insufficient. The ruleset must bind automatic horizontal travel, one
context-sensitive vertical input, at least two authored movement modes, local
scrolling lookahead, a fixed audiovisual obstacle clock, terminal collision,
checkpointless origin restart, retained best progress and one uninterrupted
level finish.

Cut the Rope shares continuous physics and live timing but lets the player cut
different spatial objects and routes a payload rather than an auto-runner.
Tin Hearts shares automatic locomotion, physics and live pressure but permits
pause, world-device editing and rewind. Braid shares platform physics and live
timing but directly steers horizontal movement and restores history instead of
replaying one fixed audiovisual route from 0%.
