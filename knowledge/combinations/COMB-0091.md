projection-to-traversal decision structure.

## Decision structure

The player first traverses the currently active front-layer collision slice,
then commands a clockwise or counterclockwise quarter-turn to an adjacent one
of four cardinal orthographic views. During the transition, depth relations
that the flat endpoint hid become visible and avatar movement is suspended.
After settling, front-to-back collision and depth correction choose a new
two-dimensional traversal slice. World objects have not moved, but their
screen-space adjacency has changed, so the avatar can continue across a route
that the prior view did not expose.

## Combination match boundary

A rotating camera is insufficient. The rotation must be a rule-bearing
cardinal view command whose settled orthographic projection changes the legal
collision or adjacency graph for a directly controlled avatar. Physically
turning platforms, changing gravity, creating geometry from an image, or
merely revealing a collectible without enabling projection-relative traversal
stays outside the boundary.
