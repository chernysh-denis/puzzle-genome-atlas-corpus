The 22-gene set is a strict subset of Palworld's 54-gene genome. It omits
general avatar navigation/combat, crafting, construction, technology, survival,
world generation, story progression and the ending while retaining the reusable
interaction that turns one captured identity into a party companion or base worker.

## Interaction

The player spends a device on a displayed probabilistic capture. Success stores
the creature as one persistent owned Pal that can later occupy a bounded party
or base slot. Party deployment produces autonomous follow/combat behaviour;
base assignment instead dispatches suitability-compatible work while food,
sanity, health, reachability, priority and storage capacity qualify production.
