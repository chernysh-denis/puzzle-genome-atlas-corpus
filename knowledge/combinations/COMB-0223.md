## Decision structure

The player continuously pilots one cockpit craft while deciding how a finite
power budget should favour engines, lasers or shields. Target geometry and
incoming warnings create immediate attack and defence demands; the player can
focus the shield reserve toward the threatened facing or spend a timed finite
countermeasure against a guided missile. These coupled live decisions are
repeated through one authored mission until its guaranteed medal settles.
