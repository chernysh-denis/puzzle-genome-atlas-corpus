## Decision structure

The player cannot steer or attack with the hero. Instead, selected world cards
create recurring future encounters on a fixed cyclic route. The hero traverses
and fights autonomously, while each completed circuit raises subsequent threat.
The player decides when the authored danger has yielded enough resources and
commits retreat, with retention conditioned by reaching the safe camp boundary.

## Combination match boundary

An auto-battler does not instantiate this combination merely because combat is
automatic. The player must place persistent world conditions that produce
future encounters on one fixed cyclic route; the same hero must traverse and
fight without movement or strike commands; each circuit must escalate danger;
and voluntary exit must bank accumulated expedition value under a location-
conditioned retention rule. Direct unit orders, finite population rescue and
pre-run-only map editing fall outside the boundary.
