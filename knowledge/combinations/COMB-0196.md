## Decision structure

The fixed participant packet enters one platform arena unarmed. Neutral pickups
become one of the selected Legend's two weapon vocabularies; attacks then build
damage that increases later launch consequence. The defender may spend dodge,
jump and Recovery opportunities to reverse an off-stage trajectory, while
ground, wall and hit transitions restore only declared options. Crossing a
blast zone consumes and resets one stock until the final loss settles the duel.

## Combination match boundary

A side-view fighter, health-bar duel or respawning arena alone is insufficient.
A supporting ruleset must make spatial pickups select a fighter-compatible
weapon vocabulary, retain damage as future launch risk, give the launched body
a bounded contact-resettable recovery budget and convert surrounding
blast-zone exits into recoverable personal stocks whose exhaustion ends the
same match.
