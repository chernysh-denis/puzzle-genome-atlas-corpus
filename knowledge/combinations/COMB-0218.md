## Decision structure

One squad repeatedly enters a symmetric Conquest field, fights and revives
around capturable points, and spends bounded health and ammunition. Authored
Fortifications turn empty outlines into cover or resupply. Cooperative actions
earn one shared point pool, but only the squad leader can spend it on a legal
Reinforcement. These local sustain choices preserve bodies and territory so
point pressure and unrevived deaths deplete the opposing ticket pool first.

## Combination match boundary

Generic Conquest combat or a buildable barricade is insufficient. A supporting
ruleset must combine reversible downing, legal redeployment, point-to-ticket
pressure, authored live construction, health/ammunition scarcity and a shared
squad budget whose typed spend is restricted to one leader role.
