## Interaction

Resting at Grace refills the current build while reviving most field enemies.
Runes earned in that renewed field can be committed to one chosen attribute or
carried onward; death moves the unspent stock to one recoverable mark and a
second death replaces it. Armament requirements, equipment-load tier, stamina,
FP and finite flask allocation determine which attacks, evasions, skills and
restorations remain legal. Torrent expands preparation routes, while one legal
Spirit Ash can provide autonomous help in a monument-bounded arena. Those
preparation choices feed the live stance-and-health fight with Margit, whose
defeat opens the first Stormveil threshold.
