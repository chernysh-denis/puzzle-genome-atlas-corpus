## Decision structure

The player spends one of four renewable slots to instantiate a body at a
line-of-sight target. Every body then receives the same movement input, while
local geometry resolves each separately. The player moves one exclusive device
locus between visible bodies to change the origin of later creation and swap
actions. Coloured regions suppress those two channels independently, and body
removal recovers scarce creation capacity while live physics continues.

## Combination match boundary

A game does not instantiate this combination merely by controlling several
characters, summoning units or teleporting. It must let the player aim-created
bodies share live movement input with local physical resolution, transfer one
exclusive device locus among those bodies through an unobstructed target,
manage a renewable body cap and encounter spatial fields that suppress creation
and transfer as distinct channels. Autonomous followers, independently steered
actors and fixed teleport destinations fail the boundary.
