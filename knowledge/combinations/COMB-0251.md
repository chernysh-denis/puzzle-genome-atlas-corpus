## Decision structure

The player traverses and fights through an authored on-foot route while each
compatible installed power module advances the same disabled allied platform
through a visible restoration stage. The final stage enables an operator link;
legal entry then replaces the on-foot locomotion, attack, ability and HUD
surface with direct control of the occupied platform. Typed defence and offence
resolve the required heavy combat before the chapter settles into retained
successor control.

## Combination match boundary

Generic vehicle entry, a repaired companion, a weapon pickup, wall-running, a
combat tutorial or a chapter transition alone is insufficient. A supporting
ruleset must use multiple accepted power-restoration stages on one allied
combat platform, require a compatible operator link and reachable entry,
transfer the complete embodied combat-control surface to that occupied ally and
carry the resulting required encounter into a retained authored successor.

## Novelty and variants

- Battlefield 2042 permits infantry-to-vehicle operation inside one Conquest
  match, but no staged restoration or authored operator-link chain creates that
  vehicle and no campaign successor retains the result.
- STAR WARS Jedi: Fallen Order retains a learned traversal capability before a
  Vault objective, but the companion remains support and does not become the
  player's direct combat body.
- Split Fiction can place one player in a vehicle and another in a turret, but
  roles remain separate human authorities rather than one linked ally replacing
  an on-foot control surface after power restoration.
- Platform, operator, power-module count, movement abilities, weapon, chapter,
  hostile and successor identity remain parameters rather than combination
  identity.
