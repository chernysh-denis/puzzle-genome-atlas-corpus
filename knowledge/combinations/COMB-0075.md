## Decision structure

The player assigns binary light sources to white cells. Each source projects
four wall-bounded orthogonal rays. The complete proposal must satisfy exact
local bulb counts at numbered walls, forbid any two sources from sharing one
unblocked ray and cover every white cell with at least one illumination set.
Coverage alone, clue satisfaction alone or source separation alone is
insufficient.

## Combination match boundary

A puzzle does not instantiate this combination merely by toggling lights or
covering cells. It must combine persistent player-assigned sources,
wall-bounded orthogonal propagation, exact local clue cardinality, pairwise
source visibility exclusion and complete ray coverage.
