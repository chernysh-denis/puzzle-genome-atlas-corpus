## Decision structure

The player traverses an authored route and deliberately activates a world
object that releases a finite hostile group and locks forward progress. During
live movement and aimed combat, damage can expose a brief close-range finisher
opportunity. Taking that positional risk defeats the target and produces
compatible recovery drops; declining it preserves distance. The exit becomes
traversable only after the entire required group is cleared, and the packet
continues to a fixed authored successor location.

## Combination match boundary

Generic shooting, an unbounded horde, a cosmetic stagger animation or a room
that opens after one arbitrary kill is insufficient. A supporting ruleset must
combine a player-settled authored trigger, finite hostile release, live aimed
combat, a visible short-range finisher window, recovery produced by that
finisher, full-clearance route gating and a fixed successor destination.

## Novelty and variants

- Serious Sam HD shares finite authored releases, ammunition pressure and a

  exchange in its reviewed Sand Canyon packet.

  pickups but uses a random room/run economy rather than a world interaction

- A different mission, hostile, weapon, pickup amount or door appearance does
  not form a new combination while this causal boundary remains unchanged.
