## Decision structure

The player spends a finite reassignable rail inventory to construct a spatial
machine before execution. Starting the run locks editing and advances several
autonomous carriages on one shared clock. The layout must encode both a legal
route and enough temporal separation to prevent collisions, while causing every
numbered carriage to reach the locomotive in the declared ascending order.

## Combination match boundary

A rail network does not instantiate this interaction merely because vehicles
follow it automatically. Construction must consume a finite reusable rail
inventory; one committed run must advance multiple vehicles synchronously;
temporal occupancy conflicts must fail; and success must assemble the complete
numbered set at a receiver in declared order. Live passenger service, repeated
traffic evaluation and a one-vehicle delivery route fall outside the boundary.
