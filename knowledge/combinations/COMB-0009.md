## Decision structure

The player spends one bounded move to exchange adjacent items into a qualifying
match. The system then removes matches, collapses vacancies, refills the board
and repeats resolution until stable, crediting removals toward declared targets
before the next move is accepted.

## Combination match boundary

A tile game does not instantiate this combination merely because equal items
disappear. It must use match-valid adjacent swaps, resolve clear-collapse-refill
cascades automatically, charge a finite move allowance and require declared
board-element targets before that allowance is exhausted.
