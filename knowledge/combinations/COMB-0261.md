## Decision structure

The primary weapon can be committed to a position in the world. Throwing it
resolves against everything on the outbound line, leaves it where it stopped,
and recalling it resolves a second time along a line the player chooses by
standing somewhere. While it is away the character fights with a different
close set, so the throw is simultaneously an attack, a piece of positioning and
a temporary change of vocabulary. Incoming attacks carry cues that sort them
into categories differing in which defensive response can succeed, so reading
the category precedes timing the response. A persistent companion both acts on
its own and answers a direct attack command whose availability recovers over
time, and accumulated stability on a hostile exposes a prompted close finish
that expires if unused.

## Combination match boundary

A thrown weapon, a block, a companion or a finisher alone is insufficient. A
supporting ruleset must let one reusable tool leave the hands and be recalled
along a second resolved line, substitute a different close action set while it
is away, hold a guard that consumes no reserve, sort incoming attacks into cue
categories that select the legal response, command a companion ability against
a recovering availability, and expose a stability-gated close finisher.

## Novelty and variants

- DOOM (2016) shares real-time combat, a prompted finisher and an exposed
  eligibility cue, but keeps every weapon permanently in hand and feeds its
  finisher into a resource economy.
- It Takes Two shares the reversible throw and recall of one reusable tool, but
  binds it to a cooperative role and a construction packet rather than to a
  combat vocabulary that changes while the tool is away.
- Resident Evil 4 (2023 remake) shares a timed defensive response, a stagger
  state and a prompted close follow-up, but spends weapon durability on its
  parry and has no recallable thrown weapon.
- DARK SOULS III shares a held guard and a mandatory guardian, but prices
  offence and defence from one shared reserve rather than typing the answer by
  cue.
- Chapter, objective, weapon, companion and exact quantity names remain
  parameters.
