## Decision structure

The manager selects only eligible players, assigns a starting eleven and bench
and declares coupled in-possession and out-of-possession shapes. Autonomous
footballers then resolve the match from that plan. Live condition, performance,
analytics and advice expose reasons to spend bounded substitutions or revise
roles and instructions, but the manager never steers a body or the ball. Final
regulation settlement becomes a persistent fixture and competition-table fact.

## Combination match boundary

Football laws or team AI alone are insufficient. A supporting ruleset must
couple eligible roster assignment, distinct possession-phase plans,
manager-only live interventions, autonomous football resolution, evolving
player evidence, bounded substitutions and persistent competition recording.
EA SPORTS FC 26 lacks the manager-only authority, dual tactical visualizer and
career-table terminal; a non-interactive match simulation lacks live revision.
