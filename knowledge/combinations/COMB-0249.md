## Decision structure

The player steers and attacks in real time, using a protected directional
movement commitment while persistent health remains at risk. Complete hostile
clearance admits a reward and successor choices; each exit forecasts its
reward class, and each upgrade offer exposes the effect and capability-slot
consequence of one exclusive temporary choice. The selected build and health
continue through boss-gated regions until escape or terminal loss clears the
attempt and returns control to a hub that retains eligible progression.

## Combination match boundary

Generic real-time combat, random loot, a dodge, a roguelike reset or permanent
upgrades alone is insufficient. A supporting ruleset must combine finite
combat-room closure with reward-labelled immediate route alternatives,
fully described compatibility-bound run-upgrade choices, carried health/build
through mandatory regional gates and a rules-defined persistent-hub return
after either scoped success or terminal defeat.

## Novelty and variants

- The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth carries a temporary item build through locked
  rooms, but explores a floor graph whose special rooms and typed keys/bombs
  replace reward-labelled forward doors and capability-slot Boon choices.
- Slay the Spire carries health and modifiers through visible branches, but
  uses a complete act map, deck construction and turn phases rather than
  direct protected action combat.
- Vampire Survivors pauses for level-up drafts, but automatic weapon clocks
  and one authored survival timer replace room clearance, door previews and
  boss-gated regional escape.
- Loop Hero returns resources from a fixed autonomous circuit and permits
  voluntary retreat; Hades directly controls combat and formalises death or
  escape, not chosen location, as the attempt terminal.
- Character, god, Boon, weapon, room, symbol, currency, region, guardian and
  numeric values remain parameters rather than combination identity.
