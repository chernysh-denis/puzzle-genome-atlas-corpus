## Claim

[Observation | Corroborated | High] Persistent work, storage and resident
policies create autonomous colony labour, but a crisis lets the player draft
specific residents and temporarily replace that autonomy with exact tactical orders.

## Causal structure

1. Plans, work priorities, bills, storage filters and personal policies create eligible errands.
2. Residents autonomously choose work, travel, haul and satisfy needs.
3. Storyteller pressure can introduce a threat that makes ordinary job routing unsafe.
4. Drafting selected residents suspends their work autonomy and accepts exact positions and targets.
5. Undrafting returns survivors to the same persistent policy-driven colony graph.

The set is a proper subset of the supporting genome: world generation,
research, body-part simulation, prisoners, caravans and ship launch remain outside it.

## Boundary notes

- This is neither permanent direct unit control nor purely indirect colony management.
- Drafting is a temporary mode switch for selected residents; persistent labour policies remain.
- Exact threat scale, work tiers and pawn count are parameters.
