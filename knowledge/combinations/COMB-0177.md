## Decision structure

Selected villagers must repeatedly reach finite sources and compatible drop-off
buildings, while other villagers turn prepaid legal foundations into production
and housing capacity. Multiple building-local training and research queues then
compete for one four-resource stockpile and population headroom. The player must
satisfy spatial building and resource prerequisites through four ordered Ages,
form and command ranged units under terrain/range constraints, protect Imperial
siege and remove the opponent's recoverable civilization-wide production state
before suffering the symmetric Conquest terminal.

## Combination match boundary

Generic real-time combat or resource production alone is insufficient. A
supporting ruleset must physically task mobile workers through a source-to-
drop-off loop, construct both capacity and independent production sites, gate
an ordered era transition through stockpile plus buildings, preserve formation-
aware group command and evaluate defeat across recoverable economic and military
production rather than one fixed target.

## Novelty and variants

- Sid Meier's Civilization VI shares generated maps, research, construction,
  fog, counters and era progression, but settles one production target per city
  across turns and does not task live workers through drop-off routes.
- Dota 2 shares continuous group pathing, combat and vision, but lacks worker
  economy, constructed housing, building-local queues and ordered Ages.
- Naval maps, allied trade, Relic victory and campaigns are larger variants
  outside this combination.
