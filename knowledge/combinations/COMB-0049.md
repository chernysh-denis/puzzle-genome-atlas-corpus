## Combination match boundary

Hidden cells or visible numbers alone are insufficient. The contents must be
fixed current state, the clue must give an exact count of one concealed target
class in a declared local neighbourhood, the field must be finite and each
input must resolve before another is accepted. Whole-line run descriptions,
future random contents and approximate local hints fail the match.

## Relation to reveal-risk and truth assertion

Minesweeper's narrower `COMB-0003` adds reveal-only input, zero-region
expansion, terminal hazards and safe-cell completion. Hexcells Infinite instead
asserts one of two fixed classes, immediately rejects mistakes without ending
play and requires every concealed cell to be classified. `COMB-0049` records
the recurring deduction substrate without falsely equating these causal loops.
