## Decision structure

The player follows a bounded repair route while finite ammunition and visible
body layers turn each cutter orientation into a regional target choice.
Accumulated damage may sever that region and remove the movement or attack
capability it carried. Finite Stasis creates a door or claw window; the local
breaker moves fixed power among visible branches. Those decisions enable the
tram, Data Board and shuttle chain, whose Chapter 2 handoff is reload-verified.

## Combination match boundary

Generic locational damage, a severing animation, slow motion, a powered door or
a chapter checkpoint alone is insufficient. A supporting ruleset must expose
regional degradation before detachment, let the player deliberately align a
linear attack, remove a live capability when an attached region is severed,
apply finite targeted slowdown to machinery, reassign a visible limited local
circuit and carry the repair into reload-verifiable successor control.

## Novelty and variants

- Resident Evil 4 (2023) shares over-the-shoulder combat, finite ammunition,
  regional hits and a chapter handoff, but focuses aim, stagger, parry and case
  packing rather than severed-capability state.
- DOOM (2016) shares authored finite-resource combat and visible hostile damage,
  but converts stagger into a close finisher rather than line-oriented regional
  dismemberment and timed maintenance.
- CONTROL shares third-person combat, targeted live effects and fixture gates,
  but its resources regenerate and its route lacks local circuit allocation or
  functional severance.
- Tool, hostile, limb, tissue, tram, claw, branch, room and chapter names remain
  parameters.
