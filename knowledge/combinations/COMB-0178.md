`ACT-319 + ACT-320 + ACT-321 + SYS-556 + SYS-557 + SYS-558 + SYS-559 + CON-471 + CON-472 + CON-473 + INF-227 + OBJ-104 + TIM-003`

This is a strict thirteen-gene subset of the fourteen-gene Microsoft Flight
Simulator 2024 genome. Cockpit-instrument information makes aerodynamic and
engine state legible, but the combination boundary is stated through causal
control, system, route, constraint and terminal genes.

## Decision structure

The player first fixes one aircraft, aeronautical route and conditions, then
must build usable propulsion and cockpit information from a cold fuel/electrical
state. Direct control continuously trades attitude, airspeed, altitude, power,
trim and runway/terrain clearance while avionics report plan deviation without
automating corrections. The unit closes only after safe destination landing,
taxi-in, stop and shutdown produce the Free Flight logbook record.

## Combination match boundary

Generic vehicle travel, aircraft animation or route planning alone is
insufficient. A supporting ruleset must let the player configure one flight,
operate causal power/engine systems, directly control fixed-wing ground and
airborne motion through a readable aerodynamic envelope, follow persistent
avionics plan state and settle arrival through parking shutdown into a durable
flight record.

## Novelty and variants

- Euro Truck Simulator 2 shares continuous direct vehicle control, route
  guidance, fuel and destination settlement, but uses authored roads,
  articulated cargo, traffic law, fatigue and commercial delivery instead of
  lift, attitude, runway operations and aircraft systems.
- Dyson Sphere Program includes manually steered mecha flight and navigation,
  but switches propulsion regimes across planetary/stellar space and does not
  require runway energy management or a parking-to-parking logbook terminal.
- IFR/ATC, airliner, Career, live-weather and multiplayer flights are larger
  variants outside this combination.
