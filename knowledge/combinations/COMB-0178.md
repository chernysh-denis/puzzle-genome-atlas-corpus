## Decision structure

The player first fixes one aircraft, aeronautical route and conditions, then
establishes usable propulsion and cockpit information from the declared cold
state. Direct inputs trade attitude, airspeed, altitude, power, trim and
runway/terrain clearance while live avionics report plan position, leg and
deviation without automating corrections. Independently, the native Free
Flight logbook adds qualifying entries and Sim Update 6 announces an addition.
This authored KBFI-to-KTIW analysis accepts only the declared safe landing,
destination parking, stop and shutdown sequence together with a verified
persistent flight entry and its notification. These are analytical acceptance
requirements, not a demonstrated necessary/sufficient native record trigger;
no exact flight or record result was executed.
