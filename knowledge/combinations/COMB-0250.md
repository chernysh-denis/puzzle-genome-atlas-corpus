## Decision structure

The player navigates one live authored incident, inspects scene evidence and
scrubs fixed reconstructions to causal points. Registered facts change which
timed negotiation responses are available or effective and update a visible
estimate without guaranteeing the future. Each closing response opportunity
changes the same accumulated incident state until the final intervention
settles one actor-outcome branch; the completed chapter then retains the exact
traversed nodes and endpoint while leaving unvisited alternatives locked.

## Combination match boundary

Generic dialogue choices, a detective overlay, a timed prompt, an ending or a
chapter map alone is insufficient. A supporting ruleset must turn inspected
authored evidence, including temporal reconstruction, into the legal or
effective state of a live negotiation; expose current but non-guaranteed
success; close response opportunities in time; resolve one protected-actor
incident; and retain the traversed path in a revisitable branch representation.
