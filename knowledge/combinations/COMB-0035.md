generic current-state visibility or rewind.

## Decision structure

The player allocates members of a finite active follower roster to a selected
world target. Target context determines autonomous work. A transport target
remains stationary until visible summed carrying strength reaches its threshold,
then the assigned followers jointly pathfind it toward the operational base.
The player can recall nearby workers and reallocate them while other followers
continue working in real time; extraction progress credits the target rather
than the carrier population.

## Combination match boundary

A game does not instantiate this combination merely through minions or object
delivery. Followers must be individually or collectively dispatched onto a
contextual target, execute the inferred task autonomously, require a visible
cooperative strength threshold for transport, route the target to an
operational base and remain recallable / reusable under a finite active roster.
Direct object carrying, indivisible squad relocation, finite skill consumption
and carrier-quota rescue fall outside the boundary.
