## Decision structure

The player commits an eligible event, reads course and rival state, directly
controls the assigned car and can rewind a costly local line. Ordered checkpoints
and laps validate the result; the result becomes retained Festival progress and
rewards. Enough progress unlocks the Invitational, whose valid completion—not
the threshold alone—settles the first Wristband.

## Combination match boundary

Direct vehicle physics or a race alone is insufficient. A supporting ruleset
must combine reversible live driving, autonomous race opponents, ordered course
validation, event result settlement, accumulated campaign progress and a
separate terminal event that grants membership. Euro Truck Simulator 2 has
direct vehicle physics but no rivals, Rewind, course ranking or Festival gate;
EA SPORTS FC 26 has live human-versus-AI competition but no vehicle course or
accumulated event threshold.
