This eight-gene set is a proper subset of the supporting eleven-gene The Room
genome. The opening fire trigger, generic fixed concealment and self-paced
timing expose the tutorial but do not define the interaction between
reconfigurable inventory objects, compatible fixtures, persistent unlocks and
the instrument-only final pattern.

## Decision structure

The player directly manipulates visible constrained components, reshapes one
held key for two different fixtures and applies acquired objects to compatible
mechanical targets. Each accepted fixture use exposes a persistent downstream
item or mechanism. The final rings become solvable only after the dependency
chain yields an optical instrument that reveals their otherwise unavailable
alignment trace; completing that trace unlatches the enclosure.

- [`GAME-0085` — The Room](../games/s-z/the-room.md)

## Combination match boundary

A puzzle does not instantiate this combination merely by containing keys,
inventory, hidden objects, rotatable parts or a sequence of locks. At least one
held object must be reconfigurable for distinct compatible fixtures, fixture
activation must persistently expose later mechanism state, and an acquired
inspection instrument must reveal information required to complete the final
staged enclosure objective.
