## Decision structure

The player commits one selectable Wanchu ground region, converts sampled loot
into a match build and uses a finite hook both for vertical rotation and combat
distance. Each melee exchange asks whether an attack is common, Focus,
Clashing or Counterable; a correct Counter can disarm before the next move.
Armour, Health and weapon Durability are repaired under live pressure while
Shadow Corruption removes safe space. The first eligible elimination consumes
the one Rebirth; the next final elimination records placement, or surviving the
remaining field settles first place and Victory.

## Combination match boundary

Battle-royale loot and a shrinking region are insufficient. A supporting
ruleset must couple a player-committed ground spawn, finite targetable hook,
live attack-class Counter relation with possible disarm, repairable weapon
Durability, one pre-cutoff Solo Rebirth and the same match's final placement or
last-survivor result.

## Novelty and variants

- PUBG shares loot, capacity, ranged combat, safe-area contraction and the
  last-Solo objective, but inserts by aircraft and makes first lethal defeat
  terminal instead of using hook-centred melee and one Rebirth.
- Apex Legends shares hero abilities, armour, loot and a contracting Ring, but
  its Core packet is a three-person squad with downing and teammate return.
- Brawlhalla shares freely timed dodge/counter play and weapon transfer, but
  its fixed platform repeatedly spends Stocks without loot, a world map or a
  contracting safe region.
- Other NARAKA heroes, maps, human queues and team modes remain separate
  modules because they change ability, opponent or recovery boundaries.
