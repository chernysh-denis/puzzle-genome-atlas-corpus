## Decision structure

The player accepts one supplied loaded truck and a deadline, customises a GPS
route, directly manages an articulated vehicle through traffic and road law,
schedules a legally sufficient rest, chooses a destination parking treatment
and turns final time, damage and parking into one settled payment and experience
award.

## Combination match boundary

Direct driving, cargo delivery or a timed contract alone is insufficient. A
supporting ruleset must combine employer-supplied vehicle and cargo, player-
edited road navigation, live regulated traffic, articulated damage, a mandatory
driving/rest limit, trailer drop-off and explicit pay/experience settlement.
Mini Metro authors an autonomous line network; Grand Theft Auto V drives in
traffic without a cargo contract, fatigue break or delivery result.
