## Decision structure

The player spends one member of a finite countable population by attaching it
as a node in a continuously force-resolved elastic network. Valid placement
automatically creates type- and range-bounded links. Loose population members
then traverse the resulting live structure toward a fixed extraction zone,
where rescue or hazard accounting determines whether enough remain to satisfy
the quota.

## Combination match boundary

A physics construction game does not instantiate this combination merely
because placed parts deform. Construction nodes must come from the same finite
population later counted at extraction; valid placement must automatically
form a bounded elastic network; loose members must traverse that live network;
and terminal zones must account for extracted versus lost population toward a
quota. Separate construction currency, one-payload delivery or non-physical
route editing falls outside the boundary.
