## Decision structure

The player advances one endpoint of an ordered articulated body on a grid.
Forward motion propagates segments through predecessor positions; reverse input
instead makes the tail advance straight from its current outward direction.
Every command therefore changes both current shape and which future endpoint
poses are reachable. Completion requires the conserved ordered body to match a
declared target footprint after complete discrete propagation.

## Combination match boundary

A permanently head-led snake, a rigid multi-cell object, historical path
rewind, ordinary one-cell navigation or evacuation through an exit does not
instantiate this interaction. The controlled body must preserve segment order,
support propagation from one endpoint, use a distinct tail-directed straight
reverse rule and reconstruct an exact target footprint under discrete
resolution.
