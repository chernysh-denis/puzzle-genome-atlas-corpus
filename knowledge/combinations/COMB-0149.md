## Interaction

Scoutflies establish a target route that the called Seikret can follow while
the player preserves manual deviation and one mounted secondary-weapon swap.
Direct attacks update localized monster body state; Focus Mode exposes an open
wound and a compatible Focus Strike destroys it for damage, control and bonus
material. The target may migrate between connected zones while Palico and
locale conditions continue autonomously. Hunter defeat consumes one finite
faint allowance and returns the attempt to camp; target defeat exposes finite
carves and settles materials, zenny and the next story gate. Those materials
then fund equipment capable of supporting the following authored hunt.
