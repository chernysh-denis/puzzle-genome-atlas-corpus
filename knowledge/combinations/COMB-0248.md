## Decision structure

The player advances through authored third-person geometry that seamlessly
changes climbing and protective posture. A held local overlay exposes current
nearby actors, usable or attackable objects and the objective direction without
revealing the complete future route. The player then preserves concealment for
an eligible close neutralisation or accepts live combat, operates and breaks
environmental gates, and carries the resulting state through checkpoints to a
retained successor location.

## Combination match boundary

Generic third-person traversal, combat, an objective beacon, breakable scenery
or autosave alone is insufficient. A supporting ruleset must combine seamless
contextual cover with invoked bounded local sensing, retain a stealth-or-combat
choice through authored environmental interactions and finish at a reloadable
successor location rather than an arbitrary campsite or collectible.

## Novelty and variants

- STAR WARS Jedi: Fallen Order shares authored traversal, combat, checkpoints
  and successor routing, but its Holomap reachability, guard/Force resources
  and retained traversal ability replace this cover-and-overlay relation.
- Far Cry 3 shares stealth, hostile perception, firearms and a retained story
  state, but its optical marks persist on specific actors and site clearance
  converts services rather than crossing an environmental ascent.
- HITMAN World of Assassination shares a bounded situational overlay, local
  awareness and stealth, but its disguise permissions and two-target scored
  debrief do not form this topology-and-breakage successor route.
- Area, character, weapon, prompt, cover surface, objective and checkpoint
  names remain parameters rather than new combination identities.
