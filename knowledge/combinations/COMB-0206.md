## Decision structure

The carrier keeps moving while the controlled car follows route guidance,
spends nitrous and selects collision angles. Counted hostile wrecks must settle
without losing the truck; each completed quota exposes another catch-up and
approach gate. A fixed set piece transfers direct control into the target car,
which must still be delivered before the mission result persists.

## Combination match boundary

Generic racing, pursuit or vehicle damage is insufficient. A supporting
ruleset must make direct driving, a finite acceleration reserve, deliberate
hostile-vehicle contact, counted takedowns, moving-target stage predicates,
authored control transfer, checkpoint failure and a later retained delivery
jointly causal inside one bounded mission.

## Novelty and variants

- Need for Speed Unbound shares driving, event commitment and persistent Story
  settlement, but earns technique Burst, ranks a race and delays exposed cash
  behind police evasion rather than wrecking fixed Enforcers around a carrier.
- Grand Theft Auto V has authored vehicle missions and protagonist switching,
  but its reviewed signature does not require this quota/carrier/handoff chain.
- Forza Horizon 6 validates ordered competitive events; it has no counted
  hostile takedowns or theft-to-airfield terminal in the scoped opening.
- Another Payback Race, Runner job or Speedcross episode changes the packet and
  requires separate analysis.
