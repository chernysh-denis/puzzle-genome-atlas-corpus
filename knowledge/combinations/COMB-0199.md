## Decision structure

The player cases one bank with a stock AI crew, commits the mask and alarm,
turns one civilian into a spatial hostage resource and installs the thermal
drill. Police response continues through lower- and higher-pressure phases while
the crew protects access, repairs jams and can cross downing into custody and a
hostage-mediated return. Opening the vault does not finish the contract: one
cash source must become a heavy bag, cross the map into the van's secure region
and then be converted by crew escape into retained payout.
