## Decision structure

A directly controlled character combines an active Skill Gem, socketed
Supports and connected passive choices, spends charged recovery and timed
directional dodge during continuous combat, returns through checkpoint-reset
failure, and defeats an authored guardian to cross the next-act threshold.

## Analysed games

- [`GAME-0162` — Path of Exile 2](../games/m-r/path-of-exile-2.md)

## Combination match boundary

An action RPG build alone is insufficient: the ruleset must make active skills
itemised through Gems and socketed Supports, tie progression to connected
passives, recover flask charges through play, reset the current campaign area
after ordinary death and end the bounded route at an authored act guardian.

Elden Ring shares checkpoint combat and a route guardian but not Skill/Support
Gem composition or local area reset. A loot game without connected passives or
checkpoint-reset failure does not satisfy the same causal contract.
