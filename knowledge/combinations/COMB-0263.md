## Decision structure

A finite authored round index exposes progress toward the declared final
round, not every future enemy type/count/spacing. Initial entries follow the
round's recorded release control; manual start and enabled AutoStart are
distinct configurations still to verify for this build. A resolved round is
not by itself an automatic next release, and transiently empty space does not
prove all scheduled arrivals and local descendants are resolved. The route
fixes each purchased defender's position for this attempt, with reach shown
before commitment; this does not claim the whole product lacks sale or
relocation. Defenders acquire and attack autonomously under an editable
target-selection rule. Damage resolves an enemy type's intrinsic local
layers/descendants, not the next authored wave; exact child offsets, damage
carryover and intermediate frames remain unverified. Destroyed layers credit
the shared spendable budget alongside accepted flat round-completion credit,
without asserting affordability legality. Escapes debit the shared stock; a
conceded leak remains a priceable cost only while stock stays above zero.
Parallel upgrade paths retain asymmetric caps. Success requires clearing the
declared final Easy round 40 with stock remaining, not merely reaching its
index. No native run is claimed.

## Combination match boundary

Placed defenders, splitting enemies, a round counter or stock alone is
insufficient. Preserve route-fixed prospective reach, editable target rules
with autonomous engagement, intrinsic local target descendants, pop-to-budget
conversion plus allowed flat round credit, leak-stock debit, asymmetric
upgrades and survival through the final authored round. Initial scheduled
arrivals and damaged-parent descendants must remain distinct. Record release
control separately from round resolution; do not infer automatic next release
from an empty track or disclose all future compositions by implication. No
exact native child offsets/carryover, affordability gate or product-wide
sale/relocation absence is certified.

## Novelty and variants

- Factorio shares automatic acquisition by placed entities and static footprint
  legality, but its placements serve a production layout, its hostile pressure
  is generated by the player's own output and its entities are freely removable.
- Bad North: Jotunn Edition shares autonomous engagement under real-time
  pressure, but its defenders are directly commanded units that move, and its
  completion predicate is neutralising a finite assault force.
- Dota 2 shares a priced purchase and autonomous structures that acquire nearby
  hostiles, but the player places none of those structures and its waves are
  symmetric.
- Map, tower, hostile, upgrade, currency and exact quantity names remain
  parameters.
