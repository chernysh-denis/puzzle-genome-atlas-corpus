## Decision structure

The threat sequence is authored and indexed by round, so the player always knows
how much remains. The defence is committed spatially and irreversibly: a
position fixes which stretch of the route a defender can act on, and the drawn
reach makes that intersection visible before commitment. Each defender then acts
on its own, under a selection rule the player may edit but an engagement the
player never commands. Hostiles are nested populations that shed one layer at a
time and release their declared children in place, so every layer destroyed both
credits the budget the attempt spends and reduces what that hostile's escape
would cost. Escapes debit the shared stock proportionally rather than failing the
round, which makes a conceded leak a priceable decision. Upgrades are bought from
that same destruction-funded budget along parallel paths whose asymmetric caps
force each defender into one specialisation, and the attempt is completed by
carrying the stock to the schedule's declared final round.

## Combination match boundary

Placed defences, splitting enemies, a wave counter or a lives meter alone is
insufficient. A supporting ruleset must fix each defender's position for the
attempt against a route known in advance, expose that defender's reach before
commitment, let the player edit its target-selection rule without commanding its
engagements, compose hostiles as ordered layers that release declared children as
they are destroyed, credit the spendable budget from those destroyed layers,
debit a shared stock in proportion to the layers an escaping hostile still
carried, gate upgrades behind asymmetric parallel path caps, and complete by
carrying that stock to the final round of a finite authored schedule.

## Novelty and variants

- Factorio shares automatic acquisition by placed entities and static footprint
  legality, but its placements serve a production layout, its hostile pressure
  is generated by the player's own output and its entities are freely removable.
- Bad North: Jotunn Edition shares autonomous engagement under real-time
  pressure, but its defenders are directly commanded units that move, and its
  completion predicate is neutralising a finite assault force.
- Dota 2 shares a priced purchase and autonomous structures that acquire nearby
  hostiles, but the player places none of those structures and its waves are
  symmetric.
- Map, tower, hostile, upgrade, currency and exact quantity names remain
  parameters.
