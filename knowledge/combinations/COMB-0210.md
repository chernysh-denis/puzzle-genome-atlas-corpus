## Combination gene set

`ACT-008 + ACT-048 + ACT-161 + SYS-146 + SYS-215 + SYS-339 + SYS-705 + SYS-706 + CON-305 + CON-556 + INF-115 + INF-271 + OBJ-026 + TIM-003`

This is a strict fourteen-gene subset of the twenty-four-gene Half-Life 2
genome. Weapon switching, reload, resource collection, authored interactions,
body-state resolution, checkpoint restoration and general HUD state remain
outside the distinctive prop-to-route-to-weapon chain.

## Decision structure

Gordon traverses an authored live route while locally perceiving headcrabs and
zombies. The Gravity Gun accepts only a compatible physics target, pulls it
into a collision-bounded held pose and returns it to world simulation on drop
or launch. A launched prop then follows ballistic collisions and may damage a
hostile. The same physical object can therefore alter line, cover, trap and
ammunition decisions before Gordon reaches the next chapter transition.

## Combination match boundary

Generic first-person combat or generic rigid-object carrying is insufficient.
A supporting ruleset must admit direct live traversal, an eligible remotely
manipulated world prop, collision-bounded holding, a launch back into ordinary
physics, attributed prop-to-hostile damage, local hostile pursuit and an
authored reachable route terminal.

## Novelty and variants

- Portal shares first-person movement, rigid-object carrying and authored
  physical spaces, but paired portals and chamber receivers dominate its
  objective while carried cubes are not ordinary hostile-damaging ammunition.
- Left 4 Dead 2 shares local zombie combat and authored chapter progression,
  but adds bots, Director variation, incapacitation/rescue and collective
  safe-room closure without remote prop manipulation.
- Cyberpunk 2077 shares first-person combat, loot and checkpoints, but its
  scanning, quickhacks and character build replace collision-bounded physical
  prop reclassification.
