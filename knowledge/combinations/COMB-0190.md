## Decision structure

One directly controlled Survivor traverses an authored hotel route with three
autonomous same-authority partners. Direct combat, finite carried supplies,
friendly-fire risk and interruptible healing or revival continue while the
Director samples team intensity and populates unseen reachable space. A Special
Infected may remove one Survivor's self-authority until another body intervenes.
The elevator commits a separate authored panic population. Success requires
every still-living party member to reach the final checkpoint and a player to
close its door, preserving state into the next map.
