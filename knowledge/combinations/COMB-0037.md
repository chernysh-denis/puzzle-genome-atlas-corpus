## Combination gene set

- System Behaviour: `SYS-031`
- Constraints: `CON-050`, `CON-051`
- Information: `INF-001`

This four-gene set is a proper subset of both supporting genomes. It captures
automatic passenger service under visible capacity and destination identity
without requiring real-time demand, an editable network, a fixed authored
route, one-seat capacity, a finite delivery objective or one-shot execution.

## Decision structure

The player configures a route or network whose vehicles resolve passenger
boarding, carrying and alighting automatically. Current demand, capacity and
destination types are visible. A full vehicle leaves otherwise eligible demand
waiting, and a passenger completes only at a destination matching its declared
type, so topology must be planned together with service order and capacity.

## Combination match boundary

A game does not instantiate this combination merely through vehicles or
delivery. Passengers must board and alight automatically along a configured
route; capacity must reject or defer otherwise eligible pickup; destination
type must determine completion; and those current states must be inspectable
before the relevant service decision. Direct vehicle steering, untyped payload
delivery, unlimited capacity and hidden destination identity fall outside the
boundary.
