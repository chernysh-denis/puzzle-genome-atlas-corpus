## Decision structure

The player composes cyclic shifts of eligible rows and columns. Each primitive
shift is exactly reversible, every length-three move preserves outer-tile
parity, and the reachable arrangement is accepted only when fixed-orientation
ports satisfy stationary shared-edge barriers and form one connected acyclic
network over every cell.

## Combination match boundary

A puzzle does not instantiate this combination merely by sliding rows,
permuting pieces, matching ports or building a tree. Reversible cyclic line
actions must operate inside a proper invariant-constrained state component,
and their fixed-orientation result must simultaneously satisfy reciprocal
shared edges, spanning connectivity and acyclicity.
