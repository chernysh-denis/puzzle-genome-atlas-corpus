## Decision structure

The player moves and orients a finite reusable inventory of physical devices
around a finite released troop. Soldiers walk autonomously; contact geometry
redirects them, and force-driven devices can launch or suspend them. A paused
projection previews the current route. When an outcome fails, the player may
rewind the live world, change a device and resume a different temporal branch
until enough soldiers reach the exit.

## Combination match boundary

A Lemmings-like rescue game does not instantiate this combination merely by
having autonomous walkers or undo. Routing must use separate finite reusable
physical devices whose geometry redirects contact; the live simulation must
include force-driven motion and terminal population accounting; prospective
route information must be available; and rewind must restore prior world state
for a branchable alternate continuation. Per-agent skills, symbolic command
markers and full-level restart fall outside the boundary.
