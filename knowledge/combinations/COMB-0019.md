## Decision structure

The player relocates one selected peg across an occupied neighbour into an
empty hole. Automatic intervening removal strictly reduces material, reverse
growth is unavailable, and play continues through discrete resolved jumps
until either the exact target configuration is reconstructed or legal jumps
are exhausted.

## Combination match boundary

A jumping or capture game does not instantiate this combination merely because
one piece passes over another. The jumped element must be removed automatically,
every forward action must irreversibly reduce material, no-jump exhaustion must
end play, and the process must pursue a declared exact configuration.
