# COMB-0274 — Break stability into sequential executions with charged in-place continuation

- Claim status: `Pattern`
- Evidence quality: `Direct`
- Confidence: `High`
- Corpus role: verified interaction pattern
- Novelty assessment: no exact duplicate among 273 prior combinations; this is
  the first portable relation in which health changes recovery of a distinct
  breakable stability state, each break can be converted into one of several
  required executions, and the player's lethal defeat opens a charge-and-lock
  governed continuation in the same encounter before checkpoint loss.

This is a strict 15-gene subset of the 38-gene Sekiro genome. Traversal,
stealth, grapple, broad combat, personal display, Perilous Attack categories,
true-death loss, route gates and real-time scheduling remain outside because
they can vary independently of the break–execution–continuation interaction.

## Decision structure

The player reads an opponent's recoverable stability state, uses timed defence
and continued pressure to drive it across its threshold, then must reach and
commit the exposed contextual execution before the opportunity closes. That
execution consumes one displayed defeat requirement; if another remains, the
same hostile returns to live combat and the cycle repeats. Lethal defeat of the
player creates a second decision layer: spend a ready in-place recovery charge
only when the post-use lock permits it, continue the same encounter, then earn
additional charge and clear the separate lock through combat progress before
relying on another recovery. The bounded route succeeds only after the
guardian's last execution requirement is consumed.

## Analysed games

## Combination match boundary

A generic stagger, parry, boss phase, revive or checkpoint system is
insufficient. A supporting ruleset must expose a breakable state distinct from
health, make health affect that state's recovery, convert a temporary break
into a player-commanded execution, require multiple such executions for one
protected hostile, expose their remaining count, and allow a charged
same-encounter recovery whose legality also depends on a post-use lock,
replenish additional charge through performance and clear the lock through
qualifying combat progress. Actor, weapon, marker icon, charge count, health
bands and guardian identity are parameters.

## Novelty and variants

- Sekiro presents the stability state as Posture, the critical execution as a
  Deathblow and the in-place recovery as Resurrection.
- A single stagger-to-finisher loop omits the sequential defeat requirement.
- An automatic extra life omits the post-defeat player command and the separate
  charge-plus-lock predicate and their independently earned readiness states.
- A health-threshold boss phase omits the independently recoverable stability
  state and player-commanded execution.
