The 17-gene set is a strict subset of Hollow Knight: Silksong's 22-gene
genome. It omits ordinary Needle attack, purchase, generic ability gate and
basic equipment or resource HUD records while retaining the route-defining
Silk, Crest, Bench, Cocoon and Act-gate coupling.

## Interaction

Needle combat fills one bounded Silk spool that can be committed either to a
full-spool Bind or to a learned route or combat Skill. Resting at a Bench fixes
the return point and restores eligible state while also repopulating ordinary
enemies. Death retains acquired route progress but places spendable Rosaries in
one recoverable Cocoon and temporarily caps Silk; another death before recovery
replaces that Cocoon and makes its stored loss permanent. Acquired dash, glide,
wall and Needolin capabilities expand compatible authored edges, while the
selected Crest changes attack geometry, Bind aftermath and legal coloured Tool
slots. Five retained regional shrine states and Needolin therefore compose a
Grand Gate predicate whose guardian must be defeated before Act 2 entry.
