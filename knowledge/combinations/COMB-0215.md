# COMB-0215 — Bounded multi-lap victory into retained career reward

- Claim status: `Observation`
- Evidence quality: `Corroborated`
- Confidence: `High`
- Corpus role: verified interaction pattern
- Novelty assessment: no exact combination-set duplicate among 214 registered
  combinations; it joins one disclosed finite race, a live rival field,
  ordered multi-lap victory and retained career reward without later tuning,
  pursuit, boost or campaign-wide progression.

## Combination gene set

`ACT-290 + ACT-293 + SYS-320 + SYS-515 + SYS-516 + SYS-519 + CON-438 + INF-205 + INF-206 + INF-208 + OBJ-134 + TIM-003`

This is a strict twelve-gene subset of the fourteen-gene Need for Speed
Underground genome and the sixteen-gene Project Gotham Racing 2 genome.
Driving-profile commitment and generic speed/route guidance remain outside
the distinctive event-to-career interaction; PGR 2's timed Kudos Stash and
visible Bank are also outside this shared set.

## Decision structure

One eligible car enters a disclosed multi-lap street race against autonomous
rivals. Steering and braking interact with continuous car motion, contact and
rival pace while the player reads place and lap progress. Only a valid
first-place finish satisfies the shared objective and transfers the declared
reward and event completion to a retained career state. For Underground that
reward is Easy `375` Bank; for PGR 2 it is the selected-difficulty medal and
completion Kudos, while separate in-race Style Kudos are not required for
this combination.

## Combination match boundary

Generic car control, a finish line or a cash reward alone is insufficient. A
  supporting ruleset must disclose and commit one bounded driving event, run a
  competitive rival field, validate its ordered multi-lap result, require the
  winning place and convert that result into both retained reward and career
  completion state. How the particular game names or measures its reward is
  a parameter; an optional style-score layer is not part of the set.

## Novelty and variants

- Need for Speed Unbound shares event commitment, rivals, ordered result and
  persistent cash, but its scoped result creates Heat and exposed earnings
  that remain unsettled until a required police escape and garage return.
- Forza Horizon 6 shares the ordinary race foundation but couples several
  opening events to Festival points, a Wristband gate and car grants.
- Trackmania retains a valid elapsed result and fixed medal class without an
  admitted rival field, first-place requirement or Bank reward.
- A later Underground Drag, Drift, tournament or tuning packet changes the
  admitted actions and result authority and requires a separate scope.
- PGR 2 adds a timed live Kudos Stash/Bank/Combo surface, but its first Duomo 2
  Street Race still preserves this finite rival-win-to-career core.
