This six-gene set is a proper subset of the supporting genome. Direct avatar
navigation, fixed capacity, static barriers and full visibility remain outside
the combination.

## Decision structure

The avatar grabs one designated rigid object and keeps it in an occupied cell
in front. Route choice must reserve the object's swept corner while turning and
must satisfy a stricter carried-object relation at each elevation transition.
The terminal route puts that specific object into a fixed fire receiver, where
the system consumes it and completes the puzzle after discrete resolution.

## Combination match boundary

Ordinary Sokoban pushing, a permanent attached tool, carrying a weight onto a
reversible button, indirect physics delivery or walking to a goal alone does
not instantiate this interaction. The object must be detachably front-carried,
occupy turning and climbing geometry, and be destroyed by the required fixed
receiver as the bounded success condition.
