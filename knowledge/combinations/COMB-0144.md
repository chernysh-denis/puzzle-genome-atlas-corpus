## Interaction

Initial lifepath and attribute allocation establish one persistent protagonist
whose later contextual dialogue, legal perks and quest opportunities depend on
retained state. Scanning exposes a partial hostile model and legal quickhacks;
RAM, target access, upload timing, queues and tracing bind those informational
actions to live stealth or combat. Cyberware slots and capacity transform the
same build into armour, attuned modifiers and active capabilities. Detection
can turn a stealth approach into direct combat, while XP, activity skills and
level scaling reshape later opposition and loot. Authored
responses and ordered main jobs retain their consequences until a bounded
terminal choice fixes the first completed ending; saves and checkpoints permit
the player to branch again from an earlier state.
