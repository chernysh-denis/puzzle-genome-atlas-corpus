## Decision structure

Authored conversations identify two required leads. Witcher Senses turn a
bounded search area into inspected clues and a followable trail; the resulting
knowledge and gathered Buckthorn enable taught preparation. Prepared direct
combat settles the griffin's defeat but not the quest: the trophy must be
looted and handed to the captain before the declared reward response closes the
contract and returns retained control.

## Combination match boundary

A generic detective search, boss fight or reward screen alone is insufficient.
A supporting ruleset must couple authored informant leads to focused local clue
inspection, make that investigation causally select or unlock preparation for
one real-time hunt, and require a retained physical result to be handed in at a
system-authored quest terminal.

## Novelty and variants

- Monster Hunter Wilds shares preparation, monster combat and hunt settlement,
  but its assignment does not require an authored forensic clue chain and
  external trophy hand-in.
- S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2 uses an active detector to locate hazardous field phenomena,
  not to convert ordered authored corpse/trail clues into bestiary preparation.
- Cyberpunk 2077 shares dialogue and retained quest state, but scanning and
  combat do not form this exact monster-contract dependency.
- Other Witcher contracts with different evidence, target or settlement remain
  variants requiring their own bounded packet.
