## Decision structure

The player edits a running network that extracts recurring shapes, transports
them through fixed geometric operations and feeds exact outputs into a central
receiver. Completing one disclosed quota unlocks another operation or scale
tool, so the same live factory-building loop advances through a finite final
qualification.

## Combination match boundary

A generic factory is insufficient. Inputs must recur without depletion, placed
machines must visibly transform structured geometric shapes, and exact-output
delivery quotas must unlock the next factory capabilities in a finite ordered
qualification. Factorio transports and transforms items but its scoped rocket
path depends on selected recipes, power, finite extraction, pollution and
combat rather than exact shape schemas and Vortex quotas.
