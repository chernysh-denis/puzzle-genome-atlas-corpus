## Decision structure

The player cycles the multiplicity of one permitted nearest-vertex edge within
a fixed bound. Every chosen unit contributes to the exact incident degree of
both endpoint vertices. Positive horizontal and vertical corridors exclude
each other where they geometrically cross, and the complete positive-edge graph
must span every required vertex in one connected component. Satisfying local
degrees or avoiding crossings alone is insufficient.

## Combination match boundary

A graph puzzle does not instantiate this combination merely by joining visible
vertices. It must combine bounded parallel-edge multiplicity, exact weighted
degrees, geometric corridor exclusion and one spanning connected component.
Slant shares exact incident degree but prohibits cycles and has no parallel
edge multiplicity or connectivity objective. Flow Free shares non-crossing
space but constructs labelled simple paths rather than a weighted multigraph.
