## Decision structure

The player freely changes observation focus within a bounded representation of
one fixed event, selecting people or details for closer inspection. The event's
people, objects, poses and represented time cannot be manipulated or advanced,
and no clock changes them while the player reasons. Progress therefore depends
on choosing useful viewpoints or hotspots and interpreting preserved evidence,
not experimenting on a mutable past.

## Combination match boundary

A static puzzle board or read-only text does not instantiate this interaction
merely because it can be viewed without a timer. The subject must represent a
bounded preserved event, permit player-directed focus among its people or
details and forbid manipulation or temporal continuation of the represented
evidence. Three-dimensional camera movement and two-dimensional pointer
hotspots are parameters; reveal of concealed truth, mutable simulation and
direct evidence-object editing fall outside the boundary.
