## Decision structure

The player selects one visible one-use movement card and a cardinal direction.
The system consumes that identity, resolves its declared ground travel through
the fixed heightfield and accepts another choice only after the ball settles.
Success requires ordering the finite cards and directions so the resulting
staged trajectory enters the authored hole exactly.

## Combination match boundary

A spatial game does not instantiate this interaction merely because it has
cards or a ball. Commands must be selected from a non-renewing finite hand,
each card and spatial target must determine one fully resolved staged
trajectory, and the complete sequence must deliver the directly commanded ball
into one fixed hole. Renewable tactical hands, freely available navigation,
continuous force steering and non-spatial card evaluation fall outside the
boundary.
