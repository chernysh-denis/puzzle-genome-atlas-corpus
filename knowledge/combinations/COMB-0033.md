## Decision structure

The player chooses two replaceable endpoints under surface-placement limits.
Together they expose a live remote view and create a bidirectional aperture
mapping. Because continuous body velocity is reoriented rather than discarded,
the player can deliberately build speed under gravity, inspect the exit frame
and convert that speed into otherwise impossible traversal while time runs.

## Combination match boundary

A game does not instantiate this combination merely by containing teleporters
or momentum. The player must place the two channel-limited endpoints on
eligible surfaces; the paired apertures must show their current remote scene,
support bidirectional body traversal and reorient incoming live-physics
velocity into the exit frame. Fixed warps, abstract route edges, solid ramps
and zero-velocity portals fail the boundary.
