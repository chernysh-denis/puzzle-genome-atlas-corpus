## Interaction

A seed fixes one finite mutable tile world. The character locally converts its
terrain into item drops, placements, crafting stations, furnished safe-walled
rooms and a combat arena. Immediate recipes, equipment, permanent health gains
and bounded healing determine readiness while the day-night clock changes local
threats. Valid housing translates currency, health, inventory and boss flags
into eligible town-NPC arrivals. Classic death preserves the constructed world
and carried build but forfeits half the coins. At night one legal Eye enters,
transforms below its health threshold and escapes at dawn unless defeated. Its
first defeat unlocks Dryad eligibility; vacant valid housing converts that flag
into her actual arrival and completes the bounded objective.
