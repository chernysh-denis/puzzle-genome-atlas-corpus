anomaly phase, follows a detector signal until an otherwise invisible artifact

## Combination match boundary

An environmental hazard, hidden collectible or quest choice alone is
insufficient. A supporting ruleset must combine probe-cycled hazard timing,
proximity-driven manifestation of an invisible object, live embodied survival
pressure, an authored item exchange and a mutually exclusive investigation
hand-in. Cyberpunk 2077 has retained quest choices but no bolt-cycled anomalies
or detector-manifested artifacts; Project Zomboid has survival pressure but no
equivalent hidden-object and authored-recipient chain.
