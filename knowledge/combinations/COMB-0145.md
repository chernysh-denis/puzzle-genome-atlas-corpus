The 24-gene set is a strict subset of Marvel Rivals' 25-gene genome. It omits
optional live team communication while retaining the complete hero selection,
Team-Up, ability/ultimate, knockout/respawn, mutable-geometry and ordered
Convergence objective loop.

## Interaction

One player selects an available hero and one Team-Up option in spawn. The
selected hero's base effect is stable, while a designated allied partner gates
its enhancement; knockout returns selection authority after a timer instead of
ending the match. Direct movement, aim and typed abilities trade health,
healing, control and ultimate energy in continuous time. Those fights matter
through objective presence: uncontested attackers first capture a mission area,
then advance a fixed-route vehicle whose speed, stopping, checkpoint floor and
reversal depend on nearby teams. Destructible non-essential geometry changes
cover and approach paths without deleting objective anchors. At clock expiry,
legal objective pressure alone sustains overtime until route completion awards
attack or cleared pressure awards defence.
