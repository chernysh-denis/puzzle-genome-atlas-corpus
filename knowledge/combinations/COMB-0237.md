This is a strict sixteen-gene subset of the twenty-seven-gene Half-Life (1998)
genome. Weapon selection/reload, pickups, hostile pursuit, save replacement,
ammunition constraints and general local/equipment information remain outside
the distinctive relation.

## Decision structure

The player traverses one damaged authored facility route while live combat and
personal health/protection continue. A contextual command toggles an eligible
world ally between following and waiting; once nearby, that actor's role can
perform an otherwise unavailable local assistance transition. The player also
chooses when to hold position at a finite restoration fixture, when to expose a
separately draining but automatically recharging personal light field and when
to damage an eligible world object until its collision is removed. These actor,
resource and topology changes must resolve in authored order before the fixed
successor location becomes reachable.

## Combination match boundary

Generic first-person combat, an automatic follower, a healing pickup, a torch
or a breakable prop alone is insufficient. A supporting ruleset must combine a
player-reversible transient follow relation whose actor supplies role-specific
local help, sustained transfer from finite world stock, toggled personal light
with automatic reserve return, damage-opened world topology and an authored
route terminal.

## Novelty and variants

- Half-Life 2 shares authored first-person combat, protection/health and fixed
  chapter routing, but its selected Ravenholm relation replaces the transient
  ally and finite facility resources with remote rigid-prop pull, hold and
  launch.
- Max Payne (2001) shares live firearm pressure, authored fixtures, recoverable
  failure and a chapter successor, but its restoration is carried and delayed,
  its tempo resource is Bullet Time and no world ally role opens the route.
- Left 4 Dead 2 has autonomous allies, finite recovery and an authored safe-room
  terminal, but does not let one player toggle a transient role-capable world
  actor to resolve a scanner-like gate or recharge a personal light off-state.
