# COMB-0231 — Redeemed survival checklist into guided service route

- Claim status: `Observation`
- Evidence quality: `Corroborated`
- Confidence: `High`
- Corpus role: verified interaction pattern
- Novelty assessment: no exact combination-set duplicate among 230 registered
  combinations; it joins material harvesting, personal crafting, individually
  claimed challenge rows and an all-row destination reward with a spatial
  service terminal.

## Combination gene set

`ACT-008 + ACT-122 + ACT-123 + ACT-402 + SYS-328 + SYS-741 + CON-297 + CON-496 + CON-573 + INF-125 + INF-132 + INF-282 + OBJ-145`

This is a strict thirteen-gene subset of the twenty-eight-gene 7 Days to Die
genome.
Hotbar selection, equipment, survival pressure, incidental combat, finite
source depletion, carrying limits and real-time scheduling remain outside the
distinctive checklist-to-destination relation.

## Decision structure

The player harvests explicit resource counts and routes them through known
personal recipes and one required equipment state. Each satisfied checklist row
still needs its own claim. Only the completely redeemed group issues a marked
world-service destination; walking to that spatial objective settles a second
reward and supplies the packet's positive terminal.

## Analysed games

- [`GAME-0233` — 7 Days to Die](../games/0-9/7-days-to-die.md)

## Combination match boundary

Generic crafting, a tutorial checklist or a waypoint is insufficient. A
supporting ruleset must expose and record multiple resource/action predicates,
require separate reward claims, turn the fully redeemed group into a new
world-destination quest and settle that spatial route into explicit value.

## Novelty and variants

- No Man's Sky also teaches harvesting and personal crafting before a marked
  destination, but its strict staged repair chain never composes separately
  claimed challenge rows into that route.
- World of Warcraft and Once Human close authored tutorials into retained
  progression, but neither admitted packet uses a material checklist whose
  group reward creates a nearest-service objective.
- Minecraft, Rust and Project Zomboid share harvesting and personal crafting,
  but their scoped terminals do not arise from redeeming a tutorial group.
