## Decision structure

The player places and configures compatible entities, supplies their recipes
through constructed logistics and power, and expands research and finite
extraction until a reusable live production network can fulfil the terminal
manufacturing dependency.

## Analysed games

- [`GAME-0119` — Factorio](../games/a-f/factorio.md)

## Combination match boundary

A generic crafting or construction game is insufficient. Production must
repeat autonomously in placed entities, material transport and power must
remain live network constraints, and research plus finite extraction must feed
the same constructed path to an explicit manufacturing objective.
