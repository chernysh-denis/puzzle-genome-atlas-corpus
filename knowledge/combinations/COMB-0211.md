## Decision structure

Cal crosses Bogano in real time, reads creature attacks and chooses attack,
sustained block, timed parry, dodge, Stim or Force Slow. Blocking spends finite
stability and a mistimed defence can expose him; successful lightsaber hits
restore Force for later combat or traversal use. Meditation restores health,
Force and Stims but returns ordinary enemies. The retained wall-run memory then
changes which map-coloured edges are usable and admits the Ancient Vault.

## Combination match boundary

Generic action combat or generic checkpoint exploration is insufficient. A
supporting ruleset must combine live melee, persistent guard plus timed defence,
attack-fed ability energy, a recovery checkpoint that respawns field enemies,
one retained traversal capability, visible route-edge reachability and an
authored objective transition.

## Novelty and variants

- Elden Ring shares guarding, dodge/parry timing, Grace restoration, field-
  enemy reset and capability-sensitive routing, but uses rune recovery,
  equipment load, attributes, Torrent and a boss gate instead of BD-1 Holomap
  colours and attack-restored Force.
- Black Myth: Wukong shares real-time melee, active abilities, finite healing,
  Shrine reset and checkpoint death return, but converts timing into Focus,
  permits temporary transformation and settles a boss chapter rather than
  relearning a traversal edge on the way to one Vault objective.
- Tomb Raider shares authored third-person traversal and environmental route
  reading but lacks the admitted meditation/respawn and Force-resource loop.
