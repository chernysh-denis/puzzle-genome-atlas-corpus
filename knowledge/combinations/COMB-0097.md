## Decision structure

The player first arms the lesson by collecting the blue cube. Keeping the
ordinary-looking doorway in view preserves its original room. Looking through
the glass window makes the doorway unobserved, permitting the system to replace
only its hidden remote destination with a second authored room. Returning to
and crossing the same local threshold then completes the changed-room route.

## Combination match boundary

An ordinary teleport, shuffled room, visible portal or cosmetic off-screen
swap is insufficient. The player must intentionally control visibility of a
stable local threshold, observation must gate an authored remote-destination
replacement, that destination must remain undisclosed until reveal or
traversal, and crossing it must reach the designated new location.
