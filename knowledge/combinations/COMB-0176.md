underwater route, gathers finite resources, catches mobile fauna and converts
world fragments into persistent blueprint counts. Known recipes and materials
produce a powered Builder, Mobile Vehicle Bay and first Seamoth; legal habitat

keeps the dry interior breathable. The energy-bearing Seamoth supplies mobile
oxygen but remains bounded by damage and an unmodified 200 m crush depth. The
verified terminal parks it outside the new habitat and ends with the survivor
