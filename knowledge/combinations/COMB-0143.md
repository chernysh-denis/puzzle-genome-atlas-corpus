The 37-gene set is a strict subset of Grand Theft Auto V's 48-gene genome. It
omits generic shopping, weapon selection, reload, thrown explosives, automatic
pickup, activity-based stat growth, ammunition capacity and their supporting
equipment or economy displays while retaining the complete multi-protagonist,
wanted-world, mission, heist and terminal-branch structure.

## Interaction

A live populated world connects direct movement, vehicle control and firearm
combat to civilian witnesses and escalating police response. GPS and wanted
search information guide a route the player still executes directly. Three
authored protagonists persist separately in that world; legal switching
transfers control and each character can spend a distinct special meter. The
mission graph restricts which protagonist, asset and operation is currently
viable and restores bounded checkpoint state after declared failure. At major
heists, approach and complete specialist-role selection determine preparation,
execution mistakes and net take. Completing The Big Score exposes one exclusive
terminal choice, whose mission fixes the surviving controllable roster and
Story Mode completion state.
