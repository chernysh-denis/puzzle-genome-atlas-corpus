## Decision structure

The player inserts destination and interaction commands for two actors with
complementary permissions on one random-access timeline. Automatic locomotion
and perception-responsive robots resolve deterministically. Exact future
inspection exposes capture or blockage; rewind restores an earlier state where
the command schedule can be revised until every required actor reaches an exit.

## Combination match boundary

A rewind puzzle does not instantiate this combination merely by restoring a
past world state. It must support timestamp-addressed command editing for
multiple automatically moving controlled actors, complementary actor
permissions, directed hostile perception with pursuit or diversion, exact
future-state inspection and full required-actor evacuation. Live physical
routing, discrete undo and a locked machine run fall outside the boundary.
