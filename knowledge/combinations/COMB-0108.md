This is a proper subset of the ten-gene Cocoon genome. Direct avatar movement,
complete local visibility and self-paced sequencing support the packet but do
not define the causal change from portable world to entered world and then to
carried traversal ability.

## Decision structure

The player carries one persistent world orb to an eligible jump pedestal and
mounts it, making that same orb's contained world enterable at a fixed arrival
point. After returning outside, the player retrieves the unchanged orb and
carries its unlocked ability to a compatible authored locus. The system then
manifests an otherwise absent bridge, allowing the avatar to cross toward the
next designated location; setting the orb down removes that ability condition.

## Analysed games

- [`GAME-0108` — Cocoon](../games/a-f/cocoon.md)

## Combination match boundary

A portable key, fixed portal or permanent avatar upgrade is insufficient. A
match must preserve one object as both a movable world representation and the
identity of its enterable contained world, require compatible pedestal mounting
for entry, and later make a world-specific spatial effect depend on carrying
that exact unlocked object at a receptive locus.
