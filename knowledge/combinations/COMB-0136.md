## Interaction

Five committed heroes convert last hits and proximity into levels, build points
and assembled items while scheduled lane waves reveal and contest space. Death,
respawn, buyback and courier logistics price each lost fight; vision determines
which fights are actionable, and ordered structure loss turns barracks pressure
into a legally exposed Ancient and terminal team victory.
