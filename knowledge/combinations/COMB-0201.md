## Decision structure

The date fixes one shared generated island while expedition loot remains
sampled. Every upward reach weighs a readable surface and rest against stamina
that hunger, injury, hazards and carried weight can obstruct. Food and remedies
restore typed parts of capacity; ropes and pitons consume finite stock to leave
persistent route affordances. Ordered biome hazards and campfires move the Scout
toward PEAK. Zero capacity may become terminal solo death; a living summit Scout
must still ignite a Flare before the helicopter and Scouting Report settle rescue.

## Combination match boundary

Physics climbing or a survival inventory alone is insufficient. A supporting
ruleset must couple civil-time-identified generated terrain, freely targeted
stamina-priced grip, multiple status/weight effects on that capacity, finite
persistent climbing aids, ordered environmental ascent and a carried signal
whose eligible use explicitly invokes the positive rescue result.

## Novelty and variants

- Human: Fall Flat shares body physics and gripping but has fixed authored
  levels, no stamina/status economy and no carried rescue signal.
- Valheim shares a generated survival world, food, stamina and inventory but
  builds persistent infrastructure and settles a boss trophy rather than one
  daily free-grip route.
- Don't Starve Together shares sampled supplies and survival afflictions but its
  top-down gathering/crafting winter terminal has no surface-grip climb.
- Co-op PEAK, higher Ascents and Nadir remain separate modules because they
  change recovery authority, constraints and the terminal predicate.
