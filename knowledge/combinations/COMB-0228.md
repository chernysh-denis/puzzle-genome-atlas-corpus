## Decision structure

The player first advances a remote support body through legal terminals and
typed actions. An authored gate then preserves that body while transferring
direct control to the released protagonist and re-exposing the support unit as
a command ability. The resulting infantry phase couples live hits, delayed
health recovery and heat-managed fire to ordered purge and airlock gates.
Checkpoint recovery keeps failure bounded, but success requires a cleanly
reloaded successor-mission state.

## Combination match boundary

A generic shooter tutorial, companion ability or checkpointed mission is
insufficient. A supporting ruleset must begin with direct control of a support
unit, preserve and rebind that same unit after an authored protagonist handoff,
then couple its typed command effect to heat-managed direct combat, ordered
mission interactions and a retained successor terminal.

## Novelty and variants

- Cyberpunk 2077 shares scan-assisted combat, abilities, health and authored
  checkpoints, but does not convert its prior controllable body into a
  persistent command ability.
- Call of Juarez: Gunslinger shares one fixed firearm mission and checkpoints,
  but uses carried ammunition, Concentration and a duel rather than weapon heat
  and a support-unit handoff.
- World of Warcraft Exile's Reach shares ordered taught predicates and a
  successor destination, but its companion fill is autonomous and never begins
  as the player's directly controlled body.
