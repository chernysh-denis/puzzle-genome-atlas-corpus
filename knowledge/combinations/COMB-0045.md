## Decision structure

The player advances an articulated body's head one grid cell. Its ordered body
follows, consuming a required target can append permanent collision / support
geometry, and the complete new shape then falls if externally unsupported.
Clearing the final target activates a fixed exit; completion requires the
controlled body to enter it after all automatic resolution. Every step must
therefore prepare both the next body pose and the future evacuation route.

## Analysed games

- [`GAME-0045` — Snakebird](../games/s-z/snakebird.md)

## Combination match boundary

Classic Snake growth without gravity, a one-cell avatar under continuous
physics, bounded scalar growth, a collection objective without an exit gate or
an automatic follower chain do not instantiate this combination. The system
must preserve ordered articulated segments, add one persistent segment from a
required contact, apply discrete whole-shape gravity after input and activate
the fixed evacuation exit only after the declared target set is cleared.
