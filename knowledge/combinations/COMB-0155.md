## Decision structure

Two human players separately control Mio and Zoe through a real-time authored
chapter. Progress repeatedly withholds resolution until both eligible actors
supply their paired inputs, while the persistent partner pane exposes the
other actor's live state. A failed actor returns into the live segment while
the partner survives; only pair failure restores the authored checkpoint. Both
actors must cross the chapter boundary together.

## Combination match boundary

Generic local co-op is insufficient: the ordinary ruleset must require exactly
two human-controlled persistent actors, expose the partner's live viewport,
use actor-specific permissions and paired-input progress gates, preserve the
segment through one partner's failure, reset it on pair failure and terminate
at a finite shared chapter boundary.

Portal 2 Cooperative Campaign requires two actors and dual completion, but its
canonical chamber does not establish the partner-view, survivor-held respawn
and pair-wipe checkpoint loop. Team shooters may respawn allies but do not use
the same paired authored-gate contract.
