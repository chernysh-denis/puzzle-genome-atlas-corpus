## Interaction

One player configures a class-bound loadout and joins a four-person squad.
Legal headquarters, held objectives, squadmates, beacons and vehicle seats
form a mutable deployment network. Class gadgets heal, resupply, spot, repair
or create forward entry opportunities while vehicles and destructible cover
change access to lettered control points. A lethal hit first creates a downed
window: completed revival restores the soldier and preserves the shared ticket;
bleedout commits death and one ticket loss. Uncontested presence changes point
ownership, and each owned point repeatedly drains the opposing ticket pool.
The same team resource therefore prices local exposure, risky revival,
vehicle preservation and sustained territory until one pool reaches zero.
