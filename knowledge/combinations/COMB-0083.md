## Decision structure

The player rotates one fixed-position tile while preserving its port shape.
Every local port must meet its reciprocal neighbour, and the resulting graph
must simultaneously include all tiles in one connected component and contain
no cycle.

## Combination match boundary

A puzzle does not instantiate this combination merely by rotating tiles,
matching adjacent edges, connecting all vertices or forbidding cycles. The
same rotation-only answer state must satisfy reciprocal port compatibility,
spanning connectivity and acyclicity together.
