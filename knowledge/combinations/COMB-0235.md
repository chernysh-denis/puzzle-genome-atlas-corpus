## Combination gene set

`ACT-008 + ACT-161 + ACT-164 + SYS-215 + SYS-222 + SYS-749 + CON-402 + CON-578 + OBJ-026 + TIM-003`

This is a strict ten-gene subset of the thirteen-gene Serious Sam HD: The First
Encounter genome. Health, armour and partial local-information presentation
remain outside the distinctive relation.

## Decision structure

The player advances along an authored route whose settled defeat and traversal
triggers release finite hostile groups into live combat. Movement, aimed fire
and weapon selection trade typed finite ammunition against an unlimited
fallback, while contact pickups replenish only compatible reserve. One swarm
must be completely cleared before its exit opens; the packet closes only when
the player reaches the fixed successor threshold.

## Combination match boundary

Generic real-time shooting, a wave-survival timer, an adaptive encounter
director or a room that opens after one enemy is insufficient. A supporting
ruleset must combine authored world-state triggers that release finite groups,
direct traversal and weapon selection, compatible finite ammunition with
spatial resupply, at least one full-clearance route gate and a fixed successor
location.

## Novelty and variants

- Left 4 Dead 2 shares a one-shot authored panic population and a fixed route,
  but its trigger is an explicit interaction, the Director also adapts ambient
  populations and four living Survivors must seal the terminal checkpoint.
- The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth shares full-clearance room gates and spatial
  pickups, but its random room graph, run build and reward sampling do not use
  a fixed first-person route with defeat/threshold-triggered groups.
- Half-Life 2 shares first-person movement, weapons, ammunition and an authored
  successor transition, but the reviewed Ravenholm packet is organised around
  Gravity Gun object manipulation rather than finite trigger-released swarms.
