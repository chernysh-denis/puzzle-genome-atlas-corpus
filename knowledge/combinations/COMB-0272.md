# COMB-0272 — Preserve a carried signal across a bounded route and activate it at the terminal

- Claim status: `Pattern`
- Evidence quality: `Corroborated`
- Confidence: `High`
- Corpus role: verified interaction pattern
- Novelty assessment: no exact duplicate among 271 prior combinations; this is
  the first portable relation in which a signal item preserved across a bounded
  survival route must be activated by a living agent in a location-bound
  terminal so the route result can settle.

## Combination gene set

`ACT-364 + SYS-669 + CON-537 + OBJ-125`

This is a strict four-gene subset of both the PEAK and The Long Dark genomes.
Movement model, survival substrate, inventory, deadline, information and
failure-state handling remain outside the combination because they differ
independently.

## Decision structure

The player must obtain or retain a compatible carried signal item while
traversing an authored bounded survival route. Reaching the terminal region is
not enough: the controlled agent must still be alive, the required route
progress must be complete and the signal must be usable there. The player then
explicitly activates the item, the system converts that request into its
authored response and the positive route result settles.

## Analysed games

- [`GAME-0203` — PEAK](../games/m-r/peak.md)
- [`GAME-0285` — The Long Dark](../games/s-z/the-long-dark.md)

## Combination match boundary

A rescue theme, carried flare, destination arrival or generic extraction alone
is insufficient. A supporting ruleset must make one usable carried signal an
explicit terminal command after bounded route progress and require the living
agent, eligible region and signal state together. Item form, activation verb,
route, location, response animation and report are parameters.

## Novelty and variants

- PEAK preserves an ignitable Flare through an island ascent and lights it in
  the PEAK region to request a helicopter and completed expedition result.
- The Long Dark retrieves a Distress Pistol and shell at a mountain summit,
  carries them across regions and fires from the Lighthouse to complete
  `Hopeless Rescue`.
- Automatic rescue on arrival, combat-only flare use and open-world survival
  without a positive terminal are outside the relation.
