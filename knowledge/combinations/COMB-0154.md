## Decision structure

The player submits one variable-length simple path through adjacent letter
cells. The fixed authored state classifies it as a theme answer, the designated
opposite-edge spangram, an eligible non-answer word or a rejection. Accepted
answer paths remain mutually disjoint and progressively reveal the hidden
full-grid partition. Eligible non-answer words instead accumulate toward staged
cell-set and traversal-order hints for an unresolved answer.

## Combination match boundary

A spatial word search is insufficient: candidate words must be submitted as
simple adjacent-cell paths, compared with one fixed concealed themed answer
set, and retained answers must form a disjoint complete partition containing a
designated opposite-edge path. Valid non-answer paths must also fund the staged
answer-route disclosure.

Wordle lacks spatial routes, full-grid partitioning and earned hints. Flow Free
has disjoint covering paths but exposes fixed endpoints and accepts any
constraint-satisfying cover rather than classifying paths through a lexicon and
hidden authored theme set.
