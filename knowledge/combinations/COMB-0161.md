## Combination match boundary

Two teams and a goal score are insufficient. The ruleset must retain one live
spatial ball, a unique transferable direct-control locus inside an eleven-player
side, teammate-targeted delivery, football offside/foul adjudication, autonomous
goalkeeping and a finite two-half result.

Rocket League lacks eleven-player role AI, control transfer and offside. A
football manager lacks direct embodied control and live ball contact. A combat
team game resolves damage and elimination rather than lawful possession and goals.
