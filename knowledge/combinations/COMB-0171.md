## Decision structure

The player addresses an unopened door, compares three authored room plans and
commits one that fits the vacant manor cell, doorway graph and current resource
state. The chosen room becomes traversable, changes the day's resource and
information state, and consumes steps as the player moves through the route.
When the route is no longer useful, the player calls the day: the drafted manor
and day-local economy reset while declared estate progress remains, enabling a
new sequence toward Room 46.

## Combination match boundary

Generic procedural rooms, inventory use or a run reset alone is insufficient.
A supporting ruleset must expose a bounded exclusive room-plan offer at an
addressed doorway, instantiate the selection inside a finite topology, bind
route traversal to visible daily resources and let the player replace the
layout while retaining declared progress toward a traversable target. Carto
rearranges owned tiles without sampled offers or daily resets; The Binding of
Isaac resolves generated rooms through real-time combat rather than drafting
each next room.
