## Decision structure

The player reads the next ordered gate and live time, steers the supplied car
through continuous tyre contact and node-beam deformation, interprets visible
structural/control changes and may request mission-authorised recovery without
gaining a skipped gate. Only the complete ordered route stops and retains the
elapsed result; restart, abort and immobilisation remain non-completion.

## Combination match boundary

A driving game, deformable car or timed checkpoint route alone is insufficient.
A supporting ruleset must bind one assigned directly controlled vehicle to
distributed soft-body/tyre state, expose that damage for revised control,
permit bounded recovery without route credit and convert ordered completion
into a retained standalone timed result.
