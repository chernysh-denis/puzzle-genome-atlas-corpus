The 34-gene set is a strict subset of Baldur's Gate 3's 43-gene genome. It
omits optional shopping, consumable, equipment, loot and informational-dialogue
actions while retaining custom creation, checked conversation, party combat,
levelling, rest, companion and retained-ending structure.

## Interaction

One custom character binds starting capabilities before a persistent authored
campaign. Dialogue options can expose a d20 check whose ability, proficiency,
bonuses, advantage and Inspiration rerolls connect character build to retained
quest and companion state. Hostility converts the same four-person party into
initiative order: each active member spends movement, Action, Bonus Action and
Reaction while attacks, spells, concentration, sight and surfaces update a
shared battlefield. Downing creates a recoverable death-save stage rather than
immediate erasure. Short and supply-backed long rests restore different
resource sets and can advance camp events. XP and choices widen builds and
gate later routes until the selected terminal branch completes the campaign.
