## Decision structure

The player exposes a manipulable subpart through close inventory inspection,
changes the held object's functional state, performs two compatible binary
combinations to assemble a three-part reach tool, and applies it before the
transient constituent state decays. Expiry makes the composite unusable but
preserves a retry path; timely use retrieves the inaccessible target item.

## Combination match boundary

A puzzle does not instantiate this combination merely because it has an
inventory, crafting recipe or countdown. One held object must reveal a
manipulable functional subpart under close inspection; the player must prepare
a temporary state, build a tool from at least three item identities through
staged combinations, and use that tool on an inaccessible scene target before
the prepared state expires.
