## Decision structure

The player fits acquired supplies and weapons inside a rectangular carried
grid, using that space and known recipes to preserve a viable live-combat
loadout. Holding aim can improve the next shot's stagger relation. At close
range, a timed parry prevents an eligible incoming attack by spending knife
durability; precise defence or a suitable hit can expose a temporary prompted
follow-up. One authored pressure encounter ends after its survival threshold
rather than total clearance. Those local resource and timing decisions must
remain viable through the chapter's final interaction, explicit completion and
retained successor control.

## Combination match boundary

Generic firearm combat, an item grid, durability, a timed block, a survival
event or a chapter checkpoint alone is insufficient. A supporting ruleset must
make rectangular inventory fit part of the same live resource plan as focused
shots and durability-backed reactive defence, expose a temporary stagger
follow-up, settle an authored pressure event without requiring all enemies to
be defeated and carry those decisions into a retained successor chapter.
