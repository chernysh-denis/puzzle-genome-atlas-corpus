## Decision structure

The player crosses a bounded authored route while hazardous atmosphere spends
the installed filter interval and replacement irreversibly spends carried
stock. In hostile interiors, world lights and the personal flashlight change
visual acquisition while a wearable indicator exposes current lit or shadowed
state. The player can wait, neutralise an unaware guard, throw an inert
diversion or accept recoverable combat, then operate fixed train gates and
verify the retained successor chapter after reload.

## Combination match boundary

Generic radiation, a cosmetic mask, darkness, ordinary detection, a thrown
object or a chapter checkpoint alone is insufficient. A supporting ruleset
must consume installed breathing-filter duration before environmental harm,
allow a deliberate finite filter exchange, expose current personal
illumination, let player-controlled local light affect hostile acquisition,
offer both an explicit stealth diversion and an unaware neutralisation, and carry the
bounded authored route into reload-verifiable successor control.
