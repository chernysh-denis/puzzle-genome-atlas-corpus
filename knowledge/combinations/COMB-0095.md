- Claim status: `Confirmed`

## Combination gene set

- Action: `ACT-008`, `ACT-097`
- System: `SYS-125`, `SYS-126`
- Information: `INF-045`
- Objective: `OBJ-022`

This six-gene set is a proper subset of the supporting twelve-gene Manifold
Garden genome. Portable-cube handling, reachable switches, generic body
physics, colour-gated cube eligibility, complete local visibility and live
scheduling support the tutorial but do not define its periodic traversal law.

## Decision structure

The player selects one visible orthogonal surface as the new down direction.
The system snaps the local physics frame to that surface and colour-codes the
result. Instead of treating an open edge as death, the player steers the avatar
through the corresponding world-period boundary; the system remaps the body to
the opposite translated copy while preserving its useful local trajectory. The
new landing makes a previously unreachable switch local, allowing the sole
controlled actor to open and traverse the fixed exit.

## Combination match boundary

Ordinary falling, camera rotation or visual repetition is insufficient. The
player must choose a physical gravity frame, a dynamic body must cross an
authored translational period boundary into the corresponding repeated local
pose, the current frame must be visibly encoded, and that remapped traversal
must enable the actor's fixed exit. Paired portals, finite wrapping boards,
screen-space path joins and object-scale changes remain outside the boundary.
