## Decision structure

The player captures a party, deploys one active partner, positions the Trainer
and commands ready moves or a legal switch under local detection, then converts
nighttime victories into a Challenger's Ticket and defeats its designated
opponent to record the next rank.

## Combination match boundary

Creature ownership alone is insufficient: the ruleset must combine direct
probabilistic world capture, one actively switched party proxy, explicitly
commanded positional real-time moves, awareness-dependent battle initiation,
night-gated point qualification and a ticket-bound promotion opponent.

Palworld shares capture and party deployment but its Pal chooses ordinary
combat skills autonomously and the scoped story is not a nighttime ticketed
rank. A conventional turn-based creature battler lacks simultaneous Trainer
positioning and per-move cooldowns. A real-time action RPG without persistent
capture and party switching also remains outside the boundary.
