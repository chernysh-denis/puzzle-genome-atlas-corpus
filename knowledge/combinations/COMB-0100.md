## Decision structure

One role inspects and controls the live problem object but lacks its
authoritative procedure. Another role can inspect that procedure but has no
view or control of the current object. They must communicate enough state and
instruction to let the acting role commit the addressed control while the
attempt deadline continues to decrease.

## Combination match boundary

Ordinary cooperative discussion is insufficient. The state/control and rule
reference must be mechanically separated between roles; communication must be
the required bridge; one role must commit a live control; and terminal time
must progress during that exchange. A shared board, asynchronous hint, solo
manual lookup or untimed information-asymmetric game falls outside the boundary.
