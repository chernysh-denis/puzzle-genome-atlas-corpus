partially revealed mission route. The player must solve its local combat and
terminal obstacles, complete the mandatory objective, reach extraction and
carry the resulting equipment growth into the next authored quest step.

## Combination match boundary

Combat, a mission exit or persistent equipment alone is insufficient. A
supporting ruleset must combine bounded preparation, a locally revealed
entry-objective-extraction route, objective-gated settlement, retained
equipment progression and a finite authored terminal. ARC Raiders risks
carried gear and supports PvPvE extraction; Helldivers 2 separates mission,
optional extraction and Galactic War settlement. Neither reproduces Warframe's
Mod-capacity preparation and ordered Orbiter restoration.
