## Decision structure

The player reads one resident's needs, emotion, current actions and the active
scenario gate, then commits a context-eligible interaction. Queued direction
and resident autonomy share the running simulation; completed social actions
change persistent relationships, and current stage predicates advance toward
one route-dependent terminal reward.

## Combination match boundary

An autonomous colony resident is insufficient: the ordinary scoped loop must
allow player-selected contextual interactions for one persistent resident,
expose that resident's live motives, emotion and action queue, retain
relationship changes, and bind the activity to visible authored stages that
terminate in a branch-labelled reward.

Dwarf Fortress and RimWorld expose needs and relationships but their admitted
colony objectives are not this directly selected, finite scenario chain. The
Stanley Parable reaches branch endings but has no simulated resident motives,
autonomy or persistent interpersonal state.
