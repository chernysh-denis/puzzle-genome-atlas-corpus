# COMB-0214 — Convert one ordered car run into a fixed medal evaluation

- Claim status: `Confirmed`
- Evidence quality: `Direct`
- Confidence: `High`
- Corpus role: verified interaction pattern
- Novelty assessment: no exact combination-set duplicate among 213 registered
  combinations; it joins dedicated live car control, ordered waypoint validity,
  retained elapsed time and fixed per-map medal thresholds without rivals,
  campaign rewards or leaderboard dependence.

## Combination gene set

`ACT-290 + SYS-320 + SYS-516 + SYS-628 + SYS-711 + CON-438 + INF-205 + INF-251 + OBJ-133 + TIM-003`

This is a strict ten-gene subset of the eleven-gene Trackmania genome. Generic
speed, gear and route-guidance presentation remains outside the distinctive
ordered-time-to-medal interaction core.

## Decision structure

The player directly steers one dedicated car while motion and the attempt clock
advance, reads ordered course progress, crosses every required waypoint and
reaches one valid finish. The system retains elapsed time, then assigns the
highest fixed Author/Gold/Silver/Bronze threshold reached; a slower valid finish
remains completion without a new medal.

## Analysed games

- [`GAME-0216` — Trackmania](../games/s-z/trackmania.md)

## Combination match boundary

A driving game, stopwatch or medal table alone is insufficient. A supporting
ruleset must bind one directly controlled vehicle to an ordered Start/
Checkpoint/Finish route, retain the completed elapsed result and classify that
same result against fixed map-local medal thresholds.

## Novelty and variants

- BeamNG.drive Road Master retains an ordered Time Trial result but has no
  fixed Author/Gold/Silver/Bronze evaluator and instead makes soft-body damage
  and authorised recovery decision-relevant.
- Forza Horizon 6 validates races and time but its scoped opening joins rivals,
  vehicle selection, Festival progress and reward settlement.
- A Trackmania ghost/personal-best loop could support a variant only after a
  separate scope admits repeated attempts and the ghost's causal information.
