## Decision structure

The player chooses one ground vehicle from a short fixed lineup, drives it into
an armour angle or capture position and uses marker, distance and penetration
forecast to commit a shot. Ballistics damage spatial crew and modules, changing
what the current vehicle can still do before it is lost. After loss another
eligible lineup member can return, while vehicle destruction and ownership of
more of three points both drain a shared team ticket pool toward the terminal.

## Combination match boundary

Generic tank combat or control points alone are insufficient. A supporting
ruleset must let a player select successive ground vehicles from one bounded
lineup, use an explicit armour forecast for direct fire, resolve internal
functional damage, contest three territorial points and convert both vehicle
loss and point majority into the same finite team terminal.

## Novelty and variants

- Battlefield 6 combines vehicles, capture and tickets but uses infantry
  downing/revival, spatial deployment sources and point-count drain rather than
  assisted armour internals and a three-vehicle personal lineup.
- PUBG exposes occupied vehicle parts and occupants but has no capture-point or
  team-ticket result.
- Ground Realistic, aircraft, naval battles, backups and account progression
  change the admitted spawn, information or body boundaries and remain outside
  this combination.
