The 27-gene set is a strict subset of PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS' 43-gene genome. It
omits generic on-foot navigation, quick-slot selection, reload, grenade and
item-drop actions, general live-combat resolution, Red Zone bombing, regional
armour automation, terrain deformation and already-visible personal status,
while retaining the reusable battle-royale arc from insertion to one survivor.

## Interaction

A sampled aircraft route gives each empty-handed participant a different set of
reachable landing regions. Controlled descent commits the opening position;
distributed loot and finite capacity then turn local exposure into a temporary
loadout. Vehicles and restorative casts exchange noise or vulnerability for
rotation and survival while successive random safe areas contract on a forced
clock. Local sight and sound never reveal the complete population, but lethal
defeat permanently removes a participant and advances the visible count until
one remains.
