## Decision structure

The player acquires actor-bound information through optical observation, then
crosses an authored route while local perception can progress into live combat.
At any moment one selected powered mode can change traversal, protection or
concealment, but all such effects compete for the same rechargeable reserve and
remain qualified by the current action. A compatible live attachment can alter
the firearm before commitment; a shot under concealment can spend the reserve
and disclose the actor. The route still ends only at its designated authored
successor.

## Combination match boundary

Generic stealth, a cooldown ability, one armour bar or a weapon attachment
alone is insufficient. A supporting ruleset must combine acquired actor marks,
readable detection, live direct combat, selectable mutually exclusive powered
capabilities, one common automatically replenished reserve, action-qualified
effects across traversal/protection/concealment and a fixed authored route
terminal.

## Novelty and variants

- Far Cry 3 shares optical marks, detection-aware approach and live firearm
  combat, but its reviewed packet has no common reserve that switches among
  traversal, damage interception and concealment.
- STAR WARS: Squadrons reallocates one craft power budget among simultaneous
  subsystems rather than selecting a personal mode whose action drains and
  replenishes one meter.
- Concrete suit, mode, weapon, attachment, mission, actor, rate or objective
  names do not form another combination while this causal boundary remains.
