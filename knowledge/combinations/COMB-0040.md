## Decision structure

The player repeatedly translates and rotates one unique persistent map
fragment under typed-edge legality. A committed arrangement immediately becomes
the traversable world's topology while retaining fragment-local contents. The
goal is then realised by navigating to the target made reachable through those
new adjacencies, so the map is both the edited plan and authoritative world
state.

## Combination match boundary

A game does not instantiate this combination merely by moving tiles, viewing a
map or loading an area. Map fragments must be unique persistent objects, their
touching typed edges must constrain legal adjacency, the player must directly
reposition / rotate them, and the resulting arrangement must propagate into the
world that the avatar traverses toward its target. Consumed placement tiles,
passive maps, temporary image overlays and menu-selected levels fail the
boundary.
