## Decision structure

The player changes the pose of one temporary active element while automatic
descent consumes a real-time placement window. Collision restricts reachable
poses; blocked descent fixes the element; completed lines are removed and
collapsed; then a successor enters. Accumulation eventually obstructs entry and
ends the attempt.

## Combination match boundary

A game does not instantiate this combination merely because pieces fall or
rows disappear. Player-controlled pose changes must occur during forced
progression, collision must restrict placement, blocked descent must fix the
active element, line completion must remove and collapse occupancy, and the
resolved cycle must introduce a successor under terminal entry pressure.
