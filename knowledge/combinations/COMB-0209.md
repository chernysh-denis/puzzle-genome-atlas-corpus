`ACT-161 + ACT-290 + SYS-701 + SYS-702 + SYS-703 + SYS-704 + CON-262 + CON-488 + CON-555 + INF-116 + INF-270 + OBJ-131 + TIM-003`

This is a strict thirteen-gene subset of the fifteen-gene World of Tanks

distinctive shot, spotting, capture and terminal chain.

## Decision structure

Moving the single tank changes position and aim uncertainty. A legal loaded AP
shot still passes through distance, angle, armour and dispersion before it can
change both vehicle hit points and separate internal functions. Detection is a
range/concealment test whose marker may be relayed to allies. An attacker can
commit to enemy-base capture, but damage can remove that tank's progress. Once
the player's tank is destroyed it cannot return, while the team still pursues
complete enemy destruction or base capture before the fifteen-minute draw.

## Combination match boundary

Generic vehicle combat is insufficient. A supporting ruleset must admit one
non-returning directly controlled armoured vehicle, finite fire whose visible
uncertainty precedes aggregate and component damage, range/concealment spotting
relayed to a team, damage-interruptible opposing-base progress and a shared
terminal that accepts either all-vehicle destruction or base capture plus a
timed draw.

## Novelty and variants

- War Thunder shares direct armoured fire and team information but uses a
  bounded multi-vehicle lineup, spatial internal damage, three control points
  and ticket exhaustion rather than one aggregate-HP tank and two bases.
- Battlefield 6 shares team vehicles and territorial pressure but allows
  infantry, deployment and revival and settles Conquest through tickets.
- Counter-Strike 2 shares one-life team pressure and a timed result, but uses
  embodied infantry, equipment economy, rounds and an asymmetric planted
  device instead of persistent tank spotting and base capture.
