## Decision structure

Cody and May are owned by two different humans who can inspect both live
panes. Cody assigns a bounded reusable nail to an eligible platform, switch or
swing target; May crosses with or strikes the complementary fixture; Cody
recalls and reassigns stock for the next dependency. One actor may return while
the other keeps the segment alive, but a pair wipe restores the checkpoint.
The same grammar culminates in Toolbox's pin, swing, lock and launch sequence
before the chapter boundary settles.

## Combination match boundary

Generic local co-op or a split screen is insufficient. A supporting ruleset
must require two human-owned roles, expose their live views, give one role a
finite recallable world-anchor stock and the other a non-substitutable fixture
tool, and make ordered reassignment of those tools causally necessary for the
same bounded authored terminal.

## Novelty and variants

- Split Fiction shares mandatory two-human split-view traversal and the same
  individual-return/pair-reset boundary, but its scoped chapter does not use a
  recallable stock that changes among pin, switch and partner-swing roles.
- Portal 2 Cooperative Campaign shares paired human traversal and authored
  gates, but both robots place portals rather than owning complementary hammer
  and nail permissions.
- A Way Out is outside the canonical corpus and cannot establish a registered
  recurrence.
