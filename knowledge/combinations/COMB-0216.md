## Decision structure

Players turn limited money into round equipment, may transfer it, and infer an
opponent route from incomplete local and team cues. Elimination, timeout,
plant, defuse and explosion award the round and update the next economy. These
rounds repeat until the shipped 20-minute map cycle evaluates the retained
team-round score as one side's lead or a tie.

## Combination match boundary

Generic team shooting or one bomb round is insufficient. A supporting ruleset
must combine gated purchases and transferable equipment, one-life planted-
device rounds, economy carried across those rounds, partial team information
and an explicit finite server-session boundary that evaluates their retained
round score.

## Novelty and variants

- Counter-Strike 2 shares the bomb-round and economy core but its scoped
  Competitive match ends through MR12 clinch/draw rules after a halftime role
  and economy reset, not a stock map-time cycle.
- Rainbow Six Siege shares asymmetric plant/disable rounds and role swaps but
  commits operators and gadgets rather than purchasable carried economy.
- A custom original Counter-Strike tournament configuration may support a
  different combination only after its exact round and side policy is bounded.
