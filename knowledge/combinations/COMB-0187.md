## Decision structure

The directly controlled avatar spends stamina on staff offence or evasion;
precise defence and staff pressure create Focus for stronger staff commitments.
Spells and Red Tides introduce separate readiness/resource layers. Earned Sparks
change the persistent build but can be reclaimed freely at a Shrine. Rest
restores the next attempt and revives ordinary opposition; death returns to that
checkpoint without a currency mark. Retained boss flags ultimately settle
Black Bear Guai into first Chapter 2 control.

## Combination match boundary

Generic checkpoint action combat is insufficient. A supporting ruleset must
connect stamina and live defensive timing to a player-built-and-spent attack
resource, active spell gates, an acquired temporary replacement body,
reallocatable character points, restorative checkpoint reset, death without a
recoverable currency object and a retained boss-to-region terminal.

## Novelty and variants

- Elden Ring shares real-time attacks, spells, flask use, rest and a boss-region
  threshold, but its death mark, attribute-price/load rules, mount and Spirit
  layers replace no-loss return, Focus and a temporary full combat body.
- Hollow Knight: Silksong shares Bench return, resource combat and an act gate,
  but its Cocoon creates a recoverable liability and its Crest/movement
  topology replaces freely reclaimable Sparks and Red Tides.
- Monster Hunter Wilds shares stamina-bounded attacks and boss reads, but a
  timed hunt, finite faints, equipment maintenance and Palico define its result.
- Later Wukong chapters, transformations, NG+ and Challenge/Gauntlet may support
  variants but remain separate scope modules.
