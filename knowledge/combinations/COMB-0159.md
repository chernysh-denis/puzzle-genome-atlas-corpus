## Decision structure

Four Survivors split timed repair and rescue work while one Killer receives
different movement evidence and tries to advance health into Hook sacrifice.
Five completed Generators unlock the Exit Gates; the last-Survivor Hatch and
Endgame Collapse replace indefinite cleanup with a terminal escape choice.

## Combination match boundary

Cooperative repair alone is insufficient: the ruleset must combine a fixed
many-versus-one role split, random timing checks, role-exclusive pursuit
evidence, staged injury and Hook rescue, an objective quota that powers escape
and a terminal gate-or-alternate-exit deadline.

A team shooter with revives lacks the Generator-to-gate quota and Hook stages.
A stealth game with an automated pursuer lacks the human role asymmetry and
cooperative rescue economy. A generic escape-room switch does not create
transient tracks or the last-Survivor Hatch/Collapse resolution.
