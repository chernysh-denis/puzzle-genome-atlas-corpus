## Decision structure

The player traces one endpoint-labelled simple path at a time through declared
adjacent positions. Every pair must be joined, ordinary positions must remain
route-exclusive, and the union must cover the complete declared answer board;
any shared-junction exception requires an additional explicit capacity rule.

## Combination match boundary

A connection puzzle does not instantiate this combination merely because it
links terminals. It must use fixed paired identities, player-traced unbranched
routes, exclusive ordinary-position occupancy and complete declared-board
coverage as one conjunctive solution test. A declared shared junction may be an
exception only when another gene constrains its use.
