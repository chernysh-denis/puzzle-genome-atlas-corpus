## Decision structure

The player searches a deterministic graph of illustrated viewpoints, moves
intact panels and detaches selected layers, then tests adjacency or overlay at
particular crops and scales. Direct manipulation of the depicted world is
forbidden. A compatible multi-panel composition instead makes separated scenes
causally continuous and advances an internal character, object or mechanism.

## Combination match boundary

An image or tile puzzle does not instantiate this combination merely because
pieces can be arranged. Panels must retain navigable internal view state;
layers must be detachable without deleting their underlays; authored visual
seam or overlay compatibility must trigger automatic cross-panel continuation;
and the depicted world must lack direct object manipulation. Static collage,
typed edge matching or direct avatar navigation falls outside the boundary.
