## Decision structure

An offered option states its own success odds and lists the individual terms
producing them, so the player's move is to accept a stated probability or to
change the terms first — most directly by changing what the character is
wearing. The resolution adds the tested value, its modifiers and two dice
against a declared difficulty, with the extreme rolls overriding the total in
both directions, so nothing is ever guaranteed or forbidden. Each failure is
settled by its declared class: reattemptable failures wait for a qualifying
change in the world, while closed failures permanently remove the outcome they
would have produced. Meanwhile the same attributes resolve continuously without
being selected, adding commentary labelled with the faculty that produced it
and silently withholding the rest. Time is spent only by engaging, two
independent personal pools each end the attempt on their own, and the episode
settles with no physical resolution anywhere in its completion condition.

## Combination match boundary

A dice roll, a dialogue tree, a skill system or a morale meter alone is
insufficient. A supporting ruleset must disclose an option's computed odds and
its contributing modifier terms before commitment, resolve it with declared
automatic extremes, sort failures into reattemptable and permanently closed
classes whose retry legality depends on a world change, let worn equipment
continuously move those odds, surface unrequested resolutions as commentary
attributed to a named internal faculty, advance its clock only on committed
interactions, carry two independent personal pools each capable of ending the
attempt, and settle its episode without any physical resolution.

## Novelty and variants

- The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt shares authored responses, learned facts and
  evidence converted into recorded progress, but resolves its consequential
  moments through real-time combat rather than through a disclosed probability.
- Detroit: Become Human shares branching authored dialogue with persistent
  results, but its branches are fixed by the option chosen rather than rolled
  against odds the player can prepare.
- Papers, Please shares a self-paced examination loop and an interaction-driven
  workday, but its verdicts are deterministic classifications.
- Skill, faculty, character, room, item and exact difficulty names remain
  parameters.
