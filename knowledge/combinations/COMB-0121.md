## Claim

[Observation | Corroborated | High] A combat turn asks the player to choose a
concealed draw source, convert friendly board presence into Blood and Bones,
place persistent creatures into paired lanes against a disclosed enemy queue,
then commit ordered attacks toward a relative damage-scale threshold.

## Causal structure

1. The player chooses the main or side deck and reveals one top card.
2. A creature placement requires an open lane and its complete declared cost.
3. Blood costs consume a player-selected sufficient subset of eligible friendly
   creatures; those deaths also increase the Bone pool.
4. The placed creature persists in one of the bounded paired lanes.
5. The hostile back row discloses which cards are committed to which lanes.
6. Ringing the bell resolves friendly attacks, advances eligible hostile cards
   and resolves hostile attacks in fixed order.
7. Opposed attacks damage cards; unopposed attacks move a shared scale, and the
   required net margin ends the battle.

The combination is a proper subset of the supporting game's complete genome:
route progression, card rewards and modifications, items, Teeth / pelts,
candles, Deathcards and cabin mechanisms remain outside this interaction.

## Boundary notes

- This is not generic deckbuilding: a held card must become a persistent lane
  combatant and board sacrifices must fund some placements.
- This is not Slay the Spire's Energy loop: the main payment decision can remove
  current friendly entities, while Bones are produced by death.
- This is not a hidden-opponent card game: the next hostile lane assignments
  are disclosed before the player commits the bell.
- Sigils, exact card values, four-lane count, five-point margin and Squirrel
  identity are parameters unless later comparison demonstrates a boundary
  failure.
