Workshop-progression loop.

## Interaction

A retained or free loadout enters one condition-bound shared raid. Local sight,
sound and ARC telegraphs make searching, looting, healing and field conversion
vulnerable real-time commitments. The Raider can protect only eligible bounded
items, then must reach and complete a live extraction interaction before defeat.
Extraction banks ordinary carried state; knockout forfeits it while preserving
valid Safe Pocket contents. Returned items, coins and components persist in
Speranza, where known station recipes, repair costs and earned skill points
reshape the next legal loadout. The result is a repeated decision loop in which
temporary possession becomes persistent capability only through explicit risk
settlement.
