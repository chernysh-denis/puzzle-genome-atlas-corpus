## Decision structure

The player advances one authored survival route while a held local overlay
temporarily exposes eligible nearby actors through occlusion. Disclosed
ingredients admit one stationless craft; local sight, an inert thrown stimulus
and unaware reach support avoidance or neutralisation before recoverable live
combat. An autonomous authored companion follows and assists without becoming
a second controlled role, and the final gate settles into a reload-verifiable
successor chapter.

## Combination match boundary

Generic stealth, crafting, a highlighted enemy, a scripted ally or a chapter
checkpoint alone is insufficient. A supporting ruleset must let the player
invoke and release a bounded occlusion-crossing actor overlay, spend disclosed
ingredients on a known personal craft, position an inert perception stimulus,
neutralise an unaware reachable hostile, receive autonomous companion help and
carry the authored survival route into reload-verifiable successor control.
