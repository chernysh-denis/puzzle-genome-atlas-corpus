## Decision structure

The system continuously propels and steers one car while the player reads and
commits offered routes or manoeuvres. Braking, drifting and airborne execution
can refill ordinary nitro; its timed spend changes speed and rival position.
Only ordered course completion classifies a finish, and only a place inside the
disclosed set retains the progression result.

## Combination match boundary

A directly steered race, passive lane runner, fixed rail or transient result is
insufficient. A ruleset must keep steering in the system, preserve prompted
route/manoeuvre authority, convert execution into spendable boost, resolve
rival place over an ordered course and retain threshold success.

## Novelty and variants

- Need for Speed Underground shares opening Career race, rivals, ordered finish
  and reward, but gives direct steering and accepts only first place.
- Trackmania shares ordered real-time racing but has no rival field, assisted
  prompt grammar or Career reward threshold in its scoped time trial.
