## Decision structure

The player directly navigates a command-placement avatar but does not steer the
crowd. At reachable grid positions the player writes persistent instructions
into the world. A recurring population is released automatically, walks under
fixed rules and executes each marker it contacts. Terminal accounting fills a
fixed goal until the required rescue flow is reached.

## Combination match boundary

A crowd-routing game does not instantiate this combination merely because many
agents walk automatically. Commands must persist at avatar-reachable world
positions and execute on contact for multiple later walkers; population release
and terminal accounting must continue in real time; and a minimum flow through
a fixed exit must complete the stage. Per-agent consumable skill assignment,
direct route drawing and infrastructure-only traversal fall outside it.
