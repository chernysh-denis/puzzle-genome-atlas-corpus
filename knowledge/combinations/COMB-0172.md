## Decision structure

Fixed teams deny operators, then commit unique role kits. During preparation,
attackers risk observation devices to reveal a site while defenders spend
limited fortification and gadgets to reshape it. In action, live observation,
communication, surface breach, combat and recoverable injury govern access to
the two bomb rooms. A completed defuser plant changes the deadline; the round
settles, local construction resets and the retained score/role schedule shapes
the next opposed plan.

## Combination match boundary

Generic team shooting, destructible cover or a bomb plant alone is
insufficient. A supporting ruleset must couple role-unique selection with a
live device-bounded observation network, defender-built fortification,
attacker-controlled structural breach, recoverable injury and rebuilt
round-local geometry around a staged bomb objective. Counter-Strike 2 lacks
operator kits, construction, observation devices and revival; Battlefield 6
lacks the same preparation-to-bomb-round reset contract.
