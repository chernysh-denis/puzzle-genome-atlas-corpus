## Decision structure

The player must orient and commit the current supplied tile to an expanding
adjacent frontier while exact successors support short-horizon planning.
Automatic spatial evaluation can score the placement and earn tiles back into
the finite supply. The same decision therefore spends one future action while
its quality determines whether the scoring horizon contracts, holds or grows.

## Combination match boundary

A tile-placement game does not instantiate this combination merely because it
uses a queue or grants score. The current tile must be mandatory but freely
orientable, placement must extend an adjacent frontier, the finite supply must
terminate play when empty, and placement quality must be able to replenish
that same supply. A fixed-orientation queue, an unlimited sandbox, a strictly
decreasing move allowance or rewards unrelated to future actions falls outside
the boundary.
