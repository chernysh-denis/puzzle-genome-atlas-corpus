## Decision structure

Recurring untranslated glyphs appear in multiple world contexts. The player
records editable hypotheses, then assigns every discovered glyph exactly once
to a complete set of illustrated meaning slots. Submitting the correct
bijection replaces the provisional notes with persistent canonical meanings;
an incorrect or incomplete page remains open to revision.

## Combination match boundary

An ordinary substitution cipher is insufficient. Stable unknown glyphs must be
encountered contextually, their provisional meanings must remain editable, an
authored illustrated page must require a complete one-to-one mapping, and a
successful submission must canonicalise the group. Free notes without system
validation, already translated labels, sentence assembly or information split
between human roles falls outside the boundary.
