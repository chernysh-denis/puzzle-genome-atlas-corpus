## Interaction

A visible initiative queue grants one character a self-paced AP decision.
Skills and Free Aim consume that character's resources, character-specific
state makes command order matter, and the chosen action becomes a live timing
test before its effect resolves. Enemy turns similarly become per-hit dodge,
parry or jump sequences whose success can deny damage, return AP or unlock a
counter. Victory awards experience and Picto mastery credit; levels permit
bounded build choices, while repeated Picto use converts a slot-bound passive
into reusable Lumina still limited by capacity. The loop therefore connects
turn planning, learned motor execution and persistent build expansion without
collapsing them into one action or reward.
