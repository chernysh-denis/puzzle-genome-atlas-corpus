## Decision structure

The player reads incoming awareness while moving through one authored target
mission. A held relocation aim previews the accepted endpoint before the body
commits directly to it, so vertical access can preserve concealment without
occupying the intervening path. One unaware actor can be neutralised and moved;
the designated target's accepted non-lethal disposition additionally requires
learned procedure, viable restrained state, a matching item and fixture. Return
and mission closure then convert recorded conduct into exposed categories and
one aggregate evaluation before successor control resumes.
