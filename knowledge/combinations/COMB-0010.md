## Decision structure

The player chooses a source and destination vessel, after which the system
moves the largest top same-colour segment that fits. LIFO access, per-vessel
capacity and empty-or-matching-top compatibility govern which temporary
separations can eventually produce full homogeneous vessels.

## Combination match boundary

A container puzzle does not instantiate this combination merely because it
sorts colours. It must expose only the top segment, resolve maximal transfer
quantity automatically, restrict destinations by both capacity and exposed
type, and pursue homogeneous full containers through discrete commanded pours.
