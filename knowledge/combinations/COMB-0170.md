## Decision structure

The player reads a fixed opponent, pose, spacing and paired HUD, then commits an
attack, guard, throw or Drive-backed response while real-time contact continues.
Drive and Super stocks change which conversions and reversals remain legal;
vitality or the timer awards one round, resets selected state and carries
eligible Super stock until the second round win settles the match.

## Combination match boundary

Generic live combat, health depletion or a finite match alone is insufficient.
A supporting ruleset must bind exactly opposed side-view combatants, explicit
attack/guard/throw decisions, a shared-use offence/defence meter, a separately
carried super meter, readable contact state and multi-round settlement. Marvel
Rivals shares live combat cues but uses teams, cooldown kits, respawn and route
objectives; Counter-Strike 2 shares rounds but uses inventory, hidden position,
team elimination and asymmetric bomb objectives.
