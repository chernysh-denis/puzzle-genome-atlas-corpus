## Decision structure

The player irreversibly removes selected support links while a payload keeps
moving under continuous constrained physics. Each cut changes the future force
topology but does not directly choose position or velocity. Timing must produce
receiver contact before the payload escapes the terminal boundary.

## Combination match boundary

A physics game does not instantiate this combination merely because gravity
moves an object. The player must sever a support irreversibly during live
motion, the released body must be an indirectly controlled payload pursuing a
fixed receiver, and boundary escape must terminate the attempt. Directly
steering a falling piece, placing a route for system flow or using reversible
supports falls outside the boundary.
