# COMB-0268 — Staying strengthens the world and leaving requires standing still

- Claim status: `Strong Pattern`
- Evidence quality: `Corroborated`
- Confidence: `High`
- Corpus role: verified interaction pattern
- Novelty assessment: no exact duplicate among 267 prior combinations; this is
  the corpus's first interaction in which one value funded by elapsed time
  simultaneously sets the strength of the opposition and the price of everything
  the player might buy, and in which the only way out of the region is a charge
  that advances only while the player stops moving.

## Combination gene set

`ACT-130 + SYS-166 + SYS-817 + SYS-818 + INF-327 + OBJ-164 + TIM-003`

This is a strict seven-gene subset of the thirteen-gene Risk of Rain 2 genome.
Movement, skill commitment, authored-object interaction, real-time combat
resolution, the health pool and skill readiness remain outside the distinctive
interaction, because they describe how the run is played rather than what makes
elapsed time, price and the exit answer each other. Pricing is inside the
interaction, but it is `SYS-817` that carries it: `TAXONOMY_CHANGE_030` moved the
coefficient-to-price and coefficient-to-reward computation into that System gene
and deprecated the Constraint that had held it, so this combination states the
same content with one gene fewer.

## Decision structure

One value rises for as long as the attempt lasts, and it rises whether or not
anything is achieved. That value sets the level, health and damage of every
opponent the system creates and the budget it spends creating them, so the
world is measurably stronger a minute later than it was. The same value prices
every purchase still to be made, and multiplies what each defeat pays, so a
player cannot answer the rising threat by saving: the money and the danger are
the same number, and buying later costs more in exactly the proportion that
earning it became more dangerous. Purchases are permanent for the attempt and
apply themselves whenever their trigger occurs, so the reason to stay is real.
The interface names the value as an ordered tier beside the clock, which makes
the trade legible without making it calculable. And the only way out of the
region is a fixture that must be activated and then charged over a declared
minimum duration, advancing only while the player remains inside its disclosed
radius, under pressure the system sustains for that whole interval. Leaving
therefore demands the one behaviour the escalation punishes, and the attempt
settles by making the transition with the accumulated cost carried forward.

## Combination match boundary

A roguelite run, a difficulty curve, a timed defence or a shop alone is
insufficient. A supporting ruleset must derive its escalation from elapsed
attempt time rather than only from progress, apply that one value to the
opposition's strength, to the price of what may be acquired and to the reward a
defeat pays, so that saving cannot outpace it, retain acquisitions that apply
themselves for the rest of the attempt, disclose the value as a readable state
before it is felt, and gate the region's only exit behind a charge that advances
only while the player holds a disclosed position for a declared minimum.

## Novelty and variants

- Hades shares the run-and-modifier substrate almost exactly, but escalates by
  chamber and by a chosen persistent setting, so its clock is free and taking
  time is rewarded.
- Vampire Survivors shares the run clock and the build display, but its pressure
  is an authored wave schedule indexed to stage minutes and does not price the
  world, and its objective is to survive a duration rather than to leave.
- Slay the Spire shares the persistent run modifiers exactly and escalates by act
  and node, with no clock at all.
- Deep Rock Galactic shares an extraction event that closes a gathering phase,
  but its pressure responds to the objective rather than rising on its own.
- Survivor, item, environment, tier names and exact rates remain parameters.
