## Decision structure

The selected ordinary encounter alternates discrete command resolution and
continuous steering during enemy attacks. ACT-019 selects among offered
commands; the route never executes FIGHT and does not reintroduce the
withdrawn graded attack Action ACT-261. In the admitted ACT-dependent branch,
non-damaging interactions prepare opponent eligibility and the interface marks
it before any release is committed. Eligibility alone removes nobody. Valid
committed Spare removes eligible opponents without their kill EXP; a group
closes only when no opponents remain. This does not assert zero GOLD or an
exact currency amount. The authored objective additionally requires no kills
throughout the run and retained state after save/reload, without assuming a
visible initial KILLS counter. The exact opponent and native
transition/reward/save trace remain to be verified for build 4279148;
initially spareable monsters, damage-induced eligibility and special bosses
are not silently added.

## Combination match boundary

A command menu, dodge phase or spare option alone is insufficient. The
selected ordinary branch requires distinct command/evasion regimes, an offered
but unexecuted destructive command, ACT-dependent eligibility preparation, its
precommit marker and valid committed non-killing release. Marker and release
are not substitutes. Whole-battle closure requires that no opponents remain;
withholding kill EXP does not require zero GOLD. The authored no-kill
save/reload requirement remains, without a visible initial KILLS assumption.
Exact build-specific transitions, rewards and persistence remain outstanding.
Initially spareable, damage-induced and special-boss branches are outside this
analytical branch.
