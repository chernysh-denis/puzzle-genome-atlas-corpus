This six-gene set is a proper subset of the supporting ten-gene Echochrome
genome. Speed control, generic contact acquisition, general current-state
visibility and real-time scheduling support the bounded stage but do not define
its camera-to-projection-to-autonomous-route decision structure.

## Decision structure

The Walker advances without directional commands. The player instead orbits a
camera around fixed three-dimensional paths until their screen projection
creates a useful relation: remote endpoints may coincide, or a nearer element
may hide a discontinuity. The live projected image discloses the same relation
that the traversal system treats as authoritative. The autonomous Walker then
crosses that temporary topology and contacts every required echo. Moving the
camera can invalidate the relation even though no world-coordinate path moved.

## Combination match boundary

A rotatable camera alone is insufficient. The player must lack ordinary direct
locomotion control, the walker must advance autonomously, and the current
screen-space coincidence or occlusion must determine its legal traversal over
otherwise fixed three-dimensional route geometry. Four discrete projection
states, direct avatar steering, physical platform movement, image-generated
geometry and passive optical illusions remain outside the boundary.
