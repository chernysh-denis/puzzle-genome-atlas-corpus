## Decision structure

The player moves a currently rule-controlled object and pushes visible word
objects into or out of grammatical alignment. The system reparses the text,
reassigns properties such as control and victory, and evaluates later actions
under the newly enacted rules.

## Combination match boundary

A puzzle does not instantiate this combination merely because it displays or
changes rules. The rule syntax must be visible manipulable state, spatial edits
must activate or deactivate it, and the resulting rules must be able to
reassign both what receives player input and what state counts as victory.
