## Decision structure

Each side alternately relocates one controlled piece under class-specific
movement rules. Every candidate move must preserve its own king, while the
long-term objective is to place the opposing king under an unavoidable attack.
The opponent is an independent decision-maker, not an automatic system step.

## Combination match boundary

A turn-based board game does not instantiate this combination merely because
it has two sides or captures. It must couple side-owned control, heterogeneous
movement, own-royal safety, alternating opposing decisions and an unavoidable
attack objective.
