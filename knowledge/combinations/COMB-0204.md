## Decision structure

One immutable loaner packet supplies the hand and field economy. The active
player spends a shared Normal Summon/Set allowance, creates delayed face-down
responses and chooses attacks within ordered phases. Eligible activations open
alternating Spell-Speed response opportunities; the closed Chain resolves in
reverse before the phase resumes. Position-based battle and card text alter
Life Points and zones until a legal loss closes the Duel and clears the Solo
chapter.

## Combination match boundary

A generic card hand, turn order or opponent-loss terminal alone is
insufficient. A supporting ruleset must admit a fixed non-editable Solo packet,
share Normal Summon and Set authority, delay face-down interaction, form typed
response links that settle as one closed reverse-order Chain, resolve separate
position-bound attacks and retain an explicit authored clear result.

## Novelty and variants

- Magic: The Gathering Arena shares concealed hands and a single-game loss,
  but it grows typed land mana and repeatedly resolves one top stack object
  between priority rounds instead of settling a closed Spell-Speed Chain.
- Inscryption uses paired lanes, Blood sacrifices and a relative damage scale;
  it lacks phases, Chain Links and Life Point battle calculation.
- Slay the Spire uses one planning hand before autonomous hostile intent and
  persistently mutates its run deck outside combat.
- Other Master Duel Solo gates, constructed queues and evolving events require
  separate fixed packets and version boundaries.
