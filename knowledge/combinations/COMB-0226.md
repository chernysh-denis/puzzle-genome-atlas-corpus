## Decision structure

Two humans retain different protagonists and can inspect both live panes. The
current chisel holder advances a personal fixture sequence only between guard
inspections; the partner tracks the approach window; exposed work must be
cancelled before inspection. After Leo completes his fixture, a direct handoff
transfers the one chisel to Vincent without resetting Leo's state. Both personal
openings and both departures are required before the retained story terminal.

## Combination match boundary

Generic local co-op, a shared screen or one stealth task is insufficient. A
supporting ruleset must require two human-owned persistent roles, expose their
live views, make one exclusive cooperative tool advance retained personal
fixture stages, transfer that tool directly between partners, require active
work to be concealed before hostile inspection and join both sequences at one
bounded authored terminal.
