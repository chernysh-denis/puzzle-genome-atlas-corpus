# COMB-0267 — Space, elapsed time and cargo value are one gathering decision

- Claim status: `Strong Pattern`
- Evidence quality: `Corroborated`
- Confidence: `High`
- Corpus role: verified interaction pattern
- Novelty assessment: no exact duplicate among 266 prior combinations; this is
  the corpus's first interaction in which the clock that bounds a gathering trip
  is advanced only by the player's own travel and actions, and in which the same
  spent time simultaneously shortens the way home and reduces the value of what
  is already packed.

## Combination gene set

`ACT-219 + ACT-245 + ACT-261 + ACT-424 + SYS-597 + SYS-816 + CON-394 + CON-612 + INF-326 + OBJ-163 + TIM-002`

This is a strict eleven-gene subset of the fifteen-gene DREDGE genome. Steering
the carrier, addressing authored characters, the mental-state meter, the shop's
price disclosure and real-time input remain outside the distinctive interaction,
because they describe how the trip is conducted and bounded rather than what
makes space, elapsed time and cargo value answer each other.

## Decision structure

The field yields nothing until it is reached, and reaching it costs the only
clock in play — a clock that moves solely because the player moved. Each
acquisition is committed against a moving indicator inside a disclosed zone, so
a well-timed input returns a larger item; the item is then offered to a bounded
grid and must be positioned and rotated until its footprint fits free cells.
That grid is also where installed capability lives: its cells are typed by
hardware class and some are unusable, so a better engine and a fuller hold
compete for one surface, and cargo may squat on the typed cells no hardware yet
occupies. Meanwhile every item already carried is ageing against the same clock
and crossing declared condition states that cut its value. The interface shows
the clock's position, the free cells and each item's condition together, so one
more acquisition is always priced three ways at once: does it fit, can it be
brought back before the declared hour, and what does the delay take from the
rest. The trip settles only when the circuit closes at the buyer with the cargo
still undegraded.

## Combination match boundary

A grid inventory, a day-night cycle, a fishing minigame or a perishable
resource alone is insufficient. A supporting ruleset must advance its world
clock only in proportion to the player's own travel and declared actions,
gather material through a timed input whose accuracy grades the result, require
each acquisition to be positioned and rotated into free cells of a bounded grid
that also hosts installed capability on typed cells, reduce the value of carried
material across declared states as that same clock advances, expose clock, free
space and carried condition together, and settle on a closed round trip to a
fixed buyer completed before a declared hour.

## Novelty and variants

- Don't Starve Together shares the perishable-goods boundary and the
  mental-state meter exactly, but its world clock advances whether the survivor
  acts or not, and its inventory has no spatial packing decision.
- Resident Evil 4 (2023 remake) shares the carried-grid manipulation and the
  footprint legality, but nothing in its case decays and no clock is spent to
  fill it.
- American Truck Simulator shares a load returned to a fixed receiver under a
  clock, but its cargo is supplied rather than gathered, has no spatial form and
  does not lose value while the clock runs.
- Deep Rock Galactic shares gathering that must be physically carried out, but
  its quota is a number rather than an arrangement and its pressure is a
  hostile population.
- Species, buyer, grid dimensions, boundary hour and exact value fractions
  remain parameters.
