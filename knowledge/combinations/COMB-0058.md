## Decision structure

The player sees an exact per-cell recipe and a finite multiset of typed food
footprints. Each selected piece may be oriented and placed once. Its covered
blocks replace the current ingredients, so every shared cell is controlled by
the last footprint that reaches it. Success requires consuming the complete
piece inventory in an order and geometry whose final visible grid exactly
reconstructs the recipe.

## Combination match boundary

A game does not instantiate this interaction merely by showing a target or
placing polyominoes. It must disclose both the complete supplied footprint set
and exact target, require every finite piece to be used, allow a later footprint
to overwrite earlier addressed contents and evaluate the resulting visible
configuration after discrete placements. Non-overlapping packing, renewable
tile supplies, repeated machine production and free-form collage without an
exact target fall outside the boundary.
