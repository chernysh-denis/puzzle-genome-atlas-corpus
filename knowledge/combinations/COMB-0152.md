## Interaction

Three players commit non-duplicated Legends and one Jumpmaster-led insertion
into a stochastic 60-player field. Ground and deathbox loot builds two bounded
weapons, compatible ammunition and attachments; Marked hop-up progress follows
its weapon object, while each weapon accepts at most one corrupted attachment.
Live sight, sound and deliberate pings reveal only partial enemy and route
state. Damage crosses armour and health into a shielded knocked state, where an
eligible ally may revive before death. After death, a survivor can instead use
the current Core deathbox or Banner-and-Beacon recovery chain. Simultaneously,
EVO thresholds expose Legend perks and successive Ring phases contract the
legal area. Full squad elimination removes a team; the last squad still able to
participate becomes Champion.
