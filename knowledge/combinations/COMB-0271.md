# COMB-0271 — Convert continuous surface coverage and gate task settlement

- Claim status: `Pattern`
- Evidence quality: `Corroborated`
- Confidence: `High`
- Corpus role: verified interaction pattern
- Novelty assessment: no exact duplicate among 270 prior combinations; this is
  the first portable relation in which a moving applicator writes accepted
  persistent treatment and that same target-coverage measure gates the bounded
  task's settlement.

## Combination gene set

`SYS-630 + CON-516`

This is a strict two-gene subset of both the fifteen-gene Farming Simulator 25
genome and the ten-gene PowerWash Simulator genome. Carrier controls, material
legality, information, objective wording and payment transitions remain outside
the combination because they differ independently.

## Decision structure

The player moves a compatible active applicator over an assigned target. The
system intersects its current footprint or stream with eligible surface state,
persists newly accepted treatment and advances task progress only for that new
treatment. The bounded task remains unsettled until the accepted treated area
reaches its declared threshold. Position, path, coverage gaps and redundant
overlap therefore affect both immediate state conversion and terminal legality.

## Analysed games

- [`GAME-0196` — Farming Simulator 25](../games/a-f/farming-simulator-25.md)
- [`GAME-0279` — PowerWash Simulator](../games/m-r/powerwash-simulator.md)

## Combination match boundary

A progress bar, area effect or completion threshold alone is insufficient. A
supporting ruleset must continuously map the applicator's traversed footprint
or stream onto newly accepted persistent surface state and use accepted target
coverage to gate settlement of the same bounded task. Applicator carrier,
target geometry, consumed material, partial or total threshold and automatic or
explicit collection are parameters.

## Novelty and variants

- Farming Simulator 25 couples a filled borrowed vehicle implement to an
  assigned field, consumes fertilizer, accepts a partial coverage threshold and
  requires a later explicit `Collect` action.
- PowerWash Simulator couples a handheld unlimited-water stream to named vehicle
  parts, accepts each part at the job's total-clean tolerance and settles the
  job automatically when the last component completes.
- Exact-once surface puzzles are outside the relation: redundant overlap here
  is legal but unproductive, not an attempt-ending violation.
- Discrete repair, harvesting and object-damage systems are outside the relation
  because they do not persist the applicator's traversed area as task progress.
