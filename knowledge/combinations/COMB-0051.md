This eight-gene set is a proper subset of both complete supporting genomes.
It isolates the shared live-demand pressure without treating ordered metro
lines, trains, passengers, branching roads or house-bound cars as equivalent.

## Decision structure

While simulation time advances, random service nodes and unpreviewed demand
expand a persistent network problem. The player adapts finite reusable
infrastructure to maintain service and maximise completed trips. Excess demand
at any one node starts a recoverable warning, but allowing that overload to
persist ends the scored session.

## Combination match boundary

Random growth alone is insufficient. New nodes and demand must arrive during
live progression, finite reusable network assets must constrain adaptation,
future growth must remain unpreviewed, sustained local overload must terminate
the score run and successful service must increase that score. The exact
transport representation is deliberately outside the combination.
