## Combination match boundary

A game does not instantiate this interaction merely because it asks for three
matching objects or uses coloured symbols. It must commit exactly three visible
cards, test every coordinate by the same all-equal / all-different ternary
relation and complete by identifying an accepted subset. Ranked poker hands,
pair matching, full-grid assignments and value-changing match-three boards fall
outside the boundary.
