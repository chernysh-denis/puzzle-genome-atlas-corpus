## Decision structure

The survivor converts partial local cues and finite shared loot into a body
that can keep moving. Concrete item pairs define crafting, repair and loading;
condition and contamination decide whether food, water or treatment helps.
Infected follow perceived cues, while nearby humans may receive speech or a
gesture without becoming trustworthy. Blood loss and shock can suspend control
without yet ending the life. The server world keeps changing through all of
these actions, and only lethal death closes the current identity.

## Combination match boundary

Generic open-world survival is insufficient. A supporting ruleset must place
one fresh identity in a changing shared entity economy, make concrete held-item
compatibility, metabolism, condition-sensitive intake, infected perception,
typed treatable illness, local untrusted human communication and recoverable
unconsciousness jointly causal, then end that identity permanently without
requiring a positive victory.

## Novelty and variants

- Project Zomboid shares one-life metabolism, local zombie perception and
  permanent death, but its reviewed Apocalypse scope uses a retained local
  save, moodles, body-part treatment, calendar attrition and incurable Knox
  Infection rather than a shared Central Economy, proximity negotiation and
  blood/shock unconsciousness.
- Rust shares persistent online survival and human threat, but its reviewed
  scope centres claimed building, upkeep, raiding, offline vulnerability,
  respawn fixtures and a scheduled wipe rather than one identity ending the
  analytical life.
- PUBG shares loot, firearms and one-life risk, but its bounded match creates a
  shrinking safe area and a positive last-survivor victory.
