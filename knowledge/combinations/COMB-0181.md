## Decision structure

The player directly shapes crowd geometry by moving one survivor while several
weapons execute their own cooldown and attack geometry. Defeated enemies become
spatial experience that must be collected; thresholds interrupt live pressure
with a constrained item draft whose choice rewrites later automatic coverage.
Boss chests can convert a mature weapon/counterpart pair into an evolution.
These loops must grow the build faster than the authored wave schedule without
losing the single health pool before the stage reaches its completion clock.

## Combination match boundary

Automatic combat alone is insufficient. A supporting ruleset must let player
movement shape automatic weapon coverage, convert defeated hostiles into
spatially collected threshold progression, interrupt the same live stage with
persistent run-build choices and test survival against an authored fixed-time
terminal. Evolution is included as a gated mid-run weapon substitution, not as
the success objective.

## Novelty and variants

- The Binding of Isaac uses direct attacks, room routing and pedestal items
  rather than a cooldown-only portfolio fed by Experience Gems.
- Loop Hero uses an autonomous fixed route and player-placed world cards rather
  than free movement, spatial pickup routing and paused level drafts.
- Endless, Arcana, co-op, Adventure and DLC variants change the terminal or
  build authority and remain outside this combination.
