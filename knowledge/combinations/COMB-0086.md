This eight-gene set is a proper subset of the supporting thirteen-gene
Machinarium genome. Generic component manipulation, applying the finished rig,
visible scene state, fixture reveal and self-paced timing support execution but
do not define the request–exchange–combination–restoration decision chain.

## Decision structure

The player changes the articulated avatar's reach to collect a specifically
requested item, gives that item to a character in exchange for a missing
capability component, collects two further scene items and combines them into
one fixture-compatible tool. That tool restores the final required avatar
component and makes the bounded scene exit traversable.

## Analysed games

- [`GAME-0086` — Machinarium](../games/m-r/machinarium.md)

## Combination match boundary

A scene does not instantiate this combination merely because it has an
inventory, dialogue, crafting or a blocked exit. The requested item must be
disclosed through the character interaction, its deliberate hand-in must grant
an avatar capability component, two held items must be combined into a new
tool identity, and the staged chain must culminate in restoring the required
avatar set and leaving the bounded scene.
