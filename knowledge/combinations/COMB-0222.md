## Decision structure

Account history first selects whether a newly created character must enter the
revamped onboarding route. In the fixed fresh-account branch, the player follows
staged guidance through direct movement, contextual actions and live combat.
Completion then records the tutorial, grants `Distant Memory` and replaces the
guided route with authority to select one of several otherwise excluded
scenario/server rulesets.

## Combination match boundary

A generic action tutorial or menu unlock is insufficient. A supporting ruleset
must gate mandatory onboarding by prior account/character history, teach direct
real-time combat through staged instructions, and settle completion into both a
retained grant and explicit authority to choose the next world ruleset.

## Novelty and variants

- Aion Classic closes one personal tutorial after counters and cube rewards;
  completion does not open a choice among separate scenario rulesets.
- World of Warcraft routes a chosen fresh character through quests and a
  follower-filled dungeon; the account history does not select a universal
  pre-realm tutorial branch.
- Skyrim converts one authored captivity route into open-world control but
  does not make its ending a server/scenario ruleset chooser.
