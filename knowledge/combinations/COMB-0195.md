Generic navigation, inventory, pickup, durability, skills, death recovery,
building stability, day/night ecology and interface genes remain outside the
distinctive first-Forsaken chain.

## Decision structure

The player reads a fixed Vegvisir to select the nearest generated Eikthyr altar,
then converts gathered Meadows resources into a supported covered Workbench,
Rested capacity, three-food survival bounds and a combat loadout. Two matching
Deer Trophies open Eikthyr's live encounter. Defeat guarantees the progression
trophy, but only carrying it back and applying it to the matching Sacrificial
Stone makes the first Forsaken Power available.

## Combination match boundary

Procedural survival, crafting or a boss alone is insufficient. A supporting
ruleset must map authored guidance onto a generated boss fixture, require a
temporary food/rest-and-production packet, consume an exact matching offering,
emit a guaranteed boss key and settle that key at a separate matching fixture
for retained capability availability.

## Novelty and variants

- Minecraft shares seeded survival production and boss-gated progression but
  locates its scoped stronghold through thrown Eyes and ends after a portal/boss
  sequence rather than mounting a guaranteed trophy for selectable power.
- Terraria shares preparation and a summoned live boss but has tile housing,
  night eligibility and NPC admission rather than a wayfinder/trophy fixture pair.
- Later Valheim Forsaken reuse the trophy/power frame with different biomes,
  offerings, production chains and fights; they are variants, not inherited here.
