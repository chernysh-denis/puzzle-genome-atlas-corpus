## Decision structure

The player picks up one existing rigid chess piece and aims it toward a chosen
background. While it is held, the system moves it to the farthest pose whose
entire sampled volume clears world geometry and rescales it in proportion to
camera depth, preserving the apparent pickup extent. The visible held piece is
the exact pending physical result. The player releases that rescaled body over
a pressure plate; sustained occupancy holds the linked exit open while the sole
required actor leaves the room.

## Combination match boundary

Ordinary object carrying or a pressure plate is insufficient. One existing
held body must acquire a persistent physical scale from a collision-bounded
camera sightline, expose that exact candidate before release, and then use its
committed occupancy to enable the actor's fixed exit. Fixed-size cubes, camera
zoom, global scale changes, image-created geometry, snap-state route graphs and
post-release-only collision checks remain outside the boundary.
