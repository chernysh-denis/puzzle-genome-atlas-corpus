## Decision structure

The fixed Murchad start makes character qualities, marriage, council offices,
titles and vassals sources of legal and military options. A visible claim
authorises one Desmond war. Realm obligations gather troops; map orders create
battle and siege; occupation and other admitted results update war score.
Enforce Demands applies the title relation and the guided tutorial explicitly
records completion before open-ended campaign play resumes.

## Combination match boundary

A relationship simulation, realm economy or real-time army battle alone is
insufficient. A supporting ruleset must couple inspectable character
eligibility and office/family choices to title-defined obligations, one legal
claim-based war, gathered realm troops, occupation-derived war score and a
system-authored tutorial settlement.
