## Decision structure

The player reads three persistent collection schemas and commits compatible
inventory quantities to their displayed slots. Two groups require every shown
type; the third accepts any two distinct options from four. Filled slots persist
across visits, each completed group is retained, and completing all three marks
the enclosing room complete. The resulting infrastructure change is scheduled
at the day boundary rather than granted as a held item: on the next day the
minecart network is permanently available.

## Combination match boundary

A fetch quest does not instantiate this combination merely because it lists
several items. Requirements must be filled through persistent typed slots,
multiple subordinate groups must feed one aggregate completion predicate, at
least one group may use an explicit distinct k-of-n quota, and the aggregate
must schedule a persistent world service rather than construct an inventory
object or grant only the individual group reward.
