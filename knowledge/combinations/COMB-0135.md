The 21-gene set is a strict subset of Counter-Strike 2's 30-gene genome. It
omits general navigation, direct attack, weapon selection, reload, broad
real-time combat, dropped-item pickup, inventory capacity, carried-equipment
display and real-time scheduling while retaining the reusable interaction that
joins round economy to bomb counterplay.

## Interaction

Teams purchase and redistribute equipment, infer opponents through partial
local/team information and contest a bomb objective under one-life rounds.
Elimination, timeout, plant, defuse and explosion feed a carried economy;
halftime reverses roles while preserving the score toward a finite match result.
