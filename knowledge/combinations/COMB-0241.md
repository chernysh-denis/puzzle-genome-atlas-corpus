## Decision structure

The player acquires target and evidence knowledge through a held scanner,
retains actor marks and reads directional awareness before contact. One inert
diversion can alter a patrol. A rear approach permits stealth neutralisation;
aimed legal authority can instead create compliance, but only continuing
player/partner coverage preserves it until living arrest. Evidence and custody
outcomes remain visible and feed an authored episode that closes only at its
own evaluated successor settlement.

## Combination match boundary

Ordinary tagged stealth, a civilian hostage command, a scripted surrender or a
shooter mission with incidental evidence is insufficient. A matching ruleset
must combine deliberate tactical analysis, retained actor marks, readable
detection, non-damaging diversion, hostile compliance that depends on ongoing
coverage, legal living custody, evidence/evaluation feedback and a discrete
authored episode terminal.

## Novelty and variants

- Far Cry 3 shares marks, diversion, detection and rear neutralisation but has
  no authority-backed hostile compliance, living arrest or case evaluation.
- PAYDAY 2/3 can control civilians or hostages but use heist alarm, restraint
  and escape-economy rules rather than scanned hostile custody.
- A later Hardline episode would be a parameter variant only if it preserves
  the same build, authority/evidence loop and episode terminal.
