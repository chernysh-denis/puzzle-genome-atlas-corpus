## Decision structure

The player launches one fixed Titan and three-weapon configuration into a solo
Normal Fireteam Op. Movement, aimed hits, finite magazines/reserves and typed
Void abilities resolve continuously while the interface exposes personal,
objective and score state. A Ghost interaction begins an authored defence;
subsequent combat clears the Walker gate and admits Sepiks. Final boss defeat
settles the same run into activity completion, a declared grade/reward tier and
an end chest, without a separate extraction decision.

## Combination match boundary

A first-person shooter, boss encounter, loadout, score or reward chest alone is
insufficient. A supporting ruleset must join prelaunch bounded weapon/ability
selection to live ammunition/readiness pressure, a player-activated authored
defence and later combat gates, then convert the final boss and objective state
into one explicitly graded reward-chest terminal without mandatory extraction.
