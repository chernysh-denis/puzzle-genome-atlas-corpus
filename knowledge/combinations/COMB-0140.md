The 45-gene set is a strict subset of Project Zomboid's 51-gene genome. It
omits generic navigation, quick-slot selection, direct attack input, ranged-hit
and live-combat resolution, and carried hotbar presentation while retaining the
complete character-build, bodily, perception, fortification, calendar and
permanent-life structure.

## Interaction

A balanced occupation and trait build enters one seeded but mostly authored
world. Local vision and emitted noise determine which zombies can pursue, while
timed looting, crafting, treatment, sleep, barricading and farming remain
vulnerable to that changing threat. Body-part wounds and graded moodles alter
what the survivor can safely do; ordinary care may stop bleeding but cannot
reverse established Knox Infection. Skill growth turns exposed work time into
new competence, barricades turn material into persistent but destructible
geometry, and the calendar removes inherited abundance through utility loss,
spoilage and seasonal crop rules. No shelter or elapsed-day milestone ends the
run: irreversible death fixes the result of that one life.
