# COMB-0265 — Assembled connectivity and ordered activation decide a trajectory

- Claim status: `Strong Pattern`
- Evidence quality: `Corroborated`
- Confidence: `High`
- Corpus role: verified interaction pattern
- Novelty assessment: no exact duplicate among 264 prior combinations; this is
  the corpus's first interaction in which the player builds the body they then
  fly, its own attachment topology decides which reservoirs feed which engine,
  and success is a property of the projected trajectory measured against a
  published altitude rather than a place, a target set or an opponent.

## Combination gene set

`ACT-028 + ACT-392 + ACT-444 + SYS-036 + SYS-811 + SYS-812 + CON-610 + INF-324 + OBJ-161 + TIM-003`

This is a strict ten-gene subset of the eleven-gene Kerbal Space Program genome.
Only the untimed pace of the assembly phase remains outside the distinctive
interaction: it describes the conditions under which the craft may be built
rather than what makes its connectivity, its ordered activation and its
projected path answer each other. `TAXONOMY_CHANGE_032` removed the
anchor-and-port placement legality that had also been outside it, because that
reuse was never evidenced for this product; the combination's own gene set never
contained it and is unchanged.

## Decision structure

The untimed phase carries almost all of the risk. Parts are placed against
compatible anchor points, and that geometry is not decoration: it decides which
reservoirs each engine can draw from once the craft is running. The same phase
distributes those parts across an ordered activation list that may be reordered
freely at rest and then, in flight, is consumed one irreversible step at a time.
Launched, the craft integrates thrust, drag, gravity and collision continuously,
while burning propellant removes mass so the same throttle accelerates harder
later. A dedicated view converts the present motion into the path the craft
would follow unpowered and labels that path's extremes, turning a continuous
physical state into two numbers the player can compare with the attracting
body's published atmospheric depth. The attempt settles when those numbers
describe a closed repeating path clear of that depth and the state survives a
reload.

## Combination match boundary

A construction phase, a physics simulation, a piloted craft or an altitude
threshold alone is insufficient. A supporting ruleset must let the player
assemble the body they will control, make that assembly's connectivity decide
resource flow rather than a global pool, reduce craft mass continuously as the
resource is consumed, author an ordered activation list at rest that is consumed
irreversibly under pressure, project the present motion into a path whose
extremes are exposed before acting, and settle on whether that projected path
closes above a published boundary.

## Novelty and variants

- Human: Fall Flat shares continuous force-constrained body dynamics under
  real-time control, but its body is authored in advance and its objectives are
  places.
- Infinifactory shares the untimed editable design phase and anchor-and-port
  placement legality, but its committed run executes automatically and is judged
  by delivered product.
- STAR WARS: Squadrons shares the generalised four-axis flight control, but its
  craft is fixed by the mission and neither its mass nor its connectivity is a
  player decision.
- Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 shares continuous atmospheric flight, but
  resolves it through fixed-wing aerodynamics and cockpit instruments.
- Part, engine, body, altitude and exact quantity names remain parameters.
