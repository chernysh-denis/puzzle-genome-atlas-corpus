## Decision structure

The player deliberately turns visible living actors into retained tactical
marks, then positions an inert stimulus to redirect one eligible observer.
Stealth may therefore remove part of the finite set before local perception
escalates into live combat. Mission-critical actors must remain viable; once
the closed set is cleared, the site changes owner and retains its travel,
service and successor affordances.

## Combination match boundary

Generic stealth, automatic enemy highlighting, a damaging throwable or a room
that merely reopens is insufficient. A supporting ruleset must admit deliberate
optical actor marks that persist beyond direct sight, a player-positioned
non-damaging perception stimulus, recoverable alert escalation, closed hostile
clearance and a persistent allied service-node conversion.
