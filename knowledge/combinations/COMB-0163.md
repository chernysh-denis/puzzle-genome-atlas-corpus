## Decision structure

Arthur routes on foot or with one persistent saddled horse, manages paired
cores and degradable firearms, reads and intervenes in witness-to-law
transitions, carries honour and bounty consequences, transfers personal value
into shared camp capacity and completes the authored Chapter 2 gate to move the
gang from Horseshoe Overlook to Clemens Point.

## Combination match boundary

An open-world action game, horse game or crime system alone is insufficient.
The ruleset must bind a persistent cared-for mount and saddle cargo, paired
body resources, maintainable firearm condition, a preventable witness report,
active pursuit plus retained regional bounty, independent honour, shared camp
investment and an authored camp-to-camp story gate in one continuous packet.

Grand Theft Auto V shares live combat, cover, wanted search, missions and
vehicles but lacks the persistent cared-for horse, witness pre-report phase,
regional bounty debt and shared camp ledger. Monster Hunter Wilds shares live
hunting, harvesting, mount travel and weapon maintenance but lacks ambient
social-law and honour consequences.
