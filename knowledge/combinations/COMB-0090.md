## Decision structure

The player permanently credits the three gate-addressed green tetrominoes from
separate solved challenges. Those exact identities become the finite selectable
inventory of one visible 4 × 3 arranger. The player may select, quarter-turn and
place each piece, but every footprint must stay inside the board and the final
layout must cover each cell exactly once. A gapless non-overlapping cover opens
the linked traversal gate immediately and persistently, without an inventory
stack payment, subordinate bundle hierarchy or calendar boundary.

## Combination match boundary

Collecting enough generic keys does not instantiate this combination. The
collected objects must retain distinct rigid footprints, populate one addressed
finite arranger, remain player-orientable and placeable, and require an exact
non-overlapping cover whose acceptance immediately changes persistent world
access. A recipe that overwrites target contents, an automatic collection
threshold or a scalar currency gate remains outside the boundary.
