## Decision structure

The player repeatedly selects an orthogonal boundary to propose one 1 × 2
pair. Every accepted final pair consumes both endpoint cells exactly once and
also consumes one unordered numeric pair identity exactly once. Completion
therefore solves two coupled exact-cover universes: all cells and the complete
pair-type inventory. A non-overlapping geometric tiling is insufficient when
it duplicates one numeric type and omits another.

## Combination match boundary

A domino tiling does not instantiate this combination merely by covering a
rectangle with adjacent 1 × 2 pieces. The selected pieces must additionally
use a declared complete unordered pair-type inventory exactly once. Conversely,
a symbol-assignment exact cover does not qualify unless one player action
creates the two-cell spatial relation that consumes both endpoints.
