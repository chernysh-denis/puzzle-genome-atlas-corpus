## Decision structure

The player first converts a timed ambush and restrained civilians into a viable
authored road. A control device then makes the transport advance only from
eligible human proximity while route fixtures and police pressure compete for
attention. A short interruptible breach exposes heavy valuables; five spatial
deposits enable escape, and escape settles the retained payout.

## Combination match boundary

A generic escort mission, shooter or loot extraction is insufficient. A
supporting ruleset must prepare route fixtures, advance an objective transport
through eligible proximity, breach a secured source, convert heavy carried
objects into spatial credit and make minimum credit plus escape settle value.
