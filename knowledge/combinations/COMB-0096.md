This six-gene set is a proper subset of the supporting ten-gene Maquette
genome. Direct walking, generic rigid-body physics, complete local visibility
and live scheduling support execution but do not define recursive-scale object
identity or its bridge-to-key transformation.

## Decision structure

The player reads the central model and surrounding courtyard as simultaneous
scale instances of one authoritative world. Moving a reachable representation
updates every homologous representation. Carrying the key across one recursion
boundary reindexes its scale: the same identity becomes a giant traversable
bridge, then a tiny usable key, then a bridge again. The route succeeds only
when the house is unlocked and its gap is spanned at the required scale.

## Combination match boundary

Merely displaying a miniature, cloning an object or rescaling a held object is
insufficient. At least two visible scale instances must share authoritative
pose state, crossing a nested-world boundary must change the object's
player-relative physical scale without changing identity, pickup must depend
on that relative scale, and the reindexed object must enable the fixed exit.
