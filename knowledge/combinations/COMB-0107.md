## Decision structure

The player repositions a finite set of intact traversal panels, then separately
pairs compatible unused door or ladder ports across those panels. The system
treats each accepted pair as a bidirectional transfer edge. After returning to
direct avatar control, the player walks, jumps and climbs through the fixed
panel interiors, enters the constructed links and reaches the designated exit.

## Analysed games

- [`GAME-0107` — The Pedestrian](../games/s-z/the-pedestrian.md)

## Combination match boundary

Moving panels without explicit links is insufficient, as is drawing a graph
that no controlled actor traverses. A match must keep panel relocation distinct
from edge creation, enforce typed one-to-one inter-panel port compatibility,
transfer the directly controlled avatar through accepted pairs and use the
resulting route to reach a designated location.
