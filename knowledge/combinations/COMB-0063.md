## Decision structure

The player self-pacedly selects one persistent rigid vehicle and translates it
along its immutable horizontal or vertical axis. The full footprint and every
crossed cell must remain collision-free, so useful vacancies have to be passed
between intersecting rows and columns in dependency order. The puzzle completes
when these slides clear the designated block's invariant lane and that block
passes through its fixed boundary opening.

## Combination match boundary

A game does not instantiate this interaction merely because it contains cars,
blockers or a grid. It must directly relocate persistent multi-cell pieces,
preserve each piece's single movement axis, reject every occupied swept
footprint and require one designated slider to leave through a fixed boundary
gap. Global slides, avatar-pushed crates, rotatable falling pieces and road
networks fall outside the boundary.
