## Decision structure

The player independently marks permitted graph edges. Each choice contributes
to as many as two exact face-boundary counts while also changing vertex degree
and global component topology. A complete proposal succeeds only when every
number is exact and all selected edges form one non-empty simple cycle; local
counts alone can admit branches, paths or disconnected loops.

## Combination match boundary

A puzzle does not instantiate this combination merely by drawing a loop or by
counting edges. It must let the player persistently select fixed edges, impose
exact counts around marked faces and require the entire selected subgraph to be
exactly one simple cycle.
