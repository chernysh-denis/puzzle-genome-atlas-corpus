# COMB-0264 — Excavated travel whose deposited yield funds its own resupply

- Claim status: `Strong Pattern`
- Evidence quality: `Corroborated`
- Confidence: `High`
- Corpus role: verified interaction pattern
- Novelty assessment: no exact duplicate among 263 prior combinations; this is
  the corpus's first interaction in which carving terrain is the ordinary means
  of travel and the same act that collects the objective material, while that
  deposited material is also the only currency for the supplies needed to keep
  collecting it.

## Combination gene set

`ACT-008 + ACT-050 + ACT-093 + ACT-130 + ACT-161 + ACT-218 + ACT-443 + SYS-116 + SYS-215 + SYS-348 + SYS-407 + SYS-810 + CON-140 + CON-609 + OBJ-160 + TIM-003`

This is a strict sixteen-gene subset of the eighteen-gene Deep Rock Galactic
genome. Contextual authored-object interaction and the personal resource display
remain outside the distinctive interaction, because both describe how the
player reaches and reads the state rather than what makes excavation, the
deposited pool and the departure answer each other.

## Decision structure

Solid terrain is not an obstacle to route around but the route itself: carving a
continuous volume opens permanent traversable space, and when that volume
contained a deposit the same input collects the objective material. Material
counts for nothing until it is irreversibly deposited into a mobile receptacle
against a visible quota, and that same deposited pool is the only balance from
which resupply can be bought, so every restock is subtracted from progress. A
persistent autonomous companion works the parts of the cave the player cannot
reach, taking its task from the class of whatever the pointer selects while
engaging threats on its own, and its small countable stock of revive charges is
the only thing standing between a downing and immediate failure. Filling the
quota does not end the attempt; it only unlocks the departure call, after which
the tunnels the player dug become the retreat they must cross before the
transport leaves without them.

## Combination match boundary

Destructible terrain, a collection quota, a companion or an extraction point
alone is insufficient. A supporting ruleset must let carving terrain open
permanent traversable space and yield the objective material by the same action,
require that material to be deposited irreversibly against a declared quota
before it counts, fund its supply drops from that same deposited pool, run a
persistent companion that takes its task from the class of a pointed target,
gate continuation on that companion's finite revive charges, and complete only
when the filled quota is carried onto a transport that departs on its own clock.

## Novelty and variants

- Once Human shares first-person traversal, aimed attacks, authored-object
  interaction and real-time combat, but gathers from discrete sources in a
  persistent world whose geometry it keeps.
- Helldivers 2 shares the extraction call and boarding boundary, but its
  mission success is owned by the main objective alone and extraction is a
  separable departure bonus.
- Stardew Valley shares the collection-slot boundary, but its bundles persist in
  a safe hub rather than gating a single timed departure.
- Factorio shares hand extraction and placed autonomous defence, but its
  terrain is not the route and its resupply is not paid out of the objective.
- Mission, mineral, class, tool, companion and exact quantity names remain
  parameters.
