## Interaction

The player places staffed chains on islands whose fertilities permit their
crops, uses supplied basic needs to fill and explicitly upgrade residences, and
thereby changes the tiered workforce available to later factories. Cargo-slot
trade routes then cycle region-specific goods between islands. Visible needs,
workforce, storage and production state guide expansion until Investors,
electricity and delivered construction materials open the World’s Fair, whose
timed exhibition supplies produce the scoped completion.
