## Decision structure

The player traverses one authored route while preserving Health, finite weapon
ammunition and finite ability energy. One shared primary input belongs either
to the retained current weapon or to the retained current powered ability, so a
live toggle precedes each cross-channel tactic. A legal ability may directly
control a hostile, activate a compatible fixture or enter one connected medium
that propagates the typed effect to eligible occupants. Immediate carried
restoratives replenish only compatible missing meters. After the final finite
group is cleared, the dependent route admits a successor area whose autosave
and manual save can be restored.

## Combination match boundary

Generic first-person shooting, a cosmetic power selector, an elemental damage
bonus or a room with water is insufficient. A supporting ruleset must expose
mutually exclusive retained weapon and finite-resource ability channels on one
live input, accept a typed ability against direct and compatible environmental
targets, propagate its result through a connected medium, use immediate finite
stock to restore compatible personal meters and carry that resource loop
through ordered finite clearance to a restorable successor location.
