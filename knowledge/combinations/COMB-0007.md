## Decision structure

The player self-pacedly transfers exposed cards among ordered cascades,
single-card buffers and suit foundations. Temporary capacity determines which
blocking cards can be displaced, while tableau and foundation order determine
whether that displacement preserves a path to the target arrangement.

## Combination match boundary

A card puzzle does not instantiate this combination merely because it has
stacks. It must expose only one directly transferable end of each ordered
stack, provide bounded one-card temporary buffers, distinguish alternating
descending tableau construction from same-suit ascending completion and ask
the player to reconstruct the complete deck through self-paced transfers.
