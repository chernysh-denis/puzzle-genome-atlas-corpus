## Claim

[Observation | Corroborated | High] The player marks spatial work and authors
priorities rather than steering workers; eligible agents claim those errands
while consuming the same oxygen, food and thermal environment that their work
orders, buildings and automation are meant to stabilise.

## Causal structure

1. The player marks work, construction and staffing policy in a live material world.
2. Duplicants filter and rank reachable errands, then travel and execute them autonomously.
3. The same open cells redistribute gas and heat while agents consume oxygen,
   food and time and generate waste.
4. Construction, pipes, power and automation redirect those material flows.
5. Skills expand worker eligibility but raise morale expectation and therefore stress risk.
6. A local design fault can interrupt labour, which delays the repair that
   would restore life support, turning logistics into a feedback loop.

The set is a proper subset of the supporting genome: research, Printing Pod
offers, germs, asteroid generation, rockets and the Temporal Tear remain outside it.

## Boundary notes

- This is not generic factory automation: workers have individual needs,
  schedules, permissions, skills and stress responses.
- This is not generic colony happiness: atmosphere and heat are spatially
  conserved engineering inputs that directly gate survival and labour.
- This is not direct unit control: the player creates and ranks eligible work,
  while agents choose and path to errands.
- Exact consumption rates, element values and UI priority numbers are parameters.
