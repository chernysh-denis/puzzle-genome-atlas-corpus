## Claim

[Observation | Corroborated | High] The player authors spatial work,
priorities, storage filters and conditional production rather than steering
workers; eligible residents claim the resulting jobs and move material through
the designed fortress until stock conditions are satisfied.

## Causal structure

1. Spatial designations, construction plans and stock filters create jobs.
2. Labour permissions, priorities, reachability, tools and materials determine eligibility.
3. Autonomous residents claim, path to and complete the highest-ranked available jobs.
4. Hauling moves compatible items into accepted stores and linked consumers.
5. Conditional manager orders replenish declared stock rather than requiring
   every workshop job to be entered manually.
6. Delayed hauling or an ineligible worker can starve the same production loop
   that was meant to supply the fortress.

The set is a proper subset of the supporting genome: world generation,
resident memories, moods, trade, military policy, sieges and capital progression remain outside it.

## Boundary notes

- This is not direct unit control: the player creates eligible work and policy.
- This is not a workerless factory: jobs require individual residents, paths,
  tools, permissions and interruptions.
- This is not generic storage: filters and directed links causally determine hauling.
- Exact priority numbers, stock thresholds and workshop recipes are parameters.
