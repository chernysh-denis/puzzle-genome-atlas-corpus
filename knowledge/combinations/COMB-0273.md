# COMB-0273 — Surveyed stellar expansion into a first established colony

- Claim status: `Pattern`
- Evidence quality: `Corroborated`
- Confidence: `High`
- Corpus role: verified interaction pattern
- Novelty assessment: no exact duplicate among 272 prior combinations; this is
  the first portable relation in which a mobile survey creates the knowledge
  required for a paid territorial project, that project makes a habitat legally
  ownable, and a separately produced transport is then consumed into the first
  established off-origin settlement.

## Combination gene set

`ACT-189 + ACT-316 + SYS-551 + SYS-843 + SYS-844 + SYS-845 + CON-225 +
CON-417 + CON-626 + CON-627 + INF-081 + OBJ-170`

This is a strict 12-gene subset of the 19-gene Stellaris genome. Procedural
generation, research, recurring budget settlement, broad command-state
disclosure, speed control and the real-time clock remain outside the combination
because they may vary independently of the survey-to-colony dependency.

## Decision structure

The player directs a mobile survey agent until one target system becomes
persistent decision-quality knowledge. A different mobile constructor may then
perform the paid outpost project only while the target remains surveyed,
unowned and otherwise legal; completion creates territorial ownership rather
than a settlement. The player separately queues a founding transport at an
eligible owned production site. Only after that transport exists and the chosen
habitat is eligible inside owned territory can another destination-bearing
order consume the transport into colony development. The chain settles when the
new off-origin settlement becomes established and the polity remains available
for continued control.

## Analysed games

## Combination match boundary

A generic exploration, city founding, territorial purchase or colony theme is
insufficient. A supporting ruleset must separate all four state transitions:
mobile survey into persistent system knowledge; paid outpost construction into
territorial ownership; site-bound production of the founding carrier; and
carrier commitment into a time-bearing established settlement. System names,
map scale, resource amounts, vehicle appearance and settlement population are
parameters.

## Novelty and variants

- Stellaris uses a science ship, construction ship, starbase shipyard, colony
  ship and habitable planet on a generated hyperlane graph.
- An instant city placement that also claims surrounding territory omits the
  independent survey, outpost and transport transitions.
- A settlement founded in already known territory without a mobile survey or a
  produced consumable carrier is outside the relation.
