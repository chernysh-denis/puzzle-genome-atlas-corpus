Use the canonical [vocabulary, genome signature and comparison

## Analysis scope

- Version / ruleset: the original English DOS `The Ultimate DOOM` v1.9 rules
  and `DOOM.WAD` identified by size `12,408,292`, MD5
  `c4fe9fd920207691a9f493668e0a2083` and SHA-1
  `9b07b02ab3c275a6a7570c3f73cc20d63a0e3833`, lawfully accessible as the
  original version within the current Steam app `2280`. Bethesda documents
  that Steam owners can install the original separately through the beta
  selector; the current enhanced KEX application is not analysed.
- Structured analysis target: licensed Steam access to the original DOS
  Ultimate DOOM data and rules; see `GAME-0352` in
  [`knowledge/platforms/games.json`](../../platforms/games.json).
- Scenario: fresh single-player `Knee-Deep in the Dead` on `Hurt Me Plenty`,
  E1M1 `Hangar`, from the first controllable tic to the exit-switch settlement
  and E1M2 intermission handoff.
- Entry: one player begins E1M1 with 100 Health, no armour or key card, the fist
  and pistol, and 50 bullets. Nothing is carried from another map.
- Primary decision loop: read the local first-person view, sound, status bar and
  explored automap; move, strafe, turn or run; use a reachable door or switch;
  choose the fist, pistol or an acquired shotgun; aim horizontally and fire;
  evade or defeat sight- or noise-awakened hostiles; collect compatible Health,
  armour, weapon and ammunition pickups; navigate the ordinary authored route;
  open the exit room and use its switch.
- Positive terminal: the exit switch calls level completion, freezes the E1M1
  attempt into the intermission statistics and selects E1M2. E1M2 play itself
  is outside this packet.
- Failure terminal: zero Health puts the player into the death state; restarting
  E1M1 reconstructs the fresh level and player state. Manual save/load exists
  but no particular save or reload trace is admitted.
- Included: direct two-axis ground traversal, running and horizontal turning;
  the engine's fixed vertical auto-aim as a firing parameter rather than a
  separate player action; fist, pistol and normally obtainable shotgun;
  active-weapon selection; finite bullets and shells; contact pickups; local
  monster sight and propagated weapon-noise wake-up; real-time pursuit and
  combat; one continuous Health pool; green/blue armour protection-factor
  damage splitting; ordinary route doors, the exit switch, the explored-line
  automap and the E1M1-to-E1M2 handoff.
- Reproducible parameterisation: use the ordinary non-secret route on `Hurt Me
  Plenty`, without cheats or saves. Open the authored doors, survive any
  encountered hostiles, take or leave non-secret pickups as current state
  permits, enter the exit room and use the exit switch. Exact movement, aim,
  kills, damage, pickup choice, ammunition, Health, armour and completion time
  remain bounded attempt parameters.
- Excluded: all three E1M1 secrets, the Ultimate-only courtyard shortcut switch,
  secret lifts, optional 100% kills/items/secrets, keyed doors (E1M1 contains no
  required key), damaging-floor optimisation, barrel tactics, save/load, pause,
  cheats, demos and speedrun exploits; `Ultra-Violence`, `Nightmare!`, other
  skill placements, E1M2 and later maps, episodes 2–4, multiplayer, source
  ports, console ports, the 2019 rerelease, current KEX enhancements, add-ons,
  mods and later DOOM games.
- Potential scoped modules: one E1M1 secret-route packet; one later keyed route;
  one multiplayer map; the enhanced current product. None is inherited here.
- Direct-play status: not conducted. No entitlement, installation, DOS
  executable, IWAD, input trace, save, screenshot, video or audio was obtained
  or inspected. The licensed-access documents, preserved manual, pinned id
  Software source lineage and two written E1M1 references support a bounded
  reconstruction, not a claimed playthrough or byte-level executable audit.

## Mechanical decomposition

### Action Genes

- `ACT-008`: directly walk, strafe, turn and run the player body through E1M1
  rather than selecting an automatic destination. Jumping and crouching are not
  available in this original ruleset.
- `ACT-161`: aim the current fist or firearm horizontally and commit its strike
  or shot; engine-side vertical slope selection is a parameter, not a separate
  player-controlled axis.
- `ACT-164`: select one owned weapon as the active attack input.
- `ACT-341`: use a reachable ordinary door or the exit fixture, changing local
  route or terminal state when its authored operation is legal.
- Claims: `UDOOM-003`, `UDOOM-004`, `UDOOM-008`.

### System Behaviour Genes

- `SYS-057`: a dormant eligible monster that sees the player or receives
  propagated player noise acquires that target, turns, pursues and attacks.
- `SYS-215`: player and monsters exchange range-, cadence-, damage-, defence-
  and defeat-dependent effects while the level continues in real time.
- `SYS-222`: touching a compatible weapon, ammunition, Health or armour item
  transfers the accepted amount and removes the world pickup when applicable.
- `SYS-578`: hostile, projectile or hazardous damage reduces one continuous
  Health pool, accepted Health pickups restore missing points and zero Health
  enters the death state.
- `SYS-949`: each compatible hit saves the armour-type fraction using Doom's
  ruleset rounding, caps the spend by current armour, subtracts that reserve and
  immediately applies the unsaved remainder to Health. Green armour uses the
  one-third class and blue armour the one-half class.
- Resolution order: commands update movement, weapon or Use intent; world and
  monster thinkers advance; eligible sight/noise changes pursuit; shots and
  contact resolve; armour saves its share before Health receives the remainder;
  pickups update capped state; zero Health enters death; accepted exit use
  changes to intermission.
- Claims: `UDOOM-004`–`UDOOM-006`, `UDOOM-008`, `UDOOM-010`.

### Constraint Genes

- `CON-578`: pistol and shotgun attacks require enough compatible finite
  bullets or shells and spend their declared cost; accepted pickups refill only
  to the typed cap. The fist remains the no-ammunition fallback.
- Scarce route state: Health, armour reserve/class, bullets, shells, current
  weapon, current position and live hostile exposure.
- Claims: `UDOOM-004`, `UDOOM-006`.

### Information Genes

- `INF-073`: the status bar exposes owned weapons, the active weapon's current
  ammunition and all typed ammunition reserves before the next local action.
- `INF-115`: first-person sight, monster pose, movement, attacks, weapon effects
  and spatial sound expose local opponent state without an omniscient hostile
  map.
- `INF-119`: status-bar numerals and key slots expose current Health, armour and
  carried-card state; E1M1 keeps all card slots empty on its ordinary route.
- `INF-125`: the automap makes already mapped wall lines and the player marker
  inspectable before a route choice, while undiscovered geometry, things and
  secrets remain outside ordinary disclosure.
- Claims: `UDOOM-007`–`UDOOM-009`.

### Objective Genes

- `OBJ-026`: traverse the authored Hangar route, make the exit room reachable
  through ordinary doors and use its designated exit switch.
- Claims: `UDOOM-008`, `UDOOM-010`.

### Time Genes

- `TIM-003`: movement, monster thinking, pursuit, attacks, projectiles, doors,
  damage and pickups resolve on the live level clock between accepted inputs.
- Claims: `UDOOM-003`–`UDOOM-006`, `UDOOM-010`.

## Strategic and experiential structure

- Local decision: trade movement, line of sight, weapon choice and finite
  ammunition against nearby attackers, projectiles, current Health and armour.
- Medium-term planning: preserve bullets/shells and protection while reading the
  explored route, opening the next door and deciding which exposed pickups are
  worth their risk.
- Long-term structure: convert one hostile authored route into a reachable exit;
  neither every kill nor any secret is required.
- Common heuristics: keep lateral room against projectiles; wake only manageable
  sightlines; take the shotgun when safely available; use the automap after
  route uncertainty; preserve enough Health and ammunition for the exit room.
- Failure attribution: the status bar distinguishes Health, armour and ammo
  depletion, while local sight/sound and automap distinguish combat pressure
  from route error.
- Player-trust factors: the exact DOS data target, HMP placement class,
  non-secret route and no-direct-play disclosure prevent enhanced-port or
  walkthrough-only behaviour from entering the genome.
- Claims: `UDOOM-003`–`UDOOM-011`.

## Replay and variation

- What changes between attempts: movement/aim timing, awakened enemies, damage,
  kills, pickup choice, ammunition, Health, armour and completion time.
- Randomness or procedural generation: geometry and HMP placements are fixed;
  attack timing, pseudorandom damage/spread and actor interactions vary within
  the deterministic engine sequence. No procedural layout is admitted.
- Multiple viable strategies: pistol, fist and obtained shotgun can support
  different ordinary routes and pickup choices; secrets and the shortcut are
  optional modules, not completion requirements.
- Typical replay motive: faster completion, better survival/resources, more
  kills/items/secrets or a different route. Only ordinary exit completion
  enters this signature.
- Claims: `UDOOM-004`–`UDOOM-010`.

## Adjacent systems and history

- Direct successor relation: Quake preserves direct real-time traversal,
  weapon choice, finite ammunition, pickups, local hostile perception, Health,
  factor-split armour and a spatial exit, but makes vertical movement and aimed
  geometry fully three-dimensional and gives its scoped route button-linked
  moving mechanisms.
- Variants: shareware/registered Doom revisions, The Ultimate DOOM, console
  ports, source ports, 2019 and 2024 rereleases differ in data, controls,
  rendering or wrapper rules and cannot be silently merged.
- Similar games: Serious Sam HD and later DOOM games share direct movement,
  active weapon choice, real-time combat, finite ammunition, pickups, visible
  resources and authored exits.
- Important difference: Doom's armour spends a fraction of each hit and passes
  the remainder to Health immediately, so `SYS-949` fits and sequential
  armour-before-Health `SYS-655` does not.
- Claims: `UDOOM-001`, `UDOOM-006`, `UDOOM-011`.
