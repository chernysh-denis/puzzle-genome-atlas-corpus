# Game: Anno 1800

## Analysis scope

- Version / ruleset: base-game PC Game Update `18.4`, the final content update
  released 5 December 2024; ordinary single-player campaign at Normal settings,
  from the prologue through Chapter 4's final battle, then continuing the same
  save through construction of the World’s Fair and one completed exhibition.
- Included: seeded Old and New World sessions; campaign quests and discovery
  expedition; island settlement, roads and buildings; residence needs, tier
  upgrades and workforce; production chains, local carts, storage and cyclic
  ship routes; fertilities and deposits; treasury, influence and newspapers;
  fires, illness and riots; oil, railways and electricity; directly commanded
  naval combat; World’s Fair construction and exhibition preparation.
- Excluded: all gameplay and cosmetic DLC; multiplayer, Sandbox, scenarios and
  Creative mode; mods; customised difficulty or seeds; diplomacy optimisation,
  hostile island takeover outside required campaign combat; repeated exhibition
  reward farming; achievements and post-objective city optimisation.
- Direct-play status: not conducted. Official product, update and development
  sources establish the final version and base systems; maintained mechanics
  references corroborate exact campaign, population, route and monument gates.

## Strategic and experiential structure

- Local decision: place or configure one road, residence, farm, factory,
  warehouse, service, power plant, ship, cargo order or newspaper replacement.
- Medium-term planning: balance tiered needs and workforce, island-specific
  inputs, storage, treasury, route capacity, influence and incident coverage.
- Long-term structure: link Old and New World production, reach Engineers and
  Investors, resolve the campaign fleet battle, electrify the capital and feed
  the World’s Fair through one complete exhibition.
- Failure attribution: needs, workforce, storage, finance, production, routes,
  expedition morale, influence and monument panels reveal most bottlenecks;
  live ship movement and incidents preserve timing pressure.

## Replay and variation

- What changes: seed, island choice, fertility distribution, city and road
  layout, production ratios, trade fleet, expedition loadout, propaganda use,
  final-battle manoeuvres and exhibition choice.
- Randomness: the seed fixes initial geography; expedition event checks,
  incidents, newspaper articles and live combat introduce bounded variation.
- Multiple viable strategies: yes; island specialisation, route topology,
  population mix, power layout, influence allocation and exhibition supply vary
  under the same campaign-plus-monument boundary.

## Adjacent systems and history

- Similar games: SimCity 4, Cities: Skylines, Factorio, Frostpunk and Tropico 6.
- Important differences: Anno 1800 replaces zoning and direct worker assignment
  with hand-placed residences, tiered needs and island-wide workforce, then
  joins local physical production to player-authored interregional ship cycles.

## Adjacent comparison

- SimCity 4 and Cities: Skylines share roads, budgets, population unlocks and
  service coverage, but their private zoning and commuter simulations do not
  create Anno's explicit goods-fed residence tiers and cargo-slot sea routes.
- Factorio shares repeated recipes and live item logistics, but uses a research
  graph and automated rail schedules rather than consumer needs, influence,
  campaign expeditions and civic incidents.
- Frostpunk shares staffed buildings, city incidents, population survival and
  scenario chapters, but assigns finite workers directly and has no regional
  fertility/trade-route economy or naval combat.
- Anno 1800's distinguishing structure is the conversion of island-specific
  production into residence needs, new workforce tiers and wider regional
  supply chains, culminating in an electrified exhibition monument.

## Taxonomy impact

- Existing road, production, service, budget, placement and population-unlock
  genes retain their boundaries; this record supplies a second or additional
  evidence case without changing their classification.
- New genes are needed where Anno's decision boundary is not captured by the
  existing factory or city records: goods-fed residence tiers, island-wide
  tiered workforce, explicit cargo-slot sea cycles, influence/newspaper,
  expedition morale, direct naval combat and supplied exhibition phases.

## Negative results

- `CON-185` was rejected because Anno does not assign finite named residents to
  individual job slots; it balances abstract tier-specific workforce per island.
- `SYS-239` was rejected because Anno ships follow authored cyclic schedules
  rather than automatically matching supply and demand stations.
- `COMB-0117` was rejected because private zoned development is absent, and
  `COMB-0118` because a queued technology graph and launch objective are absent.
- Campaign completion alone was rejected as the endpoint because it omits the
  base game's Investor, electricity and World’s Fair progression.
