# Game: Dance Central

This original released-rule reconstruction is not native play, an imported
sequel track or verification of a current executable. Full integration and
exact-source acceptance are separate from the researched packet.

## Analysis scope

- Version / ruleset: original English Xbox 360 Kinect Dance Central, the
  2010 Microsoft instruction ruleset, one complete on-disc Poker Face
  Perform It! Easy routine. Flashcards enabled, Workout disabled, an active
  saving profile with Autosave enabled. Calibrate the display/sensor before
  entry; record existing profile achievements and lock state rather than
  pretending an attributed high-score recording is a clean profile.
  Installed title-update identity, disc region and native recognition
  tolerances are not established. The manual states a three-star harder-
  routine qualification; a secondary four-star statement is conflicting
  evidence, not permission to assert later executable parity.
- Structured analysis target: PLAT-XBOX-360; original released Kinect base
  game, not Dance Central 2/3 import, Spotlight or VR. Other-release audit
  not started. Kinect is this target's input requirement, not a new platform.
- Primary decision loop: read the current dancer, upcoming bodily move and
  tracked-player feedback; perform mirrored motion in musical time, correct
  mismatching limbs and preserve consecutive quality for multiplied credit;
  pass through the declared self-chosen Freestyle interval, resume the
  authored choreography and finishing move, inspect the complete performance
  evaluation and retain only the active profile's qualified progress.
- Entry and exit: the configured Poker Face / Perform It! / Easy PLAY!
  confirmation, through the entire authored routine including Freestyle,
  resumed choreography and finishing move to the settled results and
  active-profile save/eligibility boundary before another song or replay.
  A first successful move, Freestyle start or five live stars is not the end.
  A poor but visible dancer can reach the result; disappearance of all
  tracked players instead pauses the attempt.
- Included: physical limb/torso input, authored movement/timing evaluation
  with partial credit, quality bonus, consecutive-quality multiplier,
  current/future Flashcards and double-up warning, limb-local mismatch,
  tracked active-body authority, live quality/score/stars, no early miss
  failure, tracking-loss pause, Freestyle phase, final category report,
  profile-specific saving, rank and routine-option qualifications.
- Excluded: Break It Down training and its 50-percent alternative unlock,
  Dance Battle, simultaneous duet scoring, Workout/calorie estimation,
  challenge medleys, other songs as playable packets, DLC, imported sequel
  choreography, later-series free-for-all, VR, leaderboard service operation
  and achievements as required terminals. Photo capture/replay, venue,
  dancer appearance and costumes are presentation, not extra genome types.
  Disabling photos is not proof that original Freestyle can be removed.
- Potential scoped modules: instructor training with repeated trials and
  slowed demonstrations; alternating Dance Battle; a separately evidenced
  sequel's simultaneous tracking or revised freestyle scoring. None is
  inferred into the solo original routine.
- Direct-play status: no native play. P1 is the original Microsoft manual
  retrieved through an archival mirror; P2 is a shipped-game developer
  postmortem. S1/S2 are attributed contemporary played reviews, not our
  input trace. R1 supplies two actual paused video frames at 12/45 seconds
  and an official platform preview sequence sampled every two seconds from
  54–258 seconds. The inspected preview shows original selection, whole
  choreography, Freestyle, return, finishing move and a 439,003/five-gold-
  star result, but its 270-by-180 source frames do not establish exact
  movement detectors, scoring coefficients, recording configuration,
  save writes or subsecond timing. Upscaling adds no evidence resolution.
  The creator's 100-percent title is attribution, not an independently
  read result percentage. R2 is a separately attributed launch-week recording
  identified but not frame-inspected, and does not prove missing transitions.
  S6's indexed original-game Freestyle scoring assertion has Limited/Medium
  quality; exact credit and multiplier preservation are unresolved parameters.
  An uninspected imported-track recording is explicitly rejected as original
  executable evidence. All inspection used hidden, muted background processes
  and paused images; no audio, foreground playback or fullscreen was used.

### System Behaviour Genes

  evaluate eligible tracked input under that phase, produce graded credit
  and high-quality bonus, update consecutive multiplier and accumulated

  choreography resumes and final movement precedes settlement. Report the

## Strategic and experiential structure

- Local decision: prioritise upcoming timing while correcting the presently
  mismatching limb; choose legible mirrored motion over blind full-body
  improvisation outside Freestyle.
- Medium-term planning: learn the short repeated Easy vocabulary and upcoming
  transitions; retain consecutive quality rather than relying on a single
  exceptional move after a poorly prepared change.
- Long-term structure: compare settled score/quality and eligible routine
  options for the active profile. Harder routines and training are future
  packets, not automatically imported into this genome.
- Common heuristics: keep the whole active body visible, interpret red limbs
  as local correction rather than a real-world anatomical verdict, and use
  preview cards when the next demonstration is unfamiliar.
- Failure attribution: no forced miss failure makes errors recoverable within
  the same song. Recognition quality still depends on sensor visibility,
  calibration and clothing; no perfect detector or fairness guarantee claimed.
- Player-trust factors: explicit option qualifications and profile saving
  matter; photos are optional presentation. Present source-derived thresholds
  with the unresolved secondary conflict, not as current hardware guarantees.
- Claim IDs: DC-002–009. The recommendations are bounded interpretations of
  the documented intervention-response structure, not tested optimal play.

## Replay and variation

- What changes between sessions: physical motion, detected quality, scoring
  history and active-profile achievements; the selected Easy routine remains
  authored. Replays are not re-rolls of a hidden procedural song chart.
- Randomness or procedural generation: no random next-move generator is
  evidenced in this packet. Sensor variation is not proof of random rules.
- Multiple viable strategies: learn cards or visually follow the dancer;
  better preview preparation and local correction can coexist. Freestyle
  allows self-chosen movement, without an asserted optimal credit formula.
- Typical replay motive: improve score and graded result or qualify the next
  option. Multiplayer and training remain separately bounded modules.
- Claim IDs: DC-002–009.
