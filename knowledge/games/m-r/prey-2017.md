## Analysis scope

- Version / ruleset: current unmodified English Windows Steam application
  `480490`, one-app base package `107520`, public branch Build ID `5297220`,
  built 2020-07-16 and published 2020-07-23; checked 2026-09-05. The build ID
  is a secondary distribution observation, not a publisher semantic version.
- Platform, input and difficulty: Windows, English interface and subtitles,
  keyboard and mouse, base-game Campaign, fresh `New Game`, `Normal`, no
  installed Neuromods. Other difficulties, Survival options, New Game+,
  consoles and controller input are separate packets.
- Entry: begin a fresh campaign and follow only the mandatory opening until
  `Break Out` has placed the protagonist in Talos I Lobby. Retain a Quick Save
  at the first ordinary lobby control with the actual incoming inventory
  recorded: Wrench, GLOO Cannon and its current compatible canister state,
  TranScribe and Simulation Lab keycard, with no installed Neuromods, optional
  firearm, fabricated stock or side-route reward. Exact quantities are run
  parameters and must be copied from the retained state rather than invented.
- Primary decision loop: inspect the objective, local sight and suspicious
  clusters of ordinary props; walk, sprint, crouch and lean through the direct
  lobby route; select the Wrench or GLOO Cannon; test, reveal and engage a
  hostile that may currently copy a nearby prop; spend finite GLOO ammunition
  to impose temporary control, then commit a close Wrench strike when useful;
  collect only route-compatible ammunition or resources; read Jason Chang's
  authored note/workstation evidence, apply office code `0451`, enter Morgan's
  office and activate the Utilities video interaction required by `An Office
  with a View`. Narrative video content and audio are not evidence here.
- Positive terminal: wait until `An Office with a View` completes and `Through
  a Glass Darkly` is the current objective at ordinary office control. Create
  or accept the retained save, quit to the main menu, use `Continue`, `Load
  Game` or Quick Load and verify the same office control and successor
  objective without moving or interacting further.
- Negative terminal: Health reaching zero closes the current attempt and a
  chosen Quick Load or ordinary load restores a retained authored state; that
  recovery is not success. Revealing or defeating one hostile, entering the
  office, starting the video interaction or obtaining the successor objective
  without the reload verification is not positive settlement.
- Included: direct first-person traversal, crouch and lean; current objective
  and local visual threat information; Wrench and GLOO Cannon selection;
  GLOO aiming, finite canister state, reload and temporary control; direct
  Wrench combat; hostile disguise as an apparently ordinary nearby prop and
  revealed combat form; route-compatible pickups and inventory capacity;
  code discovery, office access, authored terminal interaction, continuous
  Health, lethal failure, quick/ordinary saving and reload verification.
- Excluded: Mooncrash, Typhon Hunter and every Digital Deluxe addition; New
  Game+, Survival options, non-Normal difficulty, other platforms or inputs;
  installing Neuromods, human or Typhon powers, Psychoscope, shotgun, pistol,
  fabrication, recycling, repair, hacking, turrets, operators, side quests,
  optional exploration, security systems, GLOO-created traversal platforms,
  alternate office routes, all play after the retained terminal, the later
  station and story, achievements, collectibles, mods, cheats, debug tools,
  speedrun skips, the 2006 game, franchise unions, screenshots, official art,
  third-party assets, video and audio evidence.
- Reproducible parameterisation: install English app `480490` from package
  `107520`; select a fresh Normal campaign, keyboard and mouse; create the
  declared lobby snapshot; follow the direct stairs/ledge route without GLOO
  platform construction; inspect duplicate-looking props, reveal at least one
  copied-prop hostile, apply GLOO until a temporary control state is observable
  and use the Wrench within the same encounter; read the fixed office-code
  evidence, unlock the office, commit the required Utilities interaction and
  perform the stated successor-objective save/reload check. Exact path, prop
  copied, ammunition, damage, pickups and encounter timing remain parameters.
- Potential scoped modules: one full Campaign route; one Neuromod build; one
  fabrication/recycling economy packet; one alternate lobby traversal; one
  later named quest; Mooncrash; or Typhon Hunter each requires its own version,
  entry, loop, terminal and evidence review.
- Direct-play status: not conducted. Valve application/package data and the
  Steam product page establish lawful availability and base/DLC separation.
  Bethesda's static product and equipment articles plus the official PC mini

## Strategic and experiential structure

- Planning horizon: the objective and authored office evidence identify a
  route, but ordinary prop appearance does not settle whether nearby space is
  safe; current GLOO and Health determine how much testing risk is affordable.
- Local tactics: compare duplicate-looking props, preserve retreat space,
  choose a low-cost test, place GLOO on the revealed target and time a Wrench
  strike before temporary control expires.
- Medium-term structure: conserve finite GLOO through the direct lobby ascent,
  avoid optional systems, convert in-world office evidence into code authority
  and retain the resulting successor objective in a reloadable state.
- Reversible versus irreversible: movement and inspection can be reconsidered;
  shots, reloads and damage change live resources; a save load replaces the
  failed continuation; the office interaction advances authored quest state.
- Failure attribution: Health, active equipment, ammunition, inventory,
  objective and access feedback separate lethal timing, empty equipment,
  missing authority and wrong route from the intentionally hidden identity.
- Player trust: the hostile's visible transformation resolves one local
  ambiguity, GLOO state exposes its temporary nature and a named successor
  objective plus reload makes the terminal independently testable.
- Claim IDs: `PR-004`–`PR-014`.

## Replay and variation

- What changes between attempts: copied prop and encounter state, reveal
  timing, movement line, GLOO expenditure, reload timing, Wrench strikes,
  damage, pickups and elapsed time.
- Randomness or procedural generation: the office route, code and objective
  order are authored; hostile disguise/position and live combat may vary within
  that route but this packet does not claim generated station topology.
- Multiple viable strategies: the game supports alternate traversal, weapon,
  power and fabrication routes, but this packet fixes the direct no-Neuromod
  Wrench/GLOO path so its transitions remain comparable.
- Typical replay motive: improve prop testing and scarce-control timing while
  preserving the same retained objective handoff.
