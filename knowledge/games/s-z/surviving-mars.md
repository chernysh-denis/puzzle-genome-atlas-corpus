# Game: Surviving Mars: Relaunched

## Analysis scope

- Version / ruleset: Windows standard edition patch 1.0.7, the latest released
  gameplay baseline confirmed before 21 August 2026; fresh ordinary surface
  mission from the first rocket landing through purchased independence from the
  selected mission sponsor.
- Included: landing-site and sponsor decisions; surface scanning, deposits and
  anomalies; drones, commanders, depots, construction and maintenance; power,
  water, oxygen and storage networks; production, research and Earth resupply;
  passenger rockets, domes, colonists, work, needs, births and deaths; ordinary
  surface disasters; Earth Council, Martian Assembly, factions, laws and the
  full declaration-to-payment independence chain.
- Excluded: Prime Mission pass, Interplanetary Codex sponsor, Feeding the Future
  and Machine Utopia; mods, Creative Mode and custom game rules; direct-play
  dependence on a particular random Mystery; exhaustive Underground, asteroid,
  rival, train, animal or terraforming routes; Wonders and technologies that
  are not required before independence; post-independence goals and monument.
- Direct-play status: not conducted. Current official release, feature diaries
  and developer patch notes establish the version and reproducible rule
  transitions; no community-only claim determines the admitted genome.

## Strategic and experiential structure

- Local: place one network segment or plan, configure a depot, priority, shift,
  rocket manifest, research queue entry or law option.
- Medium term: keep drone coverage, local repair parts, grid storage, dome life
  support, food, workforce and research stable across hazards and rocket cycles.
- Long term: grow a productive surface colony, replace fragile Earth imports,
  form the Martian Assembly, satisfy conflicting political gates, endure
  declaration penalties and finance the final independence payment.
- Common heuristics: overlap commander areas near shared depots, hold water,
  oxygen and power reserve, specialize domes without stranding labour, export
  rare metals before Earth restrictions, and prepare political support before
  calling a consequential vote.
- Failure attribution: overlays and panels separate no commander, missing
  material, unreachable route, utility deficit, overdue maintenance, empty
  shift, low colonist state, unrevealed technology, failed vote and unpaid
  independence price.

## Replay and variation

- What changes: sponsor and commander profile, landing coordinates, resource
  deposits, disasters, shuffled technology positions, breakthroughs, factions,
  law choices, Mystery and rival state.
- Randomness: maps, anomalies, hazards and later events vary; the physical
  network, law vote and purchased-independence gates remain inspectable.
- Multiple viable strategies: yes; imported or local resources, alternative
  dome specialization, transport, research, law and funding approaches can
  reach the same independence state.
- Typical replay motive: harder sponsor or site, different political system,
  optional Mystery, terraforming, Underground or asteroid emphasis.

## Adjacent systems and history

- Direct predecessor: Surviving Mars (2018), whose expansions are integrated
  and reworked in Relaunched.
- Similar games: Oxygen Not Included, RimWorld, Frostpunk, Timberborn, Workers
  & Resources: Soviet Republic and Captain of Industry.
- Important differences: drones are commander-range task agents rather than
  named settlers; water and oxygen are networked survival reserves; laws pass
  through political support rather than unilateral signing; the bounded end is
  a sponsor separation contract, not a storm, constructed ship or rocket launch.
