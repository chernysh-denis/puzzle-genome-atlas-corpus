## Analysis scope

- Version / ruleset: original English Xbox Series base game, full-release
  rules reconstructed for 2025-04-08, ordinary Weaver combat and traversal,
  no invincibility, auto-completion, combat or chase skips. No installed patch,

  established. Record health, acquired upgrades and held Floof at entry;
  inherited stock is a parameter, not an invented universal starting value.
- Structured analysis target: PLAT-XBOX-SERIES; original released base campaign,
  complete Chapter 3 A Big Fish. Other-release audit not started. The Premium
  access period is source context, not the selected prerelease target.
- Primary decision loop: inspect health, independently recovering spells,
  current route and local threat; move, strike, dodge or interrupt with Weave,
  Push and Pull; optionally Unravel defeated bodies or spend the arena coil;
  clear each Stigma, follow or leave the Guiding Strand for reachable rewards,
  spend affordable Floof on available development, construct timed Echo
  crossings and combine acquired traversal; collect the bottle and memories,
  survive the escape and heal the climbed tree to release Catfish.
- Entry and exit: first ordinary control after the Chapter 3 introduction in
  a continuing fresh released campaign that completed Chapter 2; all chapter
  arenas, optional reachable branches, Rhubarb's bottle, memories, pursuit,
  tree climb and wound healing through Catfish release and ordinary Chapter 4
  Wicked Temper control, confirmed at R1 1:26:00 after its transition scene.
  The first arena or bottle acquisition is not the end.
- Included: ordinary and held attacks, optional available combat development,
  lock-on, independent spell readiness, temporary binding and displacement,
  perfect-dodge blast, defeated-body recovery, one-use ordinary arena coil,
  purified-core recovery, health failure and authored checkpoint retry,
  timed Echo structures, acquired double jump, Glide and Wall Run, removable
  branches and positionable boxes/carts, optional Floof/first Life Filament,
  readable interactions, ordered memories/bottle gates and terminal healing.
- Excluded: Chapters 1–2 as playable packets, later chapters and bosses,
  Crouton possession, late Grapple, later locked development, chapter replay,
  achievements as required terminals, NG+, imported completed saves, custom or
  lower-difficulty unlimited coils, Early Access as target, ports and editions
  released later. Three-filament maximum-health growth cannot be completed
  from Chapter 3's first filament alone and is not a current-capacity grant.
- Potential scoped modules: later Crouton enemy control and grapple route;
  distinct Mythical Creature encounters; a separately evidenced difficulty
  or assistance configuration. None is inferred into this chapter signature.
- Direct-play status: no native execution. P1 is Compulsion's official release
