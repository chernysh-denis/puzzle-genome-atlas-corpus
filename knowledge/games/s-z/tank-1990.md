# Game: Tank 1990

Use the canonical [vocabulary, genome signature and comparison
rules](../../../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#canonical-vocabulary). Parameters describe
gene instances but do not enter the signature.

## Analysis scope

- Version / ruleset: the widespread 1990 Yanshan *Tank 1990* / “Tank 28”
  Famicom-compatible multicart family, narrowed to the one-player `Tank A`
  ordinary group represented by menu positions 1–14. This is a bounded rules
  reconstruction, not a claim that every cartridge labelled *Tank 1990* has
  the same program. The MAME software list separately records `tank1990` as a
  supported Chinese clone of *Battle City*, but marks both dumped regions
  `baddump`; its hashes therefore establish an archival identity, not byte
  equivalence with the selected multicart extraction.
- Entry: choose one player, `Tank A` in the ordinary 1–14 group and Stage 1;
  accept control with the home eagle intact, three lives, zero score and the
  ordinary initial tank state. No emulator save state, cheat, rewind or level
  select is active.
- Primary decision loop: steer the tank through the fixed top-down arena; aim
  and fire through corridors while enemy tanks enter from the bounded reserve;
  destroy eligible brick or vegetation cells to reshape lines; protect the
  eagle; hit a flashing enemy to create a random bonus and decide whether to
  detour for it; use typed bonuses, including the gun's maximum firepower and
  the boat's water access, to clear the remaining assault.
- Positive terminal: eliminate the stage's complete twenty-enemy reserve and
  reach the Stage 1 result / Stage 2 boundary with the base still intact.
- Negative terminal: an eligible projectile destroys the exposed eagle/base,
  or a lethal player-tank hit consumes the last remaining life. Earlier lethal
  hits consume one life and respawn the tank while the base remains intact.
- Included: direct four-direction tank movement and cardinal fire; fixed Stage
  1 terrain; brick, steel, water, vegetation and open cells; autonomous enemy
  tanks and bullets; the twenty-enemy reserve and fixed spawn points; flashing
  bonus carriers; random stage bonuses; grenade, helmet, clock, shovel, extra
  tank, star, gun and boat effects to the extent evidenced for this family;
  the boat water-entry capability; player lives; base destruction; stage HUD
  and one stage transition.
- Excluded: two-player play; menu variants `Tank B`–`Tank N`; the H–N group in
  which enemies may collect bonuses; the 15–28 hard group; later stages;
  *Missile Tank*; later rehacks and translations as rules authorities; speedrun
  or cheat behaviour; exact timing and drop weights not established by the
  sources; any claim that the unverified ROM hashes below are canonical.
- Direct-play status: not conducted. No cartridge, ROM, emulator run,
  controller trace, screenshot, video or audio was opened. The record combines
  MAME's archival identity, a Chinese collector's dated family description,
  independent written rule summaries, an open-source reimplementation and a
  pinned original-*Battle City* disassembly for inherited substrate only.

  Famicom-compatible hack derived from *Battle City* and widely redistributed

## Mechanical decomposition

### Action Genes

- Existing `ACT-008`: directly steer the tank one cardinal direction through
  currently traversable arena cells.
- Existing `ACT-161`: aim along the current cardinal facing and fire into a
  reachable enemy, projectile, terrain cell or exposed base.
- No new Action gene is admitted. Tank sprite, pad buttons, speed and projectile
  cadence are parameters. Claims: `T90-004`, `T90-005`.

### System Behaviour Genes

- Existing `SYS-004`: a qualifying marked-enemy event selects one result from
  the stage's bonus set; exact weights are unresolved.
- Existing `SYS-045`: enemy tanks move, turn, fire and collide without an
  individual player command.
- Existing `SYS-215`: movement, projectiles, enemy attacks, base exposure and
  pickups resolve on the same real-time arena state.
- Existing `SYS-755`: a qualifying projectile destroys the exposed eagle/base
  and closes the stage as a loss.
- Existing `SYS-863`: compatible projectiles remove eligible terrain cells and
  thereby open or close later fire and movement lines.
- New `SYS-908`: fixed spawn points feed enemy tanks from a bounded stage
  reserve until all twenty have entered and been resolved.
- New `SYS-909`: hitting a designated flashing enemy creates one random
  collectible bonus in the current arena instead of merely awarding score.
- New `SYS-910`: collecting a typed bonus mutates the current stage state,
  including firepower, protection, enemy freeze, base wall, life stock or
  water-entry capability.
- New `SYS-911`: a lethal hit consumes one finite player life and respawns a
  playable tank when the life stock and protected base still permit it.
- Resolution order: player and enemy commands advance; projectiles and
  collision resolve; terrain, tank or base state changes; a marked hit may
  create a random bonus; pickup applies its typed effect; reserve and terminal
  predicates are then evaluated. Claims: `T90-004`–`T90-010`.

### Constraint Genes

- New `CON-645`: a water cell cannot be entered by the ordinary tank; current
  boat capability admits that terrain class while the capability remains.
- Steel resistance, ordinary collision and exact fire-rate caps remain
  parameters of terrain and weapon systems rather than separate constraints.
  Claims: `T90-005`, `T90-007`.

### Information Genes

- New `INF-348`: one fixed top-down stage surface exposes the current arena,
  eagle, visible tanks, bullets and bonus plus stage, remaining enemy and life
  status; vegetation may occlude bodies and the next random bonus stays hidden.
- Exact HUD glyphs and palette are parameters. Claims: `T90-011`.

### Objective Genes

- Existing `OBJ-011`: preserve the eagle/base until the finite assault is
  cleared.
- Existing `OBJ-020`: eliminate the complete twenty-enemy stage reserve.
- Score is visible but no score threshold is required for the bounded success.
  Claims: `T90-009`, `T90-010`.

### Time Genes

- Existing `TIM-003`: player input, autonomous enemies and projectiles resolve
  in real time; no planning phase suspends the arena.
- Pausing, if supplied by a wrapper, is not a separate game phase. Claims:
  `T90-004`, `T90-008`–`T90-010`.

## Strategic and experiential structure

- Local decision: choose a corridor, firing line and timing against visible
  enemy tanks, bullets, terrain and any bonus.
- Medium horizon: reshape brick or vegetation, preserve cover around the base
  and decide whether a detour to a gun, boat or defensive bonus is worth the
  live assault pressure.
- Long horizon: spend lives and terrain safely enough to exhaust all twenty
  enemies without exposing the eagle.
- Reversibility: movement can be corrected while space remains; destroyed
  terrain, consumed bonuses, lost lives and removed enemies do not return in
  the same stage.
- Failure attribution: the fixed arena exposes projectile paths, base state,
  life stock and remaining assault; vegetation's occlusion and random future
  bonus identity remain declared uncertainty.
- Player trust: the same variant group, arena state and event order must produce
  the same transition except at the explicitly random bonus selection.

## Replay and variation

- Stage 1 geometry and the hostile reserve are fixed in the scoped variant;
  enemy movement and random bonus identity create changing local states.
- Different legal lines arise from terrain destruction, base-cover retention,
  bonus detours, gun timing, boat-enabled water routes and life preservation.
- Other Tank letters and the hard group are separate rulesets, not run
  variation inside this packet.

## Adjacent systems and history

- Original *Battle City* supplies the inherited fixed-arena base-defence
  substrate, but this unit does not silently copy every original rule into the
  hack. The admitted difference is the Yanshan family's expanded typed bonuses,
  especially gun and boat.
- *Bloons TD 6* also protects infrastructure from a finite assault, but the
  player places autonomous defences. Tank 1990 instead directly steers the
  firing body through the same live arena it defends.
- *Pac-Man* shares a fixed maze and autonomous hostiles, but it has no
  destructible firing terrain, protected base or bounded spawned tank reserve.
